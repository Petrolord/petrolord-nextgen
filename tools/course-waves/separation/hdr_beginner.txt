-- FC1 ASSOCIATE DEEP SEED: Separation & Slug Catching, beginner tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE STREAM AND THE SIMPLE VESSEL. What a sizing engine
-- does and what it refuses to guess (it holds no equation of state, so a
-- split at a stated pressure and temperature is a flash living somewhere
-- else); the gas at separator conditions, which is gauge to absolute, the
-- pseudo-criticals from gravity, the DAK z factor and the range it is honest
-- in, the density and the rate the vessel actually sees; the K value, its six
-- published rows, its pressure derating and its floor; settling, the two
-- liquid densities and the rate-weighted mixture the gas load sees; and the
-- vertical vessel, whose diameter comes from the gas and whose height comes
-- from the retention volume plus an allowance.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. z enters the chain TWICE and the two paths
-- oppose: the gas density goes as one over z, and the actual rate is scaled
-- BY z. The rate wins. A reader who follows only the density path concludes
-- that holding z at 1 flatters the design, and the engine says the opposite:
-- on ABANA-1 the gas-required diameter goes from 2.052551 ft to 2.100599 ft.
-- The Associate audit found that exact mistake keyed into a question and
-- glossed into a lesson, with every number on the page correct.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A separator is sized by asking which
-- requirement binds first, and almost every number on the page came from a
-- different requirement than the reader assumes. The diameter came from the
-- gas, the height came from the liquid, and the K that set the settling
-- velocity came from a table somebody copied.
--
-- HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded: the K derating of
-- 0.01 per 100 psi above 100 psig and the 0.12 floor are the customary rule
-- of thumb as this module records it, unchecked against the source. The
-- capstone states its own vendor K for that reason, so nothing graded in any
-- tier depends on the derating or the floor.
--
-- The only numbers that travel from this tier to the next are the conditions
-- chain, the settling velocity and the vertical vessel's dimensions.

# SECTION 1: What the sizing engine does, and what it refuses (owned by Associate m01)

# App surface: the Separator & Slug Catcher Designer runs this chain live. Conditions give z and the densities, the mist extractor gives K, K and the densities give the settling velocity, and the vessel follows from the settling velocity and the retention time.
- This engine sizes a VESSEL. It says how big the drum must be for the stream to separate in it. What LEAVES each stage of a separation train at a given pressure and temperature is a flash calculation and lives in engines/fluid/separator.js.
- A named input that is missing, not a number, or outside its domain throws a SeparatorInputError whose `input` property names the input. No default, clamp or fallback stands in for it.
- A state the inputs are valid for but the method is not (a z factor outside the DAK range, a settling velocity that is not positive, an area outside the circle) comes back as an object with an `error` string, not a throw.
- The retired Suite app it replaced hardcoded z at 0.85, used one K at every pressure, sized only two-phase vessels, and took its gas velocity from the diameter of the PREVIOUS render.

Refusals, engine messages verbatim:
- kValue with no mist extractor and no override: SeparatorInputError on internalsId: "internalsId is required: name a mist extractor from K_BASE or give kOverride"
- kValue with a mist extractor that is not in the table: SeparatorInputError on internalsId: "internalsId 'verticalFoam' is not a mist extractor in K_BASE"
- kValue with no pressure: SeparatorInputError on pPsig: "pPsig must be a finite, non-negative gauge pressure (got undefined)"
- kValue at a negative gauge pressure: SeparatorInputError on pPsig: "pPsig must be a finite, non-negative gauge pressure (got -20)"
- kValue with an override of zero: SeparatorInputError on kOverride: "kOverride must be a positive K in ft/s when it is given (got 0); leave it out to use the published table"
- gas density with no pressure: SeparatorInputError on pPsia: "pPsia must be a finite, positive absolute pressure (got undefined)"
- gas density with a temperature that is not a number: SeparatorInputError on tF: "tF must be a finite temperature in degF (got warm)"
- gas density with no gas gravity: SeparatorInputError on gasSg: "gasSg must be a finite, positive gas gravity with air = 1 (got undefined)"
- a liquid level of zero: SeparatorInputError on liquidLevelFrac: "liquidLevelFrac must lie strictly between 0 and 1 (got 0)"
- a liquid level of one: SeparatorInputError on liquidLevelFrac: "liquidLevelFrac must lie strictly between 0 and 1 (got 1)"
- a sweep over an empty diameter list: SeparatorInputError on diametersFt: "diametersFt must be a non-empty list of positive diameters in ft"
- a sweep whose band runs backwards: SeparatorInputError on ldMax: "ldMax must be a number no smaller than ldMin (got 3)"
- a slug catcher with a length-to-diameter ratio of zero: SeparatorInputError on ldRatio: "ldRatio must be a positive length-to-diameter ratio (got 0)"
- a slug catcher with a negative hold time: SeparatorInputError on holdMin: "holdMin must be a non-negative hold time in minutes (got -5)"
- a slug catcher with a negative normal liquid rate: SeparatorInputError on qLiquidBpd: "qLiquidBpd must be a non-negative liquid rate when it is given (got -100)"
- a harp with two and a half fingers: SeparatorInputError on nFingers: "nFingers must be a whole number of at least 1 (got 2.5)"
- three-phase sizing with the retired droplet argument: SeparatorInputError on dropletMicron: "dropletMicron was retired: give waterDropletMicron (water drops falling out of the oil) and oilDropletMicron (oil drops rising out of the water)"

States the method has no answer for, returned rather than thrown:
- an unknown sizing mode: { error: "unknown sizing mode 'cylindrical'" }
- a settling velocity of zero in a vertical vessel: { error: "a positive settling velocity is needed" }
- a gas denser than the liquid: { error: "settling needs a positive K and a liquid denser than the gas" }
- a segment area larger than the circle: { error: "the area must lie between zero and the full circle" }
- a slug catcher with no slug: { error: "a slug volume is needed (the line sizing studio computes it)" }
- a slug catcher filled to the brim: { error: "the fill fraction must be between 0 and 1" }
- a droplet with no viscosity to fall through: { error: "settling needs a droplet size and viscosity" }
- a light phase denser than the heavy phase: { error: "the heavy phase must be denser than the light phase" }
- a layout check over something that is not a list: { error: "a list of placed items is needed" }
- a distance with one coordinate pair: { error: "two coordinate pairs are needed" }
- a flare setback with no relief rate: { error: "a flare setback needs a relief rate and a heating value" }
- a pool fire with no pool: { error: "a pool diameter is needed" }

The rule takes 0.01 off K every 100 psi, so 50 psig more of operating pressure puts that vessel on the floor. nearFloor is true when one more 100 psi step of that same rule would floor the value, so it marks the approach to the cliff that floored marks the edge of, and the two are never true together.

# HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded: the derating of 0.01 per 100 psi above 100 psig, and the 0.12 floor, are the customary rule of thumb as this module records it. The published form has not been checked against the source, so a vendor K is the only honest input where the derating bites.

# HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded: a horizontal vessel uses the Souders-Brown velocity at the horizontal K as the droplet SETTLING velocity in the gas length requirement. That packaging has not been checked against API 12J or Arnold and Stewart.
A consequence the engine gate pins: the gas length is the gas velocity over the settling velocity, times the gas height. Under the capacity rule that ratio is at most 1, so the gas length is at most the gas HEIGHT, and gas can control only a vessel that is gas overloaded or shorter than its own diameter.

# SECTION 17: What the method does not know (owned by Expert m05)

Four things this course teaches as limits and never as answers:
1. The K derating of 0.01 per 100 psi above 100 psig and the floor at 0.120000 are a rule of thumb recorded here, not a checked publication. At 600.000000 psig the vertical mesh K falls from 0.350000 to 0.300000, which moves every vessel dimension that follows it.
2. A horizontal vessel borrows the Souders-Brown velocity at the horizontal K as its droplet settling velocity. If the published method sizes the gas length from a droplet diameter instead, the gas length and every conclusion drawn from it changes.
3. The spacing table and the radiation labels are recorded values with no source checked. A site standard replaces the table wholesale.
4. The published cases in these two goldens are SYNTHETIC. They come from an independent oracle written in Python from the same physics, in SI units where the engine works in field units, which catches an arithmetic or a unit error and cannot catch a method that is wrong in both files. No measured separator is in this course.

The DAK range is enforced rather than extrapolated, and the two directions are treated differently:
- a cold gas below the range: refused, "Tpr 0.848 is below the DAK validity range of 1.0 to 3.0: the z-factor would be an extrapolation below the critical temperature, so it is refused"

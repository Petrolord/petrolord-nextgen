-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42

--
-- What this tier owns: THE GAS AND THE FLARE. A standard cubic foot is a count
-- of moles, so the analysis is read by the mole: a sheet that does not sum to
-- one is scaled and named; the heating value, the carbon and the mass in a
-- thousand cubic feet are mole-weighted; the liquids in the gas are derived
-- from the composition and the liquid densities, and a missing density leaves
-- them missing. The flare follows 40 CFR 98.233(n): the CO2 in the gas passes
-- through, the hydrocarbon carbon burns at the combustion efficiency, and the
-- methane that escapes is the methane in the gas at one less the destruction
-- efficiency.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The flare's two efficiencies set two
-- different species: destruction sets the methane, combustion sets the CO2,
-- and neither has a default.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Every unburned carbon counted as methane, the
-- gas's CO2 burned as fuel, and a blank efficiency read as a perfect flare each
-- give a figure that looks finished.
--
-- HELD, taught as a limit and never graded: the efficiency tiers (H1), the
-- unlit flare (H2), the GWP as a case input (H4). Every figure is invented and
-- illustrative. The capstone grades six engine figures, three at 1e-4 and the
-- flare's three tonnages at 0.01 t.

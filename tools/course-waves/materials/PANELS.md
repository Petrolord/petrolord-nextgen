## AN APP COURSE, AND THE PANELS

The Suite app for this course is the Materials & Spares Planner, in the
Suite's Midstream & Downstream module, which vendors the same engine. The
three calculator panels below carry every practical inside the course, so a
learner without a Suite seat does every exercise: they call the vendored
inventory engine on the Ekene register or on whatever the learner pastes, and
the learning page says so in plain words and names the Planner.

## WHAT IS SHIPPED

Under `src/components/course/panels/materials/` in the NextGen repository:

* `materialsLab.js`, the ONE teaching lab. It imports the vendored Ekene
  register (`packages/engines/test-data/supplychain/ekene-materials`), carries
  thin routes to the engine for whatever a learner types, the start cases, the
  block reader and the stated-input writer. It holds no graded answer, no
  tolerance and no capstone case.
* `gradedTolerance.js`, **the only place a grading tolerance is made**:
  `max(stated, half a unit in the last place the course prints that class)`.
  `fields.json` and `precision.json` are both written out of it.
* `gradedAnswerGuard.js`, every string shape a graded answer can reach a
  learner as.
* `RegisterCalculator.jsx` (`materials-register-calculator`),
  `StockCalculator.jsx` (`materials-stock-calculator`),
  `SparesCalculator.jsx` (`materials-spares-calculator`) and their shared bits.

The learning page is `src/pages/apps/MaterialsLearningPage.jsx`. The panels are
registered in `src/content/courses/panelRegistry.js`, and
`panelRender.test.jsx` renders every panel. The capstone case files are
generated by `gen_course.py --cases` into
`src/content/capstone-cases/materials` and are offered on the capstone card
only.

## THE THREE CALCULATORS

Every view takes the learner's own inputs, because that is how a capstone is
worked. A case file holds several calls under BLOCK KEYS: the view's own name
(`eoq`, `insuranceSpares`) or the view's name, a colon and a name
(`safetyStock:fill-rate`). Every view offers the blocks of a pasted case file
in a block selector, runs the block chosen, and writes every control into that
block. Every view shows the engine's own refusal and reasons, verbatim, and the
engine's basis.

EVERY REQUIRED STATED INPUT HAS A VISIBLE CONTROL, and no panel holds a hidden
default. A control shows the input as the box states it, or "not stated", and
writes the learner's choice into the box; an input left unstated reaches the
engine unstated and the engine refuses by name. Each view carries a start
selector over the Ekene register's stated cases and a blank case.

### `materials-register-calculator` (Associate)

Criticality (each item's weighted score, contributions and class, the override
named: PSV-KIT 68.000000, class V), ABC by annual usage value under either
boundary rule (CEM-G at 83.536840), the EOQ with its stated rounding (BARYTE
137.408584, ordered as 140.000000) and slow-moving stock (a total write-down of
97955.000000). Controls for the criteria and weights, the score scale, the
class minimums, the override list, the cut-offs, the boundary rule, the demand,
the order cost, the holding rate or holding cost, the unit cost, the rounding
rule and multiple, the bands and write-downs and the cover limit.

### `materials-stock-calculator` (Professional)

Quantity discounts in both types with every candidate (CSG-958: 349720.000000
all-units, 365590.042553 incremental), safety stock at a cycle service level or
a fill rate with continuous or periodic review (CHK-BEAN: k 1.644854, a
reorder point of 13.316294 held as 14.000000), and Poisson stock for a slow
mover (PSV-KIT: a level of 5). Controls for the demand and its spread, the lead
time and its spread, the review period, the service measure and level, the
order quantity, the safety-factor rounding and floor, the rounding rule, the
price breaks and the discount type.

### `materials-spares-calculator` (Expert)

Insurance spares with every option from 0 to the search limit (the ESP motor:
4 spares at 160003.732064 a year), Poisson stock for a spare, and lead-time risk
by the canonical Monte Carlo, always printed with the seed and the draws and
marked never graded (the mechanical seal: a stockout probability of 0.058600 on
seed 20270301 and 20000 draws), with the P90 shown as the low figure and the
engine's P-label sentence verbatim. Controls for the failure rate, the lead
time in days, the days a year, the unit cost, the holding rate, the downtime
cost, the search limit, each triangle's minimum, mode and maximum or a
constant, the reorder point, the service level, the seed and the draws.

## THE RULES FOR EVERY PANEL

1. **No graded capstone answer, in any of four shapes**, no capstone name,
   label or input. `panelCapstoneGuard.test.js` sweeps the lab, the
   calculators, the bits and the learning page.
   `panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads every graded field off the engine
   with each capstone case pasted, at full precision.
2. **No refusal message is written in a panel or the lab.** Show `r.error`, and
   a reason as the engine returns it.
3. **No clock, no timer, no random number, no path under `/root`.** The one
   Monte Carlo runs inside the engine on the stated seed.
4. **No lecture prose.** A label or a note in a panel is the course's own
   words; `gate_no_ocw_prose.py` sweeps the panels and the page.
5. **The copy rule.** No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastive, no
   "rather than", no ", never", no "instead of", no "and not", no "and never".
   `gate_copy_rule.py` sweeps the panels and the page as well as the digest.
6. **The vocabulary**, digest section 30; no "AI".

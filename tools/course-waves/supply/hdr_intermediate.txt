-- tier.

-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE RACK, THE TANK FARM AND THE LANE. A loading rack is
-- an M/M/c queue, so the probability of waiting is Erlang C and a rack near
-- full has a queue; bays are whole numbers; pumpable stock is counted tank by
-- tank above each tank's own heel; a loss has a weight only with a density;
-- trips a truck a day come from the cycle, and the cost of a litre delivered
-- is spread over what arrives; a fleet is a ceiling; and a forecourt is the
-- same queue as a rack.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. A queue has no average wait once arrivals
-- reach what the bays can load, and the engine says so instead of printing a
-- number.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Averages hide the queue: at a utilisation
-- below one a nearly full rack still makes a truck wait more often than not
-- (0.787753 at IBAFO's 0.900000), a heel netted across the farm fills one
-- tank's heel from another tank's pumpable stock, which no pump can do, and a
-- blank cost box is a missing cost.
--
-- The capstone grades a probability to four decimals (tolerance 0.0001), a
-- wait in minutes, a pumpable stock in m3 and days of cover to two decimals
-- (tolerance 0.01), a cost per litre delivered to four decimals (tolerance
-- 0.0001) and a whole number of trucks (tolerance 0.5).

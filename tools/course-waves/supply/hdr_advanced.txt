-- supply EXPERT DEEP SEED: Terminals, Depots & Fuel Supply, advanced tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE CARGO AND THE PRICE. One cargo in tonnes, cubic
-- metres, litres and barrels through a required density; the landed cost
-- walked stage by stage from FOB through C&F and CIF, each charge on the base
-- it names; insurance quoted on CIF solved in closed form; the ocean loss
-- dividing the cost over the outturn; the pump price as a waterfall from the
-- depot gate; the government's share; and the exchange rate at which a cap
-- stops covering the chain.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The engine ships no rate. Every duty, levy,
-- margin and tax in this course is invented and says so, and a build-up with
-- a rate missing is a floor.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. The order of the build-up is part of the
-- answer: a percentage bites on the base it names, a charge cannot bite on a
-- base the walk has not reached, and a loss added as a charge understates
-- the cost of a litre sold.
--
-- HELD, taught as a limit and never graded: charges levied at discharge are
-- billed on the bill-of-lading quantity, a contract term the engine does not
-- know (FINDINGS-supply H1). The capstone grades two dollar figures and an
-- exchange rate to two decimals (tolerance 0.01) and three naira-a-litre
-- figures to four decimals (tolerance 0.0001).

-- table entry, no coefficient and no opening stock is ever read as a zero,
-- and each is refused in the engine's own words.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A reconciliation whose opening stock is taken
-- from today's own dip balances every day, so it measures nothing. The gap is
-- the point of the day.
--

-- shows the form on SYNTHETIC coefficients and corrects its stocks with a VCF
-- typed off the terminal's own tables. The capstone grades six volumes in m3,
-- each to two decimals, at a tolerance of 0.01 m3.

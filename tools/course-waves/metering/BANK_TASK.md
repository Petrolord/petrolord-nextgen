# FC8 `metering`: the question bank writer's task

Read `BRIEF.md` and `LESSON_TASK.md` first. Nothing here overrides them.

## VERIFY THIS FILE YOURSELF RATHER THAN TRUSTING IT

Check the counts against `structure.py` and `wave.json`, and check every figure
you use against `digest.txt`. A brief in this programme has more than once told
a writer something the files did not say.

## THE ONE DEFECT THAT MATTERS MOST HERE

Twenty five live courses in this Academy ship banks that are **passable without
reading**, because the second longest option scored around eighty percent of the
time. The cause was mechanical: the correct option was written carefully and at
length, and the distractors were written quickly and short.

So the rule is **LENGTHEN, NEVER SHORTEN**. If the correct option is longer than
the distractors, make the distractors longer. Never trim the correct option to
match, and never edit a correct option to satisfy a length gate. A gate that is
satisfied by damaging the correct answer has made the bank worse.

## WHAT A GOOD DISTRACTOR IS IN THIS COURSE

This course is unusually well supplied with them, because the wave already
measured which wrong methods are plausible. `discriminate.mjs` carries, for each
graded field, a set of named wrong methods that a competent learner could
actually commit, and every one of them was checked to move the answer. The same
error shapes are the right distractors for bank questions:

* a quantity left in the wrong unit;
* a table value used where the project stated its own;
* an engine default used where a value was stated;
* a boundary case sized on the stated drop rather than on the drop the valve can
  use;
* a screening threshold quoted in place of the quantity it screens;
* a count quoted without the tree and the rule that made it.

A distractor that is simply a different number with no story behind it teaches
nothing.

## WHAT A QUESTION MAY NOT DO

* It may not ask for one of the eighteen graded capstone answers, at any
  rounding or in any unit.
* It may not use a capstone plant name or a capstone condition.
* It may not ask for the required emergency fire vent capacity or the
  straight-run requirement for two elbows in different planes. Both are refused
  by the engine by name. A question whose correct answer is "the engine refuses
  and here is why" is excellent; a question that asks the learner to produce
  either number is a defect.
* It may not state a relationship between two figures that the digest did not
  print.

## WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM

`digest.txt`, at the precision it prints. If a question needs a figure the
digest does not carry, the question is out of scope. Do not compute one.

`RECON.md`, `FINDINGS.md`, the vendored `FINDINGS-metering.md` and the engine
source comments are PROVENANCE. They record what the repair found rather than
what the engines now do, and no figure and no claim from any of the four may
reach a question, a distractor or an explanation.

## THE COPY RULE

No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastives, in stems, options or
explanations.

## BEFORE YOU HAND BACK

* The bank reproduces from its source.
* The answer-length check is clean, and it was made clean by lengthening.
* No graded capstone answer is reachable from any question, at any of the unit
  shiftings `wave.json` lists.

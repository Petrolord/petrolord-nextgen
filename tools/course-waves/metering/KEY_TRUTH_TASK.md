Read `BRIEF.md` first.

## VERIFY THIS FILE YOURSELF RATHER THAN TRUSTING IT

Every claim below about what the engines do was read out of `digest.txt`, which
was built by calling them. Read the digest section named beside each claim
before you write about it. `RECON.md`, `FINDINGS.md`, the vendored
`FINDINGS-metering.md` and the engine source comments are PROVENANCE and none of
the four is teaching truth, so no key truth may rest on anything in them.

## WHAT A KEY TRUTH IS

One sentence a learner should still be able to say a year later, tied to a
digest section that proves it. It is not a summary of the lesson and it is not a
slogan. If you cannot point at the digest line that settles it, it is not a key
truth.

## THE SHAPE OF THIS COURSE, WHICH THE KEY TRUTHS SHOULD CARRY

Three engines, one argument: the interesting behaviour lives at a boundary, and
the most valuable thing one of these engines does is sometimes to refuse.

* **Associate.** The orifice equation is easy. The discharge coefficient is not
  a constant and the engine will not assume one (SECTION 4). A transmitter's
  accuracy is quoted on span and its contribution to a reading rises as the
  reading falls (SECTION 11). A differential turndown and a flow turndown are
  different numbers and the customary rule is stated in the wrong one of them
  more often than not (SECTION 12). Which term dominates an uncertainty budget
  is a result the engine computes, and it changes down the span (SECTION 14).
  A turbine volume is gross and the package carries nothing that would make it
  net (SECTION 15).

* **Professional.** Past the choking boundary a larger coefficient buys nothing,
  and sizing on the stated drop undersizes the valve (SECTION 17). The
  cavitation index belongs to the drop the valve can actually use (SECTION 18).
  Flashing and cavitating are different problems with different fixes
  (SECTION 19). The expansion factor floors at two thirds and that floor is the
  choked condition (SECTION 20). Every FL and xT in the module is the engine's
  own table and a certified vendor figure replaces it (SECTION 21). A verdict
  reported over checks that did not run is not a verdict (SECTION 24).

* **Expert.** A light product makes the water test govern the shell rather than
  the product (SECTION 26). Vacuum is the venting case that destroys tanks, and
  which direction governs is one predicate computed once (SECTION 27). The fire
  duty is computed and the vent capacity is refused by name, because the two
  plausible forms of the missing relation differ by a factor of about twenty
  four (SECTION 28). What evaporates is both lost product and a reportable
  release, and it is the same arithmetic (SECTION 29). Fifteen items in these
  three engines are not carried or not cited, and the course grades none of them
  (SECTION 31).

## RULES

* Every figure comes from `digest.txt` at the precision it prints.
* No relationship between two figures unless the digest printed it.
* Engine messages are quoted verbatim inside backticks.
* No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastives.
* No capstone plant, condition or graded answer, ever.

## WHAT A KEY TRUTH MUST NOT BE

It must not be a number a learner is expected to remember. This course grades
eighteen numbers and teaches none of them as facts to memorise. A key truth is a
thing that stays true when every input changes.

-- the fall distance.

-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The holdup MOVES the drum length. The level
-- sets the vapour cross-section as the circular segment above it and the fall
-- distance as the vapour depth, two different functions of one fraction, so a
-- reader who uses one factor for both has a drum that is exactly right at half
-- full and wrong everywhere else.
--
-- The fire DUTY is never graded: 21000, 34500 and the 0.82 exponent are held
-- for literature. The capstone grades the GEOMETRY the duty stands on, and the
-- drum states its own dropout velocity and actual vapour rate, so no graded
-- value reads the sphere-drag correlation or a standard-condition base.

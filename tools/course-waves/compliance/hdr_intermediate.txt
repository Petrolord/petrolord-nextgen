-- compliance PROFESSIONAL DEEP SEED: Compliance, Audit & Quality, intermediate
-- tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE PLAN, THE NONCONFORMANCE AND THE CHECKLIST. A hold
-- point stops work and a witness point does not; a decision is a date and a
-- named verifier, and a waiver carries its reason; progress is counted from
-- the points and from the answers, never typed; a plan, an NCR, an audit and a
-- programme each close only through a gate that names what is missing; an
-- auditor may not audit their own area; and an open NCR ages until it closes.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. A request is not a result. Removing a point,
-- waiving it or passing it goes through a gate first, and a refused request
-- changes nothing on the plan.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. "Not applicable" is an answer only with its
-- reason, a failed point is the most outstanding item on a plan, and a
-- cancelled audit is not a delivered one.
--
-- ONE RULE, NOW: an audit cancelled without a written reason is outstanding in
-- programmeProgress, canCompleteProgramme and summarise alike, and every
-- percent rounds half up on the exact fraction (ASC-0). The capstone grades
-- four engine percents and two NCR ages, at a tolerance of 0.5.

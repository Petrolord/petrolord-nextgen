-- compliance ASSOCIATE DEEP SEED: Compliance, Audit & Quality, beginner tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE REGISTER AND THE CALENDAR. A calendar date is a
-- day read at local midnight and an unreadable date is no date; an
-- obligation's status is derived from its lifecycle and its dates in a fixed
-- precedence, and the reason comes with it; the lead time decides when "Due
-- soon" starts and the current period decides whether a filing still counts;
-- the schedule rolls forward from the date that was due; and a controlled
-- document earns its review date when it is issued, never when it is
-- corrected.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. One as-of date is an INPUT. Every status in
-- the register is a comparison against it, and the engine is given it rather
-- than reading a clock.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Two dates count down at once. A permit's
-- expiry and a return's due date sit on the same row, the earlier one drives
-- the warning, and a reader who counts to the due date misses a permit that
-- lapses first.
--
-- HELD, taught as a limit and never graded: the modules other than
-- complianceStatus answering an unreadable today as though nothing were due.
-- A filed One-off reads "nothing further is due" (ASC-0). The capstone grades
-- six whole numbers of days, each an engine return, at a tolerance of 0.5.

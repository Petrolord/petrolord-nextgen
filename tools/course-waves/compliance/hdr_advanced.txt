-- compliance EXPERT DEEP SEED: Compliance, Audit & Quality, advanced tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM. A conformity claim is evidence,
-- a date and a name; an auditor may not audit their own work, and ISO 19011 is
-- checked for every examiner; coverage is counted over the certification cycle
-- from reported internal audits only; a major nonconformity closes on its
-- correction, its root cause and a corrective action shown to work; and
-- certification readiness is a list of blockers.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. Readiness is not a percentage. Every blocker
-- is counted and named, and the certificate is an item on the list rather than
-- a gate on it.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. An examination is not coverage until the audit
-- that made it is internal and reported. A later examination by the
-- certification body, by an audit still in progress or by one later cancelled
-- does not move a clause's last examined date.
--
-- HELD, taught as a limit and never graded: the expiring flag that reads true
-- on a lapsed certificate (R3), the ISO 9001 citation printed whatever the
-- standard (R5), and the owner decisions of AS15 as stated policy. The capstone
-- grades two coverage counts and four day counts.

-- carbon EXPERT DEEP SEED: Carbon & Energy Efficiency, advanced tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE CURVE AND THE PATH. The cost of a tonne abated,
-- capital annualised over the measure's life by a capital recovery factor; a
-- negative cost that pays for itself; the marginal abatement cost curve ranked
-- cheapest first with its weighted average; measures that interact and claims
-- above what a source emits; a target on a partial inventory; the path and its
-- unabated gap; one saving priced in money and carbon from the same energy;
-- energy intensity against a peer the plant has the right to use.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. A blank capital cost is not free, a blank
-- rate is not 0, a rate is a fraction, and a curve whose claims exceed what a
-- source emits gives no verdict.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A one-off capital cost set against one year's
-- saving overstates the cost per tonne of a capital measure.
--
-- No NPV, IRR, Monte Carlo or decision tree is taught or graded; the levelised
-- cost per tonne is. The capstone grades four costs in US dollars per tonne and
-- one net annual cost in US dollars at 0.01, and one unabated gap in tCO2e at
-- 0.01.
-- ============================================================================

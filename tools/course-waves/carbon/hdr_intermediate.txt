-- trap as choked flow with its isentropic exponent; condensate return and its

-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The basis is declared and carried. A safe
-- oxygen floor, a radiation loss, a discharge coefficient, an exponent and a
-- boiler efficiency are asked for and never assumed.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Subtracting two efficiency percentages
-- divides the gap by a hundred instead of by the target efficiency, and
-- understates what tuning is worth.
--
-- The capstone grades two percents at 0.0001, a fuel saving to the whole GJ
-- at 1 GJ, a steam loss in tonnes a year at 0.01, and two utilities in kW at

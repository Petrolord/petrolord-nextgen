# EC10 Farm-ins, Farm-outs & Asset Valuation: the calculator panels

Read `BRIEF.md` first. Every number here is quoted from `digest.txt`, the only

## AN ENGINE COURSE

There is no Suite app for this course. The three calculator panels below ARE
the course's practicals: they call the vendored farm-out engine on the Ekene
Deep prospect or on whatever the learner pastes, and the learning page says so
in plain words.

## WHAT IS SHIPPED

Under `src/components/course/panels/farmout/` in the NextGen repository:

* `farmoutLab.js`, the ONE teaching lab. It imports the vendored Ekene fixture
  (`packages/engines/test-data/economics/ekene-farmout`) and the
  INPUTS of the vendored golden file, carries thin routes to the engine for
  whatever a learner types, the view routes, the stated-input writer
  `setStated`, a JSON parser and the teaching readers. It holds no graded
  answer, no tolerance and no capstone case.
* `gradedTolerance.js`, **the only place a grading tolerance is made**:
  `max(stated, half a unit in the last place the course prints that class)`.
  `fields.json` and `precision.json` are both written out of it.
* `gradedAnswerGuard.js`, every string shape a graded answer can reach a
  learner as.
* `EarningCalculator.jsx` (`farmout-earning-calculator`), `DealCalculator.jsx`
  (`farmout-deal-calculator`), `ValuationCalculator.jsx`
  (`farmout-valuation-calculator`) and `panelBits.jsx`.

The learning page is `src/pages/apps/FarmoutLearningPage.jsx`, routed at
`/dashboard/apps/farmout`. The panels are registered in
`src/content/courses/panelRegistry.js`, and `panelRender.test.jsx` renders
every view of every panel. The capstone case files are generated by
`gen_course.py --cases` into `src/content/capstone-cases/farmout` and are
offered on the capstone card only.

## THE THREE CALCULATORS

Every view takes the learner's own inputs as JSON, because that is how a
capstone is worked. Every view reads what is pasted through the lab's view
routes, which apply `pick` at the key the view needs (earning, deal, fee,
information, risk, price, devCarry, backIn), so a whole case file pasted into
any view runs that view's block and a box that holds one call's inputs runs as
it stands. Three view keys (deal, information, backIn) are also input keys of
their own engine calls, so `pick` reads a box at the view's key only when the
box is a case file (it carries `dataset`) or the block holds the key its call
cannot run without (`project`, `side`, `earnedPct`). `farmoutLab.test.js` proves every view gives a whole case file the
result of its block alone, and `panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads all
eighteen graded fields with each case file pasted WHOLE, as the capstone card
offers it. Every view shows the engine's own refusal and reasons, verbatim,
and a Source block with the engine's `basis.source`.

EVERY REQUIRED STATED INPUT HAS A VISIBLE CONTROL, and no panel holds a hidden
default. A control shows the term as the box states it, or "not stated", and
writes the learner's choice into the box through `setStated`; a term left
unstated reaches the engine unstated and the engine refuses by name. Each view
carries a start selector over the Ekene cases and the small cases the digest
teaches.

### `farmout-earning-calculator` (Associate)

The earning obligation of one event with its promote, carry and the other
parties' shares (on the Ekene well FIN pays 18200000.000000, EKO
14000000.000000 and PA 13800000.000000); the cash bonus and the reimbursement
with the consideration (10000000.000000) and the equivalent working interest
(51.739130 percent); the interests after the deal (EKO 40.000000, PA 30.000000,
FIN 30.000000). Controls: the share paid, the interest earned, the gross cost,
the cap and its overrun rule, the vesting rule, the events completed, the cash
bonus and the past costs.

### `farmout-deal-calculator` (Professional)

Caps and drill-to-earn events with the vesting rule; the value of the deal to
each side (EKO 18418808.982316 alone and 19833033.704181 after the farm-out,
FIN -1806224.721864); the break-even promote (35.594574 percent) and the
break-even chances; the consent fee (392000.000000) with its day rules. Controls
for every deal term, the chance and the well costs, the fee basis, the licence,
the value of the transaction and its source, the intra group flag and the
dates.

### `farmout-valuation-calculator` (Expert)

The value of information to either side (6745331.458602 to FIN); risk sharing
through the seeded portfolio Monte Carlo, whose figures are estimates and are
never graded; the price of an interest (263125.842605 a percent risked); a
development carry with a visible control for the uplift type and, for each
type, its terms: the rate and the day basis of the simple uplift, the rate of
the compound uplift, the multiple; the back-in after the farm-in; the deal
reused from the deal calculator; and the readings, each printed from the
engine's basis on the golden input where it acts.

## THE RULES FOR EVERY PANEL

1. **No graded capstone answer, in any of four shapes**, no capstone name,
   label, term or series. `panelCapstoneGuard.test.js` sweeps the lab, the
   calculators, the bits and the learning page, and plants each shape to prove
   the sweep catches it. `panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads every graded
   field off a rendered panel with the capstone case pasted, at full precision.
2. **No refusal message is written in a panel or the lab.** Show `r.error`, and
   a reason as the engine returns it.
3. **No clock, no random number, no path under `/root`.** The one Monte Carlo
   runs inside the engine on the stated seed.
4. **The copy rule.** No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastive, no
   "rather than", no ", never", no "instead of". `gate_copy_rule.py` sweeps the
   panels and the page as well as the digest.
5. **The vocabulary**, digest section 28; no "AI".

THE ONLY NUMBERS A LESSON MAY QUOTE. Every line names its source: a PUBLISHED
golden value out of packages/engines/test-data/production/goldens/rodpump_cases.json,
a DERIVED re-run or sweep on published inputs, or the one TEACHING WELL this
wave designed for itself. NOTHING in this file comes from the graded capstone.
The capstone conditions and its eighteen graded answers live in separate files
that the generator of this digest never opens.

A leak guard, pd4_leakcheck.mjs, reads the capstone graded field list and every
number on every line of this file and rejects the digest if any number lands
within TEN TIMES a graded field tolerance of that field value, in seven unit
shiftings: the three the wave standard requires (as printed, times 1000, times
0.001) and four this domain needs (times 100, times 0.01, times 12 for inches
per foot, divided by 12 for feet per inch), because inches against feet and a
percentage against a fraction are the live confusions in a rod pump digest.
academy_submit_capstone grades with abs(got - expected) <= tol, so tol is
ABSOLUTE in the field own units and is not a fraction of anything. The guard
lives in its own file and the generator never imports the capstone.

WHAT PROVENANCE LABEL MEANS WHAT
  golden ...    a value committed in rodpump_cases.json by the independent
                stdlib oracle: a finite-element eigenvalue for the string note,
                a Newton four-bar closure with implicit differentiation for the
                linkage, a staggered-grid RK4 march for the card, and Python
                complex numbers for the diagnostic. Four different routes on
                purpose. Its gates hold the two routes to 2 percent on plunger
                stroke and 3 percent on the minimum load, so anything smaller
                than that is invisible to the oracle by construction.
  derived ...   the shipped engine re-run on PUBLISHED inputs, or a sweep this
                generator ran around them. A sweep point is not a published
                case. Say so if you print one.
  teaching ...  the teaching well ODUMA-4, its design, and every sweep on it.
                Invented by this wave to carry the Expert results. Not a
                published case, not a real well, and never to be shown as
                either.

WHERE A QUANTITY IS COMPUTED TWICE, THE SECTION HEADER SAYS SO AND THE TWO
LABELS DIFFER. Four quantities in this file have two honest routes:
  the plunger stroke, from the spring rule and from the wave march (S12);
  the peak polished rod load, from prlPeakLb and from the tension envelope (S18);
  the fundamental note, from the engine scan and from a dense mode scan (S5);
  the worst section loading, as runRodPumpDesign returns it and as this
  generator recomputes it standalone from the same card (S19, S20, S23).
Never quote one of a pair without saying which route it came from.

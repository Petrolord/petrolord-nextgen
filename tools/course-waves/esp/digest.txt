THE ONLY NUMBERS A LESSON MAY QUOTE. Every line names its source: a published
golden case, a published catalogue entry, a published engine gate fixture, or
one of three teaching cases designed for this wave and labelled as one.
NOTHING here comes from the graded capstone well, whose conditions the
generator has never read.

A leak guard in the generator checks every number in every line of this file
against all eighteen graded capstone answers, in three unit shiftings (as
printed, times a thousand, divided by a thousand), at TEN TIMES the grader own
acceptance band. That band is the ABSOLUTE tolerance the grader uses
(academy_submit_capstone compares abs(got - expected) <= tol, in the field own
units), so it is very tight: ten times it is 0.018 ft on a total dynamic head
and 0.00022 A on a motor current. The verdict is printed at the end of this
file. Nothing needed withholding.

THE THESIS THIS COURSE IS BUILT ON
  An ESP is a stack of identical stages turned by a motor on the end of a
  cable, so a design is one number carried through four translations: head per
  stage becomes head required becomes shaft horsepower becomes amps at the
  surface. An error introduced at any one translation is invisible at the next.

THE TWO KINDS OF STAGE CURVE IN THIS PACKAGE, AND THEY BEHAVE DIFFERENTLY
  VENDOR FIT. fitStageCurve puts a least squares CUBIC (headDegree 3 by
  default) through the points read off a manufacturer published curve. Five
  points, four coefficients, so the fit does NOT pass through its points and
  it carries a residual that a lesson can quote and a bad transcription shows
  up in. This is the route for a real design.
  REFERENCE MODEL. referenceStageCurve generates nine points from four named
  parameters and fits them with a QUADRATIC (headDegree 2). The generating
  shape is itself quadratic in rate, so the fit recovers it to machine
  precision and its residual is nothing at all. It is a shape, not a catalogue
  entry, and no lesson may call it a manufacturer pump.
  A lesson that says "the cubic fit" means the VENDOR curve. A lesson that
  quotes a reference stage residual is quoting a zero, and should say why.

THE THREE TEACHING CASES AND WHY EACH EXISTS. Two wells and one curve, none
of them published, all three labelled on every line they appear on.
  QUA-IBOE-4  a gassy well whose gas volume fraction through the pump lands
              BETWEEN the two published thresholds, so the verdict is
              gasHandler. Neither published design reaches that verdict, and a
              lesson on the thresholds needs a case that trips the middle one.
              Its motor is deliberately undersized, so the SELECTION load
              fraction crosses one while the ELECTRICAL load fraction does
              not: the module seam, on a well a lesson may print in full.
  IBENO-2     a short shallow stack. The rounding margin on a stack of a few
              dozen stages is worth percent, where on a stack of two hundred it
              is worth tenths of a percent. It exists so a lesson can show that
              the margin is bounded by ONE STAGE and never by a percentage.
  BRASS-11    a teaching CURVE and not a well. The published vendor points as
              transcribed by somebody who made
              a mistake. Two versions, one mild and one a decimal slip, so a
              lesson can watch the fit residual and the transcription warning
              do the job they are there for.

# The identity holds to machine precision on every row above, on both published
# designs and on both teaching wells. It is not a coincidence and it is not an
# approximation: brake power is linear in head at a fixed rate and efficiency,
# so the ratio of the two powers IS the ratio of the two heads.
# WHICH POWER THE PUBLISHED METHOD TAKES. PetroWiki, ESP system selection and
# performance calculations, sizes the motor on total stages times brake power
# per stage times specific gravity, which is the SECOND of the two, the power
# at the head the stack MAKES. Everything electrical in this package is built
# on the FIRST, the power at the head required, so it understates by the
# rounding margin. That is the non conservative direction. It was RECORDED and
# NOT FIXED, because every number here is consumed by a live application.
# What that choice is worth, on every case in this file. READ THE DENOMINATOR:
# "head margin as a percentage of the requirement" and "and by" above divide
# the gap by the SMALLER quantity, the head required and the shaft horsepower,
# while "the understatement as a percentage" below divides it by the LARGER,

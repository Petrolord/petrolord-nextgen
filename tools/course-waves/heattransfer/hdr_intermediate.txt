-- ============================================================================
-- FC6 PROFESSIONAL DEEP SEED: Heat Exchange & Cooling, intermediate tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: WHAT DECIDES THE DRIVING FORCE AND WHAT DECIDES THE
-- COEFFICIENT. P and R and a correction factor computed rather than typed;
-- shells in series and the equivalent single-shell P they are read through; the
-- overall coefficient assembled from five named resistances; the wall and the
-- thin-wall limit that fixes a factor; the tube-side film, its three regimes
-- and the band it does not have; and a professional reading end to end.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The correction factor is an EQUATION and this
-- module computes it, where the predecessor Suite app made the user type an Ft.
-- The coefficient is a SUM OF FIVE NAMED RESISTANCES, and naming the largest is
-- the entire point of assembling it from parts rather than quoting one number.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A controlling resistance is a VERDICT WITH A
-- MARGIN, and the margin is what says whether the verdict is worth acting on.
-- At the studio's own shipped defaults that verdict rested on a gap of about
-- two percent and the screen printed one word and no margin. The engine now
-- returns the margin, the runner up and whether the lead is clear, and UBIT is
-- built on a stack where the lead is clear so the reader can see what clear
-- looks like before meeting a coin toss.
--
-- HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded in any tier: the
-- Dittus-Boelter coefficient and its two exponents, the Sieder-Tate viscosity
-- exponent, the laminar Nusselt number, the correlation's validity band in
-- Reynolds and in Prandtl, the cooling Prandtl exponent the module REFUSES
-- rather than invents, and the default wall conductivity, which names no
-- material. UBIT states both film coefficients and its own wall conductivity as
-- conditions of the study, so every graded coefficient on this tier is built
-- from numbers on the page and the film is never called.
--
-- The transition band between Reynolds 2300 and 10000 is REFUSED rather than
-- interpolated, and that refusal is taught as the right answer rather than as a
-- gap. It is the one place in this module where the honest output is no number.
-- ============================================================================

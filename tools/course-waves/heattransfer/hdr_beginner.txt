-- FC6 ASSOCIATE DEEP SEED: Heat Exchange & Cooling, beginner tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE DUTY, THE DRIVING FORCE AND THE SURFACE. What this
-- engine rates and what it refuses to answer; the stream reduced to the one
-- number a balance knows it by, its capacity rate; the refusals that stand
-- between a balance and a crossed pair of streams; the log mean and the two
-- pairings it is read from; the surface the duty needs and the tubes that
-- carry it; and the studio's own default case walked end to end.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. This is the only part of the module that
-- runs end to end in CLOSED FORM, so a learner watches a whole answer assemble
-- before anything iterates. The balance knows a stream by one number and
-- everything after that is about the machine.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. The tube count closes as a LOOP and the loop
-- rounds twice. A bundle divides its tubes equally between its passes, so the
-- count is rounded up to cover the area and then rounded up again to a whole
-- multiple of the pass count. A reader who takes the first rounding for the
-- answer gets the surface margin wrong, and the capstone is built on a case
-- where the second rounding MOVES the count rather than leaving it alone.
--
-- HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded in any tier: the
-- bundle constant table, the cross-flow correction factor, the fan constant,
-- the default wall conductivity, the three air-cooler machine defaults and the
-- Dittus-Boelter validity band. The capstone grades none of them. AMENAM is
-- given its overall coefficient, so no film is computed and none of the five
-- fitted constants is read; and no graded field reads a bundle or shell
-- diameter, which is the only place the held table reaches.
--
-- The only numbers that travel from this tier to the next are the log mean and
-- the shape of a surface, which the Professional tier then corrects by a route
-- with nothing in common.

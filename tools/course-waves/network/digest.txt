THE ONLY NUMBERS A LESSON MAY QUOTE. Every line names its source: a PUBLISHED
golden value out of packages/engines/test-data/production/goldens/network_cases.json,
a PUBLISHED fixture or table out of the shipped module or its gate, a DERIVED
re-run or sweep on published inputs, or the one TEACHING NETWORK this wave
designed for itself. NOTHING in this file comes from the graded capstone. The
capstone conditions and its eighteen graded answers live in separate files that
the generator of this digest never opens.

A leak guard, pd7_leakcheck.mjs, reads the capstone graded field list and every
number on every line of this file and rejects the digest if any number lands
within TEN TIMES a graded field tolerance of that field value, in five unit
shiftings: the three the wave standard requires (as printed, times 1000, times
0.001) and two this domain needs (times 100 and times 0.01, because a fraction
against a percentage is the live confusion in a conservation gap).
academy_submit_capstone grades with abs(got - expected) <= tol, so tol is
ABSOLUTE in the field own units and is not a fraction of anything. The guard
lives in its own file and this generator never imports the capstone.

UNITS. Mass rate lb/d everywhere, because the module header says so:
"MASS IS THE CURRENCY. Every flow in here is a mass rate, lb/d. Surface
volumes do not add across pressures and mixing two of them is a mistake that
hides for a long time." Absolute pressure psia, never psig. A pressure
DIFFERENCE and a wall rating in psi. Length ft, bore and wall and outside
diameter in, yield psi, oil and water stb/d, gas Mscf/d, conductance for a
turbulent branch in lb/d per root psi, conductance for a linear branch in
lb/d per psi. Those last two are NOT the same quantity and never compare.

WHAT PROVENANCE LABEL MEANS WHAT
  golden ...    a value committed in network_cases.json by the independent
                stdlib oracle: Gauss-Seidel sweeps with a bracketed bisection
                at each node, no Jacobian and no linear algebra anywhere. The
                engine solves the same networks by Newton with a numerically
                differenced Jacobian and a backtracking line search. The two
                share no numerical machinery at all.
  published ... a fixture or a table shipped inside the engine package: a row
                of PIPE_SCHEDULE, a fitting K, a grade yield, or one of the
                fixtures the gate asserts on.
  derived ...   the shipped engine re-run on PUBLISHED inputs, or a sweep this
                generator ran around them. A sweep point is not a published
                case. Say so if you print one.
  teaching ...  the network AGBADA WEST, its line, its streams, and every
                sweep on it. Invented by this wave to carry the Expert
                results. Not a published case, not a real gathering system,
                and never to be shown as either.

THE PUBLISHED CASES AT A GLANCE
  linear_star      two linear wells on one header, one linear trunk, separator
                   at 150 psia. The ONLY case with a closed form.
  turbulent_tree   three Vogel wells, two headers in series, turbulent
                   branches, separator at 180 psia. No closed form.
  looped           two Vogel wells, two parallel paths to the separator at
                   200 psia. A loop is what makes a network a network.
  wells_fight      the same header solved with one well, then two, then three,
                   separator at 180 psia.
  the line         the gate Barlow and equivalent-length case: 6.625 in od,
                   0.28 in wall, API 5L X52, design factor 0.72, bore
                   6.065 in, friction factor 0.018 and 0.012, four long
                   radius elbows and two gate valves.

THE ONE TEACHING NETWORK
  AGBADA WEST   four wells, three junctions, one loop, eight branches, seven
                unknown pressures, a separator at 265 psia. One well is held
                to a facility allocation on a flowline that cannot pass the
                whole of it, which is what makes the pinning, the residual
                blindness, the initial-guess dependence and the stream mass
                gap all reachable on one case a lesson may quote.

# WHAT IT NEVER TOUCHES:
#   the PINNING PATH. The oracle has no concept of a pinned node; bisection on a
#   flat residual simply returns a bracket endpoint. Every one of its four cases
#   is a plain Vogel-plus-turbulent system in which no node can go flat.
#   the CONVERGENCE TEST ITSELF. The oracle converges on how far the pressures
#   MOVED between sweeps; the engine converges on a mass residual scaled by a
#   factor the caller never sees. The two criteria are not comparable and the
#   goldens publish neither one.
#   checkConservation, diagnose and propagateStreams. The oracle computes its own
#   conservation gap from its own solve and never calls the engine. It never ranks
#   a bottleneck and it never propagates a stream at all.
#   solveLinearNetwork. The oracle has no linear case of its own; linear_star is
#   solved by the same Gauss-Seidel as everything else. The engine closed form is
#   exercised only by the JS gate, on a three node star with no loop.
#   pipeSchedule.js IN ITS ENTIRETY. The oracle does not import it and there is no

# SECTION 24: WHAT THIS MODULE REFUSES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT MODEL AT ALL
# Associate m01 l03, and the closing lesson of every tier. Every capability comes
# with a limit, and these are the limits stated as refusals rather than caveats.
published refusal, a node with no route to a delivery point: This node has no route to a delivery point: Manifold B. Nothing sets its pressure, so the network cannot be solved. Connect it or take it out.
published refusal, no delivery point at all: A network needs a delivery point: a node with a pressure the system is flowing against.
published refusal, a delivery point with no pressure: The delivery point "Sep" needs a pressure. It is the boundary the whole system is solved against.
published refusal, no wells at all: A network needs at least one well. Nothing else puts anything into it.
published refusal, a recirculating solved flow direction: The solved flow directions form a loop, so the network is recirculating. A gathering system does not do that; check for a branch connected backwards.
published refusal, a singular Jacobian returns ok false with the message the module writes for it: "The system is singular: two or more nodes move together, so their pressures are not separately determined. That is usually a branch connected differently from the way the drawing suggests."
published refusal, an unknown fitting id: No resistance coefficient for reducer.
published refusal, an equivalent length with no bore: An equivalent length needs a bore and a friction factor.
# AND WHAT IT WARNS ABOUT RATHER THAN REFUSING:
derived warning, a pinned node, which is reported as a fact about the answer
derived warning, a solve that stopped making progress before it met its tolerance
derived warning, a solve that ran its iteration cap without meeting its tolerance
# WHAT THE MODULE DOES NOT MODEL AT ALL, so no number in this file speaks to it:
# the pipe hydraulics themselves, which are a callback the consumer supplies;
# the well inflow, which is another callback; temperature anywhere, so no
# thermal coupling and no cooldown; slugging, holdup and any transient at all,
# because every equation here is steady state; compressibility along a branch,
# since mass in equals mass out on every branch by construction; any equipment
# between nodes, so no pump, no compressor and no choke as a node kind; a
# separator that does anything but accept whatever arrives at a fixed pressure;
# and the pipe wall, the burial and the insulation, which live in other modules
# entirely.
# THE THREE NODE KINDS ARE THE WHOLE VOCABULARY. A well injects and its pressure
# is unknown. A junction injects nothing and everything that arrives leaves. A
# sink has a FIXED pressure and takes whatever arrives. Anything a real gathering
# system has that is not one of those three has to be written as a branch
# relation or left out.

PD8 Well Intervention: TEACHING DIGEST

THE ONLY NUMBERS A LESSON MAY QUOTE. Every line names its source: a PUBLISHED
value out of packages/engines/test-data/production/goldens/intervention_cases.json,
a DERIVED re-run or sweep on published inputs, or a TEACHING case this wave
designed for itself. NOTHING in this file comes from the graded capstone. The
capstone conditions and its eighteen graded answers live in separate files that
the generator of this digest never opens.

A leak guard, pd8_leakcheck.mjs, reads the capstone graded field list and every
number on every line of this file and rejects the digest if any number lands
within TEN TIMES a graded field tolerance of that field value, in five unit
shiftings: the three the wave standard requires (as printed, times 1000, times
0.001) and two this domain needs (times 100 and times 0.01), because a fit
quality quoted as a fraction and the same fit quality quoted as a percentage are
the live confusion in a diagnostics digest. academy_submit_capstone grades with
abs(got - expected) <= tol, so tol is ABSOLUTE in the field own units and is not
a fraction of anything. The guard lives in its own file and the generator never
imports the capstone.

WHAT PROVENANCE LABEL MEANS WHAT
  golden ...    a value committed in intervention_cases.json by the independent
                stdlib oracle. Two routes, both genuinely independent: the
                log-log slope by THEIL-SEN, the median of every pairwise slope,
                which shares no mean, no square and no covariance with the
                engine ordinary least squares; and the skin uplift by a full
                radial Darcy rate in SI, permeability in square metres and
                pressures in pascals, divided as two real flow rates, against
                the engine ratio of two dimensionless groups.
  derived ...   the shipped engine re-run on PUBLISHED inputs, or a sweep this
                generator ran around them. A sweep point is not a published
                case. Say so if you print one.
  teaching ...  the teaching well ELELENWO-4, the teaching gas history, the
                three constructed demonstration series, and every sweep on
                them. Invented by this wave to carry the Expert results. Not a
                published case, not a real well, and never to be shown as
                either.

WHAT THE ORACLE NEVER CALLS, WHICH IS MOST OF THE MODULE
  The golden publishes four labelled histories, a lateDerivativeSlope for each,
  five skin pairs, one geometry floor and one power law. It publishes NO
  expected mechanism, NO expected confidence, NO expected verdict, NO expected
  refusal and NO expected block reason. chanDiagnosis, screenTreatments,
  rankTreatments and skinFromPiRatio are not asserted against anything at all.
  The only part of this module that returns a VERDICT is the part with no
  golden. Section 2 runs the classifier over the four published histories and
  prints the four assertions that were never written.

# function owns a number is most of what the Associate tier is for.
#   logLogSlope        THE MEASUREMENT. Ordinary least squares of ln y against

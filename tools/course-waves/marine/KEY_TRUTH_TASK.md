# SC4 Offshore & Marine Logistics: the key-truth writer's task

Read `BRIEF.md` first. Every number here is quoted from `digest.txt`, the only
teaching truth for this course. The engine's FINDINGS record, the oracle and
its golden, the fixture README and the engine's source comments are
PROVENANCE.

## TWO JOBS

1. **The key truths.** One sentence a learner should be able to say afterwards,
   with the number that makes it checkable.
2. **The second reader of every bank, before the migrations.** Read prompt,
   options, explanation, digest and lesson for every question, one tier at a
   time, and CALL THE ENGINE (through a calculator panel, or node on the
   vendored `packages/engines/engines/supplychain/marineLogistics.js` through
   the wave's `marine_engine.mjs`) for every keyed figure. Swap roles where the
   explanation supports a distractor.

## THE DEFECT CLASSES TO READ FOR

1. **A distractor that became true** under an input the question did not rule
   out (other activities for the weather, another rounding rule, another
   packing rule, another queue model, the concurrent choice).
2. **A stated reading keyed as the law.**
3. **A concept-only item keyed to a number** the engine does not compute.
4. **A wrong failure-mode gloss.** "Refused" said of a result with a reason.
5. **A false superlative** ("always", "every vessel") where the digest shows one
   case.
6. **An exam question that near-duplicates a module question** (audit at
   Jaccard 0.45).
7. **A sentence characterising a relationship nobody computed.**
8. **The answer printed before the work** in the prompt or a table above it.
9. **A capstone name, input, series or value** anywhere.
10. **The answer-length tell**, and three distractors opening alike.
11. **Printed alike keyed as equal.**
12. **A boundary stated globally.** Each rule draws its own (the digest's
    boundaries section): a load at capacity is feasible; exactly three voyages
    is 3 and 300.001 over 100 is 4; 1.5 vessels round to 2; a need at the
    planned capacity is not short; a wait equal to the target meets it.
13. **A message quoted inexactly.**
14. **A digest section number in learner-visible text.**
15. **The source rule broken**: a source named without its edition, a sentence
    of a source quoted or closely paraphrased, a figure the digest does not
    print.
16. **Repair history**: a sentence about former engine behaviour.
17. **A Monte Carlo estimate keyed as exact**, or quoted without its seed and
    draws.

## THE KEY TRUTHS THIS COURSE OWES ITS LEARNERS

### Associate

1. Sailing hours are distance over speed: the Ekene milk run sails 206.000000
   NM.
2. The weather factor acts only on the activities a plan names: port time
   stays at 12.000000 hours with the factor on sailing and field time.
3. Fuel follows time: 19.876364 t on the rainy-season milk run.
4. Deck area capacity is the deck area times the usable fraction: 600.000000
   m2.
5. The binding constraint has the highest utilisation: deck area at 0.900000.
6. A load of a product the vessel has no tank for is refused by name.

### Professional

7. The voyages of demand are the largest demand over capacity: 3.100000 by
   deck area.
8. Rounding voyages up asks for 10.345455 vessel-days; the average asks for
   8.017727.
9. The nearest vessel can leave the week short: by 2.916667 vessel-days.
10. First-fit decreasing packs the big units first: 599.229600 m2 carried.
11. First fit in the booked order can strand a big unit: 35.100000 m2 left
    behind.
12. The lower bound is the area bound: 2 voyages.

### Expert

13. The shore base runs on the working-hour clock: a twelve-hour day doubles
    the arrivals an hour.
14. A second berth turns a refused base into a queue: 3.180124 hours of mean
    wait as M/M/c.
15. Little's law holds at the base: a mean queue of 0.424017.
16. A constant service waits less: 1.665786 hours as M/D/c, and at one berth
    exactly half the M/M/1 wait.
17. A printed table can slip: 1.53 printed where the formula gives 1.524986.
18. A Monte Carlo figure carries its seed and is never graded: 7.977705 on seed
    20260927 and 20000 draws.

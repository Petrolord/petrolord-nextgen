-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42

-- What this tier owns: THE PLAN AND THE SCHEDULE. A blank limit is no limit

-- margin is revenue less crude and unit operating cost, and the gross margin
-- is that margin per barrel of crude; each stream balance leaves a surplus or
-- none, and each stream carries the plan's value of one more barrel; the plan
-- cascades into cargoes and weekly events dated from a fixed period start.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The plan is solved, and a stream's marginal
-- value comes with the solution. The crude course owns the LP; this tier reads
-- what the plan prints.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A cargo typed as 0 and a cargo left blank are
-- two different plans, and the crude unit's operating cost is charged on every
-- barrel of crude.
--
-- The capstone grades six numbers, each an engine return: a crude run to the
-- whole barrel, a utilisation in percent to two decimals, a margin to the
-- whole dollar, a gross margin and two stream values to the cent.

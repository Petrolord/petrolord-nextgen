-- refinery EXPERT DEEP SEED: Refinery Feasibility & Planning, advanced tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: ACTUALS, VARIANCE AND THE INVESTMENT CASE. A plan, a
-- scheduled event and a recorded actual are one shape in three ledgers; the
-- two ledgers are matched on material and type and each gap splits into a
-- volume variance, a price variance and an unexplained term; a movement in one
-- ledger only is unmatched; each line carries its direction and its effect on
-- margin, and the headline total is on margin. The feasibility streams are
-- valued through the screening engine with revenue as revenue, no royalty,
-- capital deducted in the year it is spent and the construction-year tax loss
-- carried forward.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. A positive gap on a delivery helps the
-- margin and the same gap on a receipt hurts it; the engine signs each line
-- before it adds.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Adding the revenue gaps to the cost gaps as
-- recorded gives a number with no meaning, and throwing the construction loss
-- away taxes the first operating years.
--
-- HELD, taught as a limit and never graded: capital depreciated in the year
-- it is spent (H2). The NPV is read here as the screen's answer; the
-- Economics courses grade it. The capstone grades six numbers, each an
-- engine return: four variance figures to the whole dollar and two tax
-- figures in millions to four decimals.

-- refinery ASSOCIATE DEEP SEED: Refinery Feasibility & Planning, beginner tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE MODULAR SCREEN. A blank money or size box is
-- refused by name and a utilisation outside 0 to 1 is refused; capital is
-- scaled from a vendor quotation on the stick-built and modular laws, which
-- agree at the reference size; the product slate is valued per barrel of
-- crude with the loss carried as a yield of no value; annual throughput is
-- capacity times on-stream days times utilisation, and the gross margin per
-- barrel is the slate value less crude and variable operating cost; supply
-- scenarios move the utilisation and the crude premium; the licensing
-- tracker names the next stage and flags one ticked out of order.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The economy of scale is an exponent, and the
-- exponent is an input: the screen shows both laws side by side.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. The scenario changes two things at once, the
-- run rate and the crude price, and a reader who applies one of them misses
-- the margin.
--
-- HELD, taught as a limit and never graded: the exponents 0.6 and 0.9 are
-- defaults a vendor's figures replace (H1). The capstone grades six numbers,
-- each an engine return: two capital costs and a first-year revenue to the
-- whole dollar, an annual throughput to the whole barrel, and a slate value
-- and a gross margin to the cent.

THE PETROLEUM INDUSTRY ACT 2021:

PIA s.85(2)(a): A production sharing contract lets the party that bears the financial risk recover its costs from a share of production, as the contract sets out.
> "(a) a production sharing contract for the exploration, development and production of petroleum on terms under which the financial risk-bearing party shall recover costs from a share of production as established in the contract from the applicable area ;" (PIA s.85(2)(a))

PIA s.85(2)(d): A concession agreement may include an incorporated or unincorporated joint venture with NNPC Limited.
> "(d) a concession agreement for exploration, development and production of petroleum, which may include an incorporated or unincorporated joint venture with NNPC Limited ; and" (PIA s.85(2)(d))

PIA s.85(4): A concession agreement under s.85(2)(d) must carry a carried interest provision on the terms that follow.
> "(4) A contract as provided for under section 85 (2) (d) of this Act shall include a carried interest provision, whereby" (PIA s.85(4))

PIA s.85(4)(a): The Government, through NNPC Limited, may participate up to 60 percent, or as a bid parameter states.
> "(a) the Government through the NNPC Limited has the right to participate up to 60% in the contract or identified as a bid parameter ;" (PIA s.85(4)(a))

PIA s.85(4)(b): The right to participate runs from any time after the licence or lease is granted.
> "(b) the right to participate shall be from any time upon the granting of the licence or lease ;" (PIA s.85(4)(b))

PIA s.85(4)(c): The Government refunds in full its proportionate share of the unrecovered proven costs from the date it participates.
> "(c) the contract shall stipulate that Government shall refund fully its proportionate share of the unrecovered proven costs from the date of its participation" (PIA s.85(4)(c))

PIA s.85(4)(c): The refundable costs are development and production costs, with no bonuses, penalties, interest, premium or markups.
> "such refunded cost shall relate to development and production and shall not include bonuses and penalties, interest, premium or markups on cost ;" (PIA s.85(4)(c))

PIA s.85(4)(d): The contract may not require the Government to pay anything upfront.
> "(d) the contract shall not require any upfront payment by the Government ;" (PIA s.85(4)(d))

PIA s.85(4)(e): An agreed expert determination settles the nature, validity and amount of the unrecovered costs.
> "(e) the nature, the validity and quantum of the unrecovered costs to be refunded shall be determined or verified by an agreed expert determination procedure ;" (PIA s.85(4)(e))

PIA s.85(4)(f): The refund may be paid in cash or in kind from the future share of production or entitlements.
> "(f ) the refund obligation under this subsection may be in the form of cash or kind from future share of production or entitlements from the effective participation date ; and" (PIA s.85(4)(f))

PIA s.311(2)(a)(iii): A renegotiated production sharing contract carries a cost oil limit of at most 60 percent of total oil production and a haircut of at least 55 percent on disputed amounts.
> "shall feature a cost oil limit of not more than 60% of the total oil production, a minimum of 55% haircut on disputed amount" (PIA s.311(2)(a)(iii))

THE NORWEGIAN JOINT OPERATING AGREEMENT (ATTACHMENT A, UNOFFICIAL ENGLISH TRANSLATION):

Norway JOA Art. 8.1: The parties must fund every expense of the joint venture.
> "The Parties are obliged to provide sufficient funds to cover all expenses relating to the activities of the joint venture." (Norway JOA Art. 8.1)

Norway JOA Art. 8.1: Each party contributes by its participating interest at the time of payment.
> "the amount to be contributed by each Party shall be calculated in accordance with the Participating interest at the time the payment is made." (Norway JOA Art. 8.1)

Norway JOA Art. 9.1: The non-defaulting parties advance a defaulter's unpaid amounts by their participating interests.
> "the amounts which are not paid shall be advanced by the non-defaulting Parties in accordance with their Participating interest." (Norway JOA Art. 9.1)

Norway JOA Art. 9.1: A defaulting party pays penal interest under the Accounting Agreement.
> "he shall be charged a penal interest pursuant to Article 1.2.2 of Attachment B" (Norway JOA Art. 9.1)

Norway JOA Art. 9.2: After five working days from the demand a defaulting party loses its vote and its access to data.
> "If a Party's default has not ceased within five (5) working days after he has received a demand for payment from the Operator, he looses his right to vote and his access to data and information" (Norway JOA Art. 9.2)

Norway JOA Art. 9.3: After three months from the operator's notice the other parties may demand an assignment of the defaulter's interest.
> "If a Party's default remains in effect for more than three (3) months after the Operator has informed the management committee" (Norway JOA Art. 9.3)

Norway JOA Art. 9.3: The compensation on an assignment is agreed, with the book value of the share of the investment as its ceiling.
> "The compensation shall be agreed between the Parties, but shall not exceed the book value of the Party's share of the investment" (Norway JOA Art. 9.3)

Norway JOA Art. 9.4: An assigned interest goes to the non-defaulting parties pro rata to their participating interests.
> "The assigned Participating interest shall be apportioned pro rata amongst the non-defaulting Parties in accordance with their Participating interest, unless otherwise agreed." (Norway JOA Art. 9.4)

Norway JOA Art. 12.5: The operator may exceed a budget item or an AFE by up to 10 percent.
> "In carrying out an approved work program, however, the Operator may exceed a budget item or an AFE by up to 10%." (Norway JOA Art. 12.5)

Norway JOA Art. 12.5: A budget may be exceeded by at most the lower of 5 percent and NOK 75 million in the year.
> "None of the budgets may be exceeded by more than the lower of 5% or NOK 75 million during the Accounting year." (Norway JOA Art. 12.5)

Norway JOA Art. 12.5: Work outside the programme and the budgets may reach NOK 3 million a budget in the year.
> "up to an aggregate amount for each budget of NOK 3 million during the Accounting year." (Norway JOA Art. 12.5)

Norway JOA Art. 18.6: Parties in a sole risk project take part in proportion to their participating interests.
> "In sole risk projects each Party participates in proportion to his Participating interest, unless the Parties otherwise agree." (Norway JOA Art. 18.6)

Norway JOA Art. 18.12: A party entering a sole risk project later pays one thousand percent of its proportionate share of the cost.
> "must pay one thousand (1000) % of their proportionate share of the costs of the project." (Norway JOA Art. 18.12)

Norway JOA Art. 18.12: That entry payment is shared by the initial participants by their interests in the project.
> "The payment shall be apportioned between the initial participants according to their Participating interest in the project." (Norway JOA Art. 18.12)

THE NORWEGIAN ACCOUNTING AGREEMENT (ATTACHMENT B, UNOFFICIAL ENGLISH TRANSLATION):

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1: The difference between the advances and the actual payments adjusts the next request for advances.
> "The difference between the monthly cash advances and the actual payments in each currency shall be stated, and the next request for advances shall be adjusted accordingly." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1: An excess advance is refunded, or carried to the following period when the parties agree.
> "the Operator shall refund the excess amounts as soon as possible, unless the Parties agree to transfer the amount to the following period." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1: Below NOK 5 million a month the operator need not make a cash call.
> "Where the total cash requirement is less than NOK 5 million a month, the Operator is not required to make cash calls." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1: With no cash call, each non-operator pays its share of the actual payments when billed.
> "If the Operator has made no request for advance payments, the Non-operators shall pay their proportionate share of the actual monthly payments within 15 days after receipt of the Operator's billing" (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.1)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.2: Late payment interest runs from and including the due date to, and excluding, the value date.
> "Interest is due for the period starting on and including the due date of payment and ending on, but excluding, the value date for payment." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.2)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.2: The rate is a reference rate on the due date plus three percentage points.
> "as per the due date of payment, plus three percentage points." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 1.2.2)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.2: The operator's general research and development cost is charged by per cent rates in bands of annual cost.
> "may be charged to the Joint Account according to the following per cent rates and limits based on annual costs" (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.2)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.2: The charge itself never enters the cost it is charged on.
> "Cost charged to the Joint Account in accordance with this Article is not to be included in the basis of calculation." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.2)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.2: The band limits move each year with the Norwegian consumer price index.
> "The intervals above will be adjusted at the beginning of each Year on the basis of the consumer price index as published by Statistics Norway per 15 July of the current year." (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.2)

Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.3: Corporate management and staff cost is charged at 0.65 percent of the annual cost.
> "may be charged to the Joint Account with 0.65 % of the annual costs for exploration, operation and development of the Joint operation" (Norway Accounting Agreement Art. 2.2.3)

THE KENYA MODEL PRODUCTION SHARING CONTRACT 2015, PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT:

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.2: The operator may ask each non-operator to advance its share of next month's estimated spend by a due date.
> "request a non-operator to advance a share of the estimated expenditure for the following month, stipulating the due date of payment" (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.2)

> "If any non-operator's advances for a given month exceed its share of cash disbursements for the same month, the next succeeding cash advance, after such determination, shall be reduced accordingly." (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.4)

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.4: A non-operator may ask for an excess advance back, paid within fifteen days.
> "non-operator(s) may request that excess advances be refunded. The operator shall make such refund within fifteen (15) days after date of such notice." (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.4)

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.7: Late payment interest is a reference rate plus a margin the model leaves blank.
> "A late payment shall attract interest at LIBOR plus" (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.7)

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.7: The interest compounds monthly from the due date.
> "compounded monthly and calculated from the due date of payment." (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.7)

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.7: A payment later than seventy-two hours after the due date carries interest from the due date itself.
> "A payment not received within seventy-two (72) hours of the due date shall accrue interest from the due date" (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.7)

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.8: After five days in default a party loses its vote on the operating committee.
> "A party which remains in default for five (5) days shall have no right to vote at any operating committee meeting held during the period of the default" (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.8)

> "and remains in default for ninety (90) days, the participating interest share of the defaulting party may be declared forfeit by the non-defaulting parties" (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.9)

Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.10: A forfeited share vests in the non-defaulting parties in proportion, with no compensation.
> "that share shall vest rateably, unless otherwise agreed, in the non-defaulting parties without payment of compensation" (Kenya Model PSC 2015, Participation Agreement Art. 6.10)

WORLD BANK PETROLEUM SECTOR BRIEFING NOTE NO. 8 (NOVEMBER 2007):

World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007): In the two-barrel example the royalty of 10 percent is paid first.

World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007): The example's cost oil limit is 60 percent of gross revenue.
> "the contractor recovers costs to the limit permitted, in this case 60 percent of the gross revenue or US$60." (World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007))

World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007): The note says income tax deductions carry no limit of the kind cost oil has.
> "For paying income tax, there are no limits on deductible expenses in the way there are limits on cost oil." (World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007))

World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007): Profit oil of 65 is split 60 percent to the government and 40 percent to the contractor.
> "The government takes 60 percent of US$65, or US$39, and the remaining US$26 goes to the contractor." (World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007))

World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007): The note's end result, in whole dollars.
> "The end result is that the contractor retains US$43 and the government takes $57." (World Bank Petroleum Sector Briefing Note No. 8 (November 2007))

WHAT A COMPUTED FIGURE DOES NOT SAY. A cash call is what the stated forecast and the stated clauses produce; a carry recovery is what the stated entitlement pays; a premium is a stated multiple of a stated cost. None is a forecast of what a partner will pay. An NPV depends on the discount rate and base year stated. Each figure is quoted with its terms for that reason.

# SECTION 25: Vocabulary this course legislates before a word is written (owned by Associate m01 and Professional m01 and Expert m01)

Six terms in this course carry a narrower meaning than they have in conversation. The rule for each is binding on every lesson, bank question, key truth and panel.

| term | what it can mean elsewhere | the rule here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| interest | any interest, including money charged on a loan | always qualified: participating interest, paying interest, beneficial interest or carried interest for a share of the venture; default interest for money charged on a late payment |
| carry | anything carried | a carried party's cost share paid by its carriers; a pool, a credit or a balance moved to a later period is "carried forward" or "carried to" a named period |
| recovery | any getting back | always of something named: carry recovery, premium recovery, or cost recovery under a PSC |
| premium | any extra payment | the stated multiple of a non-consenting party's proportionate share of an operation's cost |
| cash call | any request for money | the operator's monthly request for advances against a forecast; an adjustment is the difference of an earlier month applied to a later call |
| overhead | any indirect cost | the operator's charge on a stated scale over a stated base; the Norwegian scale it follows is a research and development and corporate charge |

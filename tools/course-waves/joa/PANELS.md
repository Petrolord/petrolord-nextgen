## AN ENGINE COURSE

There is no Suite app for this course. The three calculator panels below ARE
the course's practicals: they call the vendored joint venture engine on the
Ekene joint venture or on whatever the learner pastes, and the learning page
says so in plain words.

## WHAT IS SHIPPED

Under `src/components/course/panels/joa/` in the NextGen repository:

* `joaLab.js`, the ONE teaching lab. It imports the vendored Ekene fixture
  (`packages/engines/test-data/economics/ekene-jv`) and the INPUTS of the
  vendored golden file, carries thin routes to the engine for whatever a
  learner types, the view routes, the stated-input writer `setStated`, a JSON
  parser and the teaching readers. It holds no graded answer, no tolerance and
  no capstone case.
* `gradedTolerance.js`, **the only place a grading tolerance is made**:
  `max(stated, half a unit in the last place the course prints that class)`.
  `fields.json` and `precision.json` are both written out of it.
* `gradedAnswerGuard.js`, every string shape a graded answer can reach a
  learner as.
* `AccountCalculator.jsx` (`joa-account-calculator`), `RecoveryCalculator.jsx`
  (`joa-recovery-calculator`), `AgreementCalculator.jsx`
  (`joa-agreement-calculator`) and `panelBits.jsx`.

The learning page is `src/pages/apps/JoaLearningPage.jsx`, routed at
`/dashboard/apps/joa`. The panels are registered in
`src/content/courses/panelRegistry.js`, and `panelRender.test.jsx` renders
every view of every panel. The capstone case files are generated by
`gen_course.py --cases` into `src/content/capstone-cases/joa` and are offered
on the capstone card only.

## THE THREE CALCULATORS

Every view takes the learner's own inputs as JSON, because that is how a
capstone is worked. Every view reads what is pasted through the lab's view
routes, which apply `pick` at the key the view needs (interests, cashCalls,
budget, overhead, carry, backIn, default, psc, soleRisk, buyIn), so a whole
case file pasted into any view runs that view's block and a box that holds one
call's inputs runs as it stands. `joaLab.test.js` proves every view gives a
whole case file the result of its block alone, and
`panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads all eighteen graded fields with each
case file pasted WHOLE, as the capstone card offers it. Every view shows the
engine's own refusal and reasons, verbatim, and a Source block with the
engine's `basis.source`.

EVERY REQUIRED STATED INPUT HAS A VISIBLE CONTROL, and no panel holds a hidden
default. A control shows the term as the box states it, or "not stated", and
writes the learner's choice into the box through `setStated`; a term left
unstated reaches the engine unstated and the engine refuses by name.

### `joa-account-calculator` (Associate)

Participating, paying and beneficial interests (the Ekene carry: NOC paying
0.000000 on a beneficial 20.000000); monthly cash calls with the lag, the
negative call rule and the threshold as controls (January 2027's over-call of
400000.000000); budget control with the tolerances as controls (the allowed
overrun of 3000000.000000); operator overhead on a marginal scale (the total of
3580000.000000).

### `joa-recovery-calculator` (Professional)

The cash call ledger over a year with the closing balances; a carry and its
recovery ledger with the uplift type and rate, the recovery share, the basis
and a cap as controls (recovered in 2033 with the compound uplift); the
back-in under PIA s.85(4) (the refund of 98000000.000000); a default with the
rate, day basis, interest method and grace as controls (20625.000000 simple);
the PSC cost pool with the royalty, the limit and its base, the profit share,
the tax and the opening pool as controls.

### `joa-agreement-calculator` (Expert)

Sole risk and non-consent with the premium multiple and the mode as controls
(PB's premium of 10800000.000000); buy-in (27000000.000000 at the Norwegian
multiple); the carry with the NPV of every party, the PSC and the default,
reused from the recovery calculator; and the three stated readings, each
printed from the engine's basis on the golden input where it acts.

## THE RULES FOR EVERY PANEL

1. **No graded capstone answer, in any of four shapes**, no capstone name,
   label, term or series. `panelCapstoneGuard.test.js` sweeps the lab, the
   calculators, the bits and the learning page, and plants each shape to prove
   the sweep catches it. `panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads every graded
   field off a rendered panel with the capstone case pasted, at full precision.
2. **No refusal message is written in a panel or the lab.** Show `r.error`, and
   a reason as the engine returns it.
3. **No clock, no random number, no path under `/root`.**
4. **The copy rule.** No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastive, no
   "rather than", no ", never", no "instead of". `gate_copy_rule.py` sweeps the
   panels and the page as well as the digest.
5. **The vocabulary**, digest section 25; no "AI".

-- a depth sample as a row; the Euclidean distance and why a raw distance is a
-- gamma ray distance; standard scaling with the population standard deviation
-- and min-max scaling without clipping, a constant log refused; principal
-- components from the correlation matrix, their eigenvalues, explained
-- variance, loadings and scores, a new well projected; k-means with seeded
-- k-means++ seeding, Lloyd passes and inertia, several starts; centres back in
-- log units, cluster numbers as names, new rows at the nearest centre;
-- Fisher's iris as a published check. Digest sections 1 to 13, and section 28.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A cluster is a group the logs make, and its
-- number is a name: the scaling decides which rows are near, one start can
-- stop in a poor arrangement, and no cluster is a facies until it is matched
-- against core. The Associate capstone grades six Associate quantities and
-- grades no choice of k, no tree of merges and no score against core.

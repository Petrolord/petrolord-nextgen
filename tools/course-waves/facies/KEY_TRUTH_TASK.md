# D3 Electrofacies: the key-truth writer's task

Read `BRIEF.md` first. Every number here is quoted from `digest.txt`, the only
teaching truth for this course. The engine's FINDINGS record, the oracle, the
library pins and the engine's source comments are PROVENANCE.

## TWO JOBS

1. **The key truths.** One sentence a learner should be able to say afterwards,
   with the number that makes it checkable.
2. **The second reader of every bank, before `gen_migration.py`.** Read prompt,
   options, explanation, digest and lesson for every question, one tier at a
   time, and CALL THE ENGINE (through the lab or the panel, or node on the
   vendored `packages/engines/engines/dataai/cluster.js`) for every keyed
   figure. Swap roles where the explanation supports a distractor.

## THE DEFECT CLASSES TO READ FOR, from the FC, H, D1 and D2 audits

1. **A distractor that became true.** The engine returns the distractor's
   number under some stated setting the question did not rule out (another
   seed, another number of starts, another scaling, another column order).
2. **A wrong failure-mode gloss.** "Refused" said of a call that returns with a
   warning (an inertia that rises, a repeated eigenvalue, a k-means stopped at
   maxIter); "wrong" said of a value that is only outside the training range.
3. **A false superlative.** "the largest", "always", "never" where the digest
   shows one case. A held-out well is one draw.
4. **An exam question that near-duplicates a module question** (audit at
   Jaccard 0.45).
5. **A sentence characterising a relationship nobody computed.** Every "worse",
   "more", "higher" rests on two printed figures.
6. **The answer printed before the work** in the prompt or a table above it.
7. **A capstone input or value** in any prompt, option or explanation.
8. **The answer-length tell**, and the D1 tell: three distractors opening the
   same way while the key does not.
9. **Printed alike keyed as equal.** Two figures equal at six decimals are
   equal only where the digest says the engine returns them equal.
10. **A boundary stated globally.** Each rule draws its own (section 25): a
    split needs a decrease above zero, k equal to the rows is accepted, a
    distance within the band is tied.
11. **A message quoted inexactly.** A refusal, warning, basis or printed tree
    line is quoted verbatim or not at all.
12. **A digest section number in learner-visible text.** Say "the course".
13. **A cluster number read as a rock type.** Cluster 0 means nothing until it
    is matched against core with a stated seed.

## THE KEY TRUTHS THIS COURSE OWES ITS LEARNERS

### Associate

1. A raw distance is mostly a gamma ray distance: scaled, 173 of the 180 cored
   rows sit next to a row of their own facies, raw 161.
2. Clustering scales with the population standard deviation; the sample one is
   larger by 1.002789 on 180 rows.
3. The correlation matrix lets every log count: its first component carries
   0.682351 of the variance, where the covariance matrix gives GR 0.998581.
4. A loading is a weight times the square root of the eigenvalue: NPHI 0.541336
   and 0.894337 on the first component.
5. One start can stop high: seed 3 with one start reaches 58.330411, with ten
   58.289042.
6. Fisher's iris reproduces the published ratios [0.92461872 0.05306648] at all
   eight printed decimals.

### Professional

7. The elbow picks nothing: the drop fraction reads 0.668972 at k 3, 0.282430 at
   k 4 and 0.112303 at k 5.
8. The silhouette can prefer another k than the core: 0.690362 at k 3 against
   0.545063 at k 4.
9. A row alone in its cluster scores 0, as average linkage shows at k 4.
10. Majority matching rewards splitting a facies: 0.983333 at k 5 against a
    one-to-one 0.950000 at k 4.
11. The adjusted Rand index ignores the numbering: renamed clusters score
    0.872413 and 1.000000 against each other.
12. A grouping that knows nothing about rock still scores above 0: the wells,
    0.157021.

### Expert

13. A kNN scaler fitted on the new well moves it: 0.466667 against 0.833333.
14. A tied vote goes to the nearer neighbour, where scikit-learn takes the
    facies that sorts first.
15. A tie in the tree is decided by column order: NPHI and PEF split the root
    with the same decrease, 0.283413.
16. A split needs a decrease above zero, so the XOR golden stays one leaf.
17. A range check sees only the rows that leave the range: 10 flagged, 3 missed
    and all 3 inside it.
18. A deep tree fits its own rows: at maxDepth 5 the tree on every cored row
    scores 0.988889 on those rows, and the tree grown without EKENE-6 scores
    0.766667 on EKENE-6.

## THE RULES

Digest section 28's vocabulary is binding. No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X,
not Y" contrastive. No key truth may carry a capstone field name, well, stated
input or graded answer at any precision.

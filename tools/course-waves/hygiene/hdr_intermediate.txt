-- H2 PROFESSIONAL DEEP SEED: Occupational Hygiene: Noise, Chemical & Heat
-- Exposure, intermediate tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed, and the question rows from the
-- committed bank JSONs under tools/course-banks/hygiene/intermediate.
--
-- What this tier owns: PROTECTION AND CHEMICALS. LEX,8h, the daily noise
-- exposure level of Schedule 1 of the UK 2005 Regulations and ISO 1999
-- clause 3.6, which has no threshold and normalises to eight hours whatever
-- the day was; the HSE exposure points that carry the same information on a
-- scale that adds; the weekly LEX with its statutory divisor of five; the
-- four hearing protector methods, OSHA Appendix B, the OSHA fifty percent
-- field derating for the engineering-controls decision, OSHA dual protection
-- and the NIOSH derating by protector type, and which question each one
-- answers; the eight-hour chemical average of 1910.1000(d)(1), whose divisor
-- is eight whatever the sampled time was; the fifteen-minute short term
-- average; and the additive mixture index of 1910.1000(d)(2). Digest
-- sections 11 to 15, and section 24.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Two of these metrics divide by a constant
-- the regulation fixes rather than by the time that was measured, and a
-- protector estimate is only as good as the method the decision in front of
-- you calls for. The Professional capstone grades a daily LEX,8h over a day
-- longer than eight hours, a weekly LEX, the OSHA fifty percent field
-- derated sound level under an earmuff, an eight-hour benzene average from a
-- record short of eight hours, a fifteen-minute toluene average and a
-- mixture index. The NIOSH derating by type and OSHA dual protection are
-- taught here and graded nowhere.

  golden ...    a value committed in one of the four goldens by an independent

                oracle does every date arithmetic on the CALENDAR where the
                module counts epoch-millisecond day numbers, forms each window

                definition rather than the implemented inequality, and measures
                effective decline as 1 - q(365)/q(0) through the Arps rate law
                where the module evaluates a closed form; the allocation oracle
                splits the metered total as a SHARE where the module multiplies

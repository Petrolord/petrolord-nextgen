# SECTION 1: The portfolio engine, what it models and what it refuses (owned by Associate m01)

- Risked EMV per project = pos x npv_p50 - (1 - pos) x fail_cost, pos the chance of success (0 to 1, default 1), fail_cost the loss if it fails (0 or more, default 0).
- The optimizer funds each project in full or not at all (a 0/1 knapsack) and maximises the summed risked EMV with total capex within the limit on its grid.
- Grid: 1 million USD per cell when the limit and every candidate capex are whole numbers and the limit is at most 5000; otherwise limit / 2000 per cell, each project weighing max(1, round(capex / cell)) cells.
- A project with risked EMV of 0 or less is never funded; money may be left unspent.
- Risk summary: emv and stdDev in closed form; probLoss, p90 (the low case, the 10th percentile of simulated portfolio NPV) and p10 (the high case) from a seeded Monte Carlo, default seed 20260829, default iterations 10000.
- P-labels follow the exceedance convention on an NPV outcome: P90 is the low case, P10 the high case. A capex, a cost or a probability never carries a P-label.

Refusals (published optimizeRefusals, engine messages verbatim):
- negativeCapexRefused: PortfolioInputError: "Project "B" has a negative capex (-150); capex must be 0 or more"
- negativeCapexUnnamed: PortfolioInputError: "Project "1" has a negative capex (-0.5); capex must be 0 or more"

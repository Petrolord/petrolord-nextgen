-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.

-- What this tier owns: CHANGE: STAGES, SIGNATURES, ACTIONS AND EXPIRY. The
-- stage machine and its refusals; approval levels read from the rows;
-- segregation of duties (AS15 D1); the gate into Implementation and the gate
-- into Closed; a temporary change that can expire only while in effect; the
-- emergency route that goes in on its first signature and must be ratified
-- (AS15 Q9). It ends on the ESANMI register.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. A gate is a rule the engine refuses to cross,
-- with a sentence that names the way forward. An approval belongs to the person
-- it is assigned to, and an originator never decides their own change.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. Both windows are counted on calendar dates:
-- the expiry lead inclusively, and the ratification window from the actual
-- implementation date, with no recorded date failing closed. Actions on a
-- finished change are not open work. The capstone grades all three.
--
-- REPAIRED IN ASC-0 (RECON RC-3): a change in Implementation is not overdue;
-- late work shows as overdue actions. OKOMU grades action due dates and never a
-- change's overdue flag, before or after the repair.

# EC7 Petroleum Industry Act 2021 & Nigerian Fiscal Terms: the calculator panels

Read `BRIEF.md` first. Every number here is quoted from `digest.txt`, the only
teaching truth for this course. The audit, the engine's FINDINGS record, the
oracle and its golden, and the engine's source comments are PROVENANCE.

## AN ENGINE COURSE

There is no Suite app for this course. The three calculator panels below ARE
the course's practicals: they call the vendored engine
`engines/economics/cashflow.ts` on the Ekene teaching cases or on whatever the
learner pastes, and the learning page says so in plain words.

## WHAT IS SHIPPED

Under `src/components/course/panels/pia/` in the NextGen repository:

* `piaLab.js`, the ONE teaching lab. It imports the INPUTS of the vendored golden
  file (`packages/engines/test-data/economics/goldens/pia2021_cases.json`) and
  never its expected figures, carries thin routes to the engine for whatever a
  learner types (each refusal returned as the engine's own message), a JSON
  parser, and the teaching readers. It holds no graded answer, no tolerance and
  no capstone case.
* `gradedTolerance.js`, **the only place a grading tolerance is made**:
  `max(stated, half a unit in the last place the course prints that class)`.
  `fields.json` and `precision.json` are both written out of it.
* `gradedAnswerGuard.js`, every string shape a graded answer can reach a learner
  as: the full double, twelve and nine significant digits, and the six decimals
  the course prints.
* `RoyaltyCalculator.jsx` (`pia-royalty-calculator`), `HctCalculator.jsx`
  (`pia-hct-calculator`), `LedgerCalculator.jsx` (`pia-ledger-calculator`) and
  `panelBits.jsx`.

The learning page is `src/pages/apps/PiaLearningPage.jsx`, routed at
`/dashboard/apps/pia`. The panels are registered in
`src/content/courses/panelRegistry.js`, and `panelRender.test.jsx` renders every
view of every panel. The capstone case files are generated by
`gen_course.py --cases` into `src/content/capstone-cases/pia` and are offered on
the capstone card only; each is a case in the shape the ledger views accept.

## THE THREE CALCULATORS

Every calculator that runs a ledger takes the learner's own case as JSON (cfg,
prodRows, capexRows, opexRows), because that is how a capstone is worked, and
starts from an Ekene teaching case. Every calculator shows the engine's own
refusal verbatim and every note in `kpis.pia_notes`.

### `pia-royalty-calculator` (Associate)

Five views: royalty by terrain and daily rate, with the tranche boundary table
(0.050000 at 5,000 bopd and 0.062500 at 10,000 bopd onshore); the gas royalty
with the in-country share (0.037500 at half); the royalty by price with its
benchmarks on the chosen base year and the other base printed beside it; the
instruments stacked on a ledger with the government take (Ekene Alpha's is
66.564877); and a set of refusals.

### `pia-hct-calculator` (Professional)

Five views: the hydrocarbon tax rate by terrain, licence, lease, framework and
reading, refusing a new-acreage lease onshore or in shallow water until a rate
is stated; the production allowance across the new-lease cap (16000000.000000
on the new onshore lease in 2026); the capital allowance by year of life under
each law; the tax base with the cost price ratio on a ledger (93000000.000000
forfeited on the CPR case); and companies income tax on a ledger with the two
thirds restriction.

### `pia-ledger-calculator` (Expert)

Five views: the whole ledger with the framework read year by year; the
framework table under each override; one case under every stated reading side by
side (the base year, the deep offshore readings, the new-lease rates); which
provision moved when one term changes, on the engine's own totals, with the
take in the shared `fiscalConventions.js` wording; and every engine note.

## THE RULES A PANEL KEEPS

1. Every figure a panel prints is an engine return value, at six decimals.
2. A refusal is shown as the engine's own message; the panel writes none.
3. No panel preloads a capstone case, and no panel names a capstone.
4. An open reading is a setting the learner states; no panel presents one as
   the law.
5. The copy rule and the vocabulary of digest section 25 hold on every label.

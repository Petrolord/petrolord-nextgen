-- What this tier owns: IS THE DATA FIT TO USE. A flag is a rule that fired,
-- with its reason. Missing is null, undefined or NaN, and a sentinel such as
-- -999.25 is a present value; completeness, gap runs and the coverage of an
-- interval; definitional range limits keyed by unit, plausibility ranges the
-- caller supplies, units never converted, negative rates and rates while shut
-- in; the depth and time index; a cumulative against its last present value,
-- water cut on a liquid basis, parts that add to a total, frozen runs; well
-- names after a stated normalisation and the digit rule. Digest sections 1 to
-- 16, and section 32.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A check is only as good as its stated
-- convention: a sentinel reads complete, a tolerance tighter than the
-- reporting precision flags every rounded value, and a step equal to maxStep
-- covers. The Associate capstone grades six Associate quantities and grades no
-- outlier statistic and no chart.

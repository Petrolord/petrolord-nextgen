-- H5 PROFESSIONAL DEEP SEED: Quantitative Risk Assessment, intermediate tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed, and the question rows from the
-- committed bank JSONs under tools/course-banks/qra/intermediate.
--
-- What this tier owns: HOW MANY AT ONCE. Potential loss of life is the sum of
-- frequency times the expected number of deaths, which need not be whole; the
-- fatal accident rate is PLL per 100,000,000 exposed hours, the same base as
-- the safety statistics course; the F-N curve is the frequency of N OR MORE
-- deaths, a step function whose area is the expected fatalities; a curve is
-- compared with a line at its corners, and with the single R2P2 point, and a
-- curve on the line touches it. No published worked F-N example exists, so the
-- curve is checked by self-consistency. Digest sections 15 to 24, and section
-- 34.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. "More than N" drops the scenario at every
-- corner. The Professional capstone grades a PLL, a FAR, a frequency of ten or
-- more deaths, a worst ratio to the line, where a curve exceeds and a ratio to
-- the R2P2 point, and grades no ALARP band and no cost-benefit figure.

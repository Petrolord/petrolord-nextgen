## AN ENGINE COURSE

There is no Suite app for this course. The three calculator panels below ARE
the course's practicals: they call the vendored reserves and resources engine
on the Ekene field or on whatever the learner pastes, and the learning page
says so in plain words.

## WHAT IS SHIPPED

Under `src/components/course/panels/prms/` in the NextGen repository:

* `prmsLab.js`, the ONE teaching lab. It imports the vendored Ekene fixture
  (`packages/engines/test-data/economics/ekene-prms`) and the INPUTS of the
  vendored golden file, carries thin routes to the engine for whatever a
  learner types, the view routes, the block reader, the stated-input writer
  `setStated`, the rewrites (the estimates for a method, a distribution for a
  type, a correlation for a type, a movement for a type), a JSON parser and the
  teaching readers. It holds no graded answer, no tolerance and no capstone
  case.
* `gradedTolerance.js`, **the only place a grading tolerance is made**:
  `max(stated, half a unit in the last place the course prints that class)`.
  `fields.json` and `precision.json` are both written out of it.
* `gradedAnswerGuard.js`, every string shape a graded answer can reach a
  learner as.
* `ClassificationCalculator.jsx` (`prms-classification-calculator`),
  `ReservesCalculator.jsx` (`prms-reserves-calculator`),
  `AggregationCalculator.jsx` (`prms-aggregation-calculator`) and
  `panelBits.jsx`.

The learning page is `src/pages/apps/PrmsLearningPage.jsx`, routed at
`/dashboard/apps/prms`. The panels are registered in
`src/content/courses/panelRegistry.js`, and `panelRender.test.jsx` renders
every view of every panel. The capstone case files are generated by
`gen_course.py --cases` into `src/content/capstone-cases/prms` and are offered
on the capstone card only.

## THE THREE CALCULATORS

Every view takes the learner's own inputs as JSON, because that is how a
capstone is worked. A case file holds several calls under BLOCK KEYS: the
view's own name (`economicLimit`, `reconcile`) or the view's name, a colon and
a name (`classify:prospect`, `categorize:reserves`, `aggregate:contingent`).
Every view offers the blocks of a pasted case file in a block selector, runs
the block chosen, and writes every control into that block; a box that holds
one call's inputs has no blocks and runs as it stands (no engine input key is
a view name). `prmsLab.test.js` proves every block of every case file gives
the whole case the result of the block alone, and
`panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads all eighteen graded fields with each
case file pasted WHOLE, as the capstone card offers it, choosing the block in
the selector. Every view shows the engine's own refusal and reasons, verbatim,
and a Source block with the engine's basis.

EVERY REQUIRED STATED INPUT HAS A VISIBLE CONTROL, and no panel holds a hidden
default. A control shows the input as the box states it, or "not stated", and
writes the learner's choice into the box through `setStated`; an input left
unstated reaches the engine unstated and the engine refuses by name. The rows
of a technical forecast, the prices and the opex and capex rows are one row a
year, stated in the box beside the controls. Each view carries a start selector
over the Ekene cases and the small cases the digest teaches.

### `prms-classification-calculator` (Associate)

The class and sub-class of a project from its stated facts, with each
commerciality criterion and each decision in the engine's words, and the
chance of commerciality (EKN-6 20.000000 percent, EKN-7 10.500000); the
categories of a set of estimates in both forms with the P90, P50 and P10 labels
(the Ekene Main Reserves 8.890000, 16.650000 and 24.990000, with a Probable of
7.760000 and a Possible of 8.340000). Controls: the discovery status, the
recovery project, the sub-class, the two chances, the economic status, each
commerciality criterion, the time-frame, the project status, the reserves
status, the optional Nigerian declaration; the class, the method, the unit and
the estimates.

### `prms-reserves-calculator` (Professional)

The sub-classes and criteria and the categories again, and the economic limit
of three technical forecasts through the canonical cash flow of cashflow.ts:
each case's limit year, trailing years cut, undiscounted net cash flow and NPV
at 100 percent and at the working interest, the quantities beyond the licence
and beyond the limit, and the Reserves categories in both forms in oil, gas and
BOE (EKN-1: limits 2033, 2037 and 2040, a 2P of 11229764.729167 BOE on the
net-entitlement basis). Controls for the effective year, the royalty and its
form, the tax, the allowance life, the loss relief choice, the working
interest, the licence expiry and the renewal expectation, the reporting basis,
the discount rate, the BOE factor and the abandonment cost.

### `prms-aggregation-calculator` (Expert)

The total of several projects of one class: each project's low, best, high and
mean read off its stated distribution, the arithmetic sums (the Ekene Reserves
1P 15.809794), the seeded Monte Carlo P90, P50, P10 and mean, always printed
with the seed and the draws and marked never graded (17.300834 on seed
20271112 and 20000 draws), the sum of the means, the risked mean of a risked
class (10.114488) and what may be reported at the stated level; the
reconciliation of a category set with its movements, the computed and stated
closing, the difference, whether it closes, the replacement ratio and the
life index; the economic limit again, starting on the profile where the two
rules disagree, which the engine refuses in its own words; and the readings,
each printed from its case in the engine's words. Controls for the class, the
level, the unit, the correlation (a uniform rho or pairs in the box), the seed
and the draws, and each project's id, distribution and its terms and chance;
for the reconciliation the class, the unit, the period, the tolerance, the
opening and the stated closing, and each movement's type and its quantity or
its three figures.

## THE RULES FOR EVERY PANEL

1. **No graded capstone answer, in any of four shapes**, no capstone name,
   label, input or series. `panelCapstoneGuard.test.js` sweeps the lab, the
   calculators, the bits and the learning page, and plants each shape to prove
   the sweep catches it. `panelCapstoneReadable.test.js` reads every graded
   field off a rendered panel with the capstone case pasted, at full precision.
2. **No refusal message is written in a panel or the lab.** Show `r.error`, and
   a reason as the engine returns it.
3. **No clock, no timer, no random number, no path under `/root`.** The one
   Monte Carlo runs inside the engine on the stated seed.
4. **No PRMS prose.** A label or a note in a panel is the course's own words;
   `gate_no_prms_prose.py` sweeps the panels and the page.
5. **The copy rule.** No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastive, no
   "rather than", no ", never", no "instead of". `gate_copy_rule.py` sweeps the
   panels and the page as well as the digest.
6. **The vocabulary**, digest section 31; no "AI".

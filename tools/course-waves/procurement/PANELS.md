Read `BRIEF.md` first. Every number here is quoted from `digest.txt`, the only

## AN ENGINE COURSE

There is no Suite app for this course. The three calculator panels below ARE
the course's practicals: they call the vendored tender engine on the Ekene
tenders or on whatever the learner pastes, and the learning page says so in
plain words.

## WHAT IS SHIPPED

Under `src/components/course/panels/procurement/` in the NextGen repository:

* `tenderLab.js`, the ONE teaching lab. It imports the vendored Ekene tender
  fixtures themselves (`packages/engines/test-data/supplychain/ekene-tender`,
  deep-equal to the files on disk by test), carries thin routes to the engine
  for whatever a learner types, a JSON parser, and the teaching readers. It
  holds no graded answer, no tolerance and no capstone dataset.
* `gradedTolerance.js`, **the only place a grading tolerance is made**:
  `max(stated, half a unit in the last place the course prints that class)`.
  `fields.json` and `precision.json` are both written out of it.
* `gradedAnswerGuard.js`, every string shape a graded answer can reach a
  learner as: the full double, twelve and nine significant digits, and the six
  decimals the course prints.
* `EnvelopeCalculator.jsx` (`pr-envelope-calculator`), `AwardCalculator.jsx`
  (`pr-award-calculator`), `ContractCalculator.jsx` (`pr-contract-calculator`)
  and `panelBits.jsx`.

The learning page is `src/pages/apps/ProcurementLearningPage.jsx`, routed at
`/dashboard/apps/procurement`. The panels are registered in
`src/content/courses/panelRegistry.js`, and `panelRender.test.jsx` renders
every view of every panel. The capstone case files are generated by
`gen_course.py --cases` into `src/content/capstone-cases/procurement` and are
offered on the capstone card only.

## THE THREE CALCULATORS

Every calculator takes the learner's own inputs as JSON, because that is how a
capstone is worked: criteria, bids, bill lines, content items, a programme,
cost items and partners. Every calculator starts from the Ekene tenders and
shows the engine's own refusal and reason, verbatim.

### `pr-envelope-calculator` (Associate)

Score the technical envelope against a pass mark (WS5 at 70.000000 passes);
correct a priced bill with the unit rate prevailing or a declared misplaced
decimal (WS2's correction is 18000.000000); build each passing bid's evaluated
cost with the average omission rule and the completion-time adjustment (WS5 at
862141.000000); weigh the technical and commercial scores (WS3 at 96.998434);
and run the whole tender in one call.

### `pr-award-calculator` (Professional)

Build evaluated costs with a life-cycle cost (MS4 at 546244.982386); place a
technical weight in its para 5.50 cell; test bids for an abnormally low price
by the absolute or the relative approach; measure Nigerian content item by item
in the Schedule's units (MS3 at 86.240876 overall); and apply sections 14 and
16 with BOTH readings of "at least 5% higher" side by side (MS2's lead: 4.541020
points, 7.960608 percent).

### `pr-contract-calculator` (Expert)

Compare a lump sum, a day rate and a reimbursable contract on one job on a
stated seed and iteration count, with every cost percentile beside the
exceedance definition (the calculator starts at 2000 iterations; the digest's
figures run 20000); build the should-cost and screen bids against its band
(895361.041667); run a whole tender with any award basis and the content rule;
probe a boundary; and read the engine's refusals.

## THE RULES FOR EVERY PANEL

1. **No graded capstone answer, in any of four shapes**, no capstone name, bid
   code or tender number, no capstone scope, no run of capstone unit rates and
   no distinctive capstone scalar. `panelCapstoneGuard.test.js` sweeps the lab,
   the calculators, the bits and the learning page, and plants each shape to
   prove the sweep catches it.
2. **No refusal message is written in a panel or the lab.** Show `r.error`, and
   a reason as the engine returns it.
3. **No clock, no random number, no path under `/root`.** The only random
   draws are the engine's seeded Monte Carlo.
4. **The copy rule.** No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X, not Y" contrastive.
   `gate_copy_rule.py` sweeps the panels and the page as well as the digest.
5. **The vocabulary**, digest section 25: a cost P90 is labelled the LOW
   figure; no other P label; no "AI".

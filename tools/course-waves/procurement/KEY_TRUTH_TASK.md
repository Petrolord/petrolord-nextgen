# SC2 Procurement, Tendering & Contracting: the key-truth writer's task

Read `BRIEF.md` first. Every number here is quoted from `digest.txt`, the only
teaching truth for this course. The engine's FINDINGS record, the oracle and
its golden, the fixture README and writer, and the engine's source comments are
PROVENANCE.

## TWO JOBS

1. **The key truths.** One sentence a learner should be able to say afterwards,
   with the number that makes it checkable.
2. **The second reader of every bank, before `gen_migration.py`.** Read prompt,
   options, explanation, digest and lesson for every question, one tier at a
   time, and CALL THE ENGINE (through the lab or a calculator panel, or node on
   the vendored `packages/engines/engines/supplychain/tender.js` through the
   wave's `tender_engine.mjs`) for every keyed figure. Swap roles where the
   explanation supports a distractor.

## THE DEFECT CLASSES TO READ FOR, from the FC, H and D audits

1. **A distractor that became true.** The engine returns the distractor's
   number under some stated setting the question did not rule out (another
   omission rule, another schedule, no life cycle, another technical weight or
   price method, the other s.14 reading, another seed or iteration count,
   another band).
2. **A wrong failure-mode gloss.** "Refused" said of a result the engine
   returns with a reason (a bid excluded at a stage, s.14 not engaged, a null
   lead, a band flag); "rejected" said of an abnormally low bid, which the
   engine flags for clarification only.
3. **A false superlative.** "the best bid", "always", "never" where the digest
   shows one tender at one setting. The award moves with the price method and
   with the s.14 reading.
4. **An exam question that near-duplicates a module question** (audit at
   Jaccard 0.45).
5. **A sentence characterising a relationship nobody computed.** Every
   "cheaper", "more", "higher" rests on two printed figures.
6. **The answer printed before the work** in the prompt or a table above it.
7. **A capstone name, bid code, scope, price or value** in any prompt, option
   or explanation.
8. **The answer-length tell**, and three distractors opening the same way while
   the key does not.
9. **Printed alike keyed as equal.** Two figures equal at six decimals are
   equal only where the digest says the engine returns them equal.
10. **A boundary stated globally.** Each rule draws its own (the boundary
    table): a gap equal to the arithmetic tolerance is not corrected, a score at
    the pass mark passes, exactly 20 percent below the estimate is flagged, a
    price at the relative ALB limit is not.
11. **A message quoted inexactly.** A refusal, a reason or a basis is quoted
    verbatim or not at all.
12. **A digest section number in learner-visible text.** Say "the course".
13. **The regulatory rule broken.** A law named without its edition, a
    licensed text quoted, a threshold the digest does not print, or one s.14
    reading keyed as the Act's settled meaning.
14. **A cost P90 read as the high cost.**

## THE KEY TRUTHS THIS COURSE OWES ITS LEARNERS

### Associate

1. A bid that fails a mandatory requirement is not scored: WS6 fails
   signed-bid-form and carries no technical percentage.
2. A bid at the pass mark passes: WS5 scores 70.000000 against a pass mark of
   70; WS4 scores 65.000000 and its price is never opened.
3. The unit rate prevails over the line amount: WS2's coiled tubing line is
   corrected to 486000.000000 and its total rises by 18000.000000.
4. An omitted item is priced at the average of the other responsive bids: WS3's
   nitrogen adds 35400.000000.
5. No credit is given for early completion: WS1 finishes in 6 weeks and its
   schedule adjustment is 0.000000.
6. The lowest evaluated cost and the award can differ: WS5 is lowest at
   862141.000000 and WS3 is most advantageous at 96.998434.
7. The Guidance's worked example ranks D, C, B, A, and the engine agrees; the
   printed 98.34 is the exact 98.333333 rounded up.

### Professional

8. A life-cycle cost is a net present cost: MS4's five years of valve
   maintenance discount to 26535.507386 at 0.1.
9. The technical weight sits in a para 5.50 cell: 0.7 on a high-risk contract
   under US$10 million is inside the range 0.600000 to 1.000000.
10. The price method can move the award: WS3 under the lowest ratio, WS5 under
    the linear method.
11. A relative price score depends on the field: removing A widens the Chen gap
    between B and C from 15.000000 to 18.750000.
12. The relative ALB limit uses the population standard deviation: Annex I
    Example 1 gives 1348451.837504, and a flagged bid is clarified, never
    rejected automatically.
13. Content is measured in the Schedule's unit and weighted across units: MS3's
    overall content is 86.240876.
14. The Act does not say how to read "at least 5% higher": MS2's lead over MS4
    is 4.541020 percentage points or 7.960608 percent, and the reading decides.
15. Section 16 protects and selects nothing: MS3 sits 6.445634 percent above
    the lowest and is protected.

### Expert

16. Under a lump sum the company pays nothing above plan: the contractor
    absorbs the whole expected overrun, 107001.253374, and loses money in
    0.199500 of the iterations.
17. Under cost plus 12 percent the company carries more than the overrun: its
    share is 1.120000.
18. For a cost, P90 is the low figure: the day rate's P90 is 837897.131515 and
    its P10 1029964.481686, on seed 20270211 with 20000 iterations.
19. Should-cost is the company's estimate built from the programme:
    895361.041667, of which the operator carries 402912.468750.
20. When nobody passes the technical envelope, no price is opened: at a pass
    mark of 90 the award is null.
21. The Guidance disagrees with itself: Annex 2 prints B's total as 82 where
    its printed scores sum to 77.000000.
22. A tie is a stated rule: the costs 1000000.0000001 and 1000000 tie at twelve
    significant digits and the bidder id decides.

## THE RULES

Digest section 25's vocabulary is binding. No em dashes, no en dashes, no "X,
not Y" contrastive. No key truth may carry a capstone name, bid code, scope,
price, stated seed or graded answer at any precision.

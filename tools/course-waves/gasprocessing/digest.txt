# SECTION 1: What this engine conditions, and what it refuses (owned by Associate m01)

# App surface: the Gas Processing Studio runs three units over one gas stream. Dehydration takes water out with glycol, sweetening takes acid gas out with amine, and the dew point unit cools the gas by letting it down.
- This engine conditions a GAS STREAM. It answers how much water a gas carries, how much solvent it takes to remove it, what the regenerator costs to run, how wide the vessel has to be, and how far a let-down cools the gas.
- The doctrine the module was written against is stated in its own header: everything that is a DESIGN CHOICE or a chart value is an INPUT with its customary range named, and everything computable from first principles is computed. The predecessor app hid three numbers inside constants; this one asks for them.
- A state the method has no answer for comes back as an object with an `error` string. This module throws nothing. It has ONE export that breaks that contract by returning a bare number, and Section 15 reads it.
- What is NOT in this engine: no hydrate boundary, no compositional flash, no rate-based absorber model, no stage efficiency, no molecular sieve, no refrigeration and no NGL recovery. A hydrate margin is the Production module Flow Assurance engine, and the phase envelope of a reservoir fluid is the Fluid engine.

The three units, on the two teaching streams, end to end:
- OBIAFU carries 53.449474 lb of water per MMscf at 950.000000 psia and 104.000000 degF, and at 62.000000 MMscfd a spec of 7.000000 lb per MMscf means taking out 2879.8674 lb a day, which at 3.200000 gal per lb is 6.399705 gpm of glycol and 0.697255 MMBtu an hour of reboiler.

# SECTION 2: The constants this module keeps to itself (owned by Associate m01 l04 and Expert m05)

Every figure in this section is MEASURED out of the engine by asking it a question whose answer is the constant and nothing else. None is typed.
- the standard cubic feet in a pound mole: 379.490000. Measured from the BTEX mole balance at one MMscfd, a million ppmv, a unit absorbed fraction and a unit molecular weight, where the answer is a million over this number and nothing else.
- the molecular weight of water: 18.015280. Measured from the saturation answer divided by the mole fraction the same call returns, over the standard cubic feet just measured.
- the water overhead the reboiler pays for: 1100.000000 Btu per lb. Measured at a circulation ratio of one gallon per pound and no reflux, where the vaporization term IS the overhead. The module's own comment calls this a latent heat of about 970 folded together with the sensible heat to the still; only the folded figure is in the code, and the 970 is not.
- the minutes in a day: 1440.000000. Measured by dividing the gallons a day the engine returns by the gallons a minute the same call returns.
- the days in a year over the pounds in a short ton: 0.182500. Measured as one BTEX answer divided by another from the same call. The two cannot be separated from outside, because they only ever appear as this ratio.
- the hours in a day times the Btu in a MMBtu: 24000000. Measured as the gallons a day times the duty per gallon over the duty in MMBtu an hour.
- the liquid density the contactor sizes against: 69.900000 lb per ft3. Measured from the gas density and the allowed velocity the same call returns, since the velocity is the K value times the root of the density ratio.
- the pounds per gallon of water the amine gallons chain divides by: 8.340000. Measured from the acid gas the engine says it picked up, through the swing, the molecular weight and the solution gravity the amine table exports, against the circulation the engine returned.
- the contactor's standard-condition group, base pressure over base temperature: 0.028173077 psia per degR. Measured from the allowed velocity and the diameter, which together give the actual volume the engine formed.
- the gas constant over the Btu conversion in the Joule-Thomson routine: 1.985881. Measured at a unit heat capacity from the coefficient, the compressibility and the derivative the same call returns.
- the water vapour pressure at the freezing point: 0.088609355 psia. This is the Magnus coefficient itself, because the exponent is exactly zero there.
- the Rankine offset, from the gas properties module's own door: 459.670000 degR.
- the molecular weight of dry air and the gas constant, both exported rather than measured: 28.962500 and 10.731600.

Two of those are worth a second look, and BOTH comparisons below are computed on this page rather than asserted:
- the module's comment says its 379.490000 standard cubic feet per pound mole are measured at 14.65 psia. The gas constant and the Rankine offset it also uses put the pressure that gives that molar volume at 60 degF at 14.695751 psia, which is 1.003123 times the 14.65 the comment names.

# SECTION 4: Where the ideal answer stops (owned by Associate m02 l05 and Expert m05)

Two separate limits sit on the water answer, and they are not the same limit.
- THE CHART LIMIT. Above 1000.000000 psia the engine attaches a warning and keeps answering, because real-gas behaviour makes the true content higher than ideal mixing says. Read from both sides at the threshold itself: at 1000.000000 psia the warning is null and at 1000.000001 psia it is "ideal-mixing estimate: above about 1000 psia the real-gas correction of the McKetta-Wehe chart grows to tens of percent; use a chart reading for design".
- THE FIT LIMIT. The vapour-pressure fit is guarded to a temperature band and refuses outside it. At -49.000000 degF it returns 0.001615 psia and at -49.000001 degF it returns NaN. At 212.000000 degF it returns 15.095051 psia and at 212.000001 degF it returns NaN.
- The module's own docstring names a narrower upper edge than the guard enforces, at 140.000000 degF, where the fit returns 2.904091 psia. Between that and the guard the module answers without a note.
- At the guard's own upper edge the fit puts the vapour pressure of water at 15.095051 psia. Water boils at one standard atmosphere there by definition, so the fit reads 1.027157 times the pressure the definition fixes (derived, the figure on this line over the standard atmosphere).

What a refusal looks like, and what it protects:
- a temperature below the fit: { error: "water saturation fit holds from -45 to 100 C" }
- a temperature above the fit: { error: "water saturation fit holds from -45 to 100 C" }
- a total pressure below the vapour pressure: { error: "total pressure must exceed the water vapor pressure" }
- a total pressure exactly at the vapour pressure: { error: "total pressure must exceed the water vapor pressure" }
The third and fourth are the same guard read from either side of its own limit. A gas at exactly its water vapour pressure is all water and nothing else, so refusing the equality is right rather than over-strict.

Below unity the removal never passes the absorption factor however many stages are bought, and the last column says how close 200 stages get. It is exactly zero for the lower factors, because the factor raised to the stage count has fallen below anything double precision can hold and the relation collapses to the factor itself. Only near one does 200 stages fall measurably short. AT AND ABOVE UNITY THAT COLUMN MEANS NOTHING, because there is no ceiling there to measure against: it is the distance from a removal that cannot exceed one to a factor that can, and it goes more negative the larger the factor, which is arithmetic about the column rather than physics about the absorber.
At unity the closed form is indeterminate and the engine takes a separate branch, the stages over the stages plus one. Either side of unity by a billionth the answer is continuous with it, which is what says the branch is a limit rather than a patch.

# SECTION 14: WITHHELD. The Joule-Thomson chain (would be owned by Expert m01, m02 and m03)

# SECTION 16: What the method does not know (owned by Expert m05 l05)

Six things this course teaches as limits and never as answers:

4. The three amines' property set. HELD FOR LITERATURE. The molecular weights are chemistry; the typical strengths, the rich limits, the duties and the solution gravities are customary practice with no source in this package.
5. The BTEX absorbed fraction and its single molecular weight. HELD FOR LITERATURE. The engine is explicit that the fraction is a chart or operating value, and the molecular weight is one compound standing for four.
6. The published cases in this golden are SYNTHETIC. They come from an oracle written in Python against the same physics. One route in it is genuinely independent: the water content is checked against a DIFFERENT published vapour-pressure equation, and the Kremser relation is checked against a brute-force stage cascade solved as a linear system. # SECTION 17: What the published cases can and cannot catch (owned by Expert m05 l02 and l03)

A gate that restates the engine's own formula validates nothing. Three of the five routes in this module's oracle do exactly that, and the digest can show it without leaving the engine.

The two routes that ARE independent, and the evidence:
- WATER CONTENT. The oracle uses a different published vapour-pressure equation. That is why the ratios in Section 3 sit near one rather than at one: the largest gap across the four published cases is 0.006381649 (derived, the largest absolute departure from one in the Section 3 table). A gate whose two sides agree to twelve decimals has not been shown to have two sides.
- KREMSER. The oracle solves the stage cascade as a linear system. The closed form and the cascade agree to 0.000000000000 at worst across the five published cases (derived, the largest absolute departure from one in the Section 9 table), which is agreement between two genuinely different pieces of arithmetic rather than one piece run twice.

The three routes that are NOT independent. In each case the oracle carries the SAME constant and the SAME expression shape as the engine, so regenerating the golden after a change to either makes the pair agree again at any value:
- the dehydration balance carries the 1100.000000 Btu a lb overhead in both files;
- the sweetening balance carries the same pounds per gallon of water, measured in Section 2 as 8.340000, in both files;
- the contactor carries the same 69.900000 lb per ft3 liquid, the same base group and the same gas constant in both files.
Those three are why every ratio in Sections 8, 10 and 12 is one to twelve decimals while the two independent ratios are not. IDENTICAL TO TWELVE DECIMALS IS NOT A STRONGER RESULT THAN AGREEING TO SIX. It is a weaker one, because it is what two copies of one calculation produce.

And one whole routine with NO published case at all: the Joule-Thomson chain has no row in this golden, no oracle route and no check beyond a range. That is where the FC4-0 recon found its defect, and Section 14 is why this digest prints none of it.

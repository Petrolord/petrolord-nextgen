-- FC4 ASSOCIATE DEEP SEED: Gas Processing, beginner tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: WATER, AND WHAT IT COSTS TO TAKE OUT. What this engine
-- conditions and what it refuses to answer; how much water a gas carries at a
-- pressure and a temperature, and the band the fit stands behind; the water a
-- unit takes out, which is the content difference applied to the rate; the
-- circulation that takes it out; what the reboiler pays for, named part by
-- named part rather than arriving as one number; and a dehydration answer read
-- end to end.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. A water CONTENT knows nothing about the rate
-- and a water RATE is nothing but that content applied to it. The tier is built
-- on the difference between an intensive answer and an extensive one, because
-- it is the difference a reader most often loses, and the digest computes the
-- two side by side rather than asserting they differ.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. The circulation ratio is a CHOICE this engine
-- refuses to make. It takes the gallons of glycol per pound of water as an
-- input, flags a ratio outside the customary band, and computes everything
-- downstream from the number it was handed. A reader who quotes a circulation
-- without saying which ratio produced it has quoted an opinion.
--
-- HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded in any tier: the
-- McKetta and Wehe real-gas correction above the pressure the engine warns at,
-- the water overhead the reboiler pays for, the amine contactor's liquid
-- density, the customary amine duty per gallon, and the BTEX absorbed fraction
-- and molecular weight. The capstone grades none of them, and each is
-- neutralised by construction rather than by hope.
--
-- The only numbers that travel from this tier to the next are the saturation
-- fit and the shape of a circulation, which the amine half then reaches by a
-- route with nothing in common.

-- ============================================================================
-- FC3 ASSOCIATE DEEP SEED: Rotating Equipment, beginner tier.
--
-- Six modules, 26 lessons, 132 questions (15 per module bank plus a 42
-- question final exam). The structure row is GENERATED from the tier's
-- manifest.json rather than hand typed.
--
-- What this tier owns: THE PUMP AND THE SYSTEM IT WORKS INTO. What these two
-- modules size and what they refuse to guess (they size MACHINES, and what
-- the piping costs in pressure is a line sizing question living in
-- lineHydraulics.js); the fitted pump curve and the conditioning that fit
-- costs; the system curve, which is a static head plus a friction head that
-- goes as the square of flow; the duty SOLVED as their intersection; the
-- power that duty costs, hydraulic then brake then motor input; the
-- conversion of a head to a pressure; and the reading of where the duty
-- landed.
--
-- THE ENGINE FACT THIS TIER OWNS. The two field packagings are ONE constant.
-- The 2.31 ft of head per psi at specific gravity 1 and the 3960 in the
-- horsepower packaging both imply a water density of 62.33766233766234 lb per
-- ft3, and the two agree exactly. The digest computes that agreement rather
-- than claiming it.
--
-- THE TRAP THE TIER IS BUILT ON. A pump has NO operating point until it is
-- connected to something. A curve says what the machine makes and a system
-- curve says what the piping demands, and neither has a duty. Every number
-- after the duty moves when the system moves, which is why a reader who
-- quotes a flow off a catalogue curve has quoted nothing.
--
-- HELD FOR LITERATURE, taught as a limit and never graded in any tier: the
-- Hydraulic Institute viscosity correction, the trim shortfall model, the
-- operating region bands of 50, 70, 120 and 140 percent, the NPSH margin rule
-- of the larger of 3 ft and 35 percent of required, and the machine screening
-- thresholds. The capstone never calls any of them.
--
-- The only numbers that travel from this tier to the next are the duty solve
-- itself and the power packagings.
-- ============================================================================

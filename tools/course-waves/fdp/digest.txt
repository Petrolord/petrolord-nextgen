# SECTION 1: What a field development plan holds, and what the engine refuses (owned by Associate m01)

- The plan is one field: its reserves, one or more development concepts, the wells and facilities a concept implies, a schedule, a cost breakdown, a risk register, and the economics those imply.
- The studio holds the plan together. It does not re-derive the subsurface, the well designs or the facility sizing: a figure typed into it is only as good as the work behind it.
- Screening economics run through the sanctioned engine: post royalty and tax, discounted mid year. Default terms are royalty 12.5000 percent, tax 30.0000 percent, discount 10.0000 percent and variable operating cost 5.0000 USD a barrel.
- A figure the plan does not carry is refused by name. Nothing is substituted quietly.
- Until September 2026 four figures were substituted silently when the plan did not carry them: a concept capex of 100 million USD, an operating cost of 10 million USD a year, a peak rate of 50 kbpd and an oil price of 70 USD a barrel. A card built on all four still looked like an answer.

# SECTION 8: Dates that do not move (owned by Professional m02)

- A date-only string is parsed as LOCAL midnight. `new Date("2027-04-01")` is UTC midnight, which is the day before anywhere west of Greenwich, so a schedule drawn that way moved by a day depending on who looked at it.
- A span is counted in whole calendar days, not by subtracting two timestamps: across a daylight-saving change a millisecond difference is out by an hour.
- Oct 30 to Nov 3, a window that crosses a daylight-saving change in some zones: 4 days (engine, and the same in every zone).
- an activity with no readable dates: calendar span null, which is the honest answer (engine).
- an empty schedule: 0 (engine).

Milestones are the activities of zero duration, or those typed as one:
- EGINA: ["Project sanction","First oil"] (engine).

Concept schedules are dated from the concept, never from the clock:
- a FPSO sanctioned 2027-04-01: first oil 2030-04-01, 36 months (engine).
- a Platform sanctioned 2027-04-01: first oil 2029-04-01, 24 months (engine).
- a FPSO sanctioned 2028-02-29: first oil 2031-03-01, 36 months (engine).
- an unreadable start date with no today given: FdpInputError: "the concept has no start date: enter one, or pass today to date the schedule from"

# SECTION 13: The rate of return, and when there is none (owned by Expert m01 and Expert m02)

The internal rate of return is the discount rate at which the net present value is zero. It exists only when the cash flow changes sign, it is not unique when the flow changes sign more than once, and it can be outside the range a solver searches.
The engine searches between -99 and 1000 percent and reports a rate only when it is a root inside that band. Otherwise irr is null and irrStatus says which of these happened: ["ok","no-sign-change","above-clamp","multiple-roots","no-root"].
# App surface: the scenario card colours an internal rate of return of 15 percent or more green and anything below it amber, which is the only hurdle rate anywhere in the studio. It is a colour on a card and not a decision rule, and it says nothing about how much money a scenario carries.

# The grain size, and an exponent that is held

The media a bed is packed with moves its answer more than any other input, through the strongest dependence in the device. It is also the one place in this device where the model is making a statement the repository cannot check.

{{panel:pw-device-explorer}}

## Why the grain size matters so much

Two separate effects run the same way. Finer grains mean MORE collectors in a given volume of bed, and finer grains also make each collector better at interception, because the droplet has to be diverted less far to reach the surface. The interception derivation multiplies the two together and gives the inverse CUBE of the grain diameter.

That is a very strong dependence. A modest change of media is a large change in the filter coefficient:

| media micron | lambda per m | cut micron | lambda over the 800 micron row |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | 17.765065302925 | 4.416867 | 8.000000 |
| 650 | 4.140060735138 | 9.149437 | 1.864360 |
| 800 | 2.220633162866 | 12.492787 | 1.000000 |
| 1200 | 0.657965381590 | 22.950715 | 0.296296 |
| 1600 | 0.277579145358 | 35.334937 | 0.125000 |

The last column is derived, each lambda over the row at the module's own reference grain of 800 micron. It is the CUBE of the grain ratio, which the 8.000000 and the 0.125000 rows show most plainly.

## The exponent is HELD FOR LITERATURE

Now the important part. The interception derivation gives the cube, the input moves the answer by a large factor, and this repository carries no bed data to check the exponent against.

So the grain column is the MODEL'S STATEMENT rather than a measurement. A reader should treat it that way, and this course says so every time the column appears. No gate here validates that exponent, because there is nothing here to validate it with, and no citation may be invented for it.

## What held means, and what it is worth

Held is one of the four kinds of number this module deals in. It means the repository carries no publication for the value or the rule, so the module states the ABSENCE rather than guessing.

That is the honest form and it is also the harder one to write. It would have been easy to bury an exponent of three in the code and let a reader assume somebody had checked it. Stating that nobody here has is what lets a reader with real bed data know exactly which line to argue with.

## The one place the column is exactly one

At the reference grain of 800 micron the ratio is exactly 1.000000, because that is the grain the filter coefficient is declared at. Work at that grain and the held exponent is not doing anything to your answer, which is a reasonable thing to know when you want a result that does not depend on it.

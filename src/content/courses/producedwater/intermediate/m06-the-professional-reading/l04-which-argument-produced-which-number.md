# Which argument produced which number

This is the last lesson of the tier, and it has one job. Given a cut size out of this module, you should be able to say what kind of argument produced it and what would move it.

{{panel:pw-train-explorer}}

## The four arguments, in one place

A GRAVITY DEVICE inverts a surface loading. The flow over the plan area, times an allowance, is a design rise velocity, and the droplet that rises that fast is the cut. The depth does not enter it. The plan area does.

A HYDROCYCLONE marches a droplet. A field, a travel from the half area radius to the oil core, and a residence time from the liner volume over the flow through it. The liner count decides all three by deciding the turndown.

A FLOTATION CELL is a rate against a time. A gas flux over a plan area, a bubble that rises by the full drag balance, an interception rate carrying the inverse cube of the bubble diameter, and a residence long enough for the rate to use up the log of two.

A MEDIA BED is an exponential through a depth, read backwards. The filter coefficient at a declared reference triple, scaled by the grain size and the loading rate, and the cut is the droplet the bed removes half of over its own depth.

## And the one thing they share

Whatever produced the cut size, what a device DOES with it is the same operation every time: the grade curve integrated against the droplet distribution in front of it, with the surviving volume renormalised and handed on.

That is why devices this unlike can be put in one train at all. A cut size is the interface between a device and a distribution, and once a device has produced one, the rest of the module treats it identically no matter where it came from.

## The coupling, isolated

Put five IDENTICAL devices in series, each cutting at 9 micron, on one water. If a device were a fixed efficiency, every row of this column would be the same number:

| stage | removal percent this stage | outlet ppm | outlet median micron |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 65.398946 | 622.818975 | 6.100736 |
| 2 | 31.825798 | 424.601866 | 4.835193 |
| 3 | 20.530945 | 337.427088 | 4.264879 |
| 4 | 15.300074 | 285.800496 | 3.918712 |
| 5 | 12.284239 | 250.692079 | 3.670618 |

The fifth identical device removes 12.284239 percent where the first removed 65.398946, because the water reaching it has had its coarse oil taken out 4 times already.

Compounding the first stage removal five times over would predict an outlet of 8.927280 ppm. The train says 250.692079 ppm.

## What to carry out of this tier

Three habits. Ask which argument produced a number. Ask what status the number has, derived or declared or calibrated or held. And sweep the input a user is most tempted to change, to find out whether the model has a limit in it.

All three of them are portable. None of them needs produced water, and none of them needs this engine. They are what to do when somebody hands you a model and a result and asks whether the result can be used.

# Three unlike devices in one train

Everything this tier has built now goes in one vessel line. A hydrocyclone bank, a flotation cell bank and a media bed, on one water, in series.

{{panel:pw-train-explorer}}

## The water they all see

KOKORI, 75000 bwpd at 53 C and 15000 ppm TDS, 29 API. The water is 0.000531486500 Pa.s and 997.174641 kg/m3 against 857.655928 kg/m3 of oil, a difference of 139.518714 kg/m3, which is derived from the two densities.

Every cut size below stands on that density difference and that viscosity, which is the Associate tier's half of this course doing its work underneath.

## Three cut sizes from three arguments

| device | what sets its cut | cut micron | sharpness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 200 liners | a centrifugal field of 1322.687929 g over a residence of 0.975987 s | 4.430689 | 3 |
| 5 cells of 12 m3 | interception on a 400 micron bubble swarm at a holdup of 0.145092311579 | 23.734355 | 2 |
| a 20 m2 bed | depth filtration at 4.140060735138 per m over 0.8 m | 9.149437 | 2 |

Three numbers of the same kind, produced by three arguments with almost nothing in common. A field and a travel. A collision rate against a residence. An exponential penetration through a depth.

Each of them responds to a different input. The liner cut moves with the liner count, because that sets the turndown and the turndown sets the field and the residence together. The cell cut moves with the bubble size and the gas flux. The bed cut moves with the depth, the grain size and the area, and only the area reaches it through the loading rate. A designer who wants a finer train has three quite different levers.

Overall 97.139736 percent removed, outlet 25.742373 ppm, and the droplet median falls from 20.000000 to 5.557324 micron.

## Reading the middle row

The flotation stage has the coarsest cut of the three and removes 11.678001 percent. Do not read that as the cell being a poor device. Read the outlet median column above it: the water arriving at that cell has a median of 6.843000 micron, because the liner bank has already taken the coarse oil out.

A device removes the droplets it is good at, so the next device faces finer water than the inlet did and performs worse on it than its own cut size suggests. That coupling is what a table of fixed efficiencies throws away, and it is the subject of the Expert tier.

## What the outlet columns are for

Read the outlet ppm column and the outlet median column together down the table. The concentration is what a downstream process cares about. The median is what the NEXT device cares about, because it describes the water that device will be asked to work on.

## The sharpness column is not decoration

The two sharpnesses in that table are not a style choice. The 3 is DECLARED for the centrifugal device and the 2 is DERIVED for the two interception devices, and the difference is worth real percentage points of removal on the same water.

That is the last place to place a number in this tier. One of those two came from a frozen constant somebody chose and pinned. The other came from the interception law. They sit in the same column and they have completely different standing.

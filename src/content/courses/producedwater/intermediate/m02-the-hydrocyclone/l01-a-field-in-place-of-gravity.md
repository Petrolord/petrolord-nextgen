# A field in place of gravity

A de-oiling hydrocyclone has no moving parts and removes droplets a basin cannot see. It does it by spinning the water, so that the same buoyancy the Associate tier worked with acts in a field of hundreds of times gravity.

{{panel:pw-device-explorer}}

## The same balance, a bigger number in it

Nothing about the physics changes. An oil droplet in water is lighter than what surrounds it, and it migrates towards the low pressure side of whatever field it sits in. In a basin that field is gravity and the droplet goes up. In a spinning liner the field points inward, and the droplet goes to the axis.

What changes is the size of the field. The KOKORI bank in this course develops 1322.687929 g. A droplet that rises at some velocity under gravity migrates radially more than a thousand times faster than that here, which is why a liner catches oil that no plan area could buy.

## What a de-oiling liner is

A liner is a tube. Water enters tangentially and spirals down it, oil collects on the axis as a core, and cleaned water leaves at the far end. A bank is many liners in parallel in one vessel, and it is SIZED IN LINERS, which is a fact this tier spends a whole module on.

This module states the liner geometry rather than hiding it, with a bore of 0.035 m and a length of 0.7 m in the declared constants, plus a rated flow of 0.0006 m3/s per liner and the field of 1000 g that one liner develops at that flow. Those four numbers are DECLARED, so they are choices the module makes visible and a reviewer can argue with.

## Why stated geometry matters

A cut size is only checkable if the geometry that produced it is on the page. Because the bore, the length, the rated flow and the rated field are all stated, the independent oracle for this device can take the cut size the engine reports, fire droplets of that diameter from starting radii spread across the liner, march each one through the field, and count how many reach the core. It finds 0.500455 of them do, against the one half a cut size is defined as.

That is a different method arriving at the definition. A model that produced its cut from a correlation with no geometry in it could not be checked that way at all.

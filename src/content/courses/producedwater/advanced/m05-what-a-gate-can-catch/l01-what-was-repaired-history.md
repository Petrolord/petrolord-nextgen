# What was repaired in this engine, and what was not

This lesson is HISTORY. Everything below is what this module did before its repairs. Every other lesson, panel and figure here is the repaired engine answering now, and this is the only place repair history belongs.

## Before the first repair

THE HYDROCYCLONE REWARDED BUYING FEWER LINERS. The field went as the square of the flow with nothing above it, so the cut size fell without limit as liners were removed, and the studio's shipped default ran its liners at 7.7 times their design flow. THE LESSON IS THE SWEEP: sweep the input a user is most tempted to reduce, and look at the direction of the answer. One level down, the liner LENGTH was not an input at all, and the bore acted through a bare divisor rather than through any residence time.

FOUR DEVICES TRUSTED WHAT A FIFTH REFUSED. Given an oil heavier than its water, one export refused by name and four returned a cut size of NaN with no error, and the train gave a spec verdict over whatever ran. THE LESSON IS THE RULE: when two halves of one module disagree, THE TRUSTING HALF IS THE BUG.

TWO MODELS WERE DEAD. An attachment fraction of exactly one whatever a user typed made the bubble size, the gas rate and the residence time decorative. A media filter computed its removal by two routes that disagreed, and the train read the one no gate validated. THE LESSON IS THE PROBE: sweep every input and check it moves the answer.

A MEDIAN WAS REPORTED AGAINST A DIFFERENT KIND OF NUMBER. The inlet median was the typed d50 and the outlet one was measured off the bins, so a train removing nothing showed the median falling. THE LESSON IS THE BASIS.

THE GATE COULD NOT CATCH ANY OF IT.

Sixteen defects planted in the engine alone left the suite green, and six more survived being planted in the engine and the oracle together.

## Three more, found by this course

A BED AREA THAT MOVED NOTHING. The media filter clamped its own loading rate up to a floor and said nothing, so below that rate beds of very different area reported the same filter coefficient and cut size to every digit. The clamp was in the oracle too, so the independent check agreed. THE LESSON IS THE SILENT CLAMP: a guard that quietly moves an input deletes it, and the repair is to REFUSE by name.

A THRESHOLD NOBODY HAD DECLARED. The flotation residence warning compared against a bare 60 written inside its own sentence, and the droplet grid's two floors were bare the same way. THE LESSON IS THE LEDGER: a threshold a reader cannot find is a threshold nobody reviews.

A SILENT WAY BACK TO THE QUANTISED MEDIAN. The volume median interpolated when a bin carried its edges and fell back to the bin midpoint when it did not, with nothing saying which route ran. THE LESSON IS THE FALLBACK: a fallback that answers is worse than a refusal, because nobody can tell it from the right answer.

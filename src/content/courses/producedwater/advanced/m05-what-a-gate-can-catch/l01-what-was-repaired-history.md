# What was repaired in this engine, and what was not

This lesson is HISTORY. Everything below describes what this module used to do, before the repairs that produced the engine the rest of this course teaches. Every other lesson, panel and figure here is the repaired engine answering now.

## Before the first repair

THE HYDROCYCLONE REWARDED BUYING FEWER LINERS. The field went as the square of the flow with nothing above it, so the cut size fell without limit as liners were removed, and the studio's shipped default ran its liners at 7.7 times their design flow. THE LESSON IS THE SWEEP: sweep the input a user is most tempted to reduce, and look at the direction of the answer.

FOUR DEVICES TRUSTED WHAT A FIFTH REFUSED. Given an oil heavier than its water, one export refused by name and four returned a cut size of NaN with no error, and the train returned a spec verdict computed over whatever ran. THE LESSON IS THE RULE: when two halves of one module disagree, THE TRUSTING HALF IS THE BUG.

TWO MODELS WERE DEAD. An attachment fraction of exactly one across every input a user could type made the bubble size, the gas rate and the residence time decorative. A media filter computed its removal by two routes that disagreed, and the train read the one the gate did not validate. THE LESSON IS THE PROBE: sweep every input and check it moves the answer.

A MEDIAN WAS REPORTED AGAINST A DIFFERENT KIND OF NUMBER. The inlet median was the typed d50 and the outlet median was measured off the bins, so a train that removed nothing showed the median falling. THE LESSON IS THE BASIS.

THE GATE COULD NOT CATCH ANY OF IT.

Sixteen defects planted in the engine alone left the suite fully green, and six more survived planting in the engine and the oracle together.

## Three more, found by this course

A BED AREA THAT MOVED NOTHING. The media filter clamped its own loading rate up to a floor and said nothing, so under that rate four beds of completely different area reported the same filter coefficient and the same cut size to every digit. The clamp was in the oracle too, so the independent check agreed with it. THE LESSON IS THE SILENT CLAMP: a guard that quietly moves an input deletes it, and the repair is to REFUSE by name.

A THRESHOLD NOBODY HAD DECLARED. The flotation residence warning compared against a bare 60 written inside the sentence reporting it, and the droplet grid's two floors were bare the same way. THE LESSON IS THE LEDGER: a threshold a reader cannot find is a threshold nobody reviews.

A SILENT WAY BACK TO THE QUANTISED MEDIAN. The volume median interpolated when a bin carried its edges and fell back to the bin midpoint when it did not, with nothing saying which route ran. THE LESSON IS THE FALLBACK: a fallback that answers is worse than a refusal, because nobody can tell it from the right answer.

# What this engine refuses to guess

Most of a course is about answers. This lesson is about the three ways this module declines to give one, because a reader who cannot tell them apart will read a blank as a failure and a warning as a blessing.

## A refusal names the input that caused it

When the method has no answer for a state, the call comes back as an object carrying a named error string, and this module throws nothing at all. The refusal names the input. Hand the basin a short-circuit allowance of 0, and the engine says that the short-circuit factor F must be positive and this is 0, and that an F of zero or less leaves the separator undefined. Hand it -2 and it says the same thing about -2. Hand it 6 and it says that F is a turbulence allowance customarily between 1.3 and 1.8, that this module holds it to 5, and that this is 6.

## A withheld verdict names its reason

A refusal is a computation that did not happen. A withheld verdict is different: the computation ran, the numbers are all there, and the engine still will not turn them into a pass or a fail. Run the UZERE stream through a basin and a plate pack and the engine reports every figure along the way, then returns the verdict as null, because no discharge specification was given.

## A warning withholds nothing

The third act is the mildest. A warning is the engine answering in full and saying something about that answer at the same time. Reading a warning as a refusal throws away a usable number. Reading a refusal as a warning invents one.

## What the surface looks like from outside

The module exports 20 names. 16 of them are callable and 4 are frozen objects or strings. Of the callable ones, 13 carry the error contract described above, and 3 are leaves that answer with a bare number and say they have no answer with a bare NaN. A leaf cannot explain itself, and its callers are what turn its silence into a named refusal.

## What it will not guess

Six things in this module are held for literature, which means the engine states their absence rather than guessing a value. The discharge limit is the one that catches people out. This engine states none at all, and a test asserts that its declared constants carry no specification-like key. A caller may supply a specification and the engine will compare against it. The comparison is withheld when any stage of the train did not run or when the figure supplied is not positive.

## Exercise

Write one sentence each for a refusal, a withheld verdict and a warning. Then say which of the three a studio screen is most likely to paint red by mistake.

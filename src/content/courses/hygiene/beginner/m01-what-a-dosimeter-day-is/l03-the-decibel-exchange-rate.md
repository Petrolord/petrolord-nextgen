# The decibel exchange rate

{{panel:hy-noise-dosimeter}}

The OSHA criteria carry a decibel exchange rate of 5.000000000000 dB. The NIOSH noise REL carries 3.000000000000 dB. The engine measures each one by asking for the level that gives a 4 hour reference duration and subtracting the criterion level: on OSHA that level is 95.000000 dBA against 90.000000, and on NIOSH it is 88.000000 dBA against 85.000000.

The decibel exchange rate is the step in sound level that halves the allowed time. Raise the level by one step and the reference duration halves. Lower it by one step and the reference duration doubles.

## Two ladders

Read the two criteria side by side and the difference is plain. Each row below is taken from the engine's reproduction of OSHA Table G-16a and NIOSH Table 1-1.

| hours | OSHA level, dBA | NIOSH level, dBA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8.000000 | 90.000000 | 85.000000 |
| 4.000000 | 95.000000 | 88.000000 |
| 2.000000 | 100.000000 | 91.000000 |
| 1.000000 | 105.000000 | 94.000000 |
| 0.500000 | 110.000000 | 97.000000 |
| 0.250000 | 115.000000 | 100.000000 |

On OSHA each halving of time costs 5 dB. On NIOSH it costs 3 dB, so the NIOSH ladder climbs more slowly and runs out of time sooner. A quarter of an hour is reached at 100.000000 dBA on NIOSH and at 115.000000 dBA on OSHA.

## Why the rate matters more than the starting point

The two criteria also start in different places, 90.000000 dBA against 85.000000 dBA for eight hours. The starting point shifts the whole ladder. The decibel exchange rate sets its slope, and the slope is what makes loud short periods weigh so differently under the two criteria.

The OBEN record shows it. Its fifth period is 0.200000 h at 99.100000 dBA. The OSHA reference duration at that level is 2.265768 h and the NIOSH one is 0.307786 h. That period carries 8.827030 percent of the OSHA PEL noise dose and 64.980192 percent of the NIOSH noise REL. Both criteria agree on the level and the hours; they disagree about what those hours are worth.

## The words on the page

Always write the decibel exchange rate, or an exchange rate of 5 dB, in full. The Economics courses in this academy use the same two words for currency, and a bare phrase in a report can be read that way by someone arriving from those pages.

The rate is also why a criterion is more than a limit. A criterion in this engine is a criterion level, a decibel exchange rate, a threshold and a limit noise dose, all four together, and changing any one of them changes the answer for the same record.

# Published, reproduced, transcribed

The golden behind this course carries 441 cases. 372 are checked against a value a source prints and 69 against the independent oracle only. Beside them sit 5 errata and 57 refusals. Every door of the engine falls into one of four evidence classes, and the class says how much a figure from that door can bear.

## The four classes

PUBLISHED, REPRODUCED: at least one value a source prints is reproduced by the engine at the precision the source prints it. Two people copying the same page wrongly would be caught by that value. `noiseTwaFromDoseDbA` carries 235 published cases, `noiseReferenceDurationH` 101, and the daily Brief and Scala factor 4.

ARITHMETIC BY DEFINITION: a time weighted average whose only constant is its window. `wbgtTwaC`, `metabolicRateTwaW` and `chemicalStel15Min` are here.

ORACLE ONLY: the engine and an independent oracle agree, and no printed value is known to set against either. `briefScalaWeeklyRf` has 0 published cases and 4 oracle-only ones.

TRANSCRIPTION ONLY: the engine and the oracle each copied their constants from the same page. The WBGT doors, the RAL and NIOSH heat REL equations and the NIOSH heat stress assessment are here.

## A class names the strongest part

The class names the strongest thing known about a door, and a door can be weaker in part. `lexWeeklyDbA` is PUBLISHED, REPRODUCED on the strength of 1 case, five equal days at 85 dBA giving 85, which any averaging of five equal numbers reproduces. Its divisor of 5, judgement J10, is the statutory formula, and no printed case with other than five days is known, so the divisor itself is oracle only. `hearingProtectorEstimate` is published for two methods and oracle only for the NIOSH derating by type and dual protection.

## What a printed table can prove

A published table proves only what its printed precision can separate, and judgement J1 is where that matters most. OSHA prints the TWA coefficient 16.61 and NIOSH prints 10.0. The exact values, the decibel exchange rate over log10 2, are 16.609640474437 and 9.965784284662.

NIOSH Table 1-2 separates its printed 10.0 from the exact coefficient: 49 of its 83 rows reject the exact one, because the two coefficients differ by 0.034215715338 dB, and at the large noise doses the table prints that difference, multiplied by the logarithm of the noise dose ratio, outgrows the table's one printed decimal. OSHA Table A-1 cannot separate 16.61 from the exact coefficient: 0 of its 150 rows reject it, because the two differ by 0.000359525563 dB and the table prints one decimal. So Table A-1 reproduces 16.61 without proving it. What fixes 16.61 for OSHA is the text of the mandatory Appendix A, which writes the coefficient, and the course records that as a decision about the regulation's words.

## Reading a class in a report

A PUBLISHED, REPRODUCED figure can be quoted as the source's own method, an ORACLE ONLY figure as an independent agreement, and a TRANSCRIPTION ONLY figure only with its status beside it.

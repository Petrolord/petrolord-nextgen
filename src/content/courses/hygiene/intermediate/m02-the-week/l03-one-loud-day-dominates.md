## Where the energy is

On the energy scale every three decibels roughly doubles the term a day contributes. The loudest day, 88.200000 dBA, sits more than six decibels above the quietest, 81.700000 dBA, so it carries several times the quiet day's energy. The points table of the daily module shows the same doubling: 100.000000 points is 85.000000 dBA and 200.000000 points is 88.010300 dBA. A single day near 88 dBA carries about twice the energy of a day at 85 dBA.

The arithmetic mean of the five days is 84.860000 dBA and treats every day as one fifth of the answer. The weekly level of 85.461288 dBA gives the loud day the weight its energy earns. The gap between the two figures is the loud day showing through.

## What removing the day shows

Taking the loud day out of the record and keeping the divisor of 5 gives 83.414860 dBA. That is a large fall for one day out of five, and it measures how much of the week that one day was. It is also exactly what the regulation computes for a person who did the other four days and was away on the loud one: four days of energy, a nominal five-day week.

## What this means for control

A week like this one is a week whose weekly level is decided mostly on one day. Control the loud day, by the task, the time on it, or the protection worn, and the weekly figure follows. Controlling the quiet days barely moves it. The daily module told you the same thing about tasks within a day: the short loud task carries most of the points. The pattern repeats at every level of averaging, because it is the energy relation doing it.

## What it does not license

The weekly level is permitted where noise exposure varies markedly from day to day. It is not a way to average a loud day out of sight. A week with one day well above the upper action value is still a week with that day in it, and a hygienist reporting the weekly level should also report the daily levels it was built from. The five figures in the table above are part of the finding.

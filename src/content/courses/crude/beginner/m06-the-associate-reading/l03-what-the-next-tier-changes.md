# What the next tier changes

The Associate tier has taught the assay and the blend. Every property of a blend is computed on its own basis: gravity through specific gravity on volume, sulfur and the other per-mass properties on mass, viscosity through an index, and yields on volume off the curve. A blank is absent, and the engine names the crude that lacks it.

## What the Professional tier adds

The Professional tier asks the fourth of the studio's four questions: what is this crude worth against the crude already bought.

First, the blend's own curve. In this tier you read the export blend's cut yields as given. The next tier shows how the engine forms a blend's curve from its crudes' curves: yields add on volume at each temperature, and temperatures are never averaged.

Second, the fifty percent point. The temperature at which a blend's curve reaches 50 percent is read off the curve by interpolation. From that point the tier forms the Watson characterisation factor. The engine takes Watson K at the blend's fifty percent point, which is a screening basis. The strict basis is the mean average boiling point. It is a held convention, taught as a limit, as the Refutas basis was in module three.

Third, a refinery's own cut set. A refinery draws its cuts where its units need them, and moving one cut point moves barrels between two cuts and nowhere else.

Fourth, netback: the value of the products a barrel of crude makes, less the losses on the product side, the processing and the freight, and then the differential against a marker crude.

## What stays the same

The rules of this tier do not change. Every figure the Professional tier uses rests on a property blended on its own basis and on a curve read honestly. Nothing is hidden: a blank cost in a netback is taken as zero and named, and an unpriced cut is named while the valuation reports itself incomplete.

## The new case

The Professional tier works on a new invented case, a modular refinery in Delta State offered a blend of two crudes it has not seen before. Every crude, price and specification in it is invented and illustrative.

## After that

The Expert tier turns from reading a blend to choosing one. It teaches linear programming from the start: what a least-cost recipe is, which specifications bind, what one unit of relief on a binding specification is worth, and why an infeasible answer is an answer in its own right.

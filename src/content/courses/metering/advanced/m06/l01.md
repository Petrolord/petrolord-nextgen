# Fifteen things these engines do not carry

{{panel:fc-withheld-explorer}}

A calculation package is defined as much by what it does not carry as by what it does. This course keeps a register of the second list, and reading it is the last thing this tier asks of you, because everything in it is a place where a result needs a sentence beside it.

## What the register is

The register holds fifteen items across the three engines of this course. An item counts when the engine states, in its own source or its own returned text, that the package does not carry it, does not cite it, or refuses to answer it. Two of the fifteen are outright refusals to answer, which means the engine returns a refusal or a withheld flag in place of a number.

Nothing in this course is graded on any of the fifteen.

## The tank rows

The register names the engine each row belongs to, and these are its tank rows.

The API 650 minimum shell plate thickness band table is not carried, so the minimum is a stated input and the result names the value in force. The diameter above which the variable design point method replaces the one-foot method is not carried, and the engine returns a note saying a large tank must be checked against the standard. The thermal venting table above the proportional limit, together with the latitude and insulation credits, is not carried or is stated, and a tank above the stated limit is warned rather than silently extrapolated. AP-42's turnover factor Kn is not carried, and a turnover count is therefore not an input.

The last of them is the withholding this tier is built on. The relation that turns a fire duty into a required vent capacity in scfh of air equivalent is withheld by name, because the two plausible forms of it differ by a factor of about 24 and an emergency vent sized 24 times too small is how a tank is destroyed.

## Held, stated and withheld are three different things

The register uses its words carefully and so should you.

Something held is absent, and the engine says so and carries on where it can. Something stated is present as a value the package chose or was handed, which the engine names so a reviewer can challenge it. Something withheld is a question the engine will not answer at all.

A reader who collapses those three into one idea loses the most useful distinction on the list. A held item usually means go and look it up. A stated item means check whose number this is. A withheld item means the answer is not available from here at any price.

## Both refusals, proved by calling them

The register does not only assert its two refusals. Both are called and shown returning their refusals: straightRunDiameters returns withheld true for two elbows in different planes, and fireVenting returns ventWithheld true and a null vent capacity at every wetted area.

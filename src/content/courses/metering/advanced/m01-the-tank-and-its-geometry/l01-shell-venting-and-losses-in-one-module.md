# Why the shell, the venting and the losses are one module

A storage tank asks three questions that look unrelated. How thick does the bottom course of the shell have to be. How much air moves in and out of the roof space as the tank breathes. How much product leaves through that same space over a year. All three are read off the same short list of geometry, which is why they sit in one module.

## One geometry, three answers

The tank this tier works on is the OGBOGENE geometry: a diameter of 62.400000 ft, a shell height of 36.000000 ft and a design liquid level of 34.600000 ft. From those stated figures the engine returns a cross section of 3058.1520 ft2 and a nominal capacity of 19608.4845 bbl.

That capacity is never only a storage figure. The thermal inbreathing the engine returns for this tank is 19608.4845 scfh, because the thermal rate is carried per barrel of capacity. The shell calculation climbs the same height in courses, and the fire case wets the same shell and turns it into an area. Move the diameter and all three answers move together, which is why an engineer who changes a tank size and re-runs only the shell has changed the venting without looking at it.

## The equations are easy and the boundaries are not

None of the arithmetic here is hard. The one-foot shell relation is a line of algebra, the barrel conversion is exact by definition, and the loss relations are products of factors. What makes a tank a design problem is that each answer is governed somewhere other than where the ordinary equation looks.

On the shell, the product can be the thing that does not govern, because the water the tank is tested with can be heavier than the product it will hold. On the venting, the direction that governs can be the one that pulls air in. On the fire case, the duty is computable and the vent capacity it implies is refused by the engine by name.

So this module is the geometry and the modules after it are the three boundaries. Every boundary in this tier is found the same way. You ask the engine for its own word, you move one input, and you watch the word change.

## What this tier grades and what it teaches

One answer in this tier, the fire vent capacity, is refused by the engine and is graded nowhere in this course, and that is the point of it rather than a hole in it. A figure a package cannot source is not an answer.

# What a real noise prediction would need

This module gives a band. Knowing what stands between a band and a prediction is what stops the band being misused, so this lesson is about the gap itself.

## The named method

The engine names what it does not carry:

> `a screening indication only: a real noise prediction needs the IEC 60534-8-3 method with valve and pipe geometry. Use this to decide whether that method is needed, and let that method give the answer. The pressure ratio sets the band and the stream power caps and floors it, and both sets of thresholds are this engine's stated screen`

Two inputs are named there and neither one is a fluid property. Valve geometry and pipe geometry are what the screen is missing, and they are missing because the indication takes the service alone, its two pressures, its flow, its gas gravity and its temperature, and never a drawing.

## What geometry contributes

Noise generated inside the trim has to get out, and where it gets out is through the pipe wall downstream. So the sound that reaches somebody standing on the plant depends on the pipe diameter, the wall thickness, the material and whatever lagging is fitted, none of which the sizing calculation ever asks for. The trim geometry matters for the same kind of reason. A cage with many small passages and a single plug in a large port can dissipate identical power and radiate very different acoustic spectra.

A method that accounts for those produces a sound pressure level at a stated distance, in decibels, with a stated basis. That is a number an occupational exposure assessment can use. A band word is not.

## What the two sets of thresholds are

Both sets are this engine's stated screen. The pressure ratio bands and the stream power bands are exported so that they can be read and, if an organisation prefers its own, replaced. Nothing in this course grades a band, and that follows from the same statement.

## The honest way to report a screening result

Say what the tool did. It took a pressure ratio and a stream power, read them against stated thresholds, and returned a band. Say what it did not do, which is the named method with geometry. Then say what happens next, which is either that the band is low enough to close the item or that a proper prediction has been raised.

## Exercise

Write down the two kinds of geometry the engine says a real prediction needs, and say which of the two sets of thresholds behind the band belong to the engine. Then write the single sentence you would put beside a screening band in a design document so that a later reader cannot mistake it for a prediction.

# Which term dominates is a result

The budget in the first lesson of this module was computed at one reading. Move
the reading and the budget moves with it, because one of the six terms is a
transmitter figure that depends on where you are on the span. This lesson runs
the budget all the way down.

## The name in the third column changes

That is the finding. The dominant term is not a property of the meter. It is a
property of the meter at an operating point, and on this run the engine names a
different term at the bottom of the span from the one it names at the top.

   the dominant term changes name at a reading of, in H2O          15.000000
   the flow turndown there                                          3.651484
   the total uncertainty there                                      0.757503
   above that reading the engine names                             discharge coefficient

That crossover was found by bisecting the name the engine returns rather than by
reading the table and arguing about it. The distinction matters. A table has
twelve rows and a crossover is somewhere between two of them; a bisection on the
returned name gives you the reading itself.

## Only one input is moving

It is worth being precise about what changed between the rows. The plate did not
change, the fluid did not change, and five of the six uncertainties are the
engine's defaults on every row of this table. The one term that moves is the
differential pressure term, because it is derived from the transmitter at the
reading in the first column, and that is the mechanism module five spent four
lessons on. Everything in this table is that one mechanism arriving inside a
budget and changing which term the engine names.

## Why this decides what you spend money on

Suppose the budget says the discharge coefficient owns the variance. Improving
the transmitter would be money spent on a term that is not where the uncertainty
lives, and a better plate inspection, a better installation or a tighter bore
measurement is the sensible spend.

Now suppose the same meter has drifted down the span into the rows at the bottom
of the table. The engine is naming the differential pressure term, and a
respanned transmitter is now a real improvement to the measurement.

Same meter, same plate, same fluid. A different answer to the question of what
to buy, and the only way to know which case you are in is to compute the budget
at the reading the meter actually sits at rather than at the design point.

## The total is climbing too

Read the second column beside the third. The name is not the only thing moving.
The total uncertainty at the bottom of the table is a different quantity
altogether from the total at the top, and a meter operated down there is
producing figures whose worth has changed even on days when nothing failed.

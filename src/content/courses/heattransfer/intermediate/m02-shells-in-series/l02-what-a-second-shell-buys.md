# What a second shell buys

A second shell pass is usually described as buying driving force, and on most duties that is exactly what it does. On this engine it sometimes buys something larger than that, which is an answer where the single shell configuration had none at all.

## The duty one shell cannot reach

At a P of 0.720000 and an R of 0.850000 the engine refuses a one shell pass unit. The message says the configuration cannot reach this duty and tells the caller to add a shell pass. Two shells answer at the same duty, with a factor of 0.800677.

So the first thing a second shell buys here is feasibility. That is a different kind of purchase from an improvement, and it changes what a designer is deciding. At one shell there is no number to weigh against a cost, because the configuration cannot deliver the duty at any area.

It is worth seeing how ordinary the alternative case looks. The studio exchanger and ORON both answer at one shell pass, at 0.964693 and 0.957197, and neither of them needs a second shell for any reason except money. The duty in this lesson is at a much higher P, and a high P is what pushes a unit off the reachable part of the one shell curve.

## What each further shell adds

| shells in series | F |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 0.800677 |
| 3 | 0.920456 |
| 4 | 0.956604 |
| 5 | 0.972592 |
| 6 | 0.981100 |

Read that column for its direction and for nothing else. The factor rises as shells are added, and every row is the same duty, the same P and the same R. The engine computes no ratio between any two of those rows and this course prints none, so a reader who divides one row by another has manufactured a figure that no method here produced. The direction is the finding: the correction climbs toward one as the train lengthens.

That is also why the module will not let the count grow without limit, which is the next lesson.

## Reading a shell count as a design choice

Shells in series cost money and plot space, and each one adds nozzles, gaskets and another place to leak. So the question a factor column answers is narrow: for this duty, what does the correction become at each count. Weighing that against the cost of the shell is not something this engine does, and it does not pretend to.

What the engine will tell you is which counts are available at all, which count first makes the duty reachable, and what the factor is at each one. Those three together are the engineering content of this table, and the last of them is the only one that is a number.

## Exercise

Record the factor at each shell count from two through six, and record the P and R they all share. Then state what happens at one shell pass at that same P and R, and say in one sentence why a ratio between two rows of the table is a figure this course does not carry.

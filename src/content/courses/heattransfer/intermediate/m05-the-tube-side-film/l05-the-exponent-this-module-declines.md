# The exponent this module declines

The correlation carries a Prandtl exponent, and the heating form and the cooling form use different ones. Only the heating form is established in this repository. So a cooled tube side is refused. It is not answered with the heating exponent, and no cooling exponent is invented here to answer it with.

## The refusal, in the engine's own words

Asked for a film on a tube side declared as cooling, the engine refuses: this module carries only the HEATING form of Dittus-Boelter (Pr^0.4). The cooling exponent is not established in this repository, and at a Prandtl around 15 the two forms differ by about 31 percent on hi, so it is not a detail to shrug at. Type the tube-side film instead of computing it.

Read the middle clause. The refusal states the size of the thing it is declining to guess, which is the difference between a tool being careful and a tool being unhelpful. The studio case has a Prandtl number of 15.119375, so that is the neighbourhood the statement is about, and the engine reports the Prandtl number on every call so a reader can see which neighbourhood they are in.

A service the module carries no form for at all is refused differently and more briefly: service must be 'heating'; it was "condensing". Condensation is outside what this module does at all, and it says so in its own description rather than in a refusal.

## Why a refusal is the right answer here

Answering a cooled tube side with the heating exponent would be a confident wrong number, and a confident wrong number is the worst thing an engine can return. It carries no warning, it looks exactly like a result, and it gets quoted downstream by people who never saw where it came from.

Inventing the cooling exponent here would be worse. A figure with no source at all would be handed on as though it had one, and the first person to build on it would have no way of knowing. So the refusal is the correct answer, because the alternative is inventing a coefficient.

## What the answer looks like when it is given

At the same conditions with the tube side declared as heating, the engine returns a film coefficient of 547.762384 and reports the service back as heating, so a reader can see which form produced the number in front of them.

What a caller does instead is straightforward. They type the film coefficient. This module takes a stated inside film and the studio offers that box, so a cooled tube side is designed with a coefficient the engineer stands behind rather than one the engine guessed.

That is also why nothing in this course grades a cooled tube side, and why nothing in it grades a film coefficient at all. Every coefficient worked here has its films stated as conditions of the case, for exactly this reason.

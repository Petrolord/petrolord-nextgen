# What the next tier changes

This tier answered two questions about an exchanger: what decides the driving force, and what decides the coefficient. Both of them are sizing questions. They start from a duty and end with a surface. The Expert tier turns the whole thing round.

## The other question

Given a machine that already exists, what does it deliver. That is a rating rather than a sizing, and it is answered in a different currency. Effectiveness is the fraction of the most heat that could possibly move that this machine actually moves, and NTU is its surface written dimensionlessly. The two are inverses of each other and the engine is asked in both directions.

Two of the three arrangements turn out to have a ceiling they cannot pass at any area at all, and one of them has none. The arrangement with no ceiling is the one the Rating tab opens on, so it is the one a reader meets first.

## The machine that has no cooling water

Then the air cooler. It has ambient air, a fan and a bundle, and the ambient temperature is the thing its design is hostage to. Three inputs on it are worth a lesson each, and one of them is the draft type, which decides whether the fan handles ambient air or the heated air leaving the bundle. Those are different densities, so they are different volumes and different fan powers, and the engine will not pick one for you.

The Expert tier also meets a correction this module cannot source at all, and watches the engine report it as absent rather than quietly applying a one.

## The hot afternoon, and a second method

A bay is bought for its worst day, so the sharpest question in the course is what a machine still holds when the air arrives hotter than the day it was sized on. The answer is that its surface and its air mass are what it holds, and four columns of the rating do not move across any ambient at all while two of them do.

Then the whole module is audited. What was computed, what was chosen, what is pinned, what is held for the literature, and what a second method buys that a second copy of the first cannot. That last question is the sharpest thing in the course, which is why the Expert tier closes on what the method does not know.

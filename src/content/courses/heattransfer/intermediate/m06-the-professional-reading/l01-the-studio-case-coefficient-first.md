# The studio case, coefficient first

The Associate tier walked this case from the duty forward. This lesson walks it from the coefficient outward, because that is the order the Professional half of the module works in, and every figure here is on the screen of the live app when it opens.

## The coefficient, assembled

At the converged count of 74 tubes in 2 passes, which is 37 tubes a pass, the tube side Reynolds number is 44051.846000 and the Prandtl number is 15.119375. The regime is turbulent and the inside film coefficient is 547.762384.

That film goes into the stack, and the stack gives U clean 134.459410 and U dirty 92.110348, with a fouling penalty of 31.495796 percent. The controlling resistance is the outside film, the runner up is the inside fouling allowance, and the margin between them is 51.612903 percent, which the engine reports as clear of the declared threshold.

The outside film and the wall conductivity on this case are stated inputs. The inside film is the one quantity here a correlation produced, and it is the only fitted number in the module. That is why the graded work in this course states both films: a figure a reader is asked to produce should not rest on a fit whose validity band nobody here can state.

The two groups are low and the arrangement on this case takes no correction, so the driving force the surface is sized on is the counter-current log mean itself. The area follows from the duty, the coefficient and that driving force, and the tube count follows from the area by rounding up twice.

## The check that the loop closed

The chain here is a loop. The film needs the count, the count needs the area, the area needs the coefficient and the coefficient needs the film. So the last thing worth doing is to ask whether the four figures at the end satisfy the equation they were built from.

They do. The coefficient times the area times the correction times the log mean is 2750000.0000 Btu an hour, against a duty of 2750000.0000 Btu an hour. That is what a closed loop looks like, and it is the one check a reader can run against the live app in a minute.

## What to quote and how

Quote every figure at the precision this course prints it at, and quote the tube count and the passes beside any coefficient. A film coefficient without its count belongs to no bundle, and a U without its reference area belongs to no surface.

Three habits from this tier carry into the next. Read the parts before the whole, so a coefficient is five resistances and a share column before it is one number. Read the conditions before the answer, because every figure above is conditional on a film, a conductivity, two fouling allowances and a geometry that were all chosen. And treat a refusal as the answer.

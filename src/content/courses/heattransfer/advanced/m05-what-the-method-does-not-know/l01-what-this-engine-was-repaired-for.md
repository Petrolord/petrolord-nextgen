# What this engine was repaired for

Everything in this lesson is history. It describes what this engine did before it was repaired, and each item is a general lesson that happens to have an example here. Nothing here describes the engine you are using, and nothing else in this course is history.

{{panel:fc-rating-explorer}}

This module was repaired after a recon raised findings against it, its cases, its oracle and its studio. Four are worth teaching. Read every paragraph below as an account of a former state.

## Two numbers one answer could not produce

The hot-day block held the process outlet fixed and scaled the duty by the ratio of two log means. Those cannot both be true. If the duty falls the outlet rises, because the process capacity rate does not change with the weather.

The general lesson is that a result carrying several numbers has to be self-consistent, and the cheapest way to find out is to compute one from the others. On this bay the process capacity rate is 200000.0000 and the air capacity rate 666666.6667 Btu an hour per degF, each from the design duty and its own temperature change. Any duty implies a rise and an outlet through those two, whether the code computed them together or not.

## A coefficient at a contradicted count

The studio passed a hard-coded tube count into the film while printing a different count beside it. The general lesson is that a screen showing two numbers that disagree about the same thing is a screen where one was never computed, and the fix is to close the loop rather than pick a better constant.

## A capital letter that defaulted silently

The arrangement was matched against one lowercase string, so anything else became counter-current silently, which on one case was a forty percent error. The general lesson is that a defaulted input is worse than a refused one: a default answers a question the caller did not ask and never says it did.

## A gate that caught none of it

The test suite the module shipped with was green against every one of these. The general lesson: a gate that restates the formula it checks has not checked it, and the way to find out is to break the engine on purpose and watch whether the gate notices.

## Provenance

The engine records what changed in its own comments. There are 11 comment lines in that file carrying "used to", "no longer" or this repair's name, counted by reading the vendored source with that rule stated. Widen the rule to nine keywords and the same file reads 16. A count across the whole vendored tree is deliberately not quoted: it would answer to work in other modules and go stale without anything here changing.

Those comments are provenance, and so are the recon and findings records. Their numbers are stale by construction, because the repair moved the app's own defaults. A sentence lifted out of one arrives with no frame on it.

# The arrangement with no ceiling

Counter-current flow has no effectiveness it cannot pass below one. Where the other two arrangements report a ceiling, this one reports nothing at all, and the empty field is the answer rather than a value the engine failed to compute.

{{panel:fc-rating-explorer}}

## What the field says

At a capacity ratio of 0.650000 the parallel ceiling is 0.606061 and the 1-2 shell ceiling is 0.703560. The counter-current entry on the same row is none. The same holds at 0.350000 and at 1.000000. Only at a capacity ratio of 0.000000 does counter-current flow carry a number, and there every arrangement carries 1.000000.

This is an analytic truth about the relation and not a measurement. Two streams passing in opposite directions keep a driving force along the whole length, so there is no state the relation approaches and stops at. Nothing in the repository could overturn it.

That makes the empty field a check rather than a convenience. A wrong constant in the counter-current closed form would show up as a curve that levels off somewhere below one, and this module's audit lists exactly that as one of the limits its published file leans on.

## Proved by asking for the surface

The cleanest demonstration is to ask for the NTU at effectivenesses climbing toward one, at a capacity ratio of 0.650000.

| effectiveness asked for | counter-current | parallel | 1-2 shell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.900000 | 4.066024 | refused, ceiling 0.606061 | refused, ceiling 0.703560 |
| 0.990000 | 10.210712 | refused, ceiling 0.606061 | refused, ceiling 0.703560 |
| 0.999000 | 16.742253 | refused, ceiling 0.606061 | refused, ceiling 0.703560 |
| 0.999990 | 29.894634 | refused, ceiling 0.606061 | refused, ceiling 0.703560 |

The first column answers every time. The other two stop at the same pair of numbers however high the request goes, because their ceilings depend on the capacity ratio alone and on nothing that a bigger bundle changes. Read the first column as a bill rather than as a limit, since each extra nine of effectiveness costs more surface than the nine before it.

## A claim worth naming as false

Some documentation for this class of calculation states that every arrangement has a ceiling. That claim is false, it is recorded in this module's audit as having appeared in the studio help text, and the table above is what refutes it. A default arrangement makes the error expensive: counter-current flow is what the Rating tab opens on, so it is the arrangement a reader meets first and the one a wrong general claim would be applied to first.

## What the unbounded answer costs

An NTU of 29.894634 is a real number and a ruinous design. Read it beside the surface it stands for. NTU is the coefficient times the area over the smaller capacity rate, so an NTU near thirty on a fixed duty is a bundle several times the size of the one that reaches an effectiveness of 0.900000 on the same row. The engine returning it rather than refusing is the correct behaviour, because the relation has a value there and the size of that value is the information a designer needs. The limit on this arrangement is a budget rather than a physical bound, and this module leaves that judgement where it belongs.

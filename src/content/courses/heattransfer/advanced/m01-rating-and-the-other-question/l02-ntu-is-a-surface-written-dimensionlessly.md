# NTU is a surface written dimensionlessly

A surface in square feet means nothing until it is compared with the stream it is asked to heat. NTU is that comparison. It is the product of the coefficient and the area over the smaller capacity rate, which leaves a pure number, and it is the only way the surface reaches a rating.

## Reading the same curve downward

Hold the arrangement and the capacity ratio and add surface. Counter-current flow at a capacity ratio of 0.650000 answers as follows.

| NTU | effectiveness |
| --- | --- |
| 0.250000 | 0.207144 |
| 0.750000 | 0.461685 |
| 1.400000 | 0.643699 |
| 2.600000 | 0.809194 |
| 4.500000 | 0.916283 |
| 8.000000 | 0.977841 |

Effectiveness rises with NTU and the steps between the figures shrink as it does. Surface bought early moves the answer hard. Surface bought late moves it very little, and the table shows that as a direction rather than as a rate. No rate is computed here, so none should be quoted.

That shape is the whole economics of an exchanger. The first square feet of a bundle buy driving force that is standing there unused. The last square feet compete with a temperature difference the earlier surface has already spent, and the table above is what that competition looks like when it is written down.

## The same relation run backwards

The other door takes an effectiveness and returns the NTU that reaches it. That is the door a designer wants, because a specification arrives as a duty rather than as a surface. At a capacity ratio of 0.650000 in counter-current flow it answers as follows.

| effectiveness asked for | NTU |
| --- | --- |
| 0.900000 | 4.066024 |
| 0.990000 | 10.210712 |
| 0.999000 | 16.742253 |
| 0.999990 | 29.894634 |

Each extra nine costs more surface than the one before it. The last row is the honest shape of the thing. There is no effectiveness below one that counter-current flow cannot reach, and no bound on what it costs to get there.

A refusal would have been the easier answer to write and the wrong one. The relation has a value at every effectiveness below one, so the engine returns it and lets the reader see the size of the bill. Two of the three arrangements do refuse here, and they refuse for a different reason, which the next two lessons take up.

## What the answer carries

Both doors answer with three fields. The direction they were asked in, the arrangement they used, and a ceiling. On the four rows above the ceiling is empty, because counter-current flow has none, and an empty field is a statement rather than a missing value.

## Where UA enters instead

A rating of a real machine rarely starts from an NTU. It starts from UA, the coefficient times the area, in Btu an hour per degF. Divide UA by the smaller capacity rate and the NTU follows. The hot-day module in this tier never computes an area at all for exactly this reason. It holds UA and reads the NTU off it.

# What one published case cannot find

A single published case fixes one point. It says that at these inputs this implementation returns that number, and a reader who trusts the publication can trust the point. It says nothing about the shape of the relation between points, and nothing at all about an error that moved the publication and the implementation together.

## Three grades of check

They are worth ranking, because this module uses all three and the ranking decides how much each one is worth.

A restatement is the weakest. Rearranging an expression, converting to other units and back, or inverting a function all check for a slip of the hand. The form under test sits on both sides, so a wrong form passes.

An independent route is stronger. A second implementation that reaches the answer by different mathematics can fail for a reason that matters. This module's published figures come from an oracle built that way: the log mean by integrating the driving force rather than evaluating a logarithm, the bundle diameter by bisecting on the diameter rather than raising a ratio to a reciprocal exponent, the hot day by bisecting the surface equation rather than evaluating the effectiveness relation.

An analytic limit is stronger again where the method itself is what is in doubt, because it is true independently of both implementations. A log mean has to sit below an arithmetic mean whenever the two ends differ. Three arrangements have to agree when one stream stops changing temperature. Neither needs a source and neither can be argued with.

## The case that settles the ranking

The wall term in this module's coefficient carries a factor of two. Move that factor the same way in the engine and in the oracle and the two files go on agreeing with each other perfectly. Every published case stays green. The agreement is the finding, and what it finds is that both files now say the same wrong thing.

The thin-wall limit catches it, because a thin cylindrical wall has to collapse onto a flat plate and a flat plate has no factor of two to argue about. At the thinnest wall the module's audit records a ratio of 1.002005 between the cylindrical wall resistance and the flat plate, and at the thickest a ratio of 1.127984.

## What that means for reading a green suite

Every case green is a result and not a reassurance. Ask what the cases share. If a constant, a conversion or an exponent is read by the implementation and by the thing checking it, no number of green cases can speak to it, and something outside both files has to.

This module answers that question in two ways for its own constants. Where a constant can be measured out of an answer rather than cited, it is, and the measurement is asserted. Where it cannot, it is recorded as held and the return that depends on it says so.

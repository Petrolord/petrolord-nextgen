# The capstone worked

The assessment gives an air cooler and asks for the figures a rating engineer would hand over. Every one is reachable by running the chain in order and reading what the engine returns. Here is that chain worked on the teaching bay, with the reasons the study states some of its own inputs.

{{panel:fc-rating-explorer}}

## Conditions first

A duty, two process temperatures, a design ambient, an air rise and a coefficient. Then three more that a study of this kind states for itself rather than leaving to the module: the fan static pressure, the fan efficiency and the motor efficiency.

The reason is in the audit. Those three defaults, 0.600000 inches of water, 0.650000 and 0.920000, name no machine at all, and the default wall conductivity of 26 names no material. They are pinned so that moving one is a reviewed act, which is not the same as validating one. A study that states its own is reporting a fan it has chosen rather than inheriting a number with nothing behind it.

## The design point, then the basis

Work the air outlet, the log mean, the bare surface and the air mass in that order, because each one needs the one before it. On the teaching bay those are 125.000000 degF, 85.263896 degF, 52125.749338 ft2 and 2777777.7778 lb an hour.

Then read the correction field before quoting the surface. It comes back empty, and the surface is therefore a counter-current-basis surface. Write that word beside the number or do not write the number.

## The rating, which reads no constant at all

The hot-day block takes the surface and the air mass and holds the effectiveness from its definition at the design point. It reads no declared constant, assumes no arrangement and applies no correction factor, so nothing held for literature reaches it.

On this bay the fixed set is a UA of 234565.8720 Btu an hour per degF, a capacity ratio of 0.300000, an NTU of 1.172829 and an effectiveness of 0.645161. At 124.000000 degF the answer is a duty fraction of 0.812903, a duty of 16258064.5161 Btu an hour, a process outlet of 168.709677 degF and an air rise of 24.387097 degF.

## Then check it

UA times the hot-day log mean of 69.311296 degF gives 16258064.5161 Btu an hour against the rated duty of 16258064.5161. Do this before handing anything over. It is one line and it is the only step here that could have failed for a reason other than a slip of the hand.

## What the chain will not give you

Four questions come up and none is answered. What the cross-flow correction would do to the surface is held for literature. What the air-side coefficient should be for a finned bundle is an input rather than a result. What the shell-side film is stays an input everywhere in this module. And a fan as a machine belongs to the Rotating Equipment course.

Write those beside the answer rather than leaving them for a reviewer.

# Where this engine hands over

An engine is as useful as its boundaries are clear. This one answers four questions about one exchanger, rates a machine you already have in both directions, and sizes an air cooler. Everything else it says it does not do.

## What is not here

No shell-side film coefficient from stream analysis. No pressure drop. No vibration check. No condensation or boiling. No fin geometry. No rigorous cross-flow rating.

The shell side stays an input, and the module has always said so: a rigorous shell-side coefficient needs stream analysis that belongs in a dedicated rating package. Pressure drop in a line belongs to the Pipeline and Line Sizing engine, and machine work belongs to Rotating Equipment. A boundary named is a boundary a caller can plan around.

## What is held rather than absent

Held items are different from missing features. The register carries 7 entries, counted by reading the register itself, and the module's own header sentence says six. The register is the authority because it is what the returns point at, and the disagreement is a small demonstration of a large rule: measure a count rather than quoting it, even from the file that owns it.

Each of the seven is a stated limit and never an answer. The cross-flow correction sets the design area of a bay and nothing else. The fan defaults name no machine. The bundle constants carry two layouts that are identical. Nothing in this course grades any of them.

## One word that means two things

This package exports an overall coefficient twice, under two different reference areas. This module's is referred to the outside tube surface and says so on every answer. A second export in the production domain is referred to a stated bore instead. So a coefficient quoted without its reference area is not a number anyone can use, and the same caution applies to a bare surface quoted without its basis.

## What to carry forward

Three habits, and none of them is about exchangers. Compute one number on a result sheet from the others, because a result carrying several figures has to be self-consistent. Ask of any check whether it could have failed for a reason other than a typing error. And separate what a file pins from what a second route or an analytic limit has actually established.

# What the next tier changes

Two numbers in this tier were handed to you without being explained. The correction factor was one and you were told it would be. The overall coefficient arrived as a figure and you were told to take it. The next tier is where both stop being given.

## The factor stops being one

The correction factor is one on a straight counter-current unit and below one everywhere else. What sets it is a pair of dimensionless temperature groups built from the same four terminals the log mean came from. On the studio case they are 0.171875 and 2.909091, and they sat on the screen in this tier's last module without being used.

Three things about that factor are new ideas rather than new arithmetic. It is computed from a closed form rather than read off a chart, so it can be checked. It warns before it refuses. And shells in series change it, which turns the shell count into a design variable.

## The coefficient stops being given

The coefficient you took on trust is a sum of five named resistances in series, all referred to the outside tube surface. Once it is assembled from parts, the parts are visible and the one that dominates can be named. On the studio case the controlling resistance is reported as outsideFilm with insideFouling as its runner up and a margin of 51.612903 percent between them.

The wall is one of those five terms, and it comes with the most instructive check in the course: a thin wall has to behave like a flat plate, and that requirement pins a factor no comparison against a file could pin.

## The film, and a band it declines

The tube-side film is the term that makes the chain a loop. On the studio case its Reynolds number is 44051.846000, its Prandtl number is 15.119375, its regime is reported as turbulent, and the film itself is 547.762384.

Two of the honest limits in this module live here. The validity band of the correlation is something this module records that it cannot source, and it says so on its answers. And a cooling tube side is refused rather than answered with the heating form, because the exponent a cooling case needs is not something this module will invent. Both are stated limits rather than answers.

## The tier after that

Beyond the coefficient comes rating, which asks what a machine you already have will deliver, and the air cooler on a hot afternoon. That tier also audits the module: what is pinned, what is measured, what is held for want of a source, and what a second method buys.

## What stays

The discipline stays, and it is why this tier came first. Which number did the engine compute. Which one did you choose. Which one did it decline to invent. Every tier asks those three of every figure on the screen, and only the figures change.

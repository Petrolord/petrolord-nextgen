# The Abua plan end to end

Five modules have read ABUA's plan one piece at a time. This lesson reads it once, top to bottom, the way a planner reads a plan handed to them: configuration, crude, crude unit, margin, balances, limits and stream values. Every figure comes from SECTION 17's single table and the sections behind it.

## Step one: the configuration and its blanks

Before any figure, check the configuration. Every limit ABUA carries is typed. None is blank, so the plan is bounded by what the planner meant, and none is a zero that should have been a blank. Every cost and price is present, or the plan would have been refused by name. The status is optimal. Had it read infeasible or unbounded, the reading would stop here, with the engine's sentence, because there would be no plan to read.

## Step two: the crude unit

The Crude distillation unit is the only unit with no feed, and the unit table reads crude unit true against it. Total crude is 2029032.26 bbl, the crude unit's throughput is 2029032.26 bbl, and the two agree to the barrel: true. The crude unit runs at 78.04 percent and charges 2536290.32 for the month, 1.2500 a barrel on every barrel of crude. If the crude unit column had read false on every row, every figure below this step would describe a refinery whose distillation is free, and the reading would stop to fix the feed column.

## Step three: the margin

Revenue 172316812.90, less crude cost 160350322.58, less unit operating cost 4888554.84, gives the margin 7077935.48. Divided by total crude it gives the gross margin per barrel of crude, 3.4883. That is the number the course's one sentence says a refinery is judged on.

## Step four: where the plan stopped

Units at capacity: Diesel hydrotreater. Crudes at their availability: Forcados (illustrative) and Brass River (illustrative). No product reads true for "at its ceiling". Bonny Light runs 329032.26 bbl of 1500000.00 bbl available. The month is held by the hydrotreater. The crude unit has room, the markets have room, and Bonny Light has barrels, and the plan runs no more crude because the gasoil those barrels would make has no room in the hydrotreater and earns too little as Gasoil export.

## Step five: the stream values

Reformate, kero, ulsd and residue print their product prices, because each has one home with room. Offgas prints 0.0000, because it has no home and leaves 103638.71 bbl of surplus. Naphtha prints 91.4500, valued through a reformer with room. Gasoil prints 94.1016, valued through the full hydrotreater by what the plan can rearrange.

## Step six: what a planner would do next

The stream values and the limits point the same way. The reformer sweep prices another 20000.00 bbl of reformer capacity at 0.0000 a barrel as typed. SECTION 13 prices the hydrotreater left blank at a margin change of 95572.87. If the month is to earn more, the hydrotreater is where to look, and the question of whether it is worth building belongs to a valuation this tier does not do.

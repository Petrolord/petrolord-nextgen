# Weekly unit runs and lifts

Lesson 1 read the crude receipts. The other two kinds of event in ABUA's schedule are unit runs and product lifts, and both are spread in equal weekly events across the period from 2027-03-01. This lesson reads them.

{{panel:refinery-plan-explorer}}

## Weeks in the period

The digest prints weeks in the period 5 for a period of 31 days. Each unit has 5 run events, and each product that sells has 5 lift events. That gives unit runs 15 for ABUA's three units and product lifts 20.

## The unit runs

Each unit's month is split into five equal runs, dated at the start of each week:

| unit | dates | quantity each (bbl) | value each |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cdu | 2027-03-01, 2027-03-08, 2027-03-15, 2027-03-22, 2027-03-29 | 405806.45 | 507258.06 |
| reformer | 2027-03-01, 2027-03-08, 2027-03-15, 2027-03-22, 2027-03-29 | 81535.48 | 236452.90 |
| dht | 2027-03-01, 2027-03-08, 2027-03-15, 2027-03-22, 2027-03-29 | 130000.00 | 234000.00 |

The value of a unit run is its share of that unit's operating cost. A run event's value is money the refinery spends.

The last run is dated 2027-03-29. The period ends on 2027-03-31, so that week is cut short by the end of March, and the cdu run dated 2027-03-29 still carries 405806.45 bbl, the same as the four before it. The schedule's events are equal whatever the length of the week they fall in. It does not weight a short week lightly, and a planner reading actuals against it in the last days of the month should remember that. That is part of what Lesson 5 means by the shape of the month.

## The product lifts

Each product that sells is lifted in five equal events, dated at the end of each week, with the last on the period's final day:

| product | dates | quantity each (bbl) | value each |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gasoline | 2027-03-07, 2027-03-14, 2027-03-21, 2027-03-28, 2027-03-31 | 69305.16 | 7692872.90 |
| jet | 2027-03-07, 2027-03-14, 2027-03-21, 2027-03-28, 2027-03-31 | 57670.97 | 6084287.10 |
| diesel | 2027-03-07, 2027-03-14, 2027-03-21, 2027-03-28, 2027-03-31 | 126100.00 | 13215280.00 |
| fuel_oil | 2027-03-07, 2027-03-14, 2027-03-21, 2027-03-28, 2027-03-31 | 126625.81 | 7470922.58 |

The value of a lift is its share of that product's revenue. A lift is money coming in. Receipts carry the crude cost, runs carry the unit operating cost and lifts carry the revenue, so the three kinds of event between them carry every term of the plan's margin.

Only four products are lifted. Naphtha export and Gasoil export sell 0.00 bbl in the plan, and the schedule makes no events for them. A product the plan does not sell is a product the schedule does not move.

## Runs start weeks, lifts end them

Read the two sets of dates together. Runs fall on 2027-03-01, 2027-03-08, 2027-03-15, 2027-03-22 and 2027-03-29, the first day of each week. Lifts fall on 2027-03-07, 2027-03-14, 2027-03-21, 2027-03-28 and 2027-03-31, the last day of each week, with the fifth week cut short by the end of the period. Each week's product is shown as made in that week and lifted at its close.

## The totals agree with the plan

The digest sets the schedule's totals, material by material, beside the plan's. Every row agrees:

| material | type | scheduled quantity (bbl) | plan quantity (bbl) | scheduled value | plan value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cdu | unit_run | 2029032.26 | 2029032.26 | 2536290.32 | 2536290.32 |
| gasoline | delivery | 346525.81 | 346525.81 | 38464364.52 | 38464364.52 |
| fuel_oil | delivery | 633129.03 | 633129.03 | 37354612.90 | 37354612.90 |

These are three of the ten rows. The schedule is the plan cut into dated pieces. It adds no barrels and loses none, and every cost and revenue in it is the plan's.

## Exercise

Read the cdu runs: five events of 405806.45 bbl from 2027-03-01 to 2027-03-29, and the scheduled cdu total of 2029032.26 bbl beside the plan's 2029032.26 bbl. Then read the gasoline lifts: five events of 69305.16 bbl from 2027-03-07 to 2027-03-31. Say what the dates show about where in each week the schedule puts a run and a lift, and what the totals show about what the schedule may and may not change.

The screen's last table puts every configuration beside every supply scenario for OKORDIA, at 5000 bpd with a capital of 64000000.00 on the modular law and OKORDIA's prices and costs. Nine rows, one reading.

## What each axis moves

Read down a configuration's three rows and the gross value per barrel stays still: 76.9400 for topping, 83.7900 for hydroskimming, 90.6300 for conversion. The slate does not depend on supply. What moves is the throughput, set by the scenario's utilisation, and the margin, set by the scenario's premium.

Read across configurations within one scenario and the throughput stays still: 1518000.00 bbl on firm, 1237500.00 on tight, 825000.00 on disrupted. The configuration does not change how many barrels are run. What moves is the gross value, and with it the margin and the revenue.

So each axis owns one input to the margin. The configuration owns the gross value. The scenario owns the throughput and the crude premium.

## Reading the signs

The sign of the gross margin per barrel is the first thing to read in each row.

Topping is negative on all three scenarios: -2.2600 on firm, -5.2600 on tight and -8.2600 on disrupted. At OKORDIA's prices, a topping plant loses money on every barrel even with firm crude.

Hydroskimming is positive on firm and tight, at 4.5900 and 1.5900, and negative on disrupted at -1.4100. It depends on supply.

Conversion is positive on all three: 11.4300, 8.4300 and 5.4300. It is the only configuration whose margin survives disrupted supply.

These are the screen's readings at 5000 bpd and one capital figure for every row. The table holds the capital at 64000000.00 across configurations, so it is a reading of margins. A conversion plant with its fluid catalytic cracker would carry its own quotation, and the capital question is a separate step the screen answers with its own reference point.

## What the table decides

It decides which configurations earn a valuation. Topping does not, at these prices. Conversion does under every supply. Hydroskimming does only if the sponsor can show crude supply will not be disrupted, and the disrupted note in the engine says that is where most of these projects are actually decided.

## Working the table on the panel

Set the configuration picker and the supply scenario to each of the nine pairs in turn, and check the panel's gross value, throughput, margin and first year revenue against the row. Then pick hydroskimming under tight supply, at 1.5900, and raise the crude cost a step at a time until the margin changes sign. Note how far you had to move it. A reading whose sign turns on a modest change in one input is a reading that needs its inputs defended before it is reported.

## The mistake

Reading revenue as the decision. Topping under firm supply shows a first operating year revenue of 116794920.00 and a gross margin per barrel of -2.2600. Revenue counts what the products sell for. A revenue of any size on a negative margin is a loss on every barrel run, and the fixed operating cost and the capital are still to be paid on top.

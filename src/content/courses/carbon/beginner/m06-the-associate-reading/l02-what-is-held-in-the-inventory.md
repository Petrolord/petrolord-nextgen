# What is held in the inventory

## Held items are stated limits

Four items in this course are held. Each is taught as a stated limit and never graded. A held item is read beside the figures it touches. It is never a figure to compute with.

| item | the limit |
| --- | --- |
| H1 | Which IPCC assessment report a Nigerian operator files on (AR5 or AR6) is a regulatory reading and the owner's decision. The engine ships no GWP; the course prints both reports. |
| H2 | The engine's typical methane heating values are 802.6 LHV and 890.8 HHV MJ per kmol. The pair is labelled typical and is not corrected without ISO 6976 in hand; the fuel analysis governs. |
| H3 | Every escaped carbon atom is counted as methane. |
| H4 | Combustion N2O is not computed by the atom balance; it needs an emission factor line. |
| inputs | GWP values, emission factors and every price are inputs. Neither engine ships one. |

## H1: the report to file on

The course computes on "IPCC AR6 GWP100, fossil methane" and prints the other three sets beside it. It does not say which report an operator should file on. The digest records where each stands: AR5 is what UNFCCC reporting has required and AR6 is what the GHG Protocol now recommends. At Igbogene the choice between the two fossil sets moves the total from 42945.777 tCO2e on AR6 to 42977.438 tCO2e on AR5. The limit is that the choice is a regulatory reading.

## H2: the methane heating value pair

The engine's fuel reference table gives methane a typical LHV of 802.6 and a typical HHV of 890.8 MJ per kmol. The digest computes the difference as 88.2, and it computes two moles of water condensed at the engine's own latent heat as 87.985 MJ: 2 x 18.015 kg, the H2O of energyEfficiency.PRODUCT_MOLAR_MASS, x 2442 kJ/kg. Both are the digest's arithmetic. The pair is labelled typical, and the fuel analysis governs.

## H3: escaped carbon counted as methane

Every carbon atom that escapes a flame is counted as methane. The engine calls this "the usual and conservative assumption" and says to override it if you have measured otherwise. At the Igbogene flare's stated 0.98 it gives 16.306 t of methane, 485.922 tCO2e on the course's set. A method that uses the gas's own methane content is a different method and the owner's decision.

## H4: nitrous oxide from combustion

The atom balance returns co2Tonnes and ch4Tonnes and no other gas. Combustion N2O is not computed, and it would need an emission factor line of its own. The Igbogene inventory has no N2O line.

## The rules in force

Beside the held items, the digest lists the rules the engine applies now. Among them are these:

A blank or null destruction efficiency is refused; left out of the call it is complete combustion. A line that errors, and a line off Scope 1 and Scope 2, is a blocked line with its reason. An intensity carries its inventory's reportable status. The GWP set carries a methane note.

In the Igbogene figures they print as the flare's refusal, two added lines blocked with reasons, and an intensity of 0.00822761 reportable false.

In practice, a stated limit is what lets two people who disagree about a choice still agree on what the engine computed.

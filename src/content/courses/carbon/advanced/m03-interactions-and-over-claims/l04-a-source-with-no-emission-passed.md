# A source with no emission passed

The curve checks a claim against the emission it is given for that claim's source. When no emission is given, there is nothing to check. SECTION 21 prints that case on the invented AGBOR records, and this lesson reads it by the rule of the last lesson: a claim the curve cannot check against its source gets no verdict.

## The same claim, the flare not passed

SECTION 21's third curve keeps Flare gas recovery's claim at 9400 t a year, and this time the flare's emission is not passed to the curve:

| curve | total abatement t | target t | over-claims |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flare gas recovery claiming 9400 t | 18660.000 | 16830.083 | flare: claimed 9400.000 against 7562.133 emitted |
| the same claim, the flare's emission not passed | 18660.000 | 16830.083 | none |

The claim is the same and the total is the same, 18660.000 t. With the flare's 7562.133 tCO2e passed, the over-claim is found and named. Without it, the over-claims column reads none.

## What that none means

SECTION 21 says it in one sentence: "A source whose emission is not passed cannot be checked, and the claim against it stands unexamined." The none in the over-claims column says only that nothing was found. The claim of 9400 t is unexamined, so that none is no finding that the claim fits.

The digest prints a target verdict on this third row. By the rule this course reads by, a claim that is not checked gives no verdict, and this course teaches none from that row.

## Every source the curve is not given

SECTION 21 passes two sources to the curve: heaters, 34927.743 tCO2e, and flare, 7562.133 tCO2e. The acts-on column of SECTION 18 names four more places the measures act: steam, power and vents, one measure each. Repair failed steam traps claims 1150 t, Solar for purchased power 2100 t and Vapour recovery on the storage tanks 1850 t, and none of those sources has an emission passed. So on every curve in SECTION 21, those three claims stand unexamined too, and the same rule applies to reading any verdict from those rows.

## What the two rows share, and what they do not

Read the second and third rows as a pair. They share the claim, 9400 t, the total, 18660.000 t, and the target, 16830.083 t. They differ in one input, whether the flare's emission reached the curve, and in what the curve could say about the claim. With the emission passed, the claim is examined and named as an over-claim. Without it, the claim is unexamined and the over-claims column is empty.

An empty over-claims column is therefore read with the source ids beside it. It speaks only for the sources that were passed.

# Onward

A vessel that holds its liquid and carries its gas has answered two questions. A real separator is usually asked a third, because most of what arrives is neither gas nor a single liquid.

## A second liquid changes the geometry

Everything in this tier treated the liquid as one phase in the bottom of a circle. Put oil over water and there is a second surface inside that liquid area, at its own height, and the retention times the two phases are given decide where it sits.

## The chord is not the interface

That surface is not the chord. The gas-liquid chord on the 8.000000 ft drum is 8.000000 ft at a level of 0.500000, a width across the drum, and reading it as the height of a water layer is the error the retired app made. The Expert tier places the interface at its exact height instead, and then asks whether a water drop can fall out of the oil and an oil drop rise out of the water in the time the vessel gives them. Those two verdicts can condemn a vessel that passed every check in this tier.

## A family, and a preference

A sweep across diameters returns a row for every bore with its own reasons attached. Two reasons already appeared here: gas-capacity, which is a failure, and ld-out-of-band, which is a preference about slenderness against a band such as 3.000000 to 5.000000. Choosing between the rows is a judgement with a rule behind it, and the rule matters, because a row can be inside the band and still infeasible. The Expert tier works that rule and the three statuses a sweep can report.

## A layout has more than one answer

The ERHA station was read four ways at once.

| reading | value |
| --- | --- |
| comparisons with a positive requirement | 69 |
| pairs with no requirement | 21 |
| unknown type pairs | 12 |
| skipped items | 2 |
| complete | false |
| pass | false |

Six of those comparisons failed. The worst by absolute shortfall is the flare to the control room, 43.8223 m short, and the worst by proportion is one transfer pump against the other at 0.597753 of its requirement. They name different pairs, and a reviewer who reads one ranking has read half the plot.

Complete and pass answer different questions, and a check that examined nothing is not a pass. That distinction, and the four things this method does not know, is where the Expert tier goes.

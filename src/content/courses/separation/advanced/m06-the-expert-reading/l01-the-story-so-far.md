# The story so far

A three-phase vessel and the plot it stands on are one answer in two halves, and this tier has been about the places where the answer carries a status word rather than a number.

## One stream, end to end

AGBAMI at 350.000000 psig and 110.000000 degF with a gravity of 0.700000 gives Ppr 0.549797, Tpr 1.508700, z 0.947166 and a gas density of 1.276898 lb/ft3. The vane pack gives K 0.525000, settling is 3.549130 ft/s, and the gas arrives at 8.713371 ft3/s.

At 10.000000 ft the interface sits at 3.049149 ft, the vessel needs 12.311666 ft with the controlling requirement liquid-retention, and both droplet verdicts pass at the 500.000000 micron specification while the water one fails at 150.000000 micron.

## Four answers that are not numbers

The split comes back with `retentionPhase` null when the proportional rule made the two lengths agree, and with the water named when a pinned share broke them apart. A sweep comes back with a diameter and `preferredStatus` selected, or with null and either none-feasible or none-in-band. A layout comes back with `complete` and `pass` answering different questions, and `pass` null with the status nothing-checked when there was nothing to have a result about.

Each of those is a case a retired rule answered with a number or a boolean it had not earned: a chord-rule layer, the first row in list order, a pass on a plan where nothing was examined.

## One site, judged

ERHA needs 64.6458 m of flare setback from 828000.0000 kW, and its bund gives 59.5294 m from the pool centre with 50.5294 m from the pool edge. Checking the site against both plus the table leaves 6 breaches, the largest 43.8223 m and the sharpest 0.597753 of its requirement, on a plan that is not complete because 2 things were skipped.

Two rankings, two different pairs, and a completeness reading that says the review has a remainder.

## What is held

Four things are recorded and unchecked: the K derating with its 0.120000 floor, the horizontal use of Souders-Brown as a droplet settling velocity, the API 521 radiation labels, and the spacing table figures. Beside them sits a stated convention, the Stokes field constant running 1.004184 below the SI derivation on every case, kept because the field form is what the standards use.

None of those five is an answer. Each is a limit to state when the answer is handed over.

## The habit to keep

Read the status beside the number. A diameter with no `preferredStatus`, a verdict with no droplet size, a pass with no completeness reading and a setback with no note of whether it was computed or copied are all the same failure: a conclusion quoted without the thing that qualifies it.

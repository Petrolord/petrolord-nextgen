# The Ekene field and the teaching pack

The Ekene field is synthetic. It is the Petrolord demonstration field: one earth model, from which every log and every seismic trace was generated by a stated script, so every answer in this course can be checked against the model that made it. No real well, company or survey appears. The teaching pack is cut from release ekene-demo-v3 of the Ekene demonstration kit, and its petrophysical curves (PHIT, VSH and SW) are the interpretation the kit was built from, sampled at the log depths.

## Four wells over one sand

The pack carries logs for Ekene-1 to Ekene-4 over the Ekene Sand and the Ogbia Shale above it. Depths are measured depth below the kelly bushing, in feet; the kit writes metres and the course converts. The four wells are vertical.

| well | KB elevation ft | top Ogbia Shale ft | top Ekene Sand ft | base Ekene Sand ft | log samples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ekene-1 | 82.020997 | 4232.283465 | 5078.740157 | 5183.727034 | 800 |
| Ekene-2 | 82.020997 | 4262.959318 | 5134.514436 | 5252.624672 | 827 |
| Ekene-3 | 82.020997 | 4219.652231 | 5055.774278 | 5150.918635 | 781 |
| Ekene-4 | 82.020997 | 4308.070866 | 5216.535433 | 5298.556430 | 755 |

The oil-water contact is at 5118.110236 ft, the same depth in every well, and the log step is 0.500000 ft. At Ekene-2 and Ekene-4 the top of the sand already lies below that depth, so the contact falls above their sand; Ekene-1 and Ekene-3 cut it inside the sand.

## The selections the course states

A selection is a rule for choosing samples, made before any engine is called. The course states four, and every figure that depends on them says so.

| selection | rule | samples at Ekene-1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| net sand | Vsh at or below 0.350000 | 168 |
| oil leg | net sand inside the Ekene Sand, above the contact | 65 |
| water leg | net sand inside the Ekene Sand, at or below the contact | 103 |
| cap rock | the 65 ft of log above the top of the Ekene Sand | 130 |

The cut-off, the contact and the cap rock thickness are choices: move the cut-off and the legs change size, and every mean, trend and substitution read from them moves.

## What the pack holds

| part | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| logs | Vp, Vs and density from DT, DTS and RHOB, GR, and the kit's PHIT, VSH and SW, for 4 wells |
| headers | the curve list, checkshot count and survey station count of all 12 wells |
| spread traces | full-stack traces on 10 inlines by 12 crosslines, 800 to 2400 ms every 4.000000 ms |
| neighbour traces | 21 neighbouring full-stack traces along inline 1027, through Ekene-1 |
| RMS slice | RMS amplitude of the full stack on 128 inlines by 128 crosslines, 1250 to 1350 ms |
| traces at the wells | full, near, mid and far stacks at Ekene-1 to Ekene-4, 900 to 1700 ms |
| gathers | NMO-corrected gathers next to Ekene-1, 12 CDPs and 13 offsets |

The velocities come straight from the slowness logs: Vp = 304800 / DT and Vs = 304800 / DTS and density = RHOB x 1000. The Associate tier reads the logs, the headers, the spread and neighbour traces and the RMS slice. The stacks at the wells and the gathers wait for the Professional tier.

## The conditions at the sand

The conditions are stated with the kit: 180 degF, 3200 psia, brine salinity 0.035000 (weight fraction of NaCl), oil of 32 API, a gas-oil ratio of 400 scf/stb and gas gravity 0.750000. The engines hold none of them; each enters the fluid calculations as a stated input.

## Why a synthetic field

Because the earth model is known, a figure the engines return on the pack can be checked against the rock that produced it, so a method can be judged on a field whose answer exists. The pack is still logs and traces, with the curves and the interpretation a study would be handed.

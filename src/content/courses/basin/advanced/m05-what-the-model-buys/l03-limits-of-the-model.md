# Limits of the model

This course has named the model's simplifications one at a time, each where it acted. This lesson collects them into a single honest register, with each limitation's footprint on the fixture and its recorded remedy, because a modeller is exactly as trustworthy as their limitations list.

## The register

Instant deposition. Layers arrive in full at their start age, so burial is a staircase and every arrival's maturation is compressed and slightly mistimed. Footprint: sharp onsets, the 80 Ma step performing sixty megayears of deposition. Remedy on record: within-window deposition schedules. Reading rule: never date generation onset more precisely than the deposition convention resolves it.

Elastic rebound. Compaction reverses on unroofing; real rocks keep their maximum-burial porosity. Footprint here: marginal for the graded values, decisive in principle for post-event retention, the regrown bucket. Remedy: max-burial hysteresis. Reading rule: any conclusion that depends on porosity after an unroofing event is convention-sensitive.

The phantom's inferred history. An erosion event's vanished section is assumed deposited immediately after the youngest preserved layer, as shale. Footprint: the hot decade's timing and lithology are conventions, and the signature is defined on them. Reading rule: quoted erosion amounts are conditional on the assumed timing.

One temperature per layer. Kinetics run at the layer centre; a 400 m source spans about ten degrees of internal gradient. Remedy available today: subdivide the interval. Reading rule: a thick layer's single Ro is its midpoint's, not its base's.

The one-way saturation bucket. Retention is a geometric cap at ten percent saturation and 850 kg/m3, expulsion a running maximum, no fractionation, no adsorption, no fracture release. Footprint: the squeeze identity is exact within this rule. Reading rule: expelled mass is defined by the retention model, and comparing models means comparing retention rules first.

No migration. The model ends at the source's door. Everything between kitchen and trap is downstream of this course.

## What did not make the register

Equally important is what needs no apology. The kinetics are published, calibrated science with an independent oracle. Grain conservation is exact bookkeeping. The transient heat solver is the honest physics at the fixture's scales. The capstone's tolerances all comfortably cover the numerical noise between the engine and its independent implementation. The register is short and specific, which is what separates a limitations list from a disclaimer.

## Using the register

Three uses, in ascending order of skill. Defence: when a reviewer attacks a result, the register says instantly whether the attack lands on a limitation or on solid ground. Sensitivity: each entry implies the experiment that prices it, and this course ran several, the amount sweep, the hysteresis bounds, the subdivision estimate. Design: the register is the specification for the next model version, which is literally true of this engine, whose recorded follow-ons are the remedies above.

The habit that does not survive contact with real work is the fourth use, silence: shipping numbers without the register attached. Every graded value in this course came with its conventions named, and that pairing, number plus register, is the deliverable format to carry out of the tier.

## Worked example

A peer review asks: "Your expelled mass is quoted to five significant figures; defend the precision." Draft the register-based reply. The precision is the engine's, exact within its stated retention model and conventions, and the identity behind it, generation at 11 Ma minus the squeezed cap, is reproducible arithmetic. The physical uncertainty is dominated by register entries, retention threshold, rebound convention and TOC-HI inputs, comfortably wider than any rounding, and the sensitivity runs bounding them are the amount sweep and the richness scaling of module 4. Precision states what the model computed; the register states what the world may do differently. Both belong in the answer.

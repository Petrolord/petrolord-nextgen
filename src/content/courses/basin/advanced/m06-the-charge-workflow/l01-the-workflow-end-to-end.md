# The workflow end to end

The forward model's workflow is the course's three tiers run as one sequence, twice. This lesson states it at full length, once, as you will run it on the capstone and on any basin whose charge you are asked to state.

## Step one: assemble the basin description

Stratigraphy in deposition order with lithologies, present thicknesses and age windows; source intervals flagged with TOC, HI and kerogen type; surface temperature; the heat-flow history as dated points; erosion events as age-amount pairs. Every input is a claim someone must defend: thicknesses from wells, ages from biostratigraphy, richness from geochemistry, heat flow usually the least constrained and destined to be calibration's free parameter. Write the register's relevant conventions beside the inputs now, the phantom's inferred timing above all, because they are part of what the description means.

## Step two: run forward, and run the comparisons

One run answers nothing but "what does this model say". The workflow's unit of meaning is the comparison set: the reference run plus the runs that isolate causes and price uncertainties. For the capstone: the reference basin and its no-erosion twin. In practice: variants over heat-flow scenarios, erosion amounts, source richness, whatever the register and the inputs' error bars nominate. Module 3's rule governs every pair: one difference per comparison, or the difference measures nothing.

## Step three: verify before reading

The checklist of module 5, in cost order: closed forms, inherited anchors, composition checks. Three multiplications and a subtraction validate half the graded surface before any interpretation begins, and the two-run discipline validates the comparison itself. A run that fails a check is not "approximately right"; the checks are chosen precisely because failures localise causes.

## Step four: read the diaries

Extract the series, not just endpoints. Burial staircase per layer; temperature with its spikes and relaxations; the two monotone diaries, maturity and expulsion, each interrogated for when its maximum was set. Place the kitchen's dates against the basin's traps. This is where model output becomes geological narrative, and the narrative discipline is module 4's: every date quoted no sharper than the conventions resolve it.

## Step five: state the charge, with its register

The deliverable is module 5's charge statement: potential, conversion, generation, expulsion with efficiency and dates, retained mass, all at stated precision, with the limitations register attached and the sensitivity runs that price its entries. Plus, where erosion or uplift is in play, the signature analysis: what the maturity excess measures, on what convention, with what error bars from the amount-sensitivity curve.

## The workflow on the capstone

The capstone is the workflow with step one handed to you. Assemble: the golden description, module 1. Run: reference plus no-erosion twin. Verify: the three identities. Read: final temperature, Ro, TR from the reference run's source diary. State: generated, expelled, and the signature from the twin comparison. Six fields, five steps, nothing improvised.

## Worked example

Phrase a real task as the workflow. Task: "the flank block's source is 400 m shallower; will its charge differ materially?" Step one: clone the description, shift the source's structural position via the flank's thicknesses. Step two: run the clone against the reference, one difference. Step three: checks, especially the potential, which must not move since richness and grain are unchanged. Step four: compare the diaries, expecting later, cooler maturation, lower TR, possibly a cap that never binds. Step five: state both charges and the difference, attributing it wholly to the 400 m, because the comparison isolated it. The answer's form was fixed before any run: that is what a workflow is for.

## Exercise

Write the five steps from memory, one line each, and mark where the two-run discipline appears. Then answer in one sentence: why does the workflow insist the limitations register travel with the charge statement?

As a self check: assemble the description with its conventions; run the reference and its isolating comparisons; verify with the cost-ordered checks; read the series as dated diaries; state the charge with register and sensitivities. The two-run discipline lives in step two and is re-verified in step three. The register travels because the statement's numbers are exact only within the model's conventions, and a reader who cannot see the conventions cannot know which conclusions are robust and which are register-sensitive.

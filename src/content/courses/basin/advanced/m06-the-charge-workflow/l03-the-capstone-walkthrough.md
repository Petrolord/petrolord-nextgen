# The capstone walkthrough

The Expert capstone grades six values from the reference basin. This lesson is the rehearsal: each field, its tolerance, its shortest defensible route, and the trap beside it. Two model runs and four lines of arithmetic cover everything.

## Field by field

Final Ro, tolerance 0.002: 1.6718288798752388, the reference run's source maturity diary at age zero. Trap: reading the no-erosion run's 1.6151780693528823, which sits eighteen tolerances away; the with-event run is the reference.

Final temperature, tolerance 0.1: 149.76037539670858 degC at the source centre, 3000 m. Trap: quoting the layer top or bottom, module 2's centre convention being the definition, or forgetting this basin's 15 degC surface and porting the beginner column's 10.

Final TR, tolerance 0.002: 0.7423743797385286, the kerogen clock's diary. Trap: conflating with the vitrinite state anywhere, the Professional tier's first lesson to its last.

Generated mass, tolerance 5 kg/m2: $18786.405883452077 \times 0.7423743797385286 = 13946.54641524398$. The multiplication route is effectively mandatory, module 4 priced why: carry TR at full precision.

Expelled mass, tolerance 5: 10048.985378825158, and the defensible route is the identity, generation at 11 Ma minus the squeezed cap, $13907.011498614682 - 3858.026119789524$. Trap: computing generated-minus-cap at present day, 9284.204, which misses the monotone rule by 765 and the field by 153 tolerances; the squeeze lesson exists because of exactly this error.

The erosion signature, tolerance 0.001: $1.6718288798752388 - 1.6151780693528823 = 0.05665081052235643$, with-minus-without, positive. Trap: sign, or differencing anything but final Ro of the same layer at the same date, or quoting the Associate tier's rounded 0.0567 as if it were the computed value; inside tolerance, but the examiner reads routes as well as digits.

## The rehearsal

Run the sequence once cold, in panel or engine, timing yourself against understanding rather than the clock. Reference run, twin run, discipline check, three identities, six reads. Everything else this tier taught, the staircase, the transient lags, the phantom, the diaries, stands behind those reads when a grader or a reviewer asks why any of them is what it is. The capstone grades six numbers; the tier's actual product is that every one of them is, to you, a one-line consequence of machinery you can defend to arbitrary depth.

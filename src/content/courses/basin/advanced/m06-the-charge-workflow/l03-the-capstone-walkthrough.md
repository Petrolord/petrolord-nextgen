# The capstone walkthrough

The Expert capstone runs the golden reference basin with an erosion event of its own, an amount and an age, and grades six values from its source rock. This lesson is the rehearsal on the golden event, 600 m at 10 Ma, which is the teaching case: each kind of field, its shortest defensible route, and the trap beside it. None of the golden numbers is a capstone answer.

The charge explorer opens on the golden event. For the capstone, type the brief's erosion amount and age into the two boxes; the no-erosion twin runs alongside by design.

## The two runs

On your own machinery: the golden project with the brief's erosion event, and the same project with the erosion list empty. Verify the two-run discipline, identical histories before the event, and the three identities from module 5, and only then read fields.

## Field by field, on the golden event

Final Ro: 1.6718288798752388, the with-event run's source maturity diary at age zero. Trap: reading the no-erosion run's 1.6151780693528823.

Peak source temperature: the highest value in the source's temperature diary, which the erosion event shapes. The present-day temperature is set by present burial and heat flow and barely moves with the event, which is why the capstone asks for the peak. Trap: quoting the present-day tile.

Final TR: 0.7423743797385286, the kerogen clock's diary. Trap: conflating with the vitrinite state.

Generated mass: $18786.405883452077 \times 0.7423743797385286 = 13946.54641524398$. The multiplication route is effectively mandatory; carry TR at full precision.

Expelled mass: 10048.985378825158, and the defensible route is the identity, generation at the last step of the pre-event maximum minus the squeezed cap. Trap: computing generated minus cap at present day, which breaks the monotone rule; the squeeze lesson exists because of exactly this error.

The erosion signature: with-minus-without on final source Ro, $1.6718288798752388 - 1.6151780693528823 = 0.05665081052235643$, positive. Trap: sign, or differencing anything but final Ro of the same layer at the same date.

The capstone's event moves every one of these. The method does not move.

## The rehearsal

Run the sequence once cold, in panel or engine. Reference run, twin run, discipline check, three identities, six reads. Everything else this tier taught, the staircase, the transient lags, the phantom, the diaries, stands behind those reads when a reviewer asks why any of them is what it is.

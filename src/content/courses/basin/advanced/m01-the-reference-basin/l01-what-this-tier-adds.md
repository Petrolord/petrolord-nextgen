# What this tier adds

Two tiers of preparation end here. The Associate tier built burial and heat as checkable snapshots. The Professional tier added the kinetics and ran them on given temperature histories. This tier removes the last scaffold: nothing is given any more. A basin is described as geology describes it, layers with ages and a heat-flow history, and the model marches the whole system forward through 150 million years, producing every temperature track itself, running both clocks on every layer, and turning conversion into the two numbers a prospect is charged on: mass generated and mass expelled.

## The graded surface

The capstone grades six values, all read from the source rock of the golden reference basin at present day. Its final vitrinite reflectance, 1.6718288798752388. Its final temperature, 149.76037539670858 degC. Its final transformation ratio, 0.7423743797385286. The mass generated per square metre of basin, 13946.54641524398 kg. The mass expelled, 10048.985378825158 kg. And the erosion signature: rerun the same basin without its erosion event, difference the final reflectances, and report 0.05665081052235643.

Six numbers, and every one of them is the composition of things you already own. The temperature comes from the Associate tier's heat physics made transient. The reflectance and transformation come from the Professional tier's clocks, stepped at the layer centre every million years. The masses come from a potential you will compute by hand in module 4 and a retention rule with one line of algebra in it. Nothing in this tier is new physics; what is new is that the stages now feed each other instead of you.

## The two runs

Notice that the capstone's last field forces you to run the model twice. That is not an inconvenience, it is the tier's central teaching. A forward model's real product is not a number but a comparison: with the event and without it, with this heat history and with that one. The erosion signature, the difference between two runs, measures 600 metres of section that no longer exists, using the ratchet property of reflectance the Professional tier taught. The Associate tier forward-quoted the rounded result, a delta Ro of 0.0567; this tier computes it to full precision and grades it to a thousandth.

## What is deliberately still missing

Honesty about the boundary above, one last time. This model generates and expels; it does not migrate. Where the expelled mass goes, whether a trap exists on its path, and what fills at what date are secondary-migration and charge-risk questions that belong to prospect assessment, not to this course. The model's deliverable is the kitchen's output at its door, in kilograms per square metre, with its timing.

Also missing, by documented design: the version-one simplifications of the forward march, instantaneous layer deposition and elastic compaction rebound, which module 2 teaches with their consequences, because a modeller who does not know the simplifications reads artefacts as geology.

## How the modules run

Module 1 tours the reference basin itself: the four layers, the heat-flow history, and the panel that runs the model live. Module 2 is the forward march, the step loop and its geometry and heat. Module 3 is the erosion event, the phantom-layer mechanism, and the signature. Module 4 is generation and expulsion, including the sharpest fact in the course, the squeeze. Module 5 steps back to what the model buys and where its limits are. Module 6 is the workflow, quality control, the capstone walkthrough, and the close of the ladder.

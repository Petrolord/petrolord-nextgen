# Onward to Expert

The kinetics tier is complete. You can take a temperature history and turn it into a maturity and a conversion, by hand where the history is simple and with the integrator where it is not, and you can defend every constant in the machinery. What you cannot yet do is produce the history, or turn the conversion into anything with units of mass. The Expert tier does both, and this lesson is the map of the climb.

## What you now own

The two clocks and their separation: vitrinite for reflectance, fixed and calibrated; kerogen for transformation, spectrum-driven and editable. The Arrhenius machinery: rates that multiply by five per ten degrees at the front, a ladder spanning twenty-two orders of magnitude, a step that is exact at constant temperature. The graded results: both anchors as closed forms, the ramp pair and the reason the slow ramp wins, the isothermal pair and the stall behind it. And the reading discipline: windows as conventions, reflectance as a ratchet that remembers the maximum, geometry errors amplifying into chemistry errors.

The Associate tier's forward promise has been kept: "time is a reagent" is now 1.1129254516555198 against 0.9871413464062039, and you can derive both sides of it.

## What the Expert tier adds

The reference basin, run forward. Four layers of real stratigraphy over 150 Ma, a heat flow that cools from 80 to 60 mW/m2, and a 600 m erosion event at 10 Ma. The forward model marches time in 1 Ma steps: geometry by solid-thickness decompaction each step, temperature by a transient heat solver rather than a steady column, and at every step, for every layer, exactly the kinetic step you learned here, at the layer's centre temperature.

On top of the kinetics it adds the two things this tier deliberately lacked. Mass: the source's generative potential in kilograms per square metre, from its thickness, density, TOC and hydrogen index, so that TR becomes generated mass. And expulsion: a retention rule that decides how much generated mass stays in the source and how much leaves, which is the number a prospect actually charges on.

And it adds the result the Associate tier forward-quoted twice: run the basin with and without its erosion event and difference the final reflectances. The erosion signature, a delta Ro of 0.0567, is the ratchet property of module 5 turned into an instrument that measures a vanished section.

## Why the order was this way

You are climbing the pipeline in the order its errors propagate. Geometry and heat first, checkable by hand. Kinetics second, exact on given histories. The forward model last, because every number it produces is the composition of the first two stages with the third, and a modeller who cannot check the stages cannot debug the composition. When the Expert tier's basin produces a reflectance, you will know its provenance: a burial staircase, a transient column, and twenty bins draining, none of them mysterious.

## The hand-off

Three habits to carry up. State the heating pace of any history you quote, because every boundary depends on it. Keep the clocks separate in your head and your code, because the tier above multiplies one of them by a mass and calibrates against the other. And keep the stall in mind whenever a story involves a long wait at constant temperature, because the Expert tier's basin spends 76 million years cooling, and what maturity does during a cooling era is exactly what this tier taught you to expect: almost, but not quite, nothing.

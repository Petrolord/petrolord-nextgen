# Onward

What this course certified, and what it handed on.

## What this course certified

The static well control arithmetic on a surface-BOP well: the volumes and stroke counts from a span walk, the shut-in interpretation that turns two gauges and a pit gain into a formation pressure and an influx, the kill sheet's four outputs and its pressure schedule, MAASP, and single-bubble kick tolerance with both its cases.

## What it took as given

**The fracture gradient**, from a leak-off test or a prognosis. Every limit in the Expert tier is a comparison against it.

**The pore pressure**, implicitly, through the shut-in drill pipe pressure or the kick intensity.

Both come from the geomechanics course.

## What it handed on

**To casing design:** the casing point, from the mud weight at which kick tolerance crosses the design kick size.

**To the well plan:** the maximum mud weight each section can be drilled with, and the kick tolerance to compare a pit alarm against.

**To the drilling programme:** the standing part of the kill sheet, which has to be kept current at every stand.

## What Drilling Hydraulics handed to it

**The swab** that causes most tripping kicks, and the trip speed limit that prevents them.

**The equivalent circulating density**, which has to be re-established after a kill and which is compared against the same fracture gradient.

**The slow circulating rate pressure's provenance:** it is a system pressure loss, and that course is where it would be computed if it were not measured.

## The five things this course will not certify

Subsea and floating operations, gas migration and volumetric control, dissolved gas in oil-based mud, the casing pressure history, and multiphase influx behaviour.

All five are taught in Expert m05 and the go-live migration refuses to run if any graded field names one of them.

## Before you go

Three habits from this tier.

**Read MAASP as a rock limit at a stated mud weight.** It is not a casing rating and it is not fixed.

**Quote both kick tolerance cases and which one bound.** A tolerance bound circulated is a geometry problem and one bound shut in is a pressure problem, and they have different remedies.

**On a floating rig, correct for the choke line.** Every casing pressure in this course is a surface gauge reading with nothing between it and the annulus.

## The one sentence

A well is controlled when the pressure at the bottom exceeds the pore pressure and the pressure at the shoe does not exceed the fracture pressure, and this course is the arithmetic of keeping both true at once.

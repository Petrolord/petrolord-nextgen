# Boyle, and migration

Two things a gas influx does that a liquid one does not.

{{panel:wc-tolerance-explorer}}

## Boyle

    p1 V1 = p2 V2

At constant temperature, the volume of a gas is inversely proportional to its pressure.

A bubble at 30 MPa that reaches 10 MPa has tripled. One that reaches surface at 0.1 MPa has grown three hundredfold.

## Why that matters here

Because the influx enters at formation pressure, which is tens of megapascals, and it has to come out at surface, which is close to atmospheric.

Left to expand freely it would occupy a very large fraction of the annulus by the time it arrived, and the mud it displaced would be gone.

## What stops it expanding freely

The choke. Holding the bottom hole pressure constant means the pressure at the influx's position is whatever the mud column and the surface pressure make it, and the surface pressure is raised as the gas rises.

So a controlled kill does not let the gas expand as much as free expansion would, and the price is a rising casing pressure. That is exactly the trade a kill is.

## Migration

Gas rises through mud on its own, whether the well is circulating or not, at something like 100 to 300 metres per hour.

In a SHUT-IN well it cannot expand, because the volume is fixed. So it carries its pressure up with it, and both surface gauges rise together at the same rate.

## Why that is dangerous

Because the casing pressure can reach the MAASP while nothing is being done.

A well shut in for four hours with gas migrating at 200 m per hour has moved the gas 800 m up the hole, and the pressure at surface has risen by the mud head over those 800 m.

## The response

Volumetric control: bleed mud from the annulus in measured increments as the pressure rises, so that the bottom hole pressure stays constant while the gas rises.

It works, it needs careful measurement, and it is a procedure rather than a calculation.

## What this engine has

Boyle, for the kick tolerance's second case. Nothing about migration, no rate, and no time.

So a kick tolerance computed here is a statement about a circulation, and the shut-in waiting period before it is outside the model entirely.

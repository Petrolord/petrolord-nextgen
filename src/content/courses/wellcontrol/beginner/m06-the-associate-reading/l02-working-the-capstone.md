# Working the capstone

Six numbers from the well the lessons did not use.

{{panel:wc-volume-explorer}}

## What is asked

1. The string volume of the SLANT well.
2. Its annulus volume.
3. Its strokes to the bit.
4. Its bottoms up strokes.
5. Its true vertical depth at the bit.
6. Its true vertical depth at the casing shoe.

## The settings

**The well is the SLANT one**, the 40 degree well to 3000 m of measured depth with its shoe at 1400 m. The lessons took their volumes from the HORIZONTAL well, and this is the other one.

**The string and the geometry** are the fixture's own: the same drill collars, heavy weight and drill pipe, the same cased and open hole sizes.

**The pump output is 0.012 m3 per stroke.**

## The order

Fields 1 and 2 from one volumes run, and check their sum against the total circulating volume the panel reports.

Fields 3 and 4 are those two divided by the pump output. They are a division and nothing else, and they are worth doing by hand as a check on fields 1 and 2.

Fields 5 and 6 come from the survey rather than from the volumes: the true vertical depth at the bit's measured depth and at the shoe's.

## The traps

**The well is the slant one, not the horizontal one.** Every number in this capstone is different from the ones the lessons printed, and both wells share the same string, so a mistake here gives a plausible answer.

**Field 6 is at 1400 m of measured depth**, which is the slant well's shoe. The horizontal well's shoe is at 1200 m, and using the wrong one gives a number that looks entirely reasonable.

**Strokes are volume over output, not the reverse.** The answers are in the thousands, so a reciprocal is obvious once you look.

**The two depths are TRUE VERTICAL**, not measured. The measured depths are 3000 and 1400 and both graded answers are smaller than those.

## What to notice while you work

The gap between fields 5 and 6 is more than 1200 m. On the horizontal well the lessons used, the same gap is under 43 m.

That difference is the single most important fact about these two wells, and the Expert tier is built on it.

## The precision

Full precision, tight tolerances, for the reason every capstone in this series gives: the grader is checking the calculation was run rather than estimated.

## Exercise

Before opening the panel, predict whether the slant well's annulus volume will be larger or smaller than the horizontal well's, and by roughly how much.

Both wells have the same hole sizes and the same string. Only the depths and the shoe positions differ.

# Causes, and warning signs

Why the pressure falls, and how you know before the well tells you loudly.

## The four causes

**Insufficient mud weight.** The pore pressure is higher than expected, or the mud was designed for a shallower formation. The commonest planning cause.

**Swabbing.** Pulling the string reduces the pressure below the static value, and if the margin is smaller than the swab, the well takes an influx. The commonest operational cause, and it is computed in the Drilling Hydraulics course.

**Failure to fill the hole.** Every stand pulled removes steel and the level falls unless mud is added. That is what a trip tank measures.

**Lost circulation.** Mud goes into a fracture, the annulus level falls, the hydrostatic pressure falls, and a shallower or a deeper formation flows in. One problem becoming two.

## The warning signs, in the order they usually appear

**Drilling break.** A sudden increase in the rate of penetration, which can mean a more permeable and possibly higher-pressure formation.

**Flow rate out exceeding flow rate in.** The well is giving back more than it is taking. This is the primary indicator.

**Pit gain.** The volume in the active system rises. Same information, integrated.

**Pump pressure falling and pump speed rising.** A lighter fluid in the annulus needs less pressure to circulate.

**Gas or oil in the returns, or a cut mud weight.** Confirmation, and it arrives late.

**Flow with the pumps off.** The unambiguous one, and it is checked deliberately at every connection and before every trip.

## Why the first two matter most

Because they are the earliest and they are continuously monitored. Everything after them is confirmation of something that has already happened.

The flow check with the pumps off is the one that has no false positives: a well that flows when nothing is pumping is flowing from the formation.

## What is done

Shut in. Immediately, by procedure, without diagnosing first.

The diagnosis comes from the shut-in pressures, and those cannot be read until the well is shut in. The instinct to keep circulating and watch is the wrong one, and the reason is the feedback in the previous lesson.

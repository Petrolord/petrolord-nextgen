# Which one, and why

The decision, and the three things it turns on.

## The three

**How long the kill mud will take to build.** If it is minutes, wait and weight. If it is hours, the influx is sitting in the hole for hours.

**How close the casing pressure is to the MAASP.** The driller's method produces a higher peak, so a marginal shoe argues for wait and weight.

**How confident the crew is.** The driller's method's first circulation is holding one number constant, which is far easier under stress than following a schedule.

## The industry position

Both are taught, both are accepted, and different operators standardise on different ones.

Wait and weight is often preferred on paper because of the lower peak pressure. The driller's method is often what actually happens, because the mud takes time and the well is shut in while it is being built.

## The hybrid

Start the driller's method's first circulation immediately, and build kill mud while it runs. If the mud is ready before the first circulation finishes, the second circulation follows straight on.

That gets the influx out early and the kill mud in as soon as it exists, and it is what many procedures actually describe.

## What the calculation says about the choice

Almost nothing. The kill mud weight, the two circulating pressures, the strokes and the schedule are identical for both.

What differs is the CASING pressure history, which this engine does not compute at all: it produces the drill pipe schedule and leaves the annulus side to the choke operator and the MAASP check.

## What would be needed to compute it

A model of the influx's position and expansion as it moves up the annulus, with the mud displacement, the geometry changes and Boyle's law. That is a transient calculation and it is beyond this engine.

The kick tolerance calculation in the Expert tier is the closest thing to it: it asks whether the influx AT the shoe would fracture it, which is the peak of that history.

## The one thing both share

Constant bottom hole pressure. If that is held, the well is controlled, whichever mud is in the string and whichever circulation it is.

# The driller's method

Two circulations, and why the first one needs no schedule.

## The first circulation

Start the pump at the slow circulating rate, bringing it up while holding the CASING pressure constant. Once at rate, read the drill pipe pressure: it should be the initial circulating pressure, which is the slow circulating rate pressure plus the shut-in drill pipe pressure.

Then hold the DRILL PIPE pressure at that value for the whole circulation.

## Why holding it constant works

Because the string is full of original mud throughout. Nothing about the drill pipe side changes, so the pressure needed at surface to keep the bottom hole pressure at the formation pressure does not change either.

The choke does all the work: as the influx rises and expands, the choke opens or closes to keep the drill pipe gauge where it is.

## What the casing pressure does

It rises as the gas rises and expands, peaks around the time the influx reaches the choke, and then falls.

That peak is the number that has to be compared against the MAASP, and it is the driller's method's main disadvantage: the peak is higher than under wait and weight, because the whole circulation is done with the light original mud in the string.

## What the well looks like at the end

Full of original mud, no influx, still shut in.

The drill pipe pressure reads the same shut-in drill pipe pressure as before. The CASING pressure now reads the same number too, because the annulus is once again full of mud of known density.

That equality is the check that the first circulation worked. Two gauges reading the same means one fluid in both.

## The second circulation

Now pump kill mud. The drill pipe pressure follows the schedule from the initial to the final circulating pressure as the kill mud goes down the string, then is held at the final value while the annulus is displaced.

At the end both gauges read zero and the well is dead.

## The advantage

It starts immediately. No mud to build, no waiting, no volumetric control, and the influx is out of the hole in one bottoms up.

On a rig that cannot build kill mud quickly, that advantage is decisive.

## The disadvantage

Two circulations is roughly twice the time and twice the wear, and the higher peak casing pressure is a real risk on a well with a marginal shoe.

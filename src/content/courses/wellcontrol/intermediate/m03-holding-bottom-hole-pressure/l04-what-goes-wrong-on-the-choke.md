# What goes wrong on the choke

Five failures, and what each looks like on the gauges.

## One: the choke plugs

Cuttings or lost circulation material bridge in the choke. The back pressure rises sharply and cannot be reduced by opening.

**On the gauges:** both pressures rise together and the drill pipe pressure goes above the schedule.

**What is done:** switch to the spare choke on the manifold.

## Two: the choke washes out

Erosion has opened the flow path. The back pressure falls and cannot be raised by closing.

**On the gauges:** both pressures fall and the drill pipe pressure goes below the schedule.

**What is done:** switch to the spare, which is the same answer and the opposite cause.

## Three: the operator over-corrects

Chasing the gauge through the transit delay, producing an oscillation that grows.

**On the gauges:** the drill pipe pressure swings above and below the schedule with a period of roughly the transit time.

**What is done:** stop moving the choke, wait for it to settle, then resume with smaller movements.

## Four: gas reaches the choke

The fluid becomes compressible and the choke's response changes character.

**On the gauges:** the casing pressure peaks and the response to choke movements becomes larger and faster.

**What is done:** slow down, make smaller movements, and expect it. This is the part of the operation to have planned for.

## Five: the pump rate drifts

A pump slowing or speeding without the operator noticing changes the friction term.

**On the gauges:** the drill pipe pressure moves away from the schedule without the choke having been touched.

**What is done:** check the stroke counter's rate before touching the choke.

## The common pattern

Four of the five move the drill pipe pressure away from the schedule, and the schedule is what the operator is watching, so all four are detected the same way.

Distinguishing them takes a second observation: what the CASING pressure did at the same time, and whether the pump rate changed.

## The table

| failure | drill pipe | casing | pump rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| choke plugged | up | up | steady |
| choke washed out | down | down | steady |
| over-correction | oscillating | oscillating | steady |
| gas at the choke | as scheduled | peaking | steady |
| pump rate drift | up or down | up or down | changed |

Read the last column first. It is the cheapest check and it eliminates one row immediately.

## Exercise

For each of the five, say whether the bottom hole pressure went up or down while the failure was happening.

Two of the five raise it, which is the safer direction, and three lower it.

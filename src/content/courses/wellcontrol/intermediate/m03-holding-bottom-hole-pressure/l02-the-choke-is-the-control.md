# The choke is the control

The only moving part, and what it is actually doing.

## What the choke is

An adjustable restriction on the annulus return line, downstream of the blowout preventer.

Closing it raises the pressure upstream, which is the entire annulus. Opening it lowers it.

## The range

A choke has to be able to go from fully open, imposing almost no back pressure, to fully closed, imposing whatever the pump can supply.

In practice most of the useful control is in a narrow band of positions, and the operator learns where that band is on the particular choke.

## The delay

The choke is at surface and the bottom of the hole is thousands of metres away. A change at the choke takes time to be felt at the bottom, and the response the operator sees on the drill pipe gauge is delayed by the same transit.

That delay is the reason kill circulations are done slowly. At a slow circulating rate the delay is a manageable fraction of the operation; at full drilling rate it is not.

## The instability

Because of the delay, a choke operator who chases the gauge too hard will oscillate: over-correct, see the gauge move too far, over-correct back.

The technique is small movements and patience, and it is why the operator is a specific trained role rather than whoever is nearest.

## What happens when gas arrives

The fluid at the choke becomes compressible. A small choke movement now produces a large pressure change, because there is no longer an incompressible column transmitting it.

Control gets harder exactly when the pressures are highest, and that is the hardest part of any kill.

## Erosion

Gas and mud through a partly closed choke at high pressure erodes it. A choke can wash out during a long kill, which means it stops holding back pressure, which means the bottom hole pressure falls.

That is why chokes come in pairs on a manifold: one is used and the other is a spare that can be switched to.

## What this engine models

None of it. The engine produces the schedule the operator is trying to follow and knows nothing about the choke, its response, its delay or its erosion.

That is a scope statement rather than a criticism: the schedule is the calculable part and the choke is the operational part.

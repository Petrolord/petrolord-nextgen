# When to stop and think

Four situations where continuing is worse than pausing.

## One: the casing pressure approaches the MAASP

The shoe is about to be fractured. Continuing at the same rate will do it.

**What is done:** reduce the pump rate, holding the casing pressure constant while the rate comes down. A lower rate means less friction, which means less pressure at the shoe for the same bottom hole pressure.

If that is not enough, the operation is beyond the standard procedure.

## Two: the pit gain is rising

A second influx. The sheet on the desk describes a well that no longer exists.

**What is done:** shut in, re-read, recompute.

## Three: the gauges are inconsistent with each other

The drill pipe pressure and the casing pressure are telling different stories, and neither the choke, the rate nor a string fault explains it.

**What is done:** stop and work out what is happening before doing anything else. A well being circulated on a wrong model is a well being made worse.

## Four: the kill mud weight arriving is not what was planned

The mud engineer reports the mud going in is a different weight from the design.

**What is done:** recompute the final circulating pressure and the schedule from the actual weight. It takes a minute and it is a straightforward substitution.

## Why stopping is safe

Because shutting in is always available and it always works. A shut-in well with an influx in it is a stable situation that can be held indefinitely, with volumetric control for gas migration.

The pressure to keep going comes from the operation's cost and from the crew's fatigue, and neither is a reason.

## Why continuing is sometimes right

Because gas migration in a shut-in well raises both pressures over time, so an indefinite hold is not free either.

The judgement is between two costs, and it belongs to the well site supervisor rather than to a calculation.

## What this course contributes

The numbers each of those four judgements needs: the MAASP, the kick tolerance, the recomputed sheet.

None of them is the judgement itself, and a course that suggested otherwise would be teaching the wrong thing.

# What the classification is for

An expectation, not a decision.

## What it does not change

The kill mud weight. The initial circulating pressure. The final circulating pressure. The schedule. The stroke counts.

All five are identical whether the influx is gas, liquid or mixed. The kill sheet does not consult the classification at all.

## What it does change

**What you expect the casing pressure to do.** A gas influx expands and the casing pressure peaks; a liquid one does not.

**Whether the MAASP will be threatened.** That peak is what has to fit under it.

**How the choke will behave.** A compressible fluid at the choke responds differently: small movements produce large pressure changes, and the operator has less feel.

**Whether gas will be at surface.** A gas kick arrives at the choke and the separator, and that has its own procedures and hazards.

## Why it is an expectation rather than a decision

Because the procedure does not branch on it.

There is no separate gas kill sheet. The same constant-bottom-hole-pressure principle applies, the same schedule is followed, and the same choke is used. What differs is what the operator sees while doing it.

## The one place it does affect a decision

Kick tolerance, which is the Expert tier. There the influx density is an explicit input, and a lighter influx expands more and gives a smaller tolerance for the same pit gain.

So the classification changes the PLANNING question of whether the influx can be circulated out safely, without changing the procedure for doing it.

## What to do with an uncertain classification

Assume the worse case for planning and use the computed value for reporting.

If the density lands near a threshold, the sensible planning assumption is the lighter side, because that gives the larger expansion and the smaller kick tolerance.

## The reporting form

Give the density, the two gauge readings and the pit gain it was computed from, and the classification as a derived label rather than as a fact.

That is three numbers and a word, and the three numbers are what somebody else needs to check it.

# Onward from Earth Modeling

This completes the Earth Modeling ladder: a container built and clamped, wells tied to it with the disagreements read structurally, and the container cut, filled and audited per fault block. The three tiers were one model examined with rising scepticism, and the numbers you can now defend span from a clamp count of 180 to a closure of exactly zero.

## What you now hold

From this tier specifically: a census you can count by hand and defend to zero tolerance; the knowledge that a well's data can live in a different block from its rig; two population methods with opposite temperaments and the provenance discipline that keeps either honest; a variogram you know to be assumed and how to sweep it; and per-block volumes whose closure is an audit instrument, not a formality.

Held across the ladder: every number this course grades traces to an engine you could re-run, through conventions that are documented rather than discovered by disagreement. That is the transferable thing. Petroleum careers are long arguments about numbers, and the winning position is reliably the person who can name the recipe behind each one.

## Where the threads go next

The bulk volumes and per-block property maps hand off to ReservoirCalc Pro, whose ladder books barrels from exactly these containers: contacts, fluids and recovery live there, including the two-blocks-two-contacts question this course's fault raises and declines to answer. If you have that ladder's tiers, you have seen its side of the hand-off; the fixtures were designed to face each other.

The surfaces themselves came from the Mapping ladder, whose Expert tier owns the uncertainty machinery this course kept pointing at: cross-validation, jackknives, the hull rules for where a map has standing. The tie table's open contradiction, zone B against the eastern wells, is a MAPPING work order, and you now know how to write it precisely.

The wells' picks and surveys came through Well Data Manager and Well Correlation, where their own QC ladders live. And the fault polygon, here hand-drawn, is in real work an interpretation product of the Seismolord ladder, whose fault objects carry the dip this course's vertical curtain deliberately ignored.

## The habit to keep

If one habit survives the ladder, let it be the shape of every module here: derive the number, then ask what it does NOT claim. The census that is not an area, the exact fit that is not evidence, the honored well that is not trusted an inch away, the closure that certifies partitions and nothing else. Models earn trust by knowing their own edges, and so do modellers.

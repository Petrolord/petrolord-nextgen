# A target and three answers

A band says how big a risk is. It does not say whether the organisation can accept it. That second question is appetite, and in this register every risk carries its own target for it.

{{panel:rc-risk-explorer}}

## What appetite compares

Appetite compares the RESIDUAL score with the risk's own target. The residual is used because the question is whether the organisation can live with the risk as it stands, with its controls in place. The target is a score, set by people for each risk, marking the most residual risk they are prepared to carry for that hazard.

The engine has three answers: "Within appetite", "Above appetite" and "Not set".

## The probes

| appetite probe | residual score | target | appetite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| residual below the target | 6 | 8 | "Within appetite" |
| residual equal to the target | 8 | 8 | "Within appetite" |
| residual above the target | 9 | 8 | "Above appetite" |

A residual of 6 against a target of 8 is "Within appetite". A residual of 9 against the same target is "Above appetite". The row to remember is the middle one. A residual EQUAL to the target is within appetite. A residual of 8 against a target of 8 reads "Within appetite". The target is the most the organisation will accept, so reaching it exactly is still acceptable.

## On the OBODO register

| risk | residual | target | appetite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OB-01 | 10 | 10 | "Within appetite" |
| OB-02 | 8 | 6 | "Above appetite" |
| OB-04 | 9 | 9 | "Within appetite" |
| OB-05 | 5 | 4 | "Above appetite" |
| OB-07 | 8 | 8 | "Within appetite" |

OB-01 has a residual of 10 and a target of 10, so it sits exactly on its target and reads "Within appetite". OB-04, which is "Mitigated", does the same with 9 against 9, and OB-07 with 8 against 8. OB-02 has a residual of 8 against a target of 6, and reads "Above appetite". OB-05 has a residual of 5 against a target of 4 and reads "Above appetite".

## Appetite is separate from the band

Look at OB-01 and OB-02 together. OB-01's residual band is "High" and it is within appetite. OB-02's residual band is "Medium" and it is above appetite. A higher band does not mean a worse appetite answer, because each risk is measured against its own target. A hazard the organisation has chosen to tolerate at a residual of 10 is inside its appetite at 10. A hazard it wants held at 6 is outside its appetite at 8.

This is why a register needs both columns. The band ranks risks against one another on a common scale. Appetite measures each one against the level the organisation set for that particular hazard, and it is the column that says where action is owed.

## The third answer

The third answer, "Not set", is what the engine returns when there is no basis for the comparison. The next lesson covers every case that produces it.

## The mistake

The mistake is to read "Within appetite" as meaning below the target. Equal to the target is within appetite. A residual that lands exactly on its target has used all of the appetite set for it and is still inside.

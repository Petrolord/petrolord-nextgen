# The author may not validate

Validation is the step at which a lesson stops being one person's account and becomes something the organisation has accepted. The engine allows that step only to somebody who did not write the lesson.

{{panel:rc-review-explorer}}

## Where validation sits in the life of a lesson

A lesson moves "Draft" to "Submitted" to "Validated" to "Published". The legal moves, as the engine holds them:

| from | legal next statuses |
| --- | --- |
| "Draft" | "Submitted", "Archived" |
| "Submitted" | "Validated", "Draft", "Archived" |
| "Validated" | "Published", "Submitted", "Archived" |
| "Published" | "Embedded", "Superseded", "Archived" |
| "Embedded" | "Superseded", "Archived" |
| "Archived" | none |
| "Superseded" | none |

The author writes and submits. Somebody else validates. Only then can the lesson be published to everyone. The move from "Submitted" to "Validated" is where the second person's judgement is recorded.

## Why the author is the wrong person

A lesson is a claim about why something happened and what should change. The author already believes the claim. Validation asks whether somebody who did not live through the event, reading only what is written, finds the account complete and the recommendation sound. The author cannot supply that reading, however careful they are, because they fill the gaps from memory without noticing.

This is the same idea that runs through the whole of this course. The originator of a change does not approve it. The author of work under review does not review it. The author of a lesson does not validate it. In each register the engine keeps the person with the most interest in a yes away from giving it.

## An owner decision held in the engine

The rule on who validates is owner policy, decided on 2026-09-18 under AS15 as Q10, validation by typed name. The next lesson reads the typed-name half of it. The course records Q10 among the owner decisions held in the engine, the app and, where it matters, the database.

## What validation does not check

Validation checks the actor and the substance. It does not check whether the recommendation is right. That judgement belongs to the colleague doing the validating, and it is why the engine insists the colleague be independent. A validation by an independent person who reads the lesson carefully is worth something. A validation that is only a click is worth what the click cost.

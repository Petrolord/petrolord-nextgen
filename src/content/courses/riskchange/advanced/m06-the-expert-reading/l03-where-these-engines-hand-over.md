# Where these engines hand over

This last lesson draws the boundaries of the four engines in this course: what they leave to the app, to people, and to other courses.

## The engines decide and store nothing

Each engine decides a state from a record: a band and an appetite verdict for a risk, a stage move and an expiry state for a change, a closure verdict for a review, an acceptance and an embedding verdict for a lesson. None of them stores anything. The app stores the record and asks the engine what it means.

That split puts two things outside the engine. The first is what the app shows. The course records one case in this tier's register: the Lessons Learned dashboard computes reviewsOverdue and reviewsDueSoon and does not display either. On the ONNE register on 2026-10-01 they read 1 and 2. The engine answers, and whether a user sees the answer is the app's part.

The second is what the app and the database enforce. Segregation of duties in peer review, the author never reviewing the work, is held in the engine. The app and the database follow it with the Suite pull request that ships ASC-0. Until that ships, the rule this course teaches is the engine's rule.

## Where people take over

The engines check that the right people acted and that the record is complete. They do not judge the substance. A verified response can still be wrong, a validated lesson can still recommend the wrong thing, and an "Adopted" application can be done badly. Each is a judgement the engine hands to a person, and the rules in this tier exist to make sure the person is the right one.

The held items mark the other edge: where the engine trusts what it is handed, a person reading the answer has to know the limit.

## Where other courses take over

Compliance, Audit & Quality, the sibling course in the Assurance module, owns audit independence, the audit and finding lifecycles and root-cause categories. This course reads an audit finding only as the source of a lesson, the source type "Audit finding" that ON-06 records, and stops there.

The economics courses own net present value, Monte Carlo simulation and decision trees. Nothing in this course computes any of them.

## The thread through all three tiers

In assurance a status is earned by a rule over scores, dates and independent signatures. The Associate tier read the rule behind each risk band, the Professional tier the rule behind each change gate, and this tier the rules behind each review verdict and each learned lesson. The discipline in every register: read which rule decided the status, and on which date.

# A lesson into a risk and a change

This course has taught four registers one at a time. This lesson follows one loop across them: a lesson learned from an event, applied into the risk register and into the change register, each end carrying a key the other can follow.

{{panel:rc-review-explorer}}

## ON-01 into the risk register

ON-01 records a dropped object during a crane lift. It is "Published" on the ONNE register. On 2026-05-02 it was applied into the risk register, application AA-01, outcome "Adopted", naming the risk OB-07, Pump house flooded in the rainy season.

The application was allowed because it names the risk:

- ALLOWED, recording an application into the risk register that names the risk.

Without that name the engine would have refused with "Name the risk. A lesson pushed into the register points at the row it raised or changed, so the trail runs both ways."

At the other end of the trail, OB-07 is a record with its own state, decided by the riskScoring engine. It is "Realized", with a residual of 8, "Medium", and "Within appetite". The lesson does not own that state; a different engine reads it from a different record.

## ON-02 into the change register

ON-02 is "Embedded". On 2026-06-10 it was applied into the change register, application AA-04, outcome "Adopted", naming the change ES-06, Revised operating procedure for glycol regeneration. ES-06 is now in its "Closed" stage, a stage of a change, decided by the managementOfChange engine from its own approvals and actions.

ON-02's reuse record shows 1 applied into "Management of change". That application is the evidence its "Embedded" status rests on, and it names the change record it altered.

## Both ends carry a key

The lesson names the risk row or the change record, and each of those is a record with its own state. A reader starting from the lesson can open the risk or the change. A reader starting from the risk or the change can find the lesson that altered it.

The two applications into Suite registers are what the lessons summary counts as intoRiskRegister 1 and intoMoc 1 on the ONNE register.

## Where a lesson comes from

A lesson also has a source. One of the source types is "Audit finding", and ON-06 on the ONNE register records one. The audit and its finding belong to Compliance, Audit & Quality, and this course reads them only as a source.

## What the loop proves

A lesson is accepted by somebody other than its author, and learned when an application proves it changed something. The thing it changed is a record with its own rules and state, and every link can be opened by somebody who was not there.

Every engine answer here was also replayed through the independent oracles for its module, so each is two methods agreeing.

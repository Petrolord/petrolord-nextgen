# The capstone worked

The Expert assessment asks for the verdicts a review coordinator and a lessons owner would hand over. Every one is reachable by running the rules of this tier in order. Here is that chain worked on the teaching records, IK-01 and ON-01, as the engine reads them on 2026-10-01.

## The review first: resolved, then blocking

Start with each comment's status and ask whether it is resolved. The resolved statuses are "Verified", "Closed" and "Withdrawn". On IK-01 that leaves C-01, C-03, C-04, C-06, C-07 and C-09 unresolved, 6 in all, which is openComments 6.

Then ask each unresolved comment for its severity. Only "Critical" and "Major" block. C-01 is "Critical", C-03 and C-04 are "Major", and C-06, C-07 and C-09 are "Minor", "Editorial" and unrated. That is blockingComments 3. The total, every comment on the log, is totalComments 9.

Do not skip C-03 and C-04 because a response exists or a rejection was recorded. "Responded" and "Rejected" are both unresolved, and both still block.

## The verdict and its sentence

With 3 blocking comments the review cannot close. Quote the engine's sentence exactly:

- REFUSED, closing IK-01 as it stands: 1 critical and 2 major comments still need resolving. Verify, close out or withdraw them first.

The counts in the sentence must agree with the blocking list: 1 "Critical" and 2 "Major".

## The review on the calendar

IK-01 is in "Verification", an active stage, and was due 2026-09-29. On 2026-10-01 it reads overdue, the review's due date having passed. Write the date beside the verdict. A status without its as-of date cannot be checked.

## The lesson on the calendar

## What the chain will not give you

It will not say whether a response is correct or an adoption was done well. Those are judgements for people, and every figure stays what the engine returns.

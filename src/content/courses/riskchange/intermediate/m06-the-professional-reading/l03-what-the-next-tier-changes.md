# What the next tier changes

This tier read one engine, `managementOfChange`, through one kind of record: a change with its stages, approvals, actions and dates. The Expert tier turns to the other two registers the Assurance module keeps, peer review and lessons learned. The subjects change. The way of reading them does not.

## What carries over

Four habits from this tier go straight into the next one.

**A refusal names the way forward.** Every refusal in this tier carried a reason a user could act on, from the list of legal moves out of a stage to "Choose somebody independent of the change." The Expert tier's engines refuse in the same shape, with `ok` and a `reason`, and you will read their sentences the same way.

**A state is earned by a rule.** A change reached "Implementation" only through its gate, and reached the Closed stage only through a second one. The next tier reads a review that has to satisfy a rule before it can close, and a lesson that has to earn each of its states.

**Independence is written into the rules.** This tier's segregation of duties kept the originator away from the approval of their own change. The next tier meets the same principle on different records: people who may not judge their own work.

**Every dated status carries its date.** Expiry, ratification and overdue in this tier were all true on 2026-10-01. The next tier has its own dated statuses, and each one is quoted with the same as-of date.

## What is new

The records are new, and so are the verdicts. A change in this tier had a stage and a type. In the next tier you read comments with dispositions, reviews that close or cannot, and lessons that are written, checked, applied and, if they are used, embedded. The question moves from whether a change may go onto the facility to whether a review comment has been dealt with and whether a lesson has actually changed anything.

The Expert tier also reads what these engines hold back and the owner decisions behind them, of which this tier met two, D1 and Q9. It ends with one loop that runs across the registers.

## What you should bring

Bring the three lists over the stages, active, in effect and terminal, and the habit of asking which list a rule reads. Bring the calendar rule that a date equal to the as-of date has not passed. And bring the discipline this whole tier has run on: read the engine's own words, quote them exactly, and when the engine declines to answer, report the decline.

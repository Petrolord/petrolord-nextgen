# Onward

This tier sized a gas line by pressure. Two questions remain that no pressure drop can answer, and then the audit of the method itself.

## A pipe has to hold the pressure as well as carry the gas

Everything here treated the pipe as a bore. A pipe is also a wall, and the wall is not chosen by the designer. A code demands it, from the design pressure, the diameter, the grade of steel and where the line runs, so the same pipe on the same duty needs different metal in an empty place and beside a school. The Expert tier works that calculation and reads the rating back off the wall the mill actually rolled.

## A line is also a volume

The second question is what sits inside the pipe rather than what flows through it. A line holds a volume, liquid collects in it, and a pig pushes that liquid ahead of itself into whatever waits at the end. Sizing that duty needs one input this engine never computes, which has to be measured or assumed, and the Expert tier is careful about which figures are results and which are assumptions wearing a number.

## Where the method stops

The last part is the audit. Four published forms have been used here as though choosing between them were the only difficulty. Each of them has a domain, and this engine will answer outside those domains without saying so: there is a band of flow where no correlation in the module applies, a roughness past which a fitted curve is being extrapolated, and iterations that report a result and not their convergence.

Alongside that sits the question of what a refusal is. This tier met several and read each as a finding. The Expert tier reads them as a contract: what shape a refusal takes, which returns sit outside it deliberately, what each message protects, and what the engine still accepts that it arguably should not.

## What this tier established

The compressible half changed the method rather than the numbers. Pressures enter squared, the base a rate is counted at is part of the answer, the hill acts twice and never symmetrically, and the inverse has a range that a hill moves. A reader who can say which of four rates to believe, and why the outlet of a descending line can stand above its inlet, is ready for the parts of a pipeline that pressure does not decide.

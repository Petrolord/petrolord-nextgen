# The story so far

A gas line is read by asking which form was used, what the hill did to it, and which direction the question was asked in. The SOKU trunk answers all three.

## Five questions, five answers

| question | the answer on the teaching trunk |
| --- | --- |
| what does a gas form start from | a driving group of 338100.0000 psia squared |
| what rate does the line carry | four of them, from 66104956.1404 to 88369202.2673 scfd |
| what does a hill do | 1500.000000 ft of rise takes Weymouth to 61666790.6494 scfd |
| what pressure arrives at a contracted rate | 666.307057 psia at 60000000.0000 scfd |
| where is the line weakest | at its worst station, which only a march reports |

## The trunk

SOKU is 11.938000 in of bore over 32.000000 miles, 850.000000 psia at the station against 620.000000 psia at the delivery point, at a gravity of 0.670000, an average flowing temperature of 535.000000 degR, an average compressibility of 0.885000 and an efficiency of 1.000000. Those two pressures never appear on their own. They appear as 338100.0000 psia squared, counted at a base of 520.000000 degR and 14.650000 psia, over a length in miles.

Four published forms read that trunk. Weymouth gives 66104956.1404 scfd, Panhandle A 86864172.0167, Panhandle B 88369202.2673 and General Flow 73861363.0502 at a friction factor of 0.0112132010. The spread from lowest to highest is 1.336801, which makes the choice of form a larger decision than the choice of bore.

## The hill

One elevation group is shared by all four. It scales the outlet inside the driving group through e to the s, and it changes the length friction acts over through the equivalent length factor. Put the trunk up 1500.000000 ft and Weymouth falls to 61666790.6494 scfd. Put it down the same distance and it rises to 70296168.5930. Those two do not average back to the flat rate, because the term is an exponential.

## The inverse

Asked for a rate rather than a pressure, the engine bisects, and its bracket ends where the driving group vanishes. That ceiling is the inlet over the square root of e to the s: 850.000000 psia on the flat trunk, 920.423606 psia down 3000.000000 ft and 784.964657 psia up the same. At 60000000.0000 scfd the flat trunk delivers at 666.307057 psia.

## The march

A traverse returns every station. The OGBIA line flat and over a ridge both arrive at 874.339369 psia, and at the crest they stand 158.958333 psi apart. A line is sized on its worst station and not on its last one.

## What ties the five together

Every answer on that table is conditional on something stated rather than discovered. The rate depends on which of four forms was named. What the hill does depends on the direction it runs. The outlet at a contracted rate depends on a bracket whose ends the hill moves. The worst station depends on a profile the caller supplied. None of those conditions can be recovered from the answer afterwards, which is why a gas figure travels with its conditions or it does not travel at all.

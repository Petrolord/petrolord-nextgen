# The jump at the branch

The Associate tier met the band between the two friction branches and was told there is no correlation in it. This lesson measures what that costs, and the figure is larger than most readers expect.

{{panel:fc-liquid-explorer}}

## One unit of Reynolds number

On the OGBIA pipe, at its relative roughness of 0.0002255356, the friction factor is 0.0304761905 at a Reynolds number of 2099.9999999 and 0.0488545386 at 2100.0000.

That is a ratio of 1.603040 across an interval in which nothing physical happens. Read the same pair as a percentage and the friction factor rises by 60.303955 percent for a change in Reynolds number too small to measure in a real line.

## What actually changed

The fluid did not change. The pipe did not change. The engine left the laminar law and started the turbulent one, and the discontinuity is the price of having no correlation for the band between them.

A physical transition is gradual and messy. This one is a switch, placed where the engine draws its boundary, and the size of the step is a property of the two correlations meeting there rather than a property of the flow.

## The label and the arithmetic disagree

The lower value is reported as laminar. The upper value is reported as transitional, and it is computed on the turbulent branch.

So inside the band from 2100 to 4000 the word and the arithmetic are saying different things. The engine is telling a reader the flow is transitional while handing over a number produced by a turbulent correlation, and the two statements arrive in the same object with nothing marking the disagreement.

That is worth being exact about. The regime word is not wrong and the friction factor is not a fabrication. They are two answers from different assumptions, returned together.

## Held, and why that is the right call

The band from a Reynolds number of 2100 to one of 4000 is held for the literature. It is taught as a limit and it is never graded, and the step across the boundary is 1.603040 times.

Holding it is the honest response to a correlation that does not exist. The alternative is to interpolate across the band and hand back a smooth curve, which would hide the problem behind a number that looks like the others.

## The mistake

The mistake is designing in the band. A line whose duty puts it between 2100 and 4000 has a pressure drop that depends on which side of an arbitrary boundary the Reynolds number lands, and moving the bore or the rate until the flow is clearly laminar or clearly turbulent is a real engineering fix rather than a presentational one.

The second mistake is quoting a transitional friction factor to ten decimals. The digits are what the turbulent correlation produced. They are not evidence that the correlation applies where it was asked.

## Exercise

Give the two friction factors either side of a Reynolds number of 2100 on the OGBIA pipe, with the ratio and the percentage between them. Then explain what changed physically in that interval, state the disagreement between the regime word and the arithmetic in the band, and say why the band is held rather than interpolated.

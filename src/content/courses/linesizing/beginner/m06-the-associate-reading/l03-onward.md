# Onward

The Associate tier is closed form from end to end, and the next tier is where that stops. Two sentences about the OGBIA line explain why the other tiers exist.

## Nothing in it compressed

A barrel of crude entering the line is a barrel leaving it. The density of 54.500000 lb/ft3 is the same at both ends, so the velocity of 2.244621 ft/s is the same at both ends, and the pressure falls in a straight line along the pipe.

Let the fluid compress and that stops being true. The density then depends on the pressure, which is the thing being solved for, and the whole method changes shape. That is the Professional tier. Its work is stated in miles rather than in the feet this tier uses for a separate reason: miles is the unit the published transmission forms are written in.

## Nothing in it iterated except the friction factor

Every other step in the chain was a substitution. Even the friction factor, which does iterate, returns a settled number that the rest of the chain then uses as an input.

The later tiers meet answers that came out of a solver rather than out of an expression, where the thing being searched for has no closed form at all. Reading such an answer means knowing what was searched over and what the search was allowed to consider.

## What stays true

Three habits carry forward. Run the chain in order, because every number is an input to the next. Write the unit beside every figure, because feet and miles and inches all print as ordinary numbers. And read a refusal as a finding: an object carrying an error is the method reporting honestly on a state it has no answer for.

## What this tier cannot tell you

Whether a c factor is right. The three rows of 100.000000, 125.000000 and 175.000000 are held for the literature, because the recommended practice describes its own figures as conservative and the third row is operator practice with no publication behind it. Every graded erosional value in this course therefore states its own c factor.

The same discipline applies to everything the later tiers hold. Say what the method is, and do not rest a conclusion on the part of it that nothing stands behind.

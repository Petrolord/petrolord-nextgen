# What a trip sheet is for

Measuring what the calculation predicted.

## The document

A trip sheet records, stand by stand, how much mud went into the hole coming out or came out of the hole going in, against how much was calculated.

It is filled in by hand, on paper or a screen, by the driller, every stand, on every trip.

## What it is measuring

Displacement. A stand of steel coming out of the hole leaves a volume behind, and the hole must be filled with exactly that volume or the level falls.

    open string:   volume = length x (pi/4)(d_od^2 - d_id^2)
    closed string: volume = length x (pi/4) d_od^2

Exactly the two expressions the surge calculation uses, applied to a length rather than a speed.

## Why it is the primary trip-time indicator

Because a discrepancy has only two explanations.

**Taking less than calculated:** something else is filling the space, and the only candidate is formation fluid. That is a kick.

**Taking more than calculated:** mud is leaving somewhere. That is losses.

Both are detected before anything reaches surface, which is the whole point.

## The trip tank

A small calibrated tank, typically a few cubic metres, dedicated to filling the hole and measuring what it took.

Using the active pit for that measurement would be useless: a discrepancy of a few hundred litres is invisible in a tank of tens of cubic metres.

## The connection to this course

The surge and swab calculation gives the SPEED limit. The trip sheet gives the VOLUME check.

They are two independent controls on the same operation: one is a plan and the other is a measurement, and neither replaces the other.

## What the calculation cannot do

Detect a kick. It can only say how fast you may go to avoid causing one.

The measurement is what detects it, and that is the general pattern in this course: the model sets the plan and an instrument catches the departure.

## The habit

Compute the displacement for the string you are running before the trip starts, and give the driller a number per stand.

An expected value that somebody worked out is far more useful than a running tally with nothing to compare it against.

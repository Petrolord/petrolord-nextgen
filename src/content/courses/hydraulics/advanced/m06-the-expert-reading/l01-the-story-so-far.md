# The story so far

Five modules, four pressures, and one window.

## The claim

Four pressures have to fit between the pore pressure and the fracture pressure, three of them are annular friction with a different velocity in them, and the mud weight moves all four together without changing the spread.

## What each module established

**Module 1.** Moving the string produces pressure with no pump involved. Surge and swab are the same pressure with opposite signs, symmetric about the static mud weight to the last digit. The displaced volume is the whole outside area for a closed string and only the steel for an open one, which is 0.2686329600000001 of it for this drill pipe, and the ratio of the two pressures is nevertheless only about 1.17 because the clinging term is present in both. The clinging constant is 0.45, it is empirical, and the pressure rises as roughly the 0.6 power of the trip speed rather than linearly.

**Module 2.** The window between the pore pressure and the fracture pressure has to hold the static mud weight, the equivalent circulating density, the surge and the swab. On the slant well those span 1400.0934648506984 to 1498.3349880149756 kg/m3, which is 98 kg/m3 of window used by ordinary operations. The speed limit is a bisection on a monotone response, and whichever of the two limits is closer to the mud weight is the binding one. Managed pressure drilling adds a fifth lever that works when the pumps are off, which is the only one that does.

**Module 3.** Losses, a kick on a trip, and a pack-off, with a table of signatures. Pit volume separates losses from a kick; standpipe pressure identifies a pack-off, because it is the only one of the three that raises it. The model warns about geometry coverage, a low transport ratio and a high cuttings concentration, and it cannot warn about any of the three events because it has no pore pressure and no fracture gradient.

**Module 4.** Mud weight moves all four pressures and cannot change the spread; the other five levers change the spread. Break circulation is the largest routine transient and it is uncomputable here because gel strength is not in the model. Managed pressure drilling, continuous circulation and dual gradient all attack the DIFFERENCE between the pressures rather than their level. And the trip sheet is the measurement that catches what the surge calculation only planned for.

**Module 5.** Five omissions: transients and acceleration, temperature and compressibility, gel strength, eccentricity and rotation, and cuttings beds. The acceleration term alone can be comparable to the steady surge pressure. Eccentricity is conservative for the pressure and optimistic for the cleaning, and rotation helps the cleaning enormously and is absent.

## The numbers to carry

- The clinging constant: 0.45.
- The closed-to-open pressure ratio on this well at 0.5 m/s: 1.172219719213269.
- Surge pressure against speed: an exponent of about 0.6, not 1.
- The steel fraction of this drill pipe's outside area: 0.2686329600000001.
- Surge and swab are symmetric about the mud weight, exactly.

## The one sentence

Everything in this course is annular friction with a different velocity in it, and the whole of well design is making four of them fit in one window.

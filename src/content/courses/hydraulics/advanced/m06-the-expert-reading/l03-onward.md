# Onward

Where the pressure comes from, and where it goes.

## What this course certified

The steady-state hydraulics of a specified method: rheology fitted from dial readings, pressure loss in every element of the flow path, the pump pressure and its split, the equivalent circulating density profile, a vertical-transport cuttings calculation, and the surge and swab pressures from moving the string.

## What it took as given

The pore pressure and the fracture pressure. Every comparison in the Expert tier was against limits this course does not compute.

Those limits are the geomechanics course, and they are the other half of the window.

## What it handed to the module

**To torque and drag:** the cuttings beds it cannot model are the largest single thing that course's friction factor absorbs. The two courses are looking at the same missing mechanism from opposite sides.

**To well control:** the swab that can bring a kick in, and the equivalent circulating density that has to be re-established after a shut-in.

**To casing design:** the surge pressures a casing run produces, which are the worst on any well because a casing string is closed and large.

## The five things it will not certify

Transients and acceleration. Temperature and compressibility. Gel strength and break circulation. Eccentricity and pipe rotation. Cuttings beds and inclination.

All five are taught, in Expert m05, and the go-live migration refuses to run if any graded field names one of them.

## Before you go

Three habits from this tier.

**Name the pressure.** Pump, bit, annulus, equivalent circulating density, surge and swab are six different numbers, and each has an audience.

**Use the peak, not the average.** A surge calculation given an average trip speed understates the peak, and the peak is what fractures the formation.

**Quote the model with the answer.** A hydraulics number without its method specification cannot be compared against another package's, and four of that specification's seven points are conventions rather than derivations.

## The one sentence

A hydraulics calculation is a set of pressures with no limits attached, and the whole skill is knowing which limit each one belongs to.

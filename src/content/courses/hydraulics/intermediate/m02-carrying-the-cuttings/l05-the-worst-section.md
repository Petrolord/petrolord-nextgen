# The worst section

Where to look, and why it is not where you expect.

{{panel:hy-cleaning-explorer}}

## The result

On every case in this course, the worst transport ratio and the worst cuttings concentration are in the SHALLOWEST annulus interval.

That interval is the cased section: 0.2204974 m of hole with 0.127 m drill pipe in it, which is the widest annulus in the well.

## Why

Velocity is flow rate over area, and that section has the largest area. At 0.025 m3/s it runs at 0.9797133674790398 m/s against 1.8487355891241068 m/s around the collars.

The slip velocity is a little lower there too, because the mud is less sheared and therefore thicker, but not enough to compensate.

## The general rule

The worst hole cleaning is in the largest open annulus, which is usually the shallowest part of the current hole section.

That is why the flow rate for a hole section is set by its TOP rather than its bottom, and why the flow rate falls as each successive smaller section is drilled.

## What that means for a real well

A 17 and a half inch surface hole needs an enormous flow rate to reach the same annular velocity a 8 and a half inch hole reaches easily. A rig's pump capacity is usually the limit in the big sections and never the limit in the small ones.

## The exception this course does not model

A cuttings bed in an inclined section.

In a hole above about 30 degrees, cuttings do not stay suspended: they settle to the low side and form a bed, which then has to be moved by a different mechanism entirely. That bed is not where the velocity is lowest; it is where the inclination is highest.

So the real worst section on a deviated well is often the build section or the tangent, and this model has nothing to say about it. The next module says so properly.

## Reading the panel

The transport view lists every interval with its velocity, its slip, its transport ratio and its concentration.

Read the whole table rather than the summary. The summary gives the worst value; the table gives its depth, which is what tells you what to do about it.

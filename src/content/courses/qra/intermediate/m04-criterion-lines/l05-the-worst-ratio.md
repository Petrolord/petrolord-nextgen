# The worst ratio

{{panel:qr-societal}}

A comparison with a criterion line produces a state for the whole curve and a ratio at every checked corner. A report needs one number to lead with, and the engine returns it: the worst ratio, the largest value of F over the line among the corners it checked, and the N at which it sits. This lesson reads the worst ratio for the JISIKE off-site curve and says what it tells a duty holder and what it does not.

## The JISIKE result

Against the Dutch line the engine returns state EXCEEDS, worst ratio 18.000000 at N = 300.000000.

| corner N | F(N) per year | line per year | ratio F / line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12.000000 | 0.000009700000 | 0.000006944444 | 1.396800 |
| 40.000000 | 0.000001700000 | 0.000000625000 | 2.720000 |
| 300.000000 | 0.000000200000 | 0.000000011111 | 18.000000 |

The worst corner is the one where only the toxic cloud over the town is counted: a stated frequency of 2e-7 per year for a stated N of 300. The line at N = 300 allows 0.000000011111 per year, and the curve sits 18.000000 times above it.

## What the number says

The worst ratio is the factor by which the curve would have to fall at its worst corner to meet the line. It points straight at the scenarios that decide the comparison. Here that is one scenario at the top of the curve, which tells the duty holder that reducing the toxic cloud's frequency, or the number it would kill, is the lever that matters most for this criterion. The corners at N = 12 and N = 40 exceed too, by much smaller factors.

## What the number does not say

The worst ratio says nothing about the corners below the line's range: the corner at N = 3 carries the curve's highest frequency, 0.000049700000 per year, and the Dutch line does not check it. It says nothing about the PLL either. The off-site expected fatalities are 0.000336000000 per year whichever corner is worst. And it is a statement about a comparison the analyst chose to make with a line from a repealed regulation, which the report should say.

## When the line changes

The worst ratio belongs to a criterion. With the same constants capped at N = 100, stated, the worst checked point is no longer at N = 300, which is out of range; the engine checks N = 100 itself and finds a ratio of 2.000000 there, while the worst ratio across that comparison is the 2.720000 at N = 40. A different criterion can move both the worst corner and its size, so a worst ratio is always quoted with the criterion that produced it.

## Six decimals

A ratio prints to six decimals in this course, and the engine's comparison reads the full value. Quote 18.000000 as printed, with its N, and with the line's name.

## Exercise

The curve's worst ratio against the Dutch line is 18.000000 at N = 300.000000. Compute the frequency the toxic cloud over the town would need for that corner's ratio to fall to 2.720000. Then recompute F(40) and F(12) with that frequency, remembering that the toxic cloud is counted at every corner up to 300, and say which corner would then carry the worst ratio.

# Writing the demonstration

{{panel:qr-alarp}}

The engine returns numbers and words. An ALARP demonstration is the document that turns them into a decision a reviewer can check. This course grades the Expert tier's reasoning in one shape of note, and this lesson walks through that shape with the figures a note on the EDIKAN firewall would carry.

## The shape

An ALARP note, in the shape this course grades at Expert, carries in order:

- the individual risk of the most exposed person and its region;
- the societal picture, if the population warrants it;
- each measure considered, with its deltaPLL, costs, life, VPF, other harms, discounting convention and DF, each with its source;
- the cost to benefit ratio and the verdict;
- the ICAF beside the VPF;
- the measures adopted, and the ones rejected as grossly disproportionate;
- what the engine did not know.

Each item answers a question a reviewer will ask. Leaving one out does not make the question go away; it only means the reviewer asks it later.

## The firewall, as a note would carry it

| item | EDIKAN firewall |
| --- | --- |
| deltaPLL, per year | 2e-3 |
| VPF | 1000000, 2001 prices, illustrative |
| life, years | 20 |
| capital at year 0, and annual cost | 250000, and 5000 |
| convention | the 2003 checklist limits, 0.015 and 0.035 |
| cost / benefit | 9.350247 |
| verdict at DF 3 | GROSSLY_DISPROPORTIONATE |
| ICAF | 8026550.41 |

The note states each input with its source. It gives the ratio to six decimals and money to two, as the engine returns them. It sets the ICAF of 8026550.41 beside the VPF of 1000000. And it shows the verdict at the other defensible choices: undiscounted at 8.750000 and under R2P2 at 9.328625, both grossly disproportionate at DF 3, with the undiscounted verdict turning to NOT_GROSSLY_DISPROPORTIONATE at DF 10.

## What the engine did not know

The last item is the one most often left out, and for the firewall it is short. The VPF is a 2001 illustrative figure, and no current HSE value was found. The DF is the duty holder's judgement. The deltaPLL came from scenarios whose probabilities of death were stated inputs from the consequence course. No published worked F-N example exists, so any societal figure is checked for self-consistency only. Each sentence tells a reviewer where the answer could move, and why.

## Adopted and rejected

A measure rejected as grossly disproportionate is still listed, with its verdict and the choices behind it, because the list of rejected measures is the evidence that the search was made. A measure adopted is listed with its cost and what it prevents. The note ends with the individual risk after the adopted measures, in its region, so a reader sees where the case left the person it started from.

## Exercise

Write the fourth and fifth items of the note for the firewall under the undiscounted convention, using the ratio of 8.750000, the verdict at DF 3 and the ICAF of 8750000.00 beside the VPF of 1000000. Then add one sentence showing the verdict at DF 10, and say what the note must justify if the duty holder relies on that row.

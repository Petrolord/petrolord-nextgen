# The capstone brief

{{panel:qr-alarp}}

The Expert capstone asks one question: is a further measure reasonably practicable? It describes an installation of its own and one or more measures, and it states every input you need. You work each measure through the ALARP and cost benefit panel and report what the engine returns. This lesson says what the capstone asks for, at what precision, and which traps it is built to catch, using only this tier's teaching figures as illustrations.

## What you are given

For each measure the capstone states the deltaPLL, the VPF and its year, the life in whole years, the capital at year 0, the annual cost, any other harms with their cases a year and value per case, the discounting convention as three rates, and the DF. Where a prompt gives a convention by name, it also gives its rates, typed as fractions such as 0.015 and 0.035.

## What you report

| quantity | precision | an illustration from this tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cost to benefit ratio | six decimals | 9.350247 |
| ICAF | two decimals | 8026550.41 |
| largest reasonably practicable cost | two decimals | 120000.00 |

For each stated measure you report the cost to benefit ratio, the ICAF and the largest reasonably practicable cost, under the stated convention and DF. Ratios are answered to six decimals and money to two, exactly as the engine prints them. A band, a state or a verdict word on its own is never a graded answer, though the verdict shapes the reasoning around your numbers.

## The traps it is built to catch

Dividing the cost by discounted fatalities gives a figure such as 9350246.71, which the engine never returns as an ICAF; the engine divides by the undiscounted count, 0.040000 for the firewall. Using a rounded print gives a figure such as 93000 where the engine computes 92835.00. Typing a rate as a percentage instead of a fraction gives a present value with no meaning. Forgetting the capital at year 0 drops most of a cost like the firewall's 350000.00.

## Working it

Open the panel, enter the stated inputs for one measure, and read the ratio, the ICAF and the largest reasonably practicable cost. Check the ratio against the DF by the strictly greater rule before you move on. Then change only what the prompt asks you to change, a convention or a DF, and read again. Copy every figure from the engine at its printed precision. The tolerance of each answer is set in one place in the platform, so your job is to report the engine's figure faithfully.

## Exercise

Take the firewall at the checklist limits and DF 3, with a ratio of 9.350247, an ICAF of 8026550.41 and a present value of the benefit of 34337.28. Write the three quantities the capstone would ask for in the form it expects, marking which one the example leaves out, and say in one sentence why you would read it from the engine instead of multiplying the rounded 34337.28 by the DF by hand.

# Mole percent into lbmol a day

A mole percent is an intensive property. It describes the gas and knows nothing about how much of it there is. This lesson is the step where the rate arrives and the answer stops being a property of the stream and becomes a property of the plant.

## Where the rate enters

UBIE is removed of 4.599600000 mol percent of acid gas. At 88.000000 MMscfd that is 10666.2009 lbmol a day into the solution.

Those two figures are everything the step is given. The route runs from the rate in standard cubic feet a day, through the fraction of those that are acid gas, and into pound moles by way of the standard cubic feet in a pound mole, which this module derives and exports as 379.483571856287.

## Why a pound mole and not a mass

Everything downstream of this point is about moles until the very last step of the chain. The amine picks acid gas up mole by mole, the loading is written as moles of acid gas per mole of amine, and the swing is a difference of two of those. A mass would have to be converted straight back.

That is the shape of the whole sweetening half and it is the thing to hold onto. The gas side of the balance produces a molar rate. The solvent side consumes a molar rate. The conversion to gallons happens once, at the end, and every figure before it is counted rather than weighed.

## The base the pound mole is defined at

A standard cubic foot has to be defined at a stated pressure and temperature or it means nothing, and this module states its own: 14.696000 psia and 519.670000 degR. The pound mole figure above is derived from the gas constant at that base rather than quoted from a table, and the module exports it so a reader can name it.

This is not a detail. A pound mole defined at one base and used at another produces an answer that is wrong by a small, constant and entirely invisible factor, and the acid gas rate carries that factor into every figure after it. Because the module derives the number from its own base, there is only one base in play and nothing to disagree with.

## Intensive and extensive, side by side

Change the rate and 4.599600000 mol percent does not move. Change the inlet or the spec and it does. The pound moles a day move with both. Keeping those two kinds of number apart is most of the skill in reading a sweetening answer, because a plant conversation slides between them constantly and the units are the only guard.

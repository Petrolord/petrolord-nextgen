# What the next tier changes

This tier read one column three ways. The Expert tier goes to the two parts of the plant that nothing else in the package answers for, and then turns round and audits the whole module.

## The cold end

There is a third way to take a hydrocarbon dew point off a gas, and it is not a solvent at all. Let the gas down across a valve and it cools, and what condenses is caught in a cold separator. That chain is the subject of the first three Expert modules.

It matters beyond this course. This is the only module in the whole package that computes a Joule-Thomson coefficient from a correlation. The Flow Assurance engine asks a caller to type one in and never computes one, so the Expert tier is where a learner finds out what number to type into the other app. Flow Assurance owns the coefficient as a subject and owns hydrates outright, and this course starts from the plant rather than re-teaching either.

## The overhead nobody sells

The glycol loop of the Associate tier carries something out of the still besides water. Aromatics absorbed in the contactor come back up in the reboiler overhead, and the Expert tier follows that mole balance from parts per million in the gas through to short tons in a year. It is the one answer this package gives that is about what leaves the plant rather than about what it costs to run it.

## The audit

Then the module is read as an object rather than as a set of answers. Which numbers were computed, which were chosen, which the module simply keeps to itself, and what separates a published case that could have caught an error from one that could not. That last question is the sharpest thing in the course, and it is why the Expert tier closes with what this method does not know rather than with a summary of what it does.

## What carries forward from here

Three habits go with you. Read the chosen inputs before the answer. Keep the independent readings of one object apart. Treat a refusal as the answer rather than as a gap to be filled.

Everything in the next tier is harder in the same way this tier was harder than the last one. The arithmetic is not more difficult. There are simply more places where a number can be the right answer to a question nobody asked.

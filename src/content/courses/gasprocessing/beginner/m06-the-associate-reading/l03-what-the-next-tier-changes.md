# What the next tier changes

Everything in this tier has been a chain that runs one way with no loops in it. The next tier is where that stops, and it is worth knowing what changes before meeting it.

## One column, read three different ways

The Associate tier treated the contactor as a place where water leaves the gas, and never asked how. The Professional tier asks. The same column can be read as a staged device, where the question is how many theoretical stages a removal demands and what a given column can actually reach. It can be read as a mole balance, where an acid gas is picked up by a solvent with a loading swing. And it can be read as a vessel, where the question is only how wide the steel has to be.

Those three readings do not derive from each other. The mole balance says how much solution has to move and says nothing about whether the spec is reachable. The stage relation says whether the spec is reachable and says nothing about gallons a minute. The vessel knows neither.

## A ceiling that steel cannot buy past

The sharpest new idea is that a column can have a limit which more trays do not reach. In the dehydration chain, tightening the spec always worked: the load rose, the circulation rose, the duty rose, and an answer came back. A staged column is not like that. Below a certain condition the removal has an upper bound however many stages are added, and the engine says the remedy is more solvent rather than more steel.

Nothing in this tier prepares you for that, because nothing in this tier had a ceiling in it. That is the point of meeting it separately.

## The other stream

UBIE is the sour stream this course carries alongside OBIAFU. It arrives at 5.200000 mol percent CO2 and 1.400000 mol percent H2S and has to leave at 2.000000 and 0.000400. As a mole balance that is 10666.2009 lbmol of acid gas a day going into the solution. The contactor it goes up is 4.208602 ft across.

Every figure there was reached by a route with nothing in common with the glycol chain. There is no water content, no circulation ratio, and the solvent is chosen from a property table rather than assumed.

## What stays

The discipline stays, and it is the whole reason this tier came first. Which number did the engine compute, which one did you choose, and which one did it keep to itself. Every tier of this course asks those three questions and only the answers change.

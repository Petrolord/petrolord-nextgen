# Where this engine hands over

Three courses meet at the edges of this one. Knowing the boundaries is the last thing this tier teaches, because a number handed across the wrong seam is wrong without ever being miscalculated.

## To Flow Assurance, in both directions

The Joule-Thomson coefficient and the product of it with a pressure drop belong to Flow Assurance, which owns them over a whole module. That engine takes a coefficient as a typed input from its caller and never forms one. This module is the only one in the package that computes one, so the traffic goes the other way as well.

Hydrates belong there too. Subcooling, the depression correlations and the inhibitor dose all live in that course, and it computes no hydrate boundary of its own. What this engine hands over is where the cold spot is and how much free water arrives at it, with the conditions that produced them.

## To Separation

Souders-Brown, the K value, the settling velocity and the mist extractor that sets it belong to the Separation and Slug Catching course, and this course does not re-derive any of them. The Professional tier teaches the contactor's own duty and cites that course for the equation.

## To Fluid

The phase envelope of a reservoir fluid is the Fluid engine, and so is the saturation pressure the word "dew point" means there. Nothing in this module performs a compositional flash, so the hydrocarbon that condenses at a cold spot is a question this engine hands on intact.

## The chart this module does not carry

One seam is internal. The outlet water content a dehydration unit is designed to is a typed design input. The dew point a given lean glycol strength can deliver is a chart reading, and no chart is in this module. The engine reports the loop balance and states that basis on every answer.

## What to hand over, and how

Hand over the number with the state it was formed at, the inputs that were chosen rather than computed, and any note the engine attached to it. A coefficient without its pressure and temperature, a water content without its warning, and a cooling without its step count are all the same failure: a conclusion passed on without the thing that qualifies it.

That is the habit this tier exists to build. Ask of every figure whether the engine computed it, whether you chose it, or whether it is a declared constant nothing here can check.

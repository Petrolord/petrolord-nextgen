# What drops out, and where

Two saturated water contents at two states are a pair of properties. The product of the unit is the difference between them, and where that difference physically goes.

{{panel:fc-coldend-explorer}}

## The difference is the product

AGBADA carries 33.801656743 lb of water per MMscf at its inlet and can hold 18.762820770 lb per MMscf at the cold separator. The difference is 15.038835973 lb per MMscf, and that is what appears as liquid in the separator boot.

That single subtraction is the whole unit, stated in one line. Everything upstream of it exists to reach a state at which there is a difference for the boot to catch.

## Where it goes

The liquid collects in the boot of the cold separator and is drawn off. The gas leaves the separator at the cold spot, saturated at that state, which is the point: it leaves carrying the second figure rather than the first.

That has a consequence a designer has to hold onto. Downstream of the separator the gas is saturated. Any further cooling anywhere in the system, in a line, across a control valve, in an exposed length of pipe, puts more liquid out. The skid moved the problem to a colder temperature rather than removing water in the absolute sense a molecular sieve or a glycol contactor removes it.

## Per MMscf, until somebody multiplies

The difference is per MMscf, exactly like the two contents it comes from. The separator sees that figure multiplied by whatever the stream carries, and only then is there a quantity of liquid an operator has to drain, a pump has to move and a disposal route has to accept.

Keeping the two apart is the same habit the Associate tier builds on the dehydration side. An intensive answer is a property of the gas. An extensive one is a property of the plant. The engine gives the first and the multiplication that makes the second is a step you take deliberately.

## What it does not tell you

It does not tell you whether that water will form a hydrate on the way to the boot, because there is no hydrate boundary in this engine. It does not tell you what hydrocarbon comes out with it, because there is no compositional flash. It does not tell you how well the separator actually separates, because this module models no vessel internals at the cold end at all.

What it tells you is how much free water arrives at a cold spot whose temperature you now know. That pair is what the next engine asks for.

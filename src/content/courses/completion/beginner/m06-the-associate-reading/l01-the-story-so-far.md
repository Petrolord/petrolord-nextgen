# The story so far

What the Associate tier established, in the order it established it.

## Five modules

A completion is the last string in the well, made of pipe and about nine kinds of equipment, and its design problem is geometric before it is anything else.

A drift is what fits, and it is the nominal bore less a deduction that depends only on the outside diameter. Four deduction values, three casing classes and one tubing class, with inclusive boundaries at eight and five eighths and thirteen and three eighths inches.

A stack up is a telescoping sum from the hanger down, and the bottom depth equals the hanger plus the sum of the lengths. It refuses three kinds of impossible input and computes everything else.

The equipment is mostly at the coupling diameter, with four exceptions that are wider and one that is narrower. Those four exceptions are what decides the size of a completion.

Two volumes come from the string alone, capacity and displacement, and their difference is the steel.

## What holds it together

One observation: every component carries two diameters, and the two answer different questions. The outside diameter decides what gets down. The inside diameter decides what gets through and how much it holds.

Almost every mistake in this subject is one of those two numbers being used for the other one's question.

## The published string

Thirteen components, hung from surface, two thousand six hundred and six metres to the bottom. Four tubing runs and nine pieces of equipment. A safety valve high, a side pocket mandrel deep, a packer near the bottom and a small tail below it.

That string reappears in every tier of this course, and by the end of the Expert tier you will have run six different calculations on it.

## What you can claim

That you can read a completion tally, compute a drift correctly including at the class boundaries, build and check a stack up, and compute the two string volumes.

Not yet that you can say whether the string goes in the hole.

## Exercise

Without looking, write down the four drift deduction values and the two class boundaries.

Then write down the three inputs the stack up refuses and, for each, the wrong answer it avoided.

Finally, in one sentence each, state what the outside diameter and the inside diameter of a component are for.

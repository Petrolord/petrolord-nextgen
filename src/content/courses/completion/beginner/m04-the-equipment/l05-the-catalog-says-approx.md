# The catalog says approx

Every equipment row in this course is flagged as approximate, and the flag is doing real work.

## What the flag means

The outside and inside diameters in the equipment catalog are representative values for the type and size. They are not any particular vendor's product, and they are not from any particular data sheet.

Every row carries a flag saying so. It is not decoration and it is not a disclaimer for legal purposes. It is an instruction about how the numbers may be used.

## Why the course uses representative numbers

Because the point being taught is structural. The safety valve is wider than the coupling because it houses a flapper and a piston, and that is true of every safety valve ever made. The side pocket mandrel is eccentric because it carries a valve beside the bore, and that is true of every side pocket mandrel.

A course built on one vendor's catalog would teach the same structure and would also teach forty nine specific numbers that are wrong for every other vendor. Flagging them as representative separates the part that transfers from the part that does not.

## What changes with a real data sheet

The magnitudes move. A slimline safety valve from one vendor may be half an inch narrower than the representative value, which is the difference between fitting a casing and not. Nipple bores vary by profile family. Packer outside diameters vary by rating and by whether the packer is retrievable.

What does not move is the ordering. The big bodies stay the big bodies, the pipe stays the easiest thing to run, and the nipple bores stay below the tubing bore.

## The right way to use this course on a real well

Take the method and replace the numbers. Every calculation here runs identically on real data: the drift arithmetic is a specification, the stack up is addition, the clearance is a subtraction against a bore, the through bore is a minimum and the volumes are integrals.

Substitute your vendor's dimensions into the same calculations and every result in this course reappears with different digits.

## What would be wrong

Quoting a number from this catalog in a design document. Ordering equipment against it. Telling a vendor their component is too wide because a training catalog said so.

The flag is on every row precisely so that none of those can happen by accident.

## Exercise

Pick three equipment types and write down what you would need from a vendor to replace the representative row with a real one.

Then say which of the results in this course would change and which would not if you did that for every row.

Finally, say what the flag would have to say if the numbers were real, and why no such flag exists.

# The big body items

Four components that decide what size completion a well can take.

{{panel:cd-string-explorer}}

## The four

In the three and a half inch kit, four types are wider than the coupling: the safety valve at five and three quarter inches, the side pocket mandrel at five and three quarter, the sliding sleeve at four point five six, and the expansion joint.

The packer belongs on the list too, at five and seven eighths inches, although it is sized to the casing rather than to the tubing.

## They are the answer to the size question

Put the three and a half inch kit into seven inch casing at any of the three weights in the catalog and everything fits. Put the same kit into five and a half inch casing at twenty pounds per foot and three items fail: the side pocket mandrel, the safety valve and the expansion joint.

The tubing itself fits with room to spare in both. So does every nipple, every flow coupling and the entry guide. The three that fail are three of the four big bodies, and they are the same three every time.

## Why this is the trap

A size discussion that starts from the tubing gets the wrong answer. Three and a half inch tubing has a coupling of four and a half inches, and four and a half inches goes into five and a half inch casing without difficulty. Somebody who checks the pipe concludes the completion fits.

It does not, because the completion is not the pipe. The check has to be run against the widest item in the kit, and the widest item is nearly two inches larger than the pipe body.

## What you do about it

Three options, and all three are real.

Drop a tubing size, which loses production. Use a slimline version of the offending component, which most vendors offer at a price and often with a reduced rating. Or leave the component out, which means no gas lift, or no safety valve where regulation permits, or no expansion joint and therefore a different way of handling movement.

None of them is free, and the choice is made once and lived with for the life of the well.

## The general statement

A completion is sized by its widest single component, and that component is never the pipe.

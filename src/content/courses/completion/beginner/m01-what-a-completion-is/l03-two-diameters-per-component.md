# Two diameters per component

Why every component in this course carries an outside diameter and an inside diameter, and why the two are used for completely different questions.

{{panel:cd-string-explorer}}

## Outside diameter answers one question

The outside diameter of a component decides whether it can be got down the hole. It is compared against the bore of the casing it has to pass through, and if it is larger, the string stops.

That is the only thing the outside diameter is for in this course. It is not a strength number, it is not a weight number, and it does not appear in a single volume.

## Inside diameter answers two

The inside diameter decides what can be got through the component afterwards, and it decides how much fluid the component holds.

Those are genuinely two different uses of one number. The through bore question takes the smallest inside diameter along the string and reports it. The volume question takes each inside diameter and multiplies by a length. The first is a minimum and the second is a sum, and a component can dominate one while being irrelevant to the other.

## The pair does not correlate

It is tempting to assume a big body means a big bore. It does not.

A safety valve in the published string is the widest thing above the packer at five and three quarter inches outside, and its bore is two and three quarter inches, which is narrower than the plain tubing above it. A perforated joint is the narrowest body on the sheet at three and a half inches outside, and its bore is the full tubing bore.

Two of the hardest components to get down the hole are also two of the hardest to get through, and one of the easiest to get down is completely transparent to a wireline tool. Knowing one diameter tells you very little about the other.

## Which one you are being asked about

When somebody says a completion is three and a half inch, they mean the tubing size, which is an outside diameter of the pipe. When somebody says the completion has a two and a half inch restriction, they mean an inside diameter of a nipple. When somebody says a five inch casing will not take that completion, they mean the casing inside diameter against the equipment outside diameter.

Three sentences, three different diameters, one habit of saying inches without saying which. The panel prints both columns side by side for every component so that the habit cannot mislead you here.

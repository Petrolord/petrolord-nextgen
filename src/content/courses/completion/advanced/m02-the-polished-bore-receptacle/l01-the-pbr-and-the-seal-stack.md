# The PBR and the seal stack

The hardware, in enough detail to make the arithmetic obvious.

{{panel:cd-spaceout-explorer}}

## The receptacle

A polished bore receptacle is a length of pipe with a precisely machined, honed internal bore. It is made up into the top of the packer or the liner top and it does not move.

Its length is the thing this tier cares about. The published job in this course uses six point one metres.

## The seal assembly

A mandrel carrying a stack of seal elements, made up on the bottom of the tubing string. It is stabbed into the receptacle and the seals bear against the polished bore.

The seals do not grip. They seal, and they slide. That is the whole design intent: pressure integrity without a mechanical connection.

## Insertion depth

How far into the receptacle the seal stack is landed. It is a choice made on the rig floor, executed by adding or removing pup joints in the string above.

Insertion is measured from the top of the receptacle to the seals. The published job lands three metres into a six point one metre bore, a little under halfway.

## What limits the travel

Upward movement of the string pulls the seals towards the top of the bore. They can travel the distance from where they are to the top, and beyond that they come out and the well loses integrity.

Downward movement pushes them towards the bottom. They can travel the distance from where they are to the bottom, and beyond that they hit the shoulder and the string starts loading up.

## The two directions are not the same distance

If the seals are three metres into a six point one metre bore, they have three point one metres above them and three below.

Those are different numbers and they are the available travel in the two directions. Choosing the insertion depth chooses how the six point one is divided between them, and that division is the entire design decision.

## Exercise

Draw the receptacle and the seal stack, and mark the insertion depth, the travel available upward and the travel available downward.

Then say which of the two a string lengthening consumes, and which a string shortening consumes.

# What the engine will not decide

Six choices this course computes the consequences of and does not make.

## The warn margin

The engine takes it as an input. Three millimetres on clearance and half a metre on space out are the values used here, and neither is a specification.

An operator with a scaling problem should use more. One with a brand new well and a drift run in hand could justify less. The engine will report against whatever you give it and will not tell you what to give it.

## The design case list

Which length changes to design against is a judgement about what the well will see over its life. Include a stimulation that never happens and you have overspent. Exclude one that does and you have lost integrity.

The engine takes the cases it is given.

## Where in the band to land

The band is computed. The point within it is a choice, informed by which failure is worse, which case is better known, and how precisely the space out can be executed.

## Which component to move

When a clearance is tight, the fix is to move the component, to slim it, or to accept it. All three are real and the choice depends on operational value that is not in this model.

## The tubing size

The size matrix says what fits. Which size to run depends on production rate, on lift, on future intervention plans and on cost.

## Total depth and packer depth

Both are inputs here, and both are set by the reservoir.

## Why the line is drawn here

Every one of the six needs information the model does not have: an operator's risk appetite, a field development plan, a production forecast, a cost. A tool that made these choices would be making them from assumptions it invented.

Computing the consequence of a choice precisely and leaving the choice to the reader is the more useful division, and it is the one that keeps the numbers trustworthy.

## Exercise

List the six choices and, for each, name the information that would be needed to make it.

Then pick the one you think a tool could most defensibly automate, and say what it would need.

Finally, say what would be lost if the engine picked a default for all six and reported a single answer.

# What this course will not certify

The claim the Expert certificate makes, stated precisely.

## What it certifies

That you can take a component list and a casing program and produce a complete, correct geometric assessment of a completion: drifts, a stack up, an exposed profile, run in clearances with verdicts, a through bore with its extents, four volumes, and a seal space out with a band and a minimum bore length.

That you can read a tool's summary output critically enough to check it against its own detail, and that you know why that matters.

And that you know where each of those results stops being valid.

## What it does not certify

That you can design a completion. Sizing, equipment selection, materials, ratings, elastomers, artificial lift and intervention planning are all outside this course, and a competent completion designer has all of them.

That you can compute a length change. This course consumes them.

That you can run a completion. Running is a mechanics problem in a deviated hole and there is no survey here.

That any number in this course is quotable. The equipment catalog is representative and flagged as such on every row.

## Why the narrow claim

Because a certificate that claimed completion design competence on the strength of a geometry course would be worth nothing, and would be worse than nothing to somebody relying on it.

The geometry is a real and separable skill, it is the constraint that binds first, and it is frequently done badly. Certifying it precisely is useful.

## What to do with it

Use it as the geometric half of a completion review. Bring the ratings, the materials and the operational judgement from elsewhere, and bring this to the question of whether the thing goes in the hole, what gets through it, and where the seals land.

## The last word on the engine

This course found a defect in the engine it teaches, in an output that every user reads and no test checked. The fix and its guards are in the engine and in the course.

That is what a course is for, as much as the teaching: running a model hard enough, against an independent oracle, to find where it is wrong.

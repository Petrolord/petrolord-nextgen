# The safety valve and the no-go

The two components that set the through-bore in the published string, and how differently they behave.

{{panel:cd-clearance-explorer}}

## The safety valve

At one hundred and fifty point nine metres the string enters the safety valve, whose bore is two point seven five inches, below the tubing bore of two point nine nine two.

From that point on, the through bore of everything below is two point seven five, and it stays there for a very long way.

## The no-go

At two thousand six hundred and two point five metres the string enters the XN no go nipple, whose bore is two point six three five inches.

From that point the through bore is two point six three five. Below the nipple there is a perforated joint and an entry guide, about three and a quarter metres of string.

## The extents

The safety valve is the restriction over about two thousand four hundred and fifty metres of string. The no go nipple is the restriction over about three and three quarter metres.

The completion is advertised at two point six three five inches, which is the no go value, which governs about a tenth of a percent of the string.

## Two components, two roles

The safety valve's restriction is a side effect. Nobody wanted a two point seven five inch bore; it is what fitting a flapper and a hydraulic piston into a five and three quarter inch body leaves.

The no go's restriction is the entire purpose of the component. A no go nipple whose bore was not smaller than everything above it would not stop anything, which is what it is for.

So one of the two restrictions is a cost and the other is a feature, and the arithmetic treats them identically.

## What a designer does with that

Reducing the safety valve restriction is a real design option: a larger bore valve exists, at a larger outside diameter, which the clearance check may or may not allow. That is a genuine trade between the two checks in this tier.

Reducing the no go restriction is not an option, because it would defeat the component. If the no go bore is a problem, the answer is to move the nipple or to leave it out, not to make it bigger.

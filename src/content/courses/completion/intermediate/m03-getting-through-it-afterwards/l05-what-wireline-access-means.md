# What wireline access means

Turning a bore number into a statement about what can actually be done in the well.

{{panel:cd-clearance-explorer}}

## The bore is necessary, not sufficient

A tool smaller than the through bore may still not run. Length matters: a long rigid tool string in a deviated well needs more room than its diameter suggests, for the same reason a long drift mandrel is a harder test than a short one.

Deviation matters: below about seventy degrees, gravity stops delivering the tool and it has to be pushed, on coiled tubing or with a tractor.

Fluid matters: a tool has to fall through what is in the tubing, and a heavy or viscous packer fluid slows it or stops it.

## What the bore does tell you

It tells you the hard limit. Nothing larger than the running minimum at a depth can reach that depth, whatever else is favourable.

So the through bore rules things out with certainty and rules nothing in. That is a useful thing for a calculation to do, provided the reader knows which of the two it is doing.

## Sizes worth carrying

Memory or gauge carriers are commonly around one and a half to two inches. Standard shifting tools and lock mandrels are sized to their nipple profile. Production logging tools run in the same range. Coiled tubing for cleanout in a two and seven eighths or three and a half inch completion is typically one and a quarter to one and three quarter inches.

Against a through bore of two point six three five inches all of those fit. Against a through bore of one and three quarter inches, which a badly chosen nipple could produce, several do not.

## The interaction with the no-go

A no go nipple is chosen so that the tools intended to pass it can, and the tools intended to stop do. That is a size selection made against a tool list, not against a rule of thumb.

Getting it wrong in one direction means the plug does not seat. Getting it wrong in the other means the string is closed to a tool somebody needed.

## What this course can and cannot say

It can compute the bore and name the restriction and its extent. It cannot say whether a given tool will run, because it has no survey, no fluid and no tool length.

Stating that boundary is the honest end of this module.

# The story so far

What the Professional tier established.

## Five modules

The casing program became an object. Overlapping strings resolve to an exposed profile by taking the innermost bore, cut where things change, merged where they do not, and returning nothing rather than a guess across a gap.

Run in clearance compares each component against the governing drift to its depth, which is the smallest drift anywhere above it. Three verdicts, a stated margin, and the demonstration that the same component gets different answers at different depths.

The through bore is a running minimum from the top, it can only step down, and it names the restriction and how much of the string that restriction governs.

Four volumes, two of them from the string alone and two from the string against the casing, integrated exactly on merged breakpoints, with a warning rather than a refusal when the profile has a hole.

And a real defect in the engine, its cause, why nothing caught it and what the fix asserts.

## The idea that runs through it

Both fit questions are questions about a path rather than a point. Getting down depends on everything above the destination. Getting through depends on everything above the target depth inside the string.

Every surprising result in this tier is that idea applied: a valve and a mandrel of the same size with clearances differing ninefold, a restriction that governs a tenth of a percent of the string, an oversized component that passes at one depth and fails two hundred metres lower.

## What you can claim

That you can build an exposed casing profile, compute a governing drift, run and read a clearance check with a margin, compute and interpret a through bore with its extents, and integrate the four volumes correctly.

And that you can read a tool's summary output critically enough to check it against its own detail.

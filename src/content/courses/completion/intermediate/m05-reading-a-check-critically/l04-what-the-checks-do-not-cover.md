# What the checks do not cover

The honest boundary of the Professional tier.

## What is now covered

Whether every component clears the casing above it, with three verdicts and a stated margin. What can be run through the finished string, where the restrictions are and how much of the string each governs. Four volumes, integrated exactly, with a warning when the program is incomplete.

That is a complete geometric assessment of a completion in a cased hole.

## Not covered: running mechanics

Nothing here says whether the string can be pushed to depth. A deviated well takes hook load to run into, and below a certain inclination the string will not go under its own weight.

Nothing here computes drag, and nothing here knows the survey. A completion that fits perfectly can be undeliverable.

## Not covered: doglegs

The clearance check treats each component as a point at a depth. A rigid component of appreciable length going through a bend needs more room than its diameter, by an amount that grows with the square of its length and with the curvature.

A three metre packer through a five degree per thirty metre dogleg is a different fit problem from the same packer in a straight hole, and this calculation does not see the difference.

## Not covered: what the well has become

Scale, wax, corrosion product, a collapsed section, a fish, a wireline in the hole. The casing program describes what was run. Any of those makes the real bore smaller.

The margin is the acknowledgement of this and it is not a substitute for a drift run.

## Not covered: everything that is not geometry

Ratings, materials, elastomer compatibility, temperature, corrosive service, and whether the equipment does what the well needs. A perfectly fitting completion made of the wrong steel is a bad completion.

## What the tier does claim

That the geometry is right, exactly, on the data given. That is a narrow claim and a strong one, and it is worth more than a wide claim that is only approximately supported.

## Exercise

List five things the Professional tier does not cover and, for each, name the input that would be needed.

Then pick the one you think is most likely to bite on a real well, and say why.

Finally, state in one sentence what the tier does claim.

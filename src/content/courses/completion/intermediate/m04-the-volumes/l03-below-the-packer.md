# Below the packer

The smallest of the four, the simplest to compute, and the one whose definition needs stating carefully.

{{panel:cd-clearance-explorer}}

## The definition

From the packer depth to total depth, the full inside area of the casing, integrated.

Not the annular area. The full bore.

## Why the full bore

Because below the packer there is no sealed annulus. The tail of the string is open at the bottom through the entry guide and perforated at the perforated joint, so the fluid inside the tail and the fluid around it are the same body of fluid in communication.

Treating it as an annulus would exclude the volume inside the tail, which is not excluded from anything physical.

## What that means for the number

The published well has about seven point seven cubic metres below the packer, over four hundred metres of liner from the packer to total depth.

The tail of the completion occupies part of that space and the calculation does not subtract it. That is the intended behaviour and it should be understood as a gross volume rather than a net one.

## Where the gross number is right

On a kill sheet. When the well is killed, the fluid has to fill everything below the packer, and everything means everything, including the inside of the tail.

In a reservoir communication question. What is below the packer is what the reservoir sees.

## Where it would be wrong

If you were computing how much fluid a specific circulation path holds, you would want the path and not the gross volume, because a circulation down the tubing and back up outside the tail uses two areas rather than one.

This engine does not compute circulation paths, so the question does not arise here. It would arise immediately in a coiled tubing cleanout design, which is a different calculation in a different tool.

## The refusals

The packer must be at or above total depth, and total depth must be at or below the packer. Those are the same condition stated twice, and the engine states it twice because the two errors come from different places: a mistyped packer depth and a mistyped total depth.

Naming which one is out of range saves the reader from checking both.

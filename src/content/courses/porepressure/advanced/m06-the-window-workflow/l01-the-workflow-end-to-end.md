Bowers, both curves. Forward QC at known-pressure depths; here, the fitted loading curve sits 1.408 m/s under the TD sonic. Backward at TD: 47.37067524651197 MPa against Eaton's 47.408579625, agreement 0.038 MPa. Mechanism screen: no velocity reversal, no sonic-density divergence, no unloading candidate, so the loading reading stands and no unloading correction is applied; had the screens fired, sigma_max and U would enter with their provenance and the unloading pore pressure would be carried beside the loading one.

## Step 12: report

The deliverable, four clauses per key depth, as module 5 drafted: value with provenance, cross-check with size, mechanism with evidence, mud units with datum. Plus the sensitivity ledger, trend, exponent, K, each in kg/m3 at the section boundaries, and the named triggers for revision. One page, every number auditable.

## The order, again, is the content

Each new step consumes the previous. Conversion needs the prognosis; the window needs both walls converted; pricing needs the window to price against; the cross-check needs a mechanism claim to check; the report needs all of it. The workflow is a dependency chain, not a menu, and reviews that jump straight to the report work backward down exactly this chain until they find the first step that cannot defend itself.

## Exercise

A reviewer receives that six-line summary and is allowed one question per line. Write the toughest question for line five, and answer it from the course.

Self check: the toughest line-five question is some form of: your throw assumes the true exponent is one of your two presets, so what happens if the RFT endorses neither? The course's answer: the presets bracket rather than exhaust, the refit protocol fits n to the measurement, module 3's worked refit landed at 1.46 from a 42.0 MPa point, and everything downstream reruns from the fitted value; the 87.22 throw is not a claim about which n is true, it is the price of not yet knowing, quoted in mud units so the plan can be checked for robustness against it, which this plan was.

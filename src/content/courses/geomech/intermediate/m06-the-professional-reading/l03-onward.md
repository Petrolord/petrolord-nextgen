# Onward

What this tier certified, and what the last one adds.

## What this tier certified

Wellbore stability at a point: rotating a far-field stress tensor through geographic into the borehole frame, evaluating the Kirsch wall stresses round the circumference, and applying two failure criteria to bound the mud weight above and below.

Plus the closed forms for the vertical case, which is the check on all of it.

## What it took from the Associate tier

Four stresses and a strength at every depth. Nothing else, and nothing new was estimated here: this tier is pure mechanics on the previous tier's model.

That is why an error in the mechanical earth model propagates silently through everything in this tier. The stability calculation cannot tell you that its inputs were wrong.

## What it hands to the Expert tier

A stability answer at ONE depth for ONE hole attitude. The Expert tier walks it along a whole trajectory, which means evaluating it at every step and finding where the window is tightest.

That walk is where the two wells in this course finally separate, and it produces a result the lessons here could not: the tightest point on a well is not always at total depth.

## Three habits from this tier

**Compute the vertical closed form as a check.** It costs two lines and it catches conventions, signs and unit errors on any near-vertical hole.

**Ask which bound is binding and which candidate produced the lower one.** Collapse and pore pressure have different remedies, and a maximum of the two hides which.

**Treat a collapse gradient as a zero-breakout-width number.** It is the pressure at which a breakout begins, not the pressure at which the hole fails, and the difference is large.

## What the Expert tier adds

The trajectory. Sensitivity to the inputs nobody measured. The comparison against kick tolerance and equivalent circulating density from the other drilling courses. And a full statement of what this model does not contain, which is a longer list than any tier so far.

## The one sentence

A hole is a stress raiser, the mud is the only lever, and the two bounds on it are a shear failure at the hoop peak and a tension failure at the hoop dip.

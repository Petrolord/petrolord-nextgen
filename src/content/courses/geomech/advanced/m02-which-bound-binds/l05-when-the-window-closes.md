# When the window closes

The case neither well in this course reaches, and what it means.

{{panel:gm-window-explorer}}

## The condition

The lower bound exceeds the upper bound. No mud weight both holds the hole up and keeps it from splitting.

    max(pore pressure, collapse) > fracture initiation

## What the engine does

Records the shallowest measured depth at which it happens, and returns a warning naming that depth.

Neither well in this course triggers it, at either parameter set. That is worth knowing: every result in this course comes from an open window, and the closure logic is exercised by the code path rather than by these fixtures.

## The two ways a window closes

**From below.** The pore pressure climbs into the fracture gradient. That is the classic narrow-margin deepwater or overpressured setting, and it ends the section.

**From below, differently.** The collapse gradient climbs into the fracture gradient. That is a weak rock in a strongly anisotropic stress field, and it is the case that geomechanics rather than pore pressure identifies.

## What it means when it is real

The section cannot be drilled conventionally to that depth.

The options are all specialist: managed pressure drilling to hold a precise bottom hole pressure, casing while drilling, an expandable liner, or wellbore strengthening to raise the effective fracture gradient.

Every one of them is expensive, and the value of predicting the closure is that the decision gets made at the planning stage rather than at three in the morning.

## What it means when it is not real

Which is often. A closed window in a model is the model's statement, and the model has a large uncertainty.

Before believing it, check: is the collapse gradient at zero breakout width, and would a tolerated breakout open the window? Is the upper bound fracture initiation, and would using the minimum horizontal stress instead make it worse or better? Are the horizontal stresses clamped at that depth?

A closed window with any of those unexamined is not yet a finding.

## The false closure to watch for

A window that closes only in the top 1150 m of a profile whose stress ordering is broken there, which is the Associate tier's finding on this fixture.

Closure inside a region the model has already flagged as inconsistent is a symptom of the inconsistency rather than a prediction about the well.

## What to do about a genuine one

Case above it. That is the same answer as the narrowing case and it is usually the right one: set a shoe before the window closes and drill on in a smaller hole with a heavier mud.

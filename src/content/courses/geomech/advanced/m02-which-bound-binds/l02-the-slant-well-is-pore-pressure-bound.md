# The slant well is pore pressure bound

The deep case, and what it implies.

{{panel:gm-window-explorer}}

## At the tightest point

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| measured depth | 3000 m |
| pore pressure | 1178.7010484607424 kg/m3 |
| collapse | 405.14894482488035 kg/m3 |
| lower bound | 1178.7010484607424 kg/m3 |
| fracture initiation | 2220.161092149701 kg/m3 |

The pore pressure exceeds the collapse pressure by 773.5521036358621 kg/m3. It is not close.

## Why the collapse pressure is so low

Because the rock at 2507.9196993011733 m of true vertical depth is strong. Its UCS interpolates to well over 100 MPa, and a rock that strong tolerates almost any hoop stress the stress field can produce.

The collapse gradient of 405 kg/m3 is below the density of water. In practical terms the collapse criterion is not a constraint at all there.

## Why the pore pressure is high

The ramp. From 1500 m down, the pore pressure gradient climbs steadily, and by the tightest point it has reached 1178.7010484607424 kg/m3.

## The consequence for the mud programme

The mud weight at total depth is set by well control, not by stability. The floor is the pore pressure plus a trip margin, and the geomechanics contribution at that depth is the CEILING alone.

## The consequence for the whole section

Look at the trend. Over the last 150 m of hole:

| measured depth | pore pressure | collapse | window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2880 m | 1170.2809212714658 | 566.8052354121534 | 1062.8072915650192 |
| 2910 m | 1172.4454572745947 | 529.8299894346842 | 1057.3196831945167 |
| 2940 m | 1174.5695832115423 | 490.3112110701368 | 1051.934482100012 |
| 2970 m | 1176.6544202495918 | 448.57130866100954 | 1046.6488477439118 |
| 3000 m | 1178.7010484607424 | 405.14894482488035 | 1041.4600436889584 |

The pore pressure is rising and the collapse pressure is FALLING. The gap between them is widening with every metre, so the pore pressure wins by more the deeper you go.

## What is closing the window then

The fracture gradient. It falls from 2233.088212836485 to 2220.161092149701 kg/m3 over the same interval while the pore pressure rises, and the window narrows from both sides at once.

## The remedy

A casing point. The window is narrowing steadily and monotonically, and the only fix for a narrowing pore-pressure-driven window is to case off the section and drill on with a heavier mud in a smaller hole.

That is the same conclusion the well control course reached from the kick tolerance side, and the two calculations should be compared before the shoe depth is fixed.

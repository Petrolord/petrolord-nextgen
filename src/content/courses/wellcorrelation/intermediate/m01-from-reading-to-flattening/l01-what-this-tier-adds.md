# What this tier adds

The Associate tier left you able to read the Ekene section. You could pick a top and name it consistently, hang four wells at true measured depth, rank a surface from shallowest to deepest, and quote structural relief with the well count attached to it. You could also flatten a section, and you knew the rule that goes with it: flattening changes the display and never the data. That is reading a section, and reading is the right first skill.

This tier asks a different question of the same four wells.

## Two questions, not one

Reading a section answers the question of what is there. Where does each surface sit, which well is structurally highest, how far does a surface fall from the crest to the flank. Every one of those answers is a depth statement about the section as it stands now, after everything that has happened to it since the rocks were laid down.

Flattening asks what the section looked like when it was deposited. That is a question about a moment in the past, and it has a different answer, because the shape the section has now is not the shape it had then. Between then and now the structure moved. Some of that movement happened after deposition finished and some of it happened while deposition was still going on, and separating those two is the work of this tier.

Put the two side by side and the distinction is clean. The structural view is a statement about position. The flattened view is a statement about accumulation. The first is read in depths, the second is read in thicknesses, and the second cannot be read at all while the first is dominating the panel.

The Associate tier flattened a section in order to look at it. This tier flattens a section in order to measure it. That is the whole step up, and it is larger than it sounds, because a measurement has to survive being written down, quoted in a report, and argued with by somebody who was not in the room.

## What carries forward

Four things from the Associate tier are assumed here and are not revised.

The tops table is the record. Ekene's picks are what they are, and nothing at this tier edits one.

A shift is one number per well. Flattening produces a single additive shift for each well, and every drawing routine adds that shift to a measured depth at the moment of drawing.

Displayed depth is not measured depth. The moment a datum is set, every surface has two numbers against it, and you name which one you are quoting every time you quote one.

Absence is not zero. Ekene-4 carries no TOP_B, and no calculation at this tier fills that gap.

If any of those four feels loose, go back to the Associate tier before continuing. Everything below treats them as settled.

## What this tier measures

The reading this tier produces is growth: the way an interval thickens and thins across a section once the structural component has been taken out of the display.

The interval this tier works is the one between TOP_A and TOP_SAND. Across the four Ekene wells it measures 48, 53, 46 and 60 m. Those are not four attempts at one thickness with noise on them. They are four different thicknesses, and the difference between the largest and the smallest is 14 m. A section whose intervals all matched would be a layer cake, deposited on a floor that was not moving while it accumulated. Ekene is not that, and the 14 m is the size of the departure.

Getting to that number takes a chosen flattening top, a chosen datum depth, a shift for every well, and the discipline to keep displayed depths and measured depths apart while you read across the panel. Those are the modules between here and the growth number.

## What this tier does not do

Ekene-4 has three tops rather than four, because the well reached total depth above TOP_B. That gap is visible at this tier, it is counted at this tier, and it is respected at this tier. It is not filled at this tier.

Estimating where TOP_B would have been in Ekene-4 is the Expert tier's work, and it takes the growth numbers you are about to measure as its input. Predicting a missing pick before you can measure the growth that governs it is guessing with extra steps. So when the temptation arrives, and it arrives the first time you watch the TOP_B correlation line stop short of the fourth well, the correct move at this tier is to count three wells and say three.

## The register this tier expects

Three habits separate a professional reading from a competent one, and they belong at the start rather than at the end.

State the view with every number. A depth quoted without saying whether it is measured or displayed is not a finished statement, and a displayed depth quoted without its flattening top and its datum cannot be checked by anybody else.

Carry the sample size. A number computed over three wells stays a three-well number in every place it appears, including the places where it sits beside four-well numbers and looks comparable to them.

Reproduce the arithmetic by hand. The section engine at this tier is exact closed-form arithmetic, one subtraction per well and one addition per depth. There is nothing in it you cannot check on paper, which means there is no reading at this tier that you are entitled to accept without checking.

## Exercise

Write down, in your own words, the difference between the question the structural section answers and the question a flattened section answers. Then, without looking anything up, say which of the two views you would use to choose a drilling target and which you would use to argue that an interval thickens toward one side of the field. Finally, name the one thing this tier deliberately refuses to do with Ekene-4's missing TOP_B, and say which tier does it instead.

Self-check: the structural section answers where each surface is now, in real depth, and that is the view a drilling target comes off. The flattened section answers how the intervals compare once the structural component has been removed, and that is the view a thickening argument comes off. This tier refuses to estimate the missing TOP_B in Ekene-4. It counts the three wells that carry all four tops and leaves the gap open and visible. The Expert tier makes the estimate, and it uses the growth measured here to do it.

# Onward

The Professional tier spent against one authorisation. The Expert tier goes back to the portfolio with a simulation and a correlation, returns to the AFE with the partners who share its bill, and ends on the printed numbers that deserve suspicion.

## Portfolio risk by simulation

The portfolio engine's risk summary is a seeded Monte Carlo, at a default seed of 20260829 and 10000 iterations, drawing each project's success or failure and then its success spread. Before EC5-0 the engine used a normal approximation, and it failed small risked portfolios. On a single wildcat the exact probability of loss is 0.700000. The old approximation said 0.365832 and the repaired engine says 0.696100. The old P90 NPV of -150.5560 million USD lay below the worst outcome the wildcat can reach, -50.0000.

## Correlation

The engine carries one average correlation for the whole portfolio. On OKONO's funded set at a limit of 600.0000 million USD, the standard deviation of NPV is 143.8374 at a correlation of 0 and 239.8888 at 1, while the risked EMV stays at 402.7500 throughout. The probability of loss climbs from 0.001800 to 0.059000. Correlation widens the spread and never moves the mean.

## Joint venture shares

An AFE is billed by working interest. OFON-1's three partners hold 40.0000, 22.5000 and 12.5000 percent, 75.0000 in all, and the operator carries 25.0000 percent, a share of 6762500 of the 27050000 budget. Partner interests that total more than the whole leave the operator a negative share, and the engine marks that split invalid with a note. Before EC5-0 the AFE summary PDF billed two invented partners whatever had been saved.

## Numbers to distrust

CPI reads 1.000000 whenever actuals are 0, even beside OFON-1's earned value of 15231500. An invoice with a null date counts from 1970 in every bucket. OFON-1's plan stops 2597517 short of its budget. On OKONO's 450.0000 set the probability of loss reads 0.114000 at 1000 iterations and 0.123600 at 10000. A seed buys a reproducible number and iterations buy precision; neither buys a correct model of the projects.

## What to carry

Three habits survive the move. Write the as-of date beside every schedule number. Name the rule behind every forecast. Read every earned value as the progress somebody typed.

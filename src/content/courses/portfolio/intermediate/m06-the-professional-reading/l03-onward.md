# Onward

The Professional tier spent against one authorisation. The Expert tier goes back to the portfolio with a simulation and a correlation, returns to the AFE with the partners who share its bill, and ends on the printed numbers that deserve suspicion.

## Portfolio risk by simulation

The portfolio engine's risk summary is a seeded Monte Carlo, at a default seed of 20260829 and 10000 iterations, drawing each project's success or failure and then its success spread. The Expert tier sets it beside a normal approximation of the summed NPV, a method that misleads on small risked portfolios. On a single wildcat the exact probability of loss is 0.700000. The normal approximation says 0.365832 and the simulation says 0.696100. The normal P90 NPV of -150.5560 million USD lies below the worst outcome the wildcat can reach, -50.0000.

## Correlation

The engine carries one average correlation for the whole portfolio. On OKONO's funded set at a limit of 600.0000 million USD, the standard deviation of NPV is 143.8374 at a correlation of 0 and 239.8888 at 1, while the risked EMV stays at 402.7500 throughout. The probability of loss climbs from 0.001800 to 0.059000. Correlation widens the spread and never moves the mean.

## Joint venture shares

An AFE is billed by working interest. OFON-1's three partners hold 40.0000, 22.5000 and 12.5000 percent, 75.0000 in all, and the operator carries 25.0000 percent, a share of 6762500 of the 27050000 budget. Partner interests that total more than the whole leave the operator a negative share, and the engine marks that split invalid with a note. The AFE summary PDF bills the AFE's saved partners and prints that note when the split is invalid.

## Numbers to distrust

OFON-1 with every actual set to 0 reports CPI null, cpiStatus "no-spend", beside an earned value of 15231500: value earned with nothing spent has no cost efficiency to report. An invoice the engine cannot date is kept off the S-curve and counted beside it, so the published undated case reads an Actual of 0 at its first point and its last, with 2 undated invoices named. OFON-1's last monthly point plans 24452483, and only the closing point carries the budget of 27050000 and the EAC of 27600000. On OKONO's 450.0000 set the probability of loss reads 0.114000 at 1000 iterations and 0.123600 at 10000. A seed buys a reproducible number and iterations buy precision; neither buys a correct model of the projects.

## What to carry

Three habits survive the move. Write the as-of date beside every schedule number. Name the rule behind every forecast. Read every earned value as the progress somebody typed.

# The story so far

One fluid, four properties, and a question asked of each of them.

## The question

Where did this number come from. The tier answers it: measured, armed, oracle gated, lab tuned, published method, or screening. Ekene's fluid is none of those, because it was designed, and that is its own kind of answer and it belongs in the report.

## The bubble point

Standing at 32 API, 0.75 gas gravity, 180 F and the designed 400 scf/stb:

$$1912.1923059028293 \text{ psia}$$

against a designed 2000. Run it the other way and it returns 421.94 scf/stb against a designed 400. Same disagreement, twice.

Three correlations disagree with each other by hundreds of psia on this fluid, because Standing was fitted to Californian oils, Vasquez-Beggs to a worldwide set and Glaso to North Sea oils. The spread is the useful output. Do not average it.

## Volume

Standing's Bo at the designed 400 scf/stb:

$$1.2407824121407645 \text{ rb/stb}$$

against a designed 1.2, about 3.4 percent high. That is a 3.4 percent question over every volume the field books, sixty times the residual the simulation course worked to close on its structure.

Bo peaks at the bubble point. Above it the oil is being compressed and Bo falls; below it gas is leaving and Bo falls. A table without that peak has lost its bubble point row.

## Viscosity

Three correlations in series. Beal gives a dead oil of

$$2.3437444714709295 \text{ cp}$$

Beggs and Robinson take it to 0.7559673199800581 cp at 400 scf/stb, a factor of three. Vasquez and Beggs take that to 0.8035947954460412 cp at 2600 psia, six percent back the other way.

Against a designed 1.8 cp, the correlated chain is out by a factor of 2.4. The volume properties agreed to a few percent and the transport property does not agree at all, which is the normal pattern and the reason to measure viscosity if you measure anything.

## What this tier established

**Correlations are fits to somebody else's samples.** Standing had 105 bubble points from 22 Californian mixtures. That is not a criticism, it is the definition, and it is why three of them disagree.

**Every number is the end of a chain.** A viscosity is three correlations composed. A gas viscosity is five steps from a measured gas gravity. Quoting the endpoint without the chain says very little.

**The engine states its own limits.** Every correlation carries the range it was fitted over, and the warning names the correlation and the bound. Ekene as it stands raises none.

**Precision is not accuracy.** Sixteen digits from a correlation fitted to a hundred measurements is arithmetic, not knowledge.

# The story so far

Three tiers, one field. The Associate tier established that the flood replaces its voidage. The Professional tier showed that the field average hides two elements needing opposite corrections. This tier asked what the flood is actually sweeping, and answered with a number that changes the reading of both tiers below it.

## Heterogeneity

The Ekene sand is modelled as five non-communicating layers whose permeabilities are planted on an exact log-normal, so the engine's own fit recovers the design:

$$V = 0.5, \qquad \sigma = \ln 2 = 0.6931471805599453, \qquad k_{50} = 250.0000000000001$$

$V = 0.5$ is a moderately heterogeneous sand. The trailing digit on $k_{50}$ is the least squares round trip through an exponential and should be reported as 250.

The column is given in depth order and floods in permeability order, and the fastest layer is second from the top, not at the top.

Half the column is contacted when the first layer breaks through. Reaching 83 percent costs a reservoir water oil ratio above five. The two methods agree exactly at $M = 1$ and Dykstra-Parsons is 1.4 coverage points lower at $M = 1.2$.

## Areal sweep

The Craig five-spot correlation at $M = 1.2$ gives $E_{Abt} = 0.6573574366303985$, and the Dyes-Caudle-Erickson growth law reaches full areal sweep at 3.477891445202633 times the breakthrough volume.

A recovery factor is $E_D \times E_A \times E_V$, and the engine's reported 0.5545614215589451 is a fraction of the FLOODED oil in place, with $E_V$ already folded into the volume. Of the element it is 0.2854276257199057.

## The forecast

At the design rate of 2000 rb/d the element breaks through at 639.1875 days, runs to a water oil ratio limit at 2221.9375 days, and recovers 1709784.4164781766 stb.

Rate buys time and not oil: quadrupling the injection rate moves breakthrough by a factor of 3.8 and the ultimate oil by a factor of 1.0015.

Water oil ratio limits of 10 and 25 give identical answers, because the water oil ratio crosses both inside one monthly step once the areal sweep saturates.

## The reconciliation

At the rate the field actually injected, 104.68747061689818 rb/d per element, the forecast never breaks through in a thirty year horizon. The breakthrough volume would take 63.71644586899872 years to deliver.

Ekene-6 broke through in 425 days.

Inverting the breakthrough condition on the water actually allocated to Ekene-6 by that date gives an implied contacted pore volume of 164686.15596920124 rb, which is

$$\frac{164686.15596920124}{11205422.76570545} = 1.4697005138728763 \text{ percent of the element}$$

One sixty-eighth. Spread over the full element area at 20 percent porosity, that is a layer half a foot thick.

## What the three tiers say together

The flood is doing its pressure job and not its displacement job.

The Associate tier's pressure recovery of 34.4931292839633 psi is real. The Professional tier's imbalance between an element at 1.20 and one at 0.61 is real. And this tier says that most of the water in the over-injected element is short-circuiting through a small fraction of the rock, which means the correct response to the imbalance is not simply to move water from north to south. It is conformance control on the northern path first.

That conclusion required all three tiers. No one of them contains it.

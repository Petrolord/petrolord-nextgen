# The most advantageous bid

{{panel:pr-envelope-calculator}}

Every stage of this tier ends here. The technical envelope, the arithmetic, the evaluated cost and the combined score feed one decision: which bid is awarded the contract. This lesson runs the whole well services tender in one call, reads the award under both bases the engine offers, and explains why they name different bids.

## The whole tender in one call

The engine's stages basis reads:

> technical envelope (mandatory requirements, then the pass mark), then the commercial envelope of the passing bids only, then the award

Under the fixture's settings, a combined award at technical weight 0.7 with the lowest-ratio commercial score and the relative technical score, the engine awards WS3 and gives its reason:

> WS3 has the highest combined score

The award cites the combined evaluation formula of the World Bank Standard Procurement Document, Request for Bids, Works, two-envelope (September 2025), Section III. WS3 is the most advantageous bid at 96.998434. The World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers (Seventh Edition, September 2025) set out the Most Advantageous Bid at paras 5.69 and 5.70.

## The same tender at the lowest evaluated cost

Change only the award basis to lowest-cost and the engine awards WS5:

> WS5 has the lowest evaluated cost

| award basis | awarded bid | figure it wins on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| combined, technical weight 0.7 | WS3 | combined score 96.998434 |
| lowest-cost | WS5 | evaluated cost 862141.000000 |

The Nigeria Public Procurement Act 2007 (Act No. 14, Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007) speaks of the lowest evaluated responsive bid at s.24(3), and the lowest-cost basis applies that directly.

## Why the two awards differ

The combined score weighs the technical percentage at 0.7. WS3 carries the highest technical percentage and the fourth-lowest evaluated cost of the four; WS5 carries the lowest evaluated cost and the lowest technical percentage of the four. Weight quality heavily and WS3 wins; weigh cost alone and WS5 wins. Neither award is wrong. Each is the correct answer to a different question, and the tender must state which question it asks before any bid is opened.

That is also why "most advantageous" in this course means only the highest combined score under a stated weight and stated methods. It says nothing about a bid being best in every sense.

## A third basis at the next tier

An award can also go to the lowest evaluated cost with a cost over the life of the asset added in, as the materials tender does with its five years of valve maintenance. That is the Professional tier's question, and it works in the award calculator.

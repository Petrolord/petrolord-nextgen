# The unit rate prevails

{{panel:pr-envelope-calculator}}

When a line's amount disagrees with its quantity times its unit rate, one of the two must give way. The World Bank Standard Procurement Document, Request for Bids, Works, two-envelope (September 2025) settles it at ITB 35.1(a): the unit rate prevails and the amount is corrected. This lesson applies that rule to WS2 and follows the correction into the bid's total.

## WS2's coiled tubing spread

WS2 prices 18 operating days of the coiled tubing spread at a unit rate of 27000.000000 a day, and writes the amount as 468000.000000. Eighteen times 27000.000000 is 486000.000000. The engine returns:

> WS2: line ct-spread: quantity x unit rate = 486000 differs from the quoted 468000 by more than 0.005, so the unit rate prevails and the amount is corrected to 486000

| bid | quoted total | lines corrected | corrected total | correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WS2 | 849400.000000 | 1 | 867400.000000 | 18000.000000 |

The corrected total is the sum of the corrected lines. WS2 rises by 18000.000000, the difference on that one line. Every later stage of the evaluation starts from 867400.000000, the corrected price.

## Why the rate, and why a correction can go up

A unit rate is the bidder's priced commitment for each unit of work. The quantities are the company's own estimate, written into the bill, and in a contract paid by measured quantity it is the rate that is paid. A transposed digit in the amount column is the likelier slip, so the rate stands.

Notice the direction. The correction raised WS2's price. Arithmetic correction is no penalty and no gift: it restores what the bid's own rates say the work costs. A bid that looked level with WS5 at a quoted 849400.000000 is 18000.000000 dearer once its bill is read correctly.

## The law says the same

The Nigeria Public Procurement Act 2007 (Act No. 14, Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007) at s.31(4) permits the correction of arithmetic errors in a bid. The engine's rule follows the Standard Procurement Document's order, and the correction is reported line by line so the bidder can see exactly what changed.

## What the correction does not do

Under this rule the correction changes no unit rate and no quantity. It corrects no amount written in words against an amount in figures: that rule, ITB 35.1(c), is taught in this course as a concept only, because the engine does not compare words with figures. It does not decide whether a bidder may withdraw after a correction; that is a procedure of the tender, outside the arithmetic.

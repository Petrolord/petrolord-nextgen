# What the engine does not build

{{panel:pr-contract-calculator}}

An honest account of a tool includes what it leaves out. Several steps of a real procurement sit outside the tender engine. Each is taught here as a concept, with the text it would come from, so an evaluator knows which parts of the file still have to be done by hand.

## The steps outside the engine

**Domestic preference.** The World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers (Seventh Edition, September 2025, read on 2026-09-26) set a margin of preference for domestic goods and works in Bank-financed international competition, at para 5.52. The Public Procurement Act 2007 (Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007, read on 2026-09-26), at s.34, leaves Nigerian margins to regulations of the Bureau of Public Procurement, which the course did not read. The engine applies no preference margin, and no figure in this course carries one.

**Words against figures.** The two-envelope Standard Procurement Document's ITB 35.1(c) settles an amount in words that differs from the amount in figures. The engine corrects arithmetic on figures only and compares nothing written in words.

**The Board's approval steps and the Fund.** The Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (Act No. 2, commenced 22 April 2010, read on 2026-09-26) sets approval steps for the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board in s.17 to s.24, and a deduction for the Nigerian Content Development Fund in s.104. The engine measures content and applies s.14 and s.16; it takes no approval step and deducts nothing for the Fund.

**Negotiation, best and final offers, and lots.** The engine evaluates the bids as received and corrected. It computes no negotiation round, no best and final offer, and no award split into lots.

**Later content targets.** The engine holds the 2010 Schedule as enacted. A target the Board set later enters only as a stated target with its source, and a stated target with no source is refused.

## Why a list of absences belongs in the course

A reader who does not know what a tool leaves out will assume it did everything. An evaluation report built on this engine that does not mention domestic preference can mean either that none applied or that nobody checked. Naming the absent steps makes the report say which.

The absent steps also mark where a figure in this course stops. Every evaluated cost here is before any preference margin; every award is before any negotiation; every content figure is measured against the 2010 Schedule. A committee that applies any of these adds it outside the engine and records that it did.

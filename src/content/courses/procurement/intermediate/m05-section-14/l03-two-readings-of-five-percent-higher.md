# Two readings of five percent higher

{{panel:pr-award-calculator}}

Section 14 of the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (Act No. 2, commenced 22 April 2010, as enacted; read on 2026-09-26) selects the leader in the group only if its Nigerian content is "at least 5% higher" than its closest competitor's. The Act does not say whether that means five percentage points or five percent of the competitor's content. This course treats the question as open, teaches both readings side by side, and states the reading beside every figure that depends on it.

## The two readings

The engine has no default. Every s.14 call must state its reading, as ncLeadBasis, and the reading is printed in every reason. Verbatim:

> "at least 5% higher" is read as at least 5 percentage points higher (the Act does not say points or relative)

> "at least 5% higher" is read as at least 5 percent of the runner-up's content higher (the Act does not say points or relative)

A call without a reading is refused, and the refusal says why:

> ncLeadBasis must be 'points' (at least 5 percentage points more) or 'relative' (at least 5 percent more than the runner-up's content); s.14 does not say which, so there is no default

## The materials tender: the reading decides

The group is MS4, the lowest evaluated cost, and MS2, 0.296313 percent above it. MS2 leads on overall content with 61.584657 against MS4's 57.043637:

| | read as points | read as relative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| leader | MS2 | MS2 |
| runner-up | MS4 | MS4 |
| lead | 4.541020 percentage points | 7.960608 percent of the runner-up |
| s.14 applied | false | true |
| selected | MS4 | MS2 |

Read as points, MS2's lead of 4.541020 percentage points falls short of 5, and the lowest evaluated cost MS4 stands. Read as relative, MS2's lead of 7.960608 percent of MS4's content clears 5, and s.14 selects MS2 over MS4. The award turns on a reading of the Act that the Act does not settle.

This situation was planted in the synthetic tender so that the question can be seen.

## At the edges

Stated bids: LO is the lowest evaluated cost at 2000000, and K5 and K4 each sit at 2010000, inside the group:

| case | reading | contents | lead | applied | selected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a lead of exactly 5 points | points | LO 70%, K5 75% | 5.000000 | true | K5 |
| a lead one point short | points | LO 80%, K4 84% | 4.000000 | false | LO |
| the same lead, read as relative | relative | LO 80%, K4 84% | 5.000000 | true | K4 |

A lead exactly at 5 meets the test under either reading. K4's 4 points against 80 is 5 percent of the runner-up's content: short under one reading and exactly on the line under the other.

## What a report says

A report that applies s.14 names the reading beside the outcome, and on a tender like this one it shows both. Neither reading is the course's answer. The Act's text is the authority, and while the question stays open the evaluator states which reading was applied and what the other would have given. Every graded s.14 figure in this course names its reading.

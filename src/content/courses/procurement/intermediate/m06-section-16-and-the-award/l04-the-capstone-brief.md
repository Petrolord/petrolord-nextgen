# The capstone brief

{{panel:pr-award-calculator}}

The capstone for this tier grades six figures, and every one answers the Professional question: what is the lowest evaluated cost over the life of the asset, and what does the Nigerian content Act do to the award?

| graded quantity | the module it rests on |
| --- | --- |
| one bid's life-cycle cost | lowest evaluated cost over the life of the asset |
| one bid's evaluated cost, life cycle included | lowest evaluated cost over the life of the asset |
| the limit of the abnormally low test | abnormally low bids |
| one bid's overall Nigerian content | measuring Nigerian content |
| the s.14 lead, read as percentage points | Section 14 of the Act |
| the s.14 lead, read as a percent of the runner-up's content | Section 14 of the Act |

The last two fields grade the same lead under the two readings of "at least 5% higher". The course does not ask which reading is right, because the Act does not settle it; it asks you to compute each one exactly.

## What it grades and why

It grades the methods of this tier. It grades no technical percentage, arithmetic correction or combined score on its own, because those are the Associate question and its capstone asks them. It grades no contract-type comparison and no independent estimate, because those are the Expert question.

Every graded figure is a return value of the vendored tender engine on fixed inputs, run in the award calculator.

## The data are new

The capstone runs its own synthetic tender, with its own bid codes, its own scope, its own prices and its own settings. None of its values appears in any lesson, and none of the figures in this tier's lessons is a capstone answer. Use the Ekene tenders to check that you drive the award calculator correctly before you open the capstone.

## How to work it

The capstone states every setting a figure depends on: the pass mark and the weights, the omission rule, the delivery schedule, the life-cycle years and discount rate, the weights for overall content across mixed units, the cost estimate if the abnormally low test needs one, and the s.14 reading for each lead. Set each view exactly as the brief states before you read anything.

For the life-cycle cost, check the years and the rate, and check that each bid gives one annual cost per year. For the evaluated cost, check the omission rule and the schedule as well as the life cycle. For the abnormally low limit, count the substantially responsive bids first: the count decides the approach. For the overall content, check each item's measured unit and the stated weights. For the two leads, read each column of the preference view under its own reading, and confirm the group before you read a lead.

## Before you submit

Quote the numeric field the panel prints, at six decimals. A figure inside a reason is text to read. If a call is refused, the refusal names the field it could not use, and the fix is in the input.

## Exercise

Before you open the capstone, rerun the worked figures on the Ekene materials tender in the award calculator. MS2's life-cycle cost at 5 years and 0.1: 20849.327232. MS4's evaluated cost with the average omission rule, the delivery schedule and the life cycle: 546244.982386. MS3's overall content weighted by quoted amounts: 86.240876. MS2's s.14 lead over MS4: 4.541020 read as percentage points, and 7.960608 read as a percent of the runner-up's content. For the abnormally low limit, add a fifth bid of your own to the four responsive bids and check the mean, the population standard deviation and the limit by hand. For each of the six graded fields, write down which view and which setting you will check first.

# The story so far

This tier is one sentence, tested five ways: the base decides more than the rate.

## Production sharing hides its base

A PSC row reports royalty, profit oil as taxable income, tax and net cash flow, and nothing about the cost pool. AKATA under production sharing at a 60 percent cost oil cap reports NPV 29960298.75; the pool the rows never show reads 133542720.00 at the end of 2029 and 0.00 by 2034, found only by marching the cost recovery function over the rows. At a 30 percent cap the pool ends at 207346412.26 and no KPI mentions it.

## Royalty is a terrain string

Shallow water charges 0.125000 on oil at every rate; deep offshore 0.050000 to 50000 bopd and 0.075000 past it; a marginal field blends by rate. A price royalty rides on top, with anchors escalating at 2 percent a year from 2021, so 80 USD pays 0.023908 in 2025 and 0.010630 in 2035.

## Five taxes on three bases

AKATA under the PIA, shallow water, converted PML, base year 2029 so the framework is nta_2025, pays royalties 122393644.64, HCT 77020493.72, CIT 86274711.53, development levy 21007007.79 and no TET: total tax 184302213.04 against 148425219.46 under joint venture terms, NPV 42943268.01 against 72534830.66. In deep offshore the conservative reading zeroes the HCT and lifts NPV to 141623594.88; the aggressive reading gives 61725382.46; an oil price of 120 gives only 128984232.18. The terrain string moves NPV further than the price sweep from 82 to 120.

## Levies and losses

TET or the development levy is decided by the base year, once, for the whole ledger: 2025 on auto is pia_only, 2026 is nta_2025, and a 2026 row in a 2025 ledger still pays TET. Losses bank per base, HCT and CIT apart, and the levies never see the pool. A pool nothing spends is a memo line, 5000000.00 on jv_loss_unused_at_cessation, in no NPV.

## The end of the ledger

The economic limit tests revenue less royalty against opex and is off by default, so AKATA with a long tail keeps three losing years and reports 87727489.23 where the trimmed ledger reports 93890737.45. A lump sum abandonment is post-tax and unscaled; a fund is opex, relieves the profit taxes, and scales with working interest. A first oil delay moves production and opex and leaves capex where it was.

## Three numbers to distrust

The profile point at the applied rate is evaluated at 6.8 and reads 72513070.98 against the headline 72534830.66. The IRR of a vector with a terminal negative is one root of possibly two, 6.0000 where 2.0000 also zeroes the NPV. total_abandonment_cost under a fund reads 30000000.00 when 15000000.00 was collected.

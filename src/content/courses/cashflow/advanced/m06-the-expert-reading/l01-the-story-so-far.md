# The story so far

This tier is one sentence, tested five ways: the base decides more than the rate.

## Production sharing hides its base

A PSC row reports royalty, profit oil as taxable income, tax, net cash flow and the pool balance. AKATA under production sharing at a 60 percent cost oil cap reports NPV 29960298.75; psc_cost_pool_after reads 133542720.00 at the end of 2029 and 0.00 by 2034, and no column prints the cost recovered in a year.

## Royalty is a terrain and a daily rate

Onshore and shallow water weight tranches of the daily rate: the first 5000 bopd at 5 percent, the next 5000 at 7.5 percent, the rest at 15 or 12.5 percent, one weighted rate on the whole volume, so shallow water reads 0.062500 at 10000 bopd and 0.112500 at 50000. Deep offshore charges 5 percent up to 50000 bopd and 7.5 percent on the share above, 0.054167 at 60000; frontier is 7.5 percent flat. A price royalty rides on top. On the Regulations (2021) base, the engine's default, 80 USD pays 0.023910 in 2025 and 0.010632 in 2035; the Act reads the same benchmarks one year earlier, a reading the course leaves open.

## Five taxes on three bases

AKATA under the PIA, shallow water, converted PML, every year from 2029 an NTA year, pays royalties 60324870.87, HCT 91654840.32, CIT 102166298.31, development levy 23822173.11 and no TET: total tax 217643311.74 against 148425219.46 under joint venture terms, NPV 59766796.57 against 72534830.66. Deep offshore needs a stated reading of its NTA hydrocarbon tax before it runs at all: conservative_zero gives NPV 136554243.51 and aggressive_pml_30 gives 60060654.75, two readings of an open text and neither of them a finding. An oil price of 120 gives 153901708.13, so the price sweep from 82 to 120 moves NPV further than either reading does.

## Levies and losses

TET or the development levy is decided for each year of assessment: on auto a year before 2026 is pia_only and pays TET, a year from 2026 is nta_2025 and pays the levy, and one ledger across the two carries both strings. Losses bank per base, HCT and CIT apart, and the levies never see the pool. A pool nothing spends is a memo line, 5000000.00 on jv_loss_unused_at_cessation, in no NPV.

## The end of the ledger

The economic limit tests revenue less royalty against opex and is off by default, so AKATA with a long tail keeps three losing years and reports 87727489.23 where the trimmed ledger reports 93890737.45. A lump sum abandonment is post-tax; a fund is opex and relieves the profit bases; under both modes the cost entered is the share.

## Three numbers to distrust

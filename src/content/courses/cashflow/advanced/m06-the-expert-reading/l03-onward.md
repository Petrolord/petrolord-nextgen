# Onward

A fiscal ledger is a model of a law as one engine reads it. Be clear about what that earns and where it stops. PIA figures here use the Regulations (2021) price-royalty base, the engine default.

## What it earns

It turns a contract into a number with a provenance. AKATA under the PIA is NPV 59766796.57 on the real basis, end-year, base year 2029, framework nta_2025 in every year, shallow water, converted PML, and every one of those words is a lever the ledger has shown moving. It prices the levers in one currency: a CPR limit of 40 percent takes the same field to 37056190.44, and a 50 percent working interest halves NPV to 29883398.29 while leaving take at 70.6449 percent and IRR at 25.8412.

It also says what to negotiate. The oil price sweep from 82 to 120 moves AKATA furthest, to 153901708.13, yet a cap that binds or a lease status moves it by ten million or more too, and those are words in a document that a negotiator can change.

## Where it hands over

The engine models one reading of the law at a time. The conservative and aggressive readings of deep offshore HCT in an NTA year are both in it, 136554243.51 and 60060654.75 on AKATA, and it refuses to run until one is stated. That choice belongs to counsel, and a ledger that reports one without the other has taken a legal position without saying so.

It models one price path, one schedule and one working interest at a time. Ranges belong to the sweeps and to a probabilistic layer this course does not own, and a distribution built by rerunning this ledger inherits every convention the ledger carries.

It stops at the last row. What an unused loss pool, a forfeited CPR balance or unclaimed depreciation is worth to a buyer of the asset is a question this engine answers with a memo line or with silence, and the memo line is not in the NPV.

## The limit worth remembering

A number without its convention is not a number, and the fiscal edges are where that costs most: the same rows produced 72534830.66, 59766796.57, 136554243.51 and 60060654.75 without a barrel or a price changing. The first two answer different regimes; the last two answer the same question under two readings of an open text, and only the stated reading makes either one usable.

## What you have finished

Which regime, which framework, which terrain, which base each tax is charged on, what the end of the ledger does to the headline, and which three reported numbers to read with their method rather than on their own.

Write the full sentence that names 59766796.57, with every string it depends on. Then list the three questions a fiscal ledger answers and the three it hands to someone else.

# Onward

A fiscal ledger is a model of a law as one engine reads it. Be clear about what that earns and where it stops.

## What it earns

It turns a contract into a number with a provenance. AKATA under the PIA is NPV 42943268.01 on the real basis, end-year, base year 2029, framework nta_2025, shallow water, converted PML, conservative reading, and every one of those words is a lever the ledger has shown moving. It prices the levers in one currency: the terrain string is worth the distance from 42943268.01 to 141623594.88, a CPR cap of 40 percent takes the same field to -1723561.25, and a 50 percent working interest halves NPV to 21471634.00 while leaving take at 75.6789 percent and IRR at 21.3196.

It also says what to negotiate. A cap that binds, a lease status, a reading of the deep offshore rule: those move NPV further than the oil price sweep from 82 to 120 does, and they are words in a document rather than a market.

## Where it hands over

The engine models one reading of the law at a time. The conservative and aggressive readings of deep offshore HCT are both in it, 141623594.88 and 61725382.46, and it will not choose. That choice belongs to counsel, and a ledger that reports one without the other has taken a legal position without saying so.

It models one price path, one schedule and one working interest at a time. Ranges belong to the sweeps and to a probabilistic layer this course does not own, and a distribution built by rerunning this ledger inherits every convention the ledger carries.

It stops at the last row. What an unused loss pool, a forfeited CPR balance or unclaimed depreciation is worth to a buyer of the asset is a question this engine answers with a memo line or with silence, and the memo line is not in the NPV.

## The limit worth remembering

A number without its convention is not a number, and the fiscal edges are where that costs most: the same rows produced 72534830.66, 42943268.01, 141623594.88 and 61725382.46 without a barrel or a price changing, and each of the four is right for its question.

## What you have finished

Which regime, which framework, which terrain, which base each tax is charged on, what the end of the ledger does to the headline, and which three reported numbers to read with their method rather than on their own.

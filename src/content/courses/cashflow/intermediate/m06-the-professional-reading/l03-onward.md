# Onward

The Professional tier ends with IRR as a property of a curve. The Expert tier begins where those curves get their shapes: the fiscal edges that put a negative at the end of a ledger, cut it short, or split one revenue among several taxes.

## Where terminal negatives come from

AKATA with an abandonment of 60000000 in 2035 ends in -29598201.95, reports NPV 38666394.86 and returns IRR null, because a curve that ends negative crosses zero twice and the engine names a rate only where one rate in the band does. With 200000000 it reports NPV -40359955.35 and returns null for the opposite reason, its NPV negative at every sampled rate, -58362170.82 at 0 percent. Abandonment is one source of a terminal negative, and where it comes from, as a lump sum or as a sinking fund, is the Expert tier's to teach. A tail kept past its economic limit is another: at 20 percent decline the limit year is 2031 and three years are trimmed, and with the limit off those years would stay, each carrying a loss to the end of the vector.

## Where the cascade gets longer

The price sweep on oil_price_multiyear_pia_real carries columns AKATA under joint venture does not have: HCT 229314597.39 at 80 USD/bbl, CIT 271247103.86, production allowance 59028480.00, royalties 196083885.82. The PIA take holds at 69.3025 percent at 80 and 69.8048 at 120 where the JV take on AKATA falls from 87.4083 at 60 to 57.9418 at 120. Five taxes on three bases: the base each is charged on decides more than its rate, and that is the Expert tier's subject. Everything discounted in that cascade is read with the conventions this tier named, so nothing learned here is replaced there.

## What travels

The discounted readings travel with their labels: 72534830.66 end-year on the real basis, 70188970.32 mid-year, 6.796117 percent applied. Two readings travel with their own proof. The point labelled 6.8 reads 72534830.66, the headline, because it is evaluated at the exact applied rate. The vector [-100, 208, -108.12] returns null with irrStatus multiple-roots and both of its roots listed, so a reader sees that there are two before choosing either. What travels instead is the habit of asking what the engine did to reach a number.

## What to carry

Three habits. Name the convention before the number. Write the sign of the last flow before reading an IRR. Read the rows column before comparing two runs, because a sweep that trims years compares different fields.

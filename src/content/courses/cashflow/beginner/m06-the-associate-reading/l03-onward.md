# Onward

The Associate ledger stops at a running sum with no clock. Two numbers it already printed show why the other tiers exist.

## Two totals for one field

AKATA's total net cash flow is 141637829.18 USD in money of the day and 117362408.71 in 2029 money. Same rows, same cash, two totals. The Associate reading names both and quotes the basis. It does not say which one a decision should rest on, because that needs a clock, and the clock is the Professional tier: what a 2035 flow of 30401798.05 is worth in 2029, and which conventions move that answer.

The engine already prints the time-valued readings beside the ones this tier owns. Take is 66.1723 percent undiscounted and 76.1610 discounted. Payback is 3.461632 years undiscounted and 3.961607 discounted. NPV, IRR and DPI sit on the same line. Each is a reading of the ledger this tier built, and none can be read until its convention is stated.

## The edges the flat regime hides

AKATA's loss columns read 0.00 on every row, its royalty is one rate, its tax is one rate, and its field ends when the data ends. A production sharing contract recovers cost from a pool before it shares profit. The PIA charges five taxes on three bases, and the base moves more than the rate. A year whose revenue no longer covers its opex is kept unless somebody turns the economic limit on. Abandonment arrives as a lump sum or a fund. Those are the Expert tier, and each changes a row before it changes a total.

## What to carry forward

Three habits from this tier survive into the others. Build the first row by hand and prove it to the cent, because -121123680.00 is either right or it is not. Name the convention beside every number, because a payback of 3.461632 years is read from one column and a cumulative of -29534809.71 from another. And read what the engine refuses as carefully as what it returns, because a null take on zero_rates_capex_only is a truer answer than any percentage would be.

## What the tier cannot tell you

Whether AKATA is a good investment. It can say the field returns its money in 2032, spends 40.006602 USD per boe to do it and hands the government 66.1723 percent of what it creates. It cannot say whether 117362408.71 in 2029 money, arriving when it does, was worth 255000000.00 spent in 2029 and 2030.

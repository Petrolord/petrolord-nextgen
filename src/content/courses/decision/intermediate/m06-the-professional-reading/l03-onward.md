# Onward

This tier priced information when the numbers describing it could all be true. The Expert tier asks what happens when they cannot, when the lottery grows, and which printed numbers deserve less trust than their decimals suggest.

## Inputs that cannot all be true

The tree engine derives posteriors from likelihoods, so its inputs cannot contradict each other. The Analyzer takes posteriors typed by hand, and they can. On IRRI both indicators are typed 20 / 80 percent, which implies a success chance of 0.200000 against the 0.300000 stated. The repaired Analyzer keeps the EMV without information, 15.00, and the EVPI, 63.00, and withholds the rest. Before the repair it printed a gross value of information of -15.00 there, and on the published certainPosteriorsWithheld inputs 245.00 beside an EVPI of 63.00. The tier works out where the tolerance of 0.005 sits and why rounded posteriors pass or fail it.

## Bigger lotteries

The published threeOutcomesFourActions lottery has three outcomes and four actions, and a survey with three readings, threeByThree, is worth 1.2000 gross against a cost of 12.0000. The tier covers a reading that never happens, with a chance of 0.000000, and an action, Drill with partner at 54.5000 against Drill alone at 109.0000, that is never best at any probability.

## The brief

Decision Studio turns a saved tree into a brief: an Optimal EMV, a recommended first move, the next best alternative and the advantage between them. On the EKPAN information tree that is 92.5750, "Acquire CSEM survey", 75.7500 and 16.8250. An exact tie prints an advantage of 0 beside a first move that is simply the branch listed first.

## Numbers to distrust

On the EKPAN lottery Drill beats Farm out, 75.7500 against 33.2500, and loses 80.0000 with probability 0.650000; a risk neutral engine cannot prefer the farm-out. A net VOI card reads 0.00 at a survey cost of 32.996 and -0.00 at 33.004 while the verdicts say positive and negative, because the verdict reads the number before rounding.

## What to carry forward

Carry the order of work: the choice at the prior, the ceiling, Bayes, the action after each signal, the weight, the price. Carry the two bounds, because the Expert cases that go wrong break one of them or hide that they would have.

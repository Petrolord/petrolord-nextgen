# Onward

A decision tree turns uncertain money into one choice by expectations and maxima, and information is worth what it can change in that choice. Be clear about what that earns and where it hands over.

## What it earns

It makes a choice auditable: the EKPAN tree's 105.0000 million USD can be rolled back on paper by anyone. It prices flexibility: OKRIKA's option to sell after a poor appraisal is worth 71.4000 inside the appraisal branch. It prices information before it is bought: the EKPAN lottery's survey is worth 24.8250 gross and 16.8250 net at a cost of 8.0000, against a ceiling of 52.0000. And it withholds a value built on numbers that cannot all be true, as it does for IRRI.

## Where it hands over

A tree values nothing itself. Its payoffs arrive already discounted from a cash flow engine such as Petroleum Economics Studio, and a Monte Carlo summary enters at its mean alone. It holds no risk attitude, so the EKPAN lottery's drill wins on its mean while losing money with probability 0.650000, and the appetite for that loss belongs to the company. It values one prospect's actions at a time.

## Where decision analysis goes next

The Economics path takes the same two operations to bigger questions.

Capital portfolio decisions choose among many projects under one budget. The best set of projects is not the set of individually best ones, because money spent on one is unavailable to the next. Decision Studio already sets capital allocation from a saved portfolio beside the decision analysis in one brief.

Field development planning is OKRIKA at full size: appraise, choose a concept, phase the wells, and keep the options that survive a poor result. Every later choice is a decision node rolled back right to left, and every appraisal is information valued by what it can change.

## The limit worth remembering

A value of information without its actions, its tree and its consistency is not a number. The EKPAN lottery's survey is worth 24.8250 with the farm-out and 19.84 in the two-action Analyzer, on the same survey. Each is right for its question, and none is right without it.

## What you have finished

Inputs that contradict each other and the check that withholds; bigger lotteries and actions that never win; the decision brief and its provenance; refusals and the defaults that stay silent; and the numbers to read only with their method.

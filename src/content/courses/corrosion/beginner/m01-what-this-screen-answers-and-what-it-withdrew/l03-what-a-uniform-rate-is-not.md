# What a uniform rate cannot tell you

The studio prints one rate. At the shipped defaults that rate is 0.754524 mm/yr and the label beside it reads high. Before you use either figure, be clear about what kind of quantity the rate is, because the commonest misreading in this app is treating a single number as a prediction for the worst place on a line.

The rate this engine returns is a general uniform rate. The engine says so in its own list of absences, where a localised attack rate appears as something the module does not provide. Uniform means the calculation describes metal loss spread over the wetted surface at the stated conditions. It carries no geometry, no weld, no bend and no deposit, so it cannot tell you where the first leak will be.

## What a reader will look for and will not find

The module publishes 8 items it will not pretend to answer, and three of those items hold the four things people reach for first. There is no inspection interval, so the app cannot tell you when to look at the line again. There is no minimum thickness and no retirement thickness, so it cannot tell you what wall to stop at. There is no fitness for service assessment of any kind. Producing any of the four means adopting a standard this module does not carry.

Two more absences are about mechanisms rather than about limits. The engine holds no pitting criterion, so it says nothing about pit depth or pit growth. It holds no sulphide stress cracking criterion and no hydrogen induced cracking criterion, so the hydrogen sulphide comparison it does make is a screening flag and never a cracking verdict.

One more absence is a naming trap. This engine holds no erosional velocity criterion, so erosional wall loss from entrained solids or from impingement is not modelled here at all. The Casing & Tubing Design course owns that criterion, and Nodal Analysis and Gas Well Deliverability cite it too. The wall shear this module computes is used for one purpose, which is deciding whether a corrosion inhibitor film survives on the steel.

## Integrity, read narrowly

The app is called a Corrosion & Integrity studio, so take the second word narrowly while you are in it. Integrity here means one arithmetic: a corrosion allowance divided by a rate. At the shipped defaults that arithmetic gives a remaining life of 4.207953 yr against a stated design life of 20.000000 yr, and it is the whole of what the word covers on this screen.

That narrowness keeps the app honest. A screen offering an interval, a thickness and a verdict off the same unsourced chain would look far more useful and would be far harder to argue with.

## Exercise

Take the shipped case and write down the rate of 0.754524 mm/yr beside the remaining life of 4.207953 yr and the design life of 20.000000 yr. Using the allowance of 3.175000 mm the case carries, work out which of those three figures the other two would have to produce, and state which input you would have to supply yourself before this screen could offer an inspection interval. Then name one failure on a line you know that a uniform rate could not have predicted.

# The numbers with no source

Most of the numbers inside this engine have no source in this repository. The engine says so itself: it carries a list of 11 held items and returns that list with every screening, so a caller shows the reader what the answer rests on.

Read the list once and the shape of this course follows. Every constant of the reaction correlation is held. Every constant of the protective film correction is held, and so is the temperature at which the published correlation turns protective. The pH slope and its reference are held. The fugacity pressure cap of 250.000000000000 bar is held, and so is what the correlation does above it. The hydrogen sulphide screening threshold of 0.003500000000 bar is held. The two ratios that decide which corrosion product governs, 0.002000000000 and 0.050000000000, are held. The film stripping threshold of 100.000000000000 Pa and the moderate band of 50.000000000000 Pa are held. The band edges that produce the word beside the rate, 0.100000000000, 0.500000000000 and 1.000000000000 mm/yr, are held. So are this module's own friction factor constants and the switch in it at a Reynolds number of 4000.000000000000, and the Pipeline & Line Sizing course computes its own friction factor and its own Reynolds number with a different correlation.

## Held is not the same as unmeasured

Held means no source, and it says nothing about whether the number is known. Every one of these constants is measured out of the engine's behaviour by asking a question whose answer is that constant alone, and the measurement is compared against a literal in a third file. 30 constants are pinned that way. The engine exports 15 of them by name, so what it declares and what it does can be compared, and the vendored oracle carries 28 of them in a block of its own as a fourth copy.

The reason for so many copies is arithmetic. A constant that lives in the engine and in the oracle cannot be validated by comparing the engine with the oracle. Both files move together and the comparison stays green, which is what a paired experiment on this module found. A pin is a comparison against a third copy and it is still not a validation against a source.

## What follows for this course

Because the correlation constants are held, every rate the correlation produces is held with them, and this course grades no correlation rate at all. It grades no band label, no threshold verdict, no severity region and no material choice. What it grades is the bookkeeping around them: partial pressures from mole fractions, a ratio of two mole fractions, this module's Reynolds number from its own definition, the corrosion inhibitor arithmetic over two typed percentages and the allowance arithmetic over a stated rate.

That split is the only way to examine a learner on something this engine can stand behind.

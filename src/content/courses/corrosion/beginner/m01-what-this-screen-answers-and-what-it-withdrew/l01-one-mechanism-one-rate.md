# One mechanism, one rate

This studio is a carbon dioxide corrosion rate screen with a remaining life calculation on the end of it. It answers one question about one mechanism, and the list of what it does not answer is longer than the list of what it does. Starting there is the fastest way to read the screen correctly, because most mistakes a new user makes are asking this app for a number it never claims to hold.

The engine behind the screen exposes a set of doors. One returns the carbon dioxide fugacity, one the reaction term, one the transport term, one the protective film multiplier, one the pH multiplier, one the wall shear, one the sour screening comparison, one the corrosion product regime and one the remaining life. A final door reconciles all of them into a single screening and names the limit that governs the answer. At the shipped studio defaults that screening returns a rate of 0.754524 mm/yr, which the same screen prints as 29.705656 mpy, and a remaining life of 4.207953 yr.

## The absences are stated rather than discovered

The engine carries two lists of its own limits and returns both with every screening, so a caller shows them rather than finding them out later. One list holds 8 items the module will not pretend to answer. The other holds 11 numbers that have no source anywhere in this repository. Those two lists are the honest frame around every figure above.

The single rate this engine returns is a general uniform rate. It is not a pitting rate, it is not a weld rate and it is not a top of line rate, and the engine says exactly that in its own list. A learner who reads it as a wall loss prediction for the worst spot on a line has read it wrong, and the module cannot warn them, because it holds no localised model to compare against.

## Where this course sits beside the others

The Well Integrity and P&A course in the Drilling module owns barrier envelopes and abandonment, and it states in its own scope that it carries no corrosion model, no wall loss and no remaining life. This course fills exactly that refusal. Keep the two vocabularies apart: a barrier envelope is a pressure containment argument, and a corrosion allowance is a thickness this studio divides by a rate.

One more boundary belongs on the first page. This engine holds no erosional velocity criterion, so mechanical erosion from entrained solids or from impingement is outside it. The Casing & Tubing Design course owns that criterion. The wall shear here serves a different purpose, which is whether a corrosion inhibitor film survives on the steel.

## Exercise

Open the studio on its shipped case and write down the rate in both of the units the screen prints, 0.754524 mm/yr and 29.705656 mpy, beside the remaining life of 4.207953 yr. This course states the conversion between the two rate units as a division by 25.4 and a multiplication by a thousand. Apply it yourself to the printed mm/yr figure and say whether the mpy figure on the screen is the one that conversion gives. Then list which of the 8 stated absences you had expected this screen to answer, and name where each of those questions belongs instead.

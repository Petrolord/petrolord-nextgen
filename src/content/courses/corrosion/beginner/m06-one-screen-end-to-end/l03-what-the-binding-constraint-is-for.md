# What the binding constraint is for

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

A screen that returns seven independent numbers and reconciles none of them leaves the summarising to the reader, and most readers summarise by looking at the largest number on the page. This engine names the limit that governs the answer instead, and it derives that limit from figures it has already computed rather than from anything new.

On the shipped case the binding limit is the corrosion allowance against the design life, and the engine states what it turns on in its own words:

> at 0.755 mm/yr the allowance runs out in 4.2 years against a 20 year design life, short by 11.92 mm of allowance.

The value it turns on is 4.2 yr of 20 yr. Nothing in that sentence is new information. The rate, the life, the design life and the shortfall are all on the rail above it. What the sentence adds is a judgement about which of them is doing the work.

## The same case, moved

Change one input at a time and watch the named limit move with it. Take the velocity from 10 to 60 ft/s and the wall shear becomes 362.474888 Pa against the measured stripping threshold of 100.000000 Pa. The film risk turns high, the corrosion inhibitor credit is removed, and the rate becomes 13.080024 mm/yr against a credited 1.896603 mm/yr, a ratio the engine reports as 6.896552. The remaining life falls from 4.207953 yr to 0.242737 yr and the named limit becomes the wall shear on the corrosion inhibitor film.

Take the hydrogen sulphide from 0.1 to 1 mol% instead and the ratio becomes 0.333333333333, the regime becomes sulphide, and the rate stays exactly where it was at 0.754524 mm/yr. The category and the life are withheld, the rate is kept only as a stated upper bound, and the named limit becomes that the model does not apply. Choose oil wet and the same limit is named for a different reason.

Two of those four cases name a limit that stops the screening from offering a rate verdict at all, and that is the behaviour to notice. A summary that always said the same thing would be a heading rather than a summary, and the studio reaches four different limits across the six streams its source material walks.

## What it is and what it is not

The named limit tells you what to change first. It is silent about what steel to buy, when to inspect and what thickness to retire at, because the module has none of those. Read it as a pointer into the rail rather than as a recommendation, and check the number it cites before you act on the word.

## Exercise

Run the four cases above and write down the named limit for each, with the one number it turns on. Then take the pair at 60 ft/s, the uncredited 13.080024 mm/yr and the credited 1.896603 mm/yr, and form their ratio yourself against the reported 6.896552. Say which single change to the line would move that case back to the limit the shipped case names.

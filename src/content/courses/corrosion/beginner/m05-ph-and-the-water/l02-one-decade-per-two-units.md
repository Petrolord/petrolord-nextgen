# One decade per two units

{{panel:fc-rate-explorer}}

The pH correction has a shape you can check without knowing a single constant behind it. Walk the pH upward and across any two pH units the factor falls by a factor of ten, wherever in the band you start.

Read the factors down the column. At a pH of 4.000000 the factor is 1.000000. At 5.000000 it is 0.316228. At 6.000000 it is 0.100000. At 7.000000 it is 0.031623. At 8.000000 it is 0.010000. At 9.000000 it is 0.003162. The half unit steps fill the same pattern in: at 4.500000 the factor is 0.562341 and at 5.500000 it is 0.177828.

## A scale free property is a checkable one

Measured across that swept band, the ratio of the factor at one pH to the factor two units below it comes out at 0.100000000000 four times over. Every one of the four is the same number. That is what one decade per two pH units means, and it is a property of the form of the correction rather than of the size of its slope, so it can be checked from the outside on any machine.

The same walk shows up in the rate. On one of the engine's worked streams the rate falls from 4.724817 mm/yr at a pH of 4.000000 to 2.656960 mm/yr at 4.500000, 1.494118 mm/yr at 5.000000, 0.840204 mm/yr at 5.500000, 0.472482 mm/yr at 6.000000, 0.149412 mm/yr at 7.000000, 0.047248 mm/yr at 8.000000 and 0.014941 mm/yr at 9.000000. Between the two ends of that column the digest reports a factor of 316.227766.

A property like this one is worth more than an agreement to six decimals against some expected number. An expected number produced by anything that shares a constant with the engine cannot test that constant. A scale free ratio tests the structure of the expression and needs no expected number at all, which is why this course leans on properties of this kind wherever it can find them.

## Strictly falling, and asserted as such

The rate falls at every step of that column, and each step down is smaller than the one above it. The generator behind this course asserts that on every rebuild rather than printing it once and trusting it, which is the difference between a claim and a check.

Two cautions belong beside the column. The slope itself is held for literature, so the decade property tells you the correction is a clean power law and tells you nothing about whether the power is the published one. And the band above the reference is the only place this walk is defined at all, which is the subject of the next lesson.

## Exercise

Take any two of the factors in this lesson that are two pH units apart and divide the upper by the lower. Do it for three different pairs and compare your three answers with the 0.100000000000 the digest reports. Then take the rates at a pH of 4.000000 and 9.000000, 4.724817 mm/yr and 0.014941 mm/yr, form their ratio, and compare it with the 316.227766 above. Say what each of the two checks would catch that the other would not.

# Integrity, read narrowly

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

This app is called a Corrosion and Integrity Studio and a reader will arrive
expecting the second word to mean what it usually means. It does not. In this
course integrity means one arithmetic: a corrosion allowance divided by a rate.
Saying so wherever the word appears is a rule in this wave rather than a
stylistic preference, because the gap between the two readings is where a screen
gets over-read.

## Four things a reader will look for

| what a reader will look for | what this module has | what it does not have |
| --- | --- | --- |
| when to inspect next | a remaining life against a stated allowance | an interval, a risk basis and any inspection standard |
| what thickness to retire at | a corrosion allowance the caller types in | a minimum thickness and any retirement criterion |
| whether the line is fit for service | nothing | a fitness-for-service method of any kind |
| whether the velocity is erosional | a wall shear and a film-risk word | an erosional-velocity criterion |

All four absences are declared. The engine exports a list of what it does not
provide, that list carries eight items, and the screening returns it so a caller
displays the absences rather than discovering them one at a time. A separate
list of eleven items covers the numbers that are held for literature, which is a
weaker statement: held means a source might settle it, and not provided means
the module has taken a decision.

## Two more absences on the same footing

The module has no pitting criterion and no localised rate, and it has no
sulphide stress cracking or hydrogen induced cracking criterion. That first pair
matters for how the single rate is read. The one rate this engine returns is a
GENERAL UNIFORM rate and the engine says so in its own list. It is not a weld
rate, a bend rate, a top-of-line rate or a pit rate, and because the module
carries no localised model it cannot warn a reader who reads it that way.

Nor does it model mechanical erosion or erosional wall loss from solids or
impingement. There is no erosional-velocity criterion here at all, and the
Casing and Tubing Design course at its Expert tier owns that criterion on this
platform.

## Why declaring an absence is worth doing

A field that is missing and a field that is declared absent look identical to a
reader skimming a screen, and they are completely different claims. A missing
field might be an oversight, a failed calculation or a value nobody supplied. A
declared absence is the module stating that it will not answer, which tells the
reader to go and find the answer elsewhere rather than to look harder at this
page. That is why the absences here are printed as rows with the words NOT
PROVIDED in them instead of being left off the screen.

## Reading the studio title honestly

So the honest expansion of the name is a CO2 corrosion rate screen with a
remaining-life division on the end of it. That is a useful tool and it is a
narrower one than the title suggests. A reader who takes the remaining life for
an inspection plan has crossed from what the module computes into what a
standard would have to supply, and this repository carries no such standard.

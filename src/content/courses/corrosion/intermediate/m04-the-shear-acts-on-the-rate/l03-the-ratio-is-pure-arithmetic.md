# The ratio is pure arithmetic

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

When the credit is removed, the ratio of the stripped rate to the credited rate
is not a property of the correlation. It is the reciprocal of the fraction of
the metal loss the corrosion inhibitor programme leaves behind, and nothing
else. The whole de Waard-Milliams chain, every held constant in it, the fugacity
coefficient, the protective-film factor, the pH factor and the wetting factor
all appear identically in both rates and divide out exactly.

The engine asserts that property to twelve figures on every rebuild of this
course's digest.

## Two cases, two ratios

For the Tunu stream the stripped rate is 17.138674 mm/yr and the credited rate
is 3.647110 mm/yr, and the stripped rate is 4.699248 times the credited rate.

Take the studio's shipped case to 60 ft per second and the rate is 13.080024
mm/yr against a credited 1.896603 mm/yr, and the ratio between those two is
6.896552.

Those two cases have different temperatures, different pressures, different
chemistry and different lines. The two ratios differ because the two corrosion
inhibitor programmes differ, and for no other reason.

## Why that is worth a lesson

Almost every number this module produces leans on a constant nobody in this
repository can source: every de Waard-Milliams constant, the scale constants,
the pH slope and reference, the H2S threshold value, both shear thresholds and
the rate category bands. This ratio is the counterexample. It is useful and it
is provably free of all of them, which is why a graded capstone field in this
course can be a ratio of two lives.

## The same property in the life column

The remaining life is a remaining allowance divided by a rate. The allowance is
the same on both sides, so the ratio of the two lives is the ratio of the two
rates the other way up. For the Tunu stream the life is 0.161915 yr against
0.760876 yr with the credit kept, and those two figures carry the same
information as the two rates do.

## What the property does not tell you

The ratio being free of every held constant says nothing about whether the
credit should have been removed in the first place. That verdict rests on the
stripping threshold of 100.000000 Pa, which is held for literature like
everything else in this module's own list. So the ratio answers what the verdict
costs and leaves open whether the verdict is right, and those two questions
should be kept apart in any note you write.

## How to use it

When a case comes back with the credit removed, the ratio tells you what the
verdict cost and it tells you so without any of the held machinery. If you
distrust the absolute rate, and there is a good deal in this module's held list
to justify distrusting it, you can still trust the ratio. It is arithmetic over
the corrosion inhibitor programme you typed in, and nothing else reaches it.

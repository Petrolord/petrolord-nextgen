# The thresholds carry no source

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

Two numbers turn the wall shear into a word. Measured out of the engine by
bisecting the velocity at which the risk word changes, the film-stripping
threshold is 100.000000 Pa and the moderate band is 50.000000 Pa. Both are held
for literature, which in this repository means no source for either value exists
anywhere in it. The engine declares both in its own held list and the studio
prints that list behind a disclosure.

## What the thresholds drive

They drive three things. A coloured word on the screen, a warning paragraph, and
the corrosion inhibitor credit itself. The third is the one that changes the
arithmetic, and it is why the status of these two numbers deserves a lesson at
this tier rather than a footnote. An unsourced number that colours a label is a
presentation choice. An unsourced number that removes a credit from a rate is
part of the calculation.

## The stripping velocity is a number

For the Tunu stream, found by bisecting the velocity at which the film-stripped
flag turns true, the stripping velocity is 8.126015 m/s, and the wall shear
there is 100.000000 Pa against the measured threshold of 100.000000 Pa. So the
threshold is not a region or a judgement. It is a single value, reachable at a
single velocity on a stated stream, and the engine will tell you that velocity
to six decimals.

That precision is honest about the arithmetic and says nothing about the
physics. The velocity is exactly where this module's threshold puts it. Where a
real corrosion inhibitor film actually fails on a real line is a question this
repository cannot answer.

## What a held threshold changes about your reading

A verdict of high film risk means the shear exceeded 100.000000 Pa. It does not
mean anybody measured a film failing at 100.000000 Pa. Two consequences follow
for a reader at this tier. First, a case whose shear sits close to either
threshold should be read as close to a boundary of unknown position rather than
as being on one side of a settled line. Second, a case far above the stripping
threshold is a stronger statement than a case just above it, because the
conclusion then survives a wide range of possible true thresholds.

| velocity m/s | wall shear Pa | film risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.000000 | 8.018151 | low |
| 8.000000 | 97.225957 | moderate |
| 18.000000 | 418.514742 | high |

The middle row is the one to argue about. The bottom row is not.

## And what is still absent

None of this is an erosional velocity limit. The module has no
erosional-velocity criterion and no model of mechanical erosion or erosional
wall loss, and the Casing and Tubing Design course at its Expert tier owns that
criterion. A high film risk here is a statement about a corrosion inhibitor
film and about nothing else.

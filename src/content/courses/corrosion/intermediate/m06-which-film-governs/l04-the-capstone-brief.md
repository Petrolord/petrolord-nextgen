The capstone for this tier asks you to work a stream end to end and to record
six figures. Every one of them comes from the two halves of the module you can
check by hand: the corrosion inhibitor arithmetic and the allowance arithmetic.

## What it grades and why those things

It grades the retained fraction of the metal loss, the corrosion inhibitor
shortfall in percentage points, an inhibited rate, a remaining life, the
allowance a design life demands, and the life the corrosion inhibitor
availability costs.

None of those is a corrosion rate the correlation produced from its own
constants. None is a band label, a threshold verdict, a severity region or a
material choice. Every one of them is either arithmetic over two typed
percentages, or a division of an allowance by a rate the brief states from an
inspection survey rather than asking the correlation for.

That is deliberate and it is the spine of this whole course. Eleven items in
this engine are held for literature and one was withdrawn, so a graded field
that reached any of them would be grading a learner on a number nobody can
source. Work through the held list and you will find that the corrosion
inhibitor arithmetic and the allowance division are what is left standing.

## The conditions are stated in the engine's units

The brief gives you temperature in degrees Celsius, pressure in bar, velocity
in metres a second, diameter in metres and allowances in millimetres. It does
not give you Fahrenheit, psig, feet a second or inches for you to convert.

That is not a convenience. The studio converts a psig pressure to bar by
dividing by a truncated factor, and the engine exports the exact one, so a
pressure taken through the app and a pressure taken through the engine differ in
the sixth significant figure and so does every rate downstream. That difference
is far too small for any screening decision and quite large enough to fail a
tolerance. Grading you on which rounding an app happened to use would be
measuring the app rather than measuring corrosion.

## How to work it

Compute the corrosion inhibitor arithmetic first, because nothing else in the
brief depends on the correlation and you can verify every step. Then take the
stated rate into the allowance division. Keep your intermediate figures at full
precision and round only at the end, and record which field came from which
step so that a disagreement tells you where to look.

Say out loud, before you submit, which of your six figures would change if the
rate category bands were tightened by a step. The answer should be none of
them, and knowing why is the point of the tier.

## Exercise

Before you open the capstone, record from the shipped studio case the effective
corrosion inhibition, the shortfall in percentage points, the rate, the remaining
allowance and the remaining life. Then state, for each of those five, whether it
rests on a held constant or on arithmetic you can check yourself.

# Seven numbers and no verdict

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

A screen that returns seven independent numbers and reconciles none of them is a screen the reader has to summarise themselves. They will do it, because a decision has to come out of the exercise somehow, and they will do it by reading the largest number on the page. That is the failure mode this module's binding constraint exists to close.

## Why the largest number wins

Nothing on an unreconciled screen ranks its own contents. A rate, a wall shear, an effective protection percentage, a remaining life, a required allowance, a shortfall and a decade count are all rendered the same size in the same typeface. A reader's attention goes to whichever one looks alarming, and that is a property of the units rather than of the engineering. A wall shear of 370 Pa looks worse than a life of 0.161915 yr, and on that stream the life is the consequence and the shear is the cause.

The same trap runs the other way. A comfortable-looking figure sits beside a fatal one and the eye averages them. Nothing in a list of seven numbers says which of them is governing the answer.

## What the engine returns instead

The screening door names which of its own limits governs, and it does so in words a reader can act on. Across the six teaching streams it produces four different constraints: mass transfer to the wall, the corrosion allowance against the design life, the model does not apply, and wall shear on the corrosion inhibitor film. A summary that always said the same thing would be a heading rather than a summary, and the fact that these four appear across six streams is what makes it one.

Each constraint carries the value it turns on. Etelebou binds on the allowance against the design life at 1.7 yr of 20 yr. Kanbi binds the same way at 7.7 yr of 20 yr. Tunu binds on wall shear at 370 Pa. Opukushi binds on mass transfer at a 2.34 mm/yr transport limit, and Angiama at 0.09 mm/yr. Diebu binds on the model not applying at all, with no value, because no rate verdict is being offered.

## Derived, never invented

Every one of those constraints is derived from something already computed. The shear comparison uses the shear and the threshold the engine already has. The allowance test uses the life and the design life already returned. The transport test compares two terms the rate already carries. No new correlation was written to produce the summary, and no new constant entered the module with it. That matters, because a summary layer that introduced a fitted number of its own would be another held item hiding behind a sentence.

## Exercise

Take three streams with different binding constraints and write down, for each, the value the constraint turns on and where in the earlier output that value came from. Then pick the largest number on one of those three screens and say whether it is the constraint, and if it is not, what it is.

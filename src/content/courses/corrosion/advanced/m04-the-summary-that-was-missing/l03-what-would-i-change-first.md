# What would I change first

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

The binding constraint is written to answer one question, and it is the question an engineer actually has after a screening run. Not how bad is it. What would I change first. Those are different questions, and only the second has an action on the end of it.

## The four answers this module can give

Across the six teaching streams the engine produces four constraints, and each implies a different first move.

Wall shear on the corrosion inhibitor film points at the line speed. Tunu binds there at 370 Pa against the 100 Pa at which this module takes the film to be stripped, the credit is removed, and the engine says in its own words that slowing the line changes this answer before anything else does.

The corrosion allowance against the design life points at the allowance or at the design life. Etelebou binds at 1.7 yr of 20 yr, short by 30.75 mm. Kanbi binds at 7.7 yr of 20 yr, short by 4.45 mm. Both are asking for a specification decision rather than a chemistry one.

Mass transfer to the wall points at velocity and line size. Opukushi binds at a 2.34 mm/yr transport limit against a 27.66 mm/yr reaction term, and Angiama at 0.09 mm/yr against 10.01 mm/yr. The engine adds the consequence explicitly: velocity and line size move this rate and the chemistry does not. On a transport controlled stream, changing the temperature or the carbon dioxide moves the larger term and the answer barely follows.

The model does not apply points at the screening itself. Diebu lands there, and the right move is to read the withheld block, because no rate verdict is being offered.

## The shipped case and four changes to it

The same logic is visible in the studio's own default and in three changes a user can make to it. Unchanged, the case binds on the corrosion allowance against the design life at 4.2 yr of 20 yr. Push the velocity from 10 to 60 ft per second and it binds on wall shear at 362 Pa. Raise the H2S from 0.1 to 1 mol percent and it binds on the model not applying. Switch the wetting regime to oil wet and it binds on the model not applying again.

Those last two are the same constraint reached by different roads, and in both the reader's next action is to read the withheld block rather than to act on a number.

## The discipline

A constraint is only useful if the reader lets it override their instinct about which number looked worst. The instinct is reliable about magnitude and unreliable about order. The engine has already walked the order, and it shows the value the constraint turns on so that the claim can be checked rather than taken.

For each of the four constraint types, write down the first action it implies and the number you would watch to see whether the action worked. Then take the shipped case at 60 ft per second, record its constraint and its value, and say what the constraint would become if the action succeeded.

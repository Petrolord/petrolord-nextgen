# The order is the claim

{{panel:fc-inhibitor-integrity-explorer}}

The binding constraint is chosen by walking a fixed sequence of questions and stopping at the first one that fires. The sequence is the engineering content of the summary. Change the order and the same stream gets a different headline out of the same numbers, so the order is a claim about what matters first and it deserves to be read as one.

## The sequence

The engine asks, in this order: does the model apply at all; is the corrosion inhibitor film being stripped; does the allowance fail the design life; are the two resistances comparable; is transport controlling; and otherwise kinetics.

Each step answers the question what would I change first, and a lower step is reached only because every step above it is satisfied. That is the property to hold on to. A stream reported as mass transfer controlled has already passed the model applicability test, the film survival test and the design life test. The constraint names the first limit that bites rather than the largest number on the page.

## Why applicability comes first

The first question is whether a CO2 rate model describes the surface at all. The Diebu stream fails it: the H2S to CO2 ratio puts it in the sulphide regime, and the engine returns the model does not apply as its binding constraint with no value attached. Its category and its life are withheld and the rate is retained only as a stated upper bound of 0.513135 mm/yr.

Put that step anywhere else in the order and the summary would rank a limit inside a model that has already stopped describing the stream. A film stripping verdict on a surface the correlation does not cover is a precise answer to the wrong question.

## Why the film test comes before the life test

The rate depends on the shear verdict, because a stripped film removes the corrosion inhibitor credit before the rate is formed. So a life computed without knowing the shear verdict is a life computed at the wrong rate. The Tunu stream is the case: it binds on wall shear at 370 Pa against the 100 Pa at which this module takes the film to be stripped, and the engine says slowing the line changes this answer before anything else does.

Both thresholds in that comparison are held. The order argument stands on the structure of the calculation rather than on the value of either number, which is why it survives the thresholds being unsourced.

## The lower steps

If the model applies and the film survives and the allowance still fails the design life, the allowance is what binds. Etelebou reports 1.7 yr of 20 yr, short by 30.75 mm of allowance, and Kanbi reports 7.7 yr of 20 yr, short by 4.45 mm. If the allowance holds too, the summary drops to which resistance is controlling: Opukushi binds at a 2.34 mm/yr transport limit against a 27.66 mm/yr reaction term, so velocity and line size move that rate and the chemistry does not.

## Exercise

Write the six questions out in order. Then take a stream that binds on mass transfer and state, for each question above it in the sequence, what its answer was and which returned number the engine read to settle it.

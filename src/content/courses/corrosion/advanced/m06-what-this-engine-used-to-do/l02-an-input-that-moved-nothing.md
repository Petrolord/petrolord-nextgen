# An input that moved nothing

This module's subject is what this engine did before the repair that preceded the course, and this item is the second of them. The general lesson is about a box on a screen that has stopped reaching the answer.

## The shape to look for

When one input reaches the answer by two routes and the route that decides the headline ignores it, the box on the screen is decoration. The reader types into it, watches a number change somewhere, and concludes that the input matters. It may matter to the number they were watching and not at all to the number they will act on.

The test for it is arithmetic and it takes one sweep. Walk the input across its range and print the spread of the answer. A spread of zero is the whole finding, and no amount of reading the code is needed to reach it.

## What the sweep found here

Before the repair the pH correction returned one for any pH at or below its reference, so pH 2.0, 3.0, 3.5 and 4.0 all produced the identical rate to sixteen figures. Two decades of hydrogen-ion activity moved the headline number by exactly nothing, while a second calculation on the same screen moved its answer four times across the same span. The screen said the input mattered and the rate said it did not.

A factor of one was also the least limiting possible answer to a question the module could not answer. A more acid water is not a less corrosive one, so returning one there was a reassurance built out of an absence.

## Where it stands now

The rate is strictly monotonic in pH across the whole band above the reference: from 4.724817 mm/yr at pH 4.000000 down to 0.014941 mm/yr at pH 9.000000, a factor of 316.227766, with every step down the column smaller than the one above it. The course asserts that ordering on every rebuild rather than quoting it.

Below the reference the engine refuses, and the refusal names the reference so a caller can print the boundary:

> the pH correction is only defined at or above its reference pH of 4, and pH 3.5 is below it. What the correlation does below the reference is not established in this module, so no factor is returned rather than a factor of 1

Read the refusal and the monotonic column together. The engine now moves where it has grounds to move and declines where it does not, and the reference pH of 4 is still held, so the position of the boundary carries no more authority than it did.

## Exercise

Pick any input to this engine and design the sweep that would show whether it reaches the answer. State the range, the step and the quantity you would print. Then run that sweep on pH above the reference and record the spread, and say what result would have made you suspect a decorative box.

# What a synthetic case can prove

The golden file behind this course carries 110 rows in 17 blocks, and `provenance.published` is false. The file says why: no published de Waard-Milliams case, no clause of either sour-service standard and no corrosion rate-band table exists anywhere in this repository, so every row is synthetic, and a number recalled from memory is not a published datum. That last clause is the one to sit with.

## What synthetic costs

A synthetic case cannot tell you that a correlation describes steel. It comes from the same physical claim it is checking, so it can only confirm that the claim was implemented consistently. Everything this course says about the rate follows from that: the rate is a screening argument to read and argue with, and no graded field here is a rate the correlation produced.

## What synthetic still buys

Consistency is worth more than it sounds. Route independence is what carries the load here, and the golden's own work is split three ways with the file saying which is which.

The series combination is solved by bisection on the reciprocal rather than formed as a reciprocal, and the residual identity is checked separately. The inhibitor time average is rebuilt as an explicit 8760 hour duty cycle, which rounds to whole hours, so the agreement is loose and that looseness is the independence. The wall shear is reached through a momentum balance over a stated length. The film onset is found by bisection on the unclamped expression and cross-checked against the closed form. The remaining life is reached by marching the wall loss forward in small steps rather than by dividing, held to one step absolute. The allowance shortfall is formed as a deficit of years times the rate rather than as a subtraction of two allowances. The H2S to CO2 ratio is formed from mole fractions, so it needs no pressure at all. The sour comparison is done entirely in psia while the engine does it in bar.

Agreement between two separate derivations is a result with a size, and the digest prints the size on every one of the seventeen whole cases. Most sit in the last bits of a double. Case 7 comes in at 8.901e-5 against a tolerance of 1e-3, which is the duty-cycle route's whole-hour rounding appearing exactly where the file says it will.

## The route that earns its keep

The mole-fraction ratio is the route worth studying. Both arguments to the ratio are partial pressures, each the total pressure times a mole fraction, so the pressure divides out and the ratio equals the ratio of the mole fractions at any pressure whatsoever. Reaching it that way catches an H2S partial pressure built from the total pressure with the mole fraction dropped, and it catches an H2S partial pressure fed a CO2 fugacity where the partial pressure belongs. Writing the engine's own ratio out again would catch neither.

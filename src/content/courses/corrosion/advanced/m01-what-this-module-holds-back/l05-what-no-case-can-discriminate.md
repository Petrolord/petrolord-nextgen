# What no case here can discriminate

A validation case is worth what it can tell apart, and it can be worth nothing in two ways. A route with no case at all is an obvious gap and a listing will find it. A route with a case that cannot discriminate looks like coverage, runs green, and checks nothing, because the thing it is supposed to test is shared with whatever produced the expected answer.

The digest names the second class rather than leaving it to be discovered. Every held constant in the pin table is undiscriminable by any case in the golden file. That is arithmetic rather than a criticism of the cases. An oracle sharing a constant with the engine cannot test that constant, at any tolerance, on any number of rows.

## What each route misses

The route table carries a column for exactly this, and it is the column to read first. The wall shear route goes through a momentum balance over a stated length, with the pipe force balance checked as an identity, and it cannot check the Blasius pair, the laminar constant or the branch switch. The film onset route is bisected on the unclamped expression and cross-checked against the closed form, and it cannot check the three scale constants. The H2S to CO2 ratio route is formed from mole fractions, and it cannot check the two boundary ratios. The sour comparison is done in psia while the engine works in bar, and it cannot check the threshold value.

Four routes carry nothing in that column. The series combination, the inhibitor time average, the remaining life and the allowance shortfall are clear, and it is worth asking why. Each of them is arithmetic over quantities the caller supplies rather than a correlation with fitted constants inside it. That is the same property that decides which of the eighteen graded fields this course can carry.

## What carries them instead

Pins carry them, and a pin is a weaker instrument than a validation. It compares a measurement against a third copy and proves that nobody moved the number quietly. It says nothing about whether the number is right. Thirty constants are pinned that way, with the golden's own block as a fourth copy.

Constant-free invariants carry a different load. The series residual holds to twelve figures on every row. The scale factor is exactly one at its own computed onset. The pH correction gives one decade per two pH units, measured across the swept band as 0.100000000000 four times over. The mole-fraction ratio is provably free of pressure, and its difference column against the partial-pressure route is zero at every pressure. None of these depends on a fitted value, so each tests the form of an expression rather than its coefficients.

Put the three together and the honest position falls out. The shape of this model is checked. The constants inside it are pinned. Nothing in this repository validates them against a source, which is why this course grades no correlation rate.

## Exercise

Take the four routes with an empty cannot-check column and the four with an entry. For each, write down what it would still catch if its held constants were wrong. Then pick a graded capstone field and trace it back to the routes it rests on.

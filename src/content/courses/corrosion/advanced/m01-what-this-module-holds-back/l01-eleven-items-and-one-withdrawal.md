# Eleven items and one withdrawal

This engine publishes its own account of what it cannot stand behind, and that account is the right first thing to read. `HELD_FOR_LITERATURE` carries 11 items and `NOT_PROVIDED` carries 8. The whole screening door returns both in its `limits` and `notProvided` fields, so a caller displays the absences rather than meeting them one surprise at a time.

## What held means here

Held means the number is in the code and its source is nowhere in the repository. Every de Waard-Milliams constant is held, and so therefore is every corrosion rate the correlation builds out of them. The scale factor constants 2400, 0.6 and 6.7 are held, and so is the temperature at which the published correlation turns protective. The pH correction slope of -0.5 per pH unit and its reference pH of 4 are held, and so is what the correlation does below that reference. The 250 bar fugacity cap is held and so is anything above it. The H2S partial pressure threshold of 0.0035 bar is held. The two H2S to CO2 transition ratios are held. The 100 Pa film stripping threshold and the 50 Pa moderate band are held. The Blasius coefficients 0.046 and -0.2 and the laminar to turbulent switch at Reynolds 4000 are held. The rate category bands of 0.1, 0.5 and 1.0 mm/yr are held, which means the category word is held with them.

One entry on that list is a question rather than a constant. Whether the protective scale factor belongs on the reaction term or on the combined rate is unresolved. This module applies it to the combined rate, after the series combination, and the two readings give materially different answers whenever mass transfer controls. Which of them the published correlation intends is not established here.

## What not provided means, which is stronger

The eleventh held item shades into the second list: an erosional velocity criterion, a pitting criterion and a sulphide stress cracking or hydrogen induced cracking criterion are absent altogether. Alongside them `NOT_PROVIDED` names an inspection interval, a minimum or retirement thickness, a fitness-for-service assessment, and the statement that the one rate here is a general uniform rate. Held items are waiting on a source. These are gone from the scope.

## The twelfth item, which is neither

A sour-service severity region and the material guidance that ran off it were withdrawn. They are absent from both lists, because they are not waiting on a source and they are not a gap in scope. They were removed because a curve carrying a standard's name and telling an engineer what steel to buy is a claim that retuning cannot repair. The engine returns `regionProvided` false and `materialGuidanceProvided` false today and will keep doing so, so the absence arrives as a field and a caller cannot read it as an unset property.

Every one of the eighteen graded fields in this course sits clear of all twelve.

## Exercise

Open the shipped case and copy out the `limits` and `notProvided` blocks. Count the entries in each. Then take the four things a reader of this studio will look for, which are an inspection interval, a minimum thickness, a retirement thickness and a fitness-for-service assessment, and say for each one which list it falls on and what that difference commits the engine to.

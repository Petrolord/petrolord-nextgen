# Onward

You can now say how much a live annulus may carry. The next tier deals with the well you are never going to touch again.

## What this tier gave you

A defensible operating limit on an annulus, and the ability to name which element produced it, at what vertical depth, under which role factor, and whether the answer was a pressure at all.

## What it did not give you

Any way to make the pressure stop mattering.

Every tool in this tier manages a well still in service, with a valve, a gauge and somebody watching it. When module 5 handed you a well that could not hold what was in it, the tier had nothing beyond the finding, because every remedy is a change to the well rather than to the sum.

## The Expert tier

Taking the well permanently out of service.

Permanent abandonment removes the operator from the loop. No gauge, no bleed, no annual test, nobody on site. What stands between the source and the surface has to do it unattended, for as long as the source has pressure.

That changes the object of study. Instead of an element with a rating you get a plug, a column of set cement placed across the hole, and the tier starts with placing one so it stays where you put it. A balanced plug is a displacement problem before it is a barrier.

Then the rules a permanent barrier has to satisfy. It has to be long enough, it has to sit on something, it has to cover the source with margin above it, and where the barrier is the cement outside the pipe rather than inside it, whether anybody logged it changes what is required.

## Where else this goes

**Cementing** designs the slurry and the placement this tier only assumes.

**Well Design and Surveys** supplies the trajectory every vertical depth comes from.

**Well Control** handles the pressure source while the well is still open.

## One thing to keep

Keep asking what the difference is between. It served every number in this tier and it does not stop at the end of it: a plug that holds is a plug whose resistance exceeds what pushes on it, and that is a difference too.

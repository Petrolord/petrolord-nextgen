# The story so far

Five modules, one arithmetic, and a single question that answers every one of them.

## The claim

Every number in this tier is a DIFFERENCE, not a level. A reader who asks "difference between what and what" gets each of them right, and a reader who treats any of them as an absolute pressure gets them wrong in a predictable direction.

## Where each module put the difference

**Module 1.** A sealed annulus pressures up because it has nowhere to put expansion, and the limit protects the weakest thing bounding it. MAASP is the limit on the annulus; MAWOP is the working limit under the RP 90 convention. This tier computes rows, picks a governing row, clamps the result and raises a flag. It computes no leak rate, no cause and no time.

**Module 2.** One row per bounding element, each a rating term less a hydrostatic term. The governing row is the MINIMUM over the rows and not the first of them. A selection over rows is logic rather than data, and it is where a table gets read wrong.

**Module 3.** The RP 90 factors attach to a role rather than to a pipe: 0.5 outer casing burst, 0.8 inner casing burst, 0.75 inner tubing collapse, 1 for a shoe or a bare rating. An outer string is derated hardest because it is the one you can least easily inspect and repair. On the published well that gives the inversion: the 40000000 Pa outer casing allows 17606905.05541501 Pa while the weaker 35000000 Pa liner allows 26855228.63225454 Pa. The role, not the steel, set the order.

**Module 4.** A rating is a differential across a wall, so only the density difference is charged as head. A heavier annulus buys margin on the string it sits outside of and takes it away from the next one in.

**Module 5.** Push the far side to nothing and the difference stops fitting inside the rating. The row goes negative, the engine reports the maximum of zero and the governing row, and the flag turns the output from a pressure into a finding.

## The one question

Every quantity above is the gap between two things:

- A rating is the difference the wall can stand between inside and outside.
- A head is the difference between two columns, charged over a true vertical depth.
- An allowable is a rating less a head.
- A governing value is the smallest of those allowables.
- A negative row is a difference that ran out before you applied anything.

## The one sentence

Nothing in this tier is a pressure at a point. Everything in it is a gap, and the well's limit is the smallest gap it has.

## Exercise

Take each of the five bullets above and write, in your own words, what the two things being differenced are.

Then take any MAASP figure you have used in your own work and say which of the five it was.

# What the rule does not check

A programme that passes has satisfied a checklist, and this lesson is about the distance between that and a well that is properly abandoned.

## The secondary test, stated in full

The engine header defines the secondary as a plug that "backs it up from above (length rule, set entirely above the source top; a second source-covering plug also qualifies)".

That is the whole test. Above the source top, long enough, and the plug is that zone's secondary. Nothing asks what the plug is set against, or what lies in the annulus behind it.

The standard the rules are drawn from asks for more. A permanent barrier is expected to sit against a competent formation with annular isolation behind it, so pressure cannot pass around the cement in the hole. The engine tests neither condition, and does not claim to.

## What that lets through

P3 intermediate runs 1700 to 1810 m MD. The reservoir sand top is 2500 m MD. P3 is listed as the reservoir sand's secondary, because it is above the source top and it is long enough.

Between the top of the reservoir and the base of that plug lies a great deal of hole the checklist never inspects. There may be a permeable interval behind casing, there may be no cement in the annulus, and the rule as written will not notice. A plug a long way above a zone still qualifies as that zone's secondary.

## Why this is a scope limit and not a defect

The header says what the whole thing is: "This is a PLANNING checklist in the well programme tradition, not an operational procedure." It also says the rule set is a starting point, "defaults overridable; the standard document governs".

Read that way, the output is honest. It tells you the geometry of your proposed plugs satisfies a stated set of length and position rules. It never claimed to have checked anything else.

## The rest of what is absent

There is no formation competence test anywhere in the programme builder. There is no annular isolation check behind the secondary: the annular cement check from module four is a separate function the programme never calls, so the 30 m and 100 m figures play no part in the zone verdict. There is no cost, no time, and no operational sequence beyond the phase ordering, which sets barrier plugs deepest first, then the casing cut, then the surface phase.

The rule checks also run on the plug interval you proposed, not on the settled top the placement arithmetic produced. The two are different numbers, as module two showed.

3. Decide what evidence you would attach to a passing programme before signing it.

# The displacement

This is the number the driller actually pumps, and it is the last chance to put the cement in the right place.

{{panel:wi-pa-explorer}}

## What you are filling

By the time the cement is in the hole, the stinger bore contains, from the bottom up, slurry to the balanced height, then spacer behind, then whatever you pump next. The displacement is what it takes to fill the rest of that bore.

The engine writes it as the inside capacity times the plug base depth less the balanced height, minus the spacer behind. That first term is the bore from surface down to the as-pumped top, which is the whole of the string standing above the slurry. The second term removes the part of it the spacer behind already occupies.

On the published fixture that is 16.301 m3, with 1 m3 of spacer ahead in play. With no spacer at all the same well takes 16.688 m3, because the full bore above the slurry is then yours to fill.

| Spacer ahead, m3 | Displacement, m3 |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 16.688 |
| 0.5 | 16.495 |
| 1 | 16.301 |
| 2 | 15.915 |
| 4 | 15.142 |

## More cement means less displacement

The excess sweep on the same well runs the displacement the other way, from 16.608 m3 at zero excess down to 15.076 m3 at 100 per cent excess. That reads backwards until you see what the slurry is doing. A bigger job stands taller inside the string as well as outside it, so it has already filled part of the bore you would otherwise be displacing.

If you carry a habitual displacement volume from job to job, changing the excess alone is enough to make it wrong.

## Why the error is invisible

Nothing about a mis-displaced plug announces itself. Over-displace and you chase slurry out of the shoe, unbalance the columns and leave the top deeper and leaner than designed. Under-displace and you pull the stinger with cement still in it, which strips slurry out of the plug on the way up and leaves the top high, contaminated, or both.

In each case the pumps run smoothly, the pressures look ordinary, and the first real evidence arrives hours later when you tag. That is why the displacement is calculated rather than judged, and why it is recalculated whenever the geometry, the spacer or the excess changes.

# The story so far

Five modules, one idea: every rule in this tier is a proxy for evidence you cannot collect once the rig has left.

## The rules are all standing in for something

An abandonment is judged long after the last person leaves. Nobody will pressure test that plug again, nobody will look at the cement, and nobody will be there when the zone starts to move. So the rules do not ask whether the barrier works. They ask for something you can demonstrate now, standing in for the thing nobody will ever check.

Length stands in for a seal you cannot test again. One hundred metres of cement in open hole is not a measurement of sealing quality. It is the amount of cement that makes a sealing failure unlikely enough to accept without further proof.

A foundation stands in for a plug that cannot fall. Put the same plug on a mechanical or tagged foundation and the requirement drops from 100 m to 50 m. Half the cement, because the thing the extra cement was insuring against has been demonstrated instead.

A log stands in for cement you cannot see. Annular cement needs 100 m unlogged and 30 m logged, so the log is worth 70 m of cement. You are being paid, in metres, for having looked.

## The arithmetic follows the same logic

Module two's identity is the cleanest case. At zero excess the settled top lands exactly on the design top, 1850 m MD in the worked example, while the as-pumped top sits shallower because the slurry is still spread between annulus and stinger bore. Add 20 per cent excess and the settled top comes in at 1820 m MD, 30 m above the design top.

You cannot watch any of that happen. You compute it before you pump and tag it afterwards, and the tag is the evidence that survives you.

## And what the rules do not stand in for

Module five's last lesson is the counterweight. The checklist tests position and length. It does not test formation competence or the annulus behind a secondary, and it says so in its own header. A passing programme means nothing in this rule set objects. It does not mean the abandonment is complete.

Hold both. The tier teaches you to satisfy the rules and to know what satisfying them has and has not proved.

## Exercise

3. List the three things you would still want to see before signing a programme this engine has passed.

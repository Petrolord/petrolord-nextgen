# Why the note matters

The fundamental is the only number a string can produce that stops a design before a card is ever marched.

{{panel:pd-string-explorer}}

## The refusal

Ask the teaching well ODUMA-4 for 60 spm against its own fundamental of 59.134268422 spm and the design returns ok false with one error: "At 60 strokes a minute this string is being driven at or above its own natural frequency (59.1 spm). Nothing predicted there would be trustworthy: slow the unit down, shorten the string or run a stiffer taper."

Nothing else comes back. No card, no loads, no stroke. The highest speed that string will accept is anything strictly below 59.134268422 spm.

## Why a refusal rather than a warning

Because the alternative is a number. A march that is driven at the string's own resonance does not settle into a repeating cycle in any meaningful sense, and whatever it returned would carry the same field names, the same units and the same air of a result as an answer from a well behaved design. A warning attached to that would be read once and clicked past.

The message is also unusual in naming three remedies rather than one, and they are the three levers the fundamental actually has: the speed, the length and the taper.

## Where working designs sit

ODUMA-4 runs at 10 spm against a fundamental of 59.134268422 spm, a ratio of 0.169106683. The published taper's second mode stands 90.364754474 spm above its first, and ODUMA-4's stands 93.437045323 spm above its own, so a real design at a working speed is nowhere near either the fundamental or anything above it.

## The note is a design lever

Walking the split of a 5000 ft two size string moves the fundamental from 48.865672625 spm to 53.642386647 spm without changing the length, the rod sizes or the fluid. Length moves it further: a uniform 7/8 string over 6000 ft rings at 40.721902461206 spm, while the 5000 ft published taper rings at 53.362124006 spm. Depth is the biggest lever on this number and usually the one thing a designer cannot change.

## What the gate does not check

It checks one speed against one number, and it is a strict inequality. A design just under its fundamental passes exactly as a design at a sixth of it does, with no note in the output about which one it was. And it checks the number the engine found, not the number the string has. Those are the same thing on the strings in this course, but the check itself cannot tell the difference.

## Exercise

Write the refusal ODUMA-4 gives at 60 spm and name each of the three remedies the message offers.

Then say what the ratio 0.169106683 tells you about that design, and what a design with a ratio close to one would still be told by this engine.

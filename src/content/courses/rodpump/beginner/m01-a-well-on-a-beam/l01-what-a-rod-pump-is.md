# What a rod pump is

A crank turns, a beam rocks, and a steel string thousands of feet long carries that motion down to a plunger that moves a different distance.

## The machine, top to bottom

A motor drives a gearbox, the gearbox turns a crank, the crank drives a four-bar linkage, and the linkage rocks a beam whose front end lifts the polished rod. The published linkage gives a stroke of 106.687717 in and spends 0.544444 of the cycle going up, so the machine is not symmetric in time. Below the polished rod hangs the rod string, and the published taper is 7/8 rods over 3000 ft on top of 3/4 rods over 2000 ft, 9940.000000 lb of steel in air. At the bottom is a barrel, a standing valve, and a plunger carrying a travelling valve.

## The stroke at the top is not the stroke at the bottom

The rod string is a spring before it is anything else. The published taper has a spring rate of 267.091373300 lb/in, so a fluid load of 5000 lb stretches it 18.720185 in. The plunger cannot begin to lift until that stretch is taken up, and the surface stroke pays for it.

That is the static story and not the whole one. The string is also a wave guide. A 7/8 rod carries a stress wave at 16288.760984482 ft/s, so the motion the polished rod starts arrives at the pump late, reflects, and returns while the polished rod is still travelling. What the plunger does is the answer to a wave problem.

## The string has a note

A bar held at the top and free at the bottom rings at a frequency of its own. The published taper rings at 53.362124006 spm, and the published uniform 7/8 string, 6000 ft of it, at 40.721902461206 spm. That is a gate rather than a curiosity: a design at or above its own string's fundamental is refused, with a message naming the speed.

## What is settled before anything is marched

Weight in air 9940.000000 lb, buoyed weight 8673.757961783 lb, spring rate 267.091373300 lb/in, fundamental 53.362124006 spm, linkage stroke 106.687717 in. Every one is closed form and independent of how fast the unit runs.

Everything else waits. The plunger stroke, both polished rod loads, the card area, the horsepower and every warning about them come out of a solved card, and a card takes a march.

## Exercise

Write down the five numbers the published taper and the published linkage settle before a single time step is taken, and beside each one say whether pumping speed, fillage or plunger diameter would change it.

Then say in one sentence why 18.720185 in of stretch under 5000 lb is not yet an answer for how far the plunger travels.

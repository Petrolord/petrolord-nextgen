# The story so far

Five modules, four objects, and not one time step anywhere in them.

## Four objects, and three of them are closed form

The string is a compliance sum, Archimedes and a stepped bar eigenvalue. The linkage and the gearbox are a four-bar closure and the derivative of it. The pump is a differential times an area and a volume per stroke. The fourth object, the card, needs a march, and that is where this tier stops.

## The string

The published taper is 7/8 rods over 3000 ft above 3/4 rods over 2000 ft: 9940.000000 lb in air, a buoyancy factor of 0.872611464968 in fluid of specific gravity 1.00, and a buoyed weight of 8673.757962 lb. Its elastic constant is 3.744037060e-3 in/lb and its spring rate 267.091373300 lb/in.

Compliances add and stiffnesses do not. The two sections contribute 1.962893216e-3 and 1.781143844e-3 in/lb, summing to 3.744037060e-3. Adding their spring rates instead gives 1070.889032254 lb/in, which is 4.009448 times the true rate.

The buoyancy factor is Archimedes and nothing else, one minus the fluid specific gravity over the steel value of 7.85, and at specific gravity 1.00 the published taper loses 1266.242038 lb to the fluid it hangs in.

The taper earns its complication: against the uniform 7/8 string over 6000 ft it is lighter by 3404.000000 lb in air, 25.509592 percent, and stiffer by 12.365341 lb/in, 4.854369 percent.

## The note

The engine scan returns a fundamental of 53.362124005810 spm on the published taper, and a dense mode scan on the same transfer matrix agrees to every figure printed. Its higher modes sit at 143.726878480 and 244.330411470 spm, ratios of 2.693424993 and 4.578723505 against the fundamental. A uniform bar would give three and five. A stepped bar is not a uniform bar.

## The linkage

Six lengths give a stroke of 106.687716837 in, an upstroke fraction of 0.544444444444 and a largest torque factor of 56.305306799 in. The torque factor is in inches, so it is half of a torque and the closure supplies no load to complete it.

## The pump

A 1.7500 in plunger has an area of 2.405281875 in2 and carries 4690.299657039 lb against 1950.0 psi of differential. Over the published stroke it sweeps 0.026449601 bbl per stroke, and at 10 spm the rating reads 380.874258458 bbl/d.

## What this tier does not answer

What the plunger travels, and what the well makes. Every number here is available before anything is marched, and the fluid load that drives the pump has already stretched the string 17.560655738 in before the plunger has moved.

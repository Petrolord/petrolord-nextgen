# Onward

Everything so far has been a spring at rest. The Professional tier lets it move, and the answers change.

## The static rule, stated in full

Subtract the string's stretch under the fluid load from the surface stroke. On the published taper at a surface stroke of 64.000000 in, a fluid load of 5000.000000 lb and an elastic constant of 3.744037060e-3 in/lb, the static stretch is 18.720185299 in and the spring rule gives 45.279814701 in.

That number does not depend on speed. A string driven slowly enough is only a spring, and the spring rule is right about it.

## The wave answer is longer

Marching the damped wave equation on the same case gives 45.286791250 in at 0.5 spm, 0.015408 percent above the spring rule, and 53.042713176 in at 15.0 spm, 17.144281 percent above it. It is longer at every speed on that ladder, and the extra travel is inertia: the string is still moving when the polished rod turns round.

The ladder between those two ends is not a smooth climb. Individual speeds step back below the speed before them, because the settled cycle a march lands on depends on where the valves transfer relative to the wave returning from the pump. Three rising rows are not a trend.

## The loads move further than the stroke does

At 0.5 spm the peak polished rod load is 13756.379902 lb against a buoyed weight plus fluid load of 13673.757962 lb, and the minimum is 8586.448887 lb against a buoyed weight of 8673.757962 lb. Both land where a spring says they should. At 15.0 spm the same string gives a peak of 18752.731892 lb and a minimum of 3651.294230 lb, with nothing about the string, the fluid or the geometry changed.

## And then what the card hides

The Expert tier asks how much of a reported number is a measurement and how much is an artefact of sampling. At 5 spm the march takes 11728 steps in a cycle and the card read for the loads has 181 points. The loads a design reports come off the second number, not the first.

## What you take with you

The string's weight, spring rate and note. The linkage's stroke and its torque factor in inches. The pump's area, load and rating. Above all, the habit of asking what a number assumed before quoting it.

## Exercise

Write the spring rule for the published taper from its three inputs, then the wave answer at 0.5 spm and at 15.0 spm beside it.

Say which you would put on a design sheet, and what you would need to know to defend it.

# What the subsample costs

The size of the sampling error is a property of the case, not of the engine. On some designs it is a rounding difference and on others it reverses the verdict.

{{panel:pd-balance-explorer}}

## The published cases make it look harmless

On the published taper at 9 spm the default card reports a peak 0.546462 percent low and a minimum 1.061998 percent high. At 5 spm the peak is 0.029497 percent low and the minimum 1.148619 percent high. Anyone who priced the defect on those two cases would file it as immaterial.

On ODUMA-4 the same default reports the minimum 24.755505 percent high.

## The emptier the barrel, the sharper the transfer

Fillage moves it further. The teaching sweep holds everything else and walks the barrel down.

| Fillage | Reported over marched minimum | Peak reported low, percent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0000 | 1.018085 | 1.769006 |
| 0.9500 | 1.042170 | 1.401848 |
| 0.9000 | 1.247555 | 1.896103 |
| 0.8500 | 1.409938 | 2.290146 |
| 0.8000 | 3.434915 | 0.000000 |
| 0.7500 | 4.106471 | 6.062056 |
| 0.7000 | 7.188966 | 6.241462 |

A partly filled barrel sharpens the load transfer, and a sharp transfer is what a coarse card misses. At a fillage of 0.7000 the reported minimum is 1161.037714 lb where the march found 161.502733 lb.

## The rows where the sign changes

Across the speed sweep at the shipped grid the ratio reaches 3.380387 at 10.5 spm, where the reported minimum is 1843.274152 lb and the marched one 545.284933 lb. At 12.0 spm the reported minimum is 45.754029 lb and the marched minimum is -466.104183 lb. At 12.5 spm they are 504.816493 lb and -27.487266 lb.

On those two rows the two answers have opposite signs. A negative minimum load means the polished rod is pushing down and the rods are in compression, which is the single verdict the minimum exists to deliver, and the reported number delivers the opposite one.

## What it costs at the design point

On ODUMA-4 at the shipped defaults the reported minimum is 2625.472706 lb and the marched minimum is 2104.494479 lb, so the reported value is 1.247555 times the real one. The buoyed rod weight is 9466.342675 lb: the reported minimum sits 6840.869969 lb below it and the real one 7361.848196 lb below it.

## What it refuses

There is no single percentage to quote. The error is set by how fast the card is moving where the minimum falls, so it must be measured on the case in hand rather than carried over from another.

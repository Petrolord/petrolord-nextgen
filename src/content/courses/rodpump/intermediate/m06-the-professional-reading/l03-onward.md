# Onward

One card, and the three questions that follow: which of its numbers were sampled, which converged, and which inputs were read at all.

## What the Expert tier adds

**Which numbers were sampled.** The march accumulates a tension envelope over every one of its steps at every node, then decimates the surface card to a few hundred points and reads the peak and minimum polished rod loads off that subsample. On the teaching design, 186 points survive out of 6110. The reported minimum is 24.755505 percent above the marched one, the reported peak 1.896103 percent below it, and the plunger stroke does not move by a single figure, because it was never decimated. Two samplings of one march, in one return object, and they disagree: the envelope implies a peak of 19851.465785541 lb where the reported field says 19545.877783339 lb, a gap of 305.588002202 lb.

**Which numbers converged.** Raise the node count and the plunger stroke barely moves while the loads move by hundreds of pounds. Refining is not a proof either: the notPeriodic flag is not monotone in resolution.

**Which inputs were read.** Three arguments the design accepts are never used, proved by strict equality across its outputs, and the balance it does read is an input to the function that computes the card it derives from.

**And what a tempting result is not.** A speed sweep dips 1.470226 percentage points on the worst section loading. Moving the node count, which changes nothing about the well, moves the same number 4.283675 percentage points at the speed before that dip and 6.553883 at the speed after. The dip is not an optimum.

## Before you go

**Everything from this tier stays in force.** The card is still the design, the spring rule is still the static limit of a marched stroke, the horsepower is still measured at the polished rod, and a produced rate still stands on two numbers a caller typed.

**Print the threshold beside the verdict.** A warning is a comparison against a constant. The number it compared is always the better thing to report.

## The one sentence

You can read a card and say which of its numbers are design decisions. The next tier asks which of them survive the way they were computed.

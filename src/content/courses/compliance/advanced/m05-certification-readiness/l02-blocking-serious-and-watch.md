# Blocking, serious and watch

Lesson 1 read the ORASHI readiness list as a whole. This lesson reads its three severities, what sits under each, and which record in the register each item comes from. The as-of date is 2026-10-15, and every item is true at that date.

{{panel:compliance-readiness-explorer}}

## Blocking

Four items read blocking:

- "1 major nonconformity is open." ISF-2026-004 is the open Major nonconformity in the findings table.
- "3 applicable clauses have never been examined by an internal audit." The coverage table reads never for clauses 6.1.2, 7.2 and 7.5.3.
- "1 clause is marked conformant with no evidence reference recorded." In the register, clause 5.2 is the one row that claims conformity with its evidence record reading false.
- "1 clause is assessed nonconformant and not yet resolved." Clause 6.1.3 reads Nonconformant.

The first blocking item gives the reason in the engine's own words: a certification body will not recommend certification over one. Each of the four is a direct gap in what a management system has to show: a nonconformity unanswered, clauses the organization has not audited, a claim without its evidence, and a known failure still standing.

Read the third item beside clause 5.2's row. The sentence names the part the claim lacks, and it names one part: the evidence reference. missingEvidenceParts for clause 5.2 prints the same words, evidence reference. The register row agrees: clause 5.2's assessed column reads true, and its evidence record reads false. The sentence, the missing parts and the row all point at the one thing to record before the claim is complete.

## Serious

Three items read serious:

- "1 clause was last audited before this certification cycle began." Clause 4.3 reads stale true.
- "1 applicable clause has never been assessed at all." Clause 7.2 reads Not assessed.
- "1 corrective or preventive action is past its due date." ac1 reads overdue true.

A serious item is a gap the organization must close. A stale clause was audited once; it needs auditing again inside the cycle. An unassessed clause has no verdict yet. An overdue action is late, and it is still in progress.

## Watch

Four items read watch:

- "1 minor nonconformity is open." ISF-2026-005.
- "1 clause review is past due." Clause 4.3's next review, 2026-10-01, reads review overdue true.
- "1 finding is past its due date." ISF-2026-004 reads overdue true.
- The certificate item, which lesson 3 reads.

A watch item needs attention before it becomes something worse. The list places all four below the blocking and serious items.

## One record, more than one item

Some records raise more than one item. ISF-2026-004 is on the list twice: as the open Major nonconformity, blocking, and as the finding past its due date, watch. Clause 4.3 is on the list twice: stale, serious, and its review past due, watch. Clause 7.2 is on the list twice: never examined, blocking, and never assessed, serious. Each item asks a separate question of the record, and one record can fail more than one.

# What the next tier changes

This tier read one quality plan, its NCRs, one checklist audit and one audit programme. Every reading came back to the course's one sentence: nothing is typed as a status, and every gate refuses until the evidence, the date and the named person it asks for are on the record. The Expert tier keeps that sentence and moves it onto a management system certified against an ISO standard.

## What carries forward

Four habits from this tier carry straight into the next one.

1. **A request is a question put to a gate.** A refused request changes nothing on the record, and the refusal names the next thing missing.
2. **A decision is a date and a name.** Passing a hold point, waiving a point, agreeing a disposition, approving a programme: each needs its date and its person, and a waiver or a set-aside hold point needs its reason.
3. **Progress is counted.** planProgress, checklistProgress and programmeProgress each count from the record, and each prints null when the record is empty.
4. **Age is measured to a date.** An open NCR ages to the as-of date and a closed one stops at its closed date.

## What the Expert tier adds

The Expert tier works in the isoCompliance module, on a clause register for a management system. Where this tier read a checklist answer, that tier reads a claim that a clause of the standard is met, and the claim needs evidence, a date and a name before the engine records it.

Independence widens. This tier checked the lead auditor against the auditee. The Expert tier checks every examiner who records a result on a clause, and reads the lead auditor against every clause in the audit's scope.

Coverage enters. A clause is covered only by an audit that counts, and the Expert tier reads which audits count and over what period. That is where the reported audit from module five matters most: a report is what makes an examination count.

Findings deepen. This tier saw a finding raised with its type, its statement and its evidence, and a stop-work finding with its correction. The Expert tier reads a finding through to closure: the correction, the corrective action, the root cause and the check that the action worked.

Readiness replaces the percent. This tier's progress readings were percents counted from records. The Expert tier's certification readiness is a list of blockers, each one named, with no percent at all.

## What stays with the sibling course

A finding or an NCR can feed a risk register or a lesson learned. Risk scores, bands and the approval rules for change belong to the sibling course, Risk, Change & Learning, and this course sends you there for them.

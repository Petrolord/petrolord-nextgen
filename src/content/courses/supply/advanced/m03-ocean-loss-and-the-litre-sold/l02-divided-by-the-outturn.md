# Divided by the outturn

The importer pays the landed total and sells the outturn. So the cost of one litre sold is the landed total divided by the outturn litres. The digest puts it in one short sentence: the loss divides the cost. The landed total stays the same and the litres it is spread over fall.

## The sweep

The course holds the BADAGRY build-up fixed, with insurance on CIF and every rate invented for this course, and sweeps the ocean loss. The bill of lading is 45772751.75 litres throughout. The exchange rate, 1520.4000 naira to the dollar, is invented for this course as well.

Read the landed total column first. It prints 26513943.86 USD on every row. The ocean loss does not touch a single charge in the walk, because every charge is levied on the bill-of-lading quantity or on a value formed from it, and neither changes with the loss.

Then read the outturn column. At a loss of 0 the outturn is the bill of lading, 45772751.75 litres. At the BADAGRY loss of 0.45 percent it is 45566774.37 litres.

Then read the cost of a litre sold. At a loss of 0 it is 0.579252 USD. At 0.45 percent it is 0.581870 USD, which the engine turns into 884.6753 naira a litre at the invented exchange rate. That naira figure is the landed cost at the depot gate, the figure the pump price build-up in the next module starts from. The row at a loss of 1 percent reads the same way: 26513943.86 USD over 45315024.23 litres, printed as 0.585103 USD a litre sold.

## Why dividing is the right operation

The engine does not add the lost litres to the build-up as a charge. The landed total stays the same, and the litres it is spread over fall from the bill-of-lading litres to the outturn litres. On the BADAGRY cargo that is 26513943.86 USD over 45566774.37 outturn litres, which is the bill of lading less the BADAGRY loss of 0.45 percent of it.

Dividing by the outturn states what did happen. Every dollar in the walk was paid, and every one of them has to be recovered from the litres that arrived. The cost per litre sold is the landed total over the outturn. The engine prints it to six decimals of a dollar, and every later price in this tier is built on it.

## The per outturn litre column

The walk's own table carries a column in USD per outturn litre, and it is this division line by line. On the BADAGRY cargo FOB is 0.513357 USD per outturn litre, the freight 0.019773 and the course's invented import duty 0.030704. Each line is its amount over the same outturn, 45566774.37 litres. The column shows which line carries which part of the cost of a litre sold, in the unit that litre is sold in.

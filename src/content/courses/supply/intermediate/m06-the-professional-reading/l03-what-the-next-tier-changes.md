# What the next tier changes

This tier followed product already in the depot: the rack loaded it, the farm held it, the lane carried it, the forecourt sold it. The Expert tier goes back up the chain to where the product came from and forward to what the motorist pays. Its case is BADAGRY, one petrol cargo landed and priced to the nozzle, invented for this course like AKODO and IBAFO.

## What stays the same

The method does not change. A figure is a chain of measured inputs walked in a stated order, and the engine refuses or names every link nobody measured. The rack refused a bay count of 2.5. The lane named a blank driver cost. The Expert tier's engine refuses a cargo with no density, names every rate left blank, and labels an incomplete build-up as a floor in its own words.

The habits carry across: quote the engine's precision, keep units and currencies beside figures, and read a none as a missing input and never as a zero.

## What changes

**Units multiply.** A cargo is traded in tonnes, stored in cubic metres, sold in litres and quoted in barrels. The Expert tier converts one cargo between all four, and the conversion between mass and volume needs a density the engine never assumes.

**Order matters in a new way.** The landed cost of a cargo is walked in stages, from the price at the loading port through freight and insurance to the charges levied on arrival. Each charge names the base it bites on, and a base is fixed when the walk reaches it. A charge that tries to bite on a value not yet formed is refused. Its order is part of the answer.

**Rates are everywhere, and all of them are invented.** Freight, insurance, duty, port charges, margins, levies, taxes and exchange rates enter the Expert tier's calculations. The engine ships no rate for any of them, by design, and every rate in the course is invented for it. Never read one as a current figure in any market. Real rates are set by regulation and contract, differ by market and change.

**Losses divide.** On the lane a transit loss spread the trip's cost over fewer litres delivered. The next tier meets the same effect on a whole cargo at sea: the importer pays for what was loaded and sells what arrives.

**The price is a waterfall.** The pump price is built element by element from the landed cost at the depot gate. One of those elements is the transport to the station, the kind of cost this tier's lane computed per litre delivered. The tier then asks at what exchange rate a price cap stops covering the chain.

## What the next tier leaves out

The Expert tier prices a cargo and a litre. It does not value a business. No lesson in the course teaches discounting, a decision tree or a probabilistic valuation. Where a cargo's quality comes from, its assay and its blend, belongs to the `crude` course; a refinery's margin belongs to `refinery`.

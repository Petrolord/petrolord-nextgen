# What the next tier changes

## Where this tier stops

The Associate tier has stayed inside one terminal on one morning and one day. Every figure was a volume, and every figure traced back to a tape, a table or a typed input.

The Professional tier leaves the tank's measurement behind and asks what a depot does with its stock. It moves inland to IBAFO, an invented depot, and it adds three things this tier never touched: time spent waiting, money, and carbon.

## The loading rack as a queue

At IBAFO, trucks arrive at the loading rack at random and each spends a random time on a bay. The Professional tier models the rack as a queue with a stated number of bays and reads how often an arriving truck has to wait and for how long. The same rule you learned here carries straight across. A missing arrival rate or load time is refused, and a bay count must be a whole number. When the rack cannot keep up with its arrivals, the engine refuses to print an average wait, because none exists.

## The tank farm

IBAFO has several tanks, each with a heel. The Professional tier counts pumpable stock tank by tank, each tank's stock above its own heel and never below zero, and turns it into days of cover and turns a year. It is the first time a stock figure is used for planning.

## Money and carbon

The depot earns a throughput fee and carries costs, and the product it loses has a weight and emissions. Throughput economics keeps a money ledger and a carbon ledger side by side. From here on, rates appear. Every fee, cost, margin and emission factor in the course is invented for it, and each lesson says so the first time it uses one. A missing emission factor is not replaced by a guess; the carbon side is reported as not computed.

## The lane, the fleet and the station

Product leaving IBAFO travels by truck. The Professional tier walks a truck lane's cycle and the cost of a litre delivered, sizes a fleet as a whole number of trucks, and sizes a filling station's forecourt with the same kind of queue as the loading rack.

## What carries over unchanged

The one rule of this tier travels with you. A missing value stays missing. The Professional tier adds new inputs to that list, a bay count, a heel, a distance, a payload, and treats each one the same way.

The Expert tier then follows one petrol cargo, BADAGRY, from its purchase price to the price at the pump.

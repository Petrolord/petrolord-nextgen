# What LOPA does not do

A method is only usable when its edges are known. This lesson lists what this engine does not do, so that nothing in the tier is read as a claim it never made. Every item here is a declared limit, and each has somewhere else it properly belongs.

## LOPA does not grade a risk matrix

A LOPA row is FREQUENCY BASED. It multiplies a frequency per year by probabilities and compares the result with a tolerable frequency per year. Nothing in this engine takes a consequence category, a matrix score or a matrix band, and nothing in this course grades one.

The academy already teaches two risk matrices. The risk and change course grades the five by five matrix on its own bands, and the field development course teaches a second scale. Both rank a risk on scored bands. This course never re-grades either. A LOPA consequence is described in words, a fatality or a release reaching the jetty, and carried into the row through its tolerable frequency.

The engine's units line marks the boundary, verbatim: "frequencies per year; probabilities and PFDs dimensionless".

## The engine has no data of its own

It invents no number. The initiating event frequency, every enabling condition and conditional modifier probability, every IPL PFD, every failure rate and the tolerable frequency are inputs. It carries no failure rate data, and the rates used for teaching in this course are illustrative teaching inputs. No licensed table is reproduced anywhere in it.

## Two checks that are somewhere else

| not in this engine | where it belongs |
| --- | --- |
| a hardware fault tolerance check | a normative table in a licensed standard, which the engine does not restate |
| a high demand or continuous mode | every band in this engine is LOW DEMAND |

Both are real requirements on a real project. A function that meets its required PFDavg can still be inadmissible on architectural grounds, and a function called on often enough is in a different mode with a different measure. The Expert tier treats these as limits of the engine. What matters here is that a clean result from this engine is not, on its own, a complete safety requirement.

## What LOPA is good at

The list above is long, and the method survives it because its own job is narrow and well defined. Given one cause, one consequence, a set of named factors and a tolerable frequency, it produces a defensible statement of how much risk reduction is still missing, with every intermediate figure named and every judgement recorded as a flag or a probability somebody signed for. That is the product, and it is worth more than a broader method whose answers nobody can take apart.

# Onward

A diagnosis is a small object with a long reach. Be exact about what it earns and where it stops.

## What it earns

It turns a ratio history into a mechanism, a treatable flag, two slopes, two fit qualities, a span in log cycles, a window start in days and a confidence, then into seven gated verdicts whose reasons name the evidence that would reverse them. It prices the alternative in the same call chain: on the teaching geometry of ELELENWO-4, a case this course built rather than a published one, a drainage radius of 1180 ft and a wellbore radius of 0.354 ft, an acid job from a skin of 7.5 down to -2.2 is worth a multiplier of 2.879215612184 against a floor of -7.361728083308. And it declines in a form you can act on: "The history does not settle the question. That is an answer: it says do not spend money on a treatment chosen by guesswork."

## Where it hands over

It sizes nothing. No cost, no volume, no duration, no rate and no pressure appear anywhere, and the only dimensional number it returns is the window start in days. There is no reservoir model under it: the ratio history and the derivative column beside it are inputs, and nothing here computes either. The screening reads five fields off a well row and one off the diagnosis.

## The limit worth remembering

For any power-law history the ratio and its derivative carry the same log-log slope, so nothing separates ordinary displacement from channelling except how steep the climb is. The engine says so itself: "that boundary is the weak part of the reading". A channelling boundary of 1.3 and an ambiguous band of 0.25 are choices about where to cut, not measurements. And the window is worth more than the cut: on one teaching history it moves the derivative slope by 0.370920348, while the margin carrying the recommendation was 0.142132492322.

## What is checked, and what is not

Two pure arithmetic functions get an independent route each, the log-log slope by Theil-Sen and the skin multiplier by a radial Darcy rate in SI. `chanDiagnosis`, `screenTreatments`, `rankTreatments` and `skinFromPiRatio` are asserted against nothing, so every verdict you produce is your own reading carrying the engine's name.

## What you have finished

Read a diagnosis, name which threshold decided it, say what the screening did with the answer. Quote the window beside every slope and the mechanism beside every verdict.

## Exercise

Write the three things a diagnosis establishes and the four it does not.

Then say what a ratio history can be doing that one reading, taken on one window, would never show.

# What a block reason says

A block reason is a fixed string chosen by the mechanism id. It carries no number, and only one of the two the module writes tells you what would change its mind.

{{panel:pd-channel-explorer}}

## The two of them, in full

Read a displacement mechanism and the water shutoff blocks with "The diagnostic says ordinary displacement. The water is arriving because the reservoir is swept, which is not a well problem and no treatment on this well will change it."

Hand the screening no diagnosis at all and it blocks with "The mechanism has not been established. A shutoff squeeze on a coning well is money down a hole, and this history does not say which it is. Get more production history, or a production log, before spending anything."

The first closes the question. The second names the two things that would reopen it, and it is the honest refusal here.

## The reason line beside the block is not a measurement

On teaching well ELELENWO-4, a case this course built rather than a published one, the displacement block arrives with one reason: "Water cut is 75 percent and the derivative is flat." That reading was taken at a late fraction of 0.9, where the same 38 samples return a derivative slope of 1.254360095 at a fit quality of 0.995286721 as a fraction. Nothing about that is flat.

The word is a label for the branch. At the default late fraction of 0.5 the same well takes the channelling branch and the string becomes "Water cut is 75 percent and the derivative is climbing, which says the water has a path of its own." at a derivative slope of 1.442132492. Two strings, one calling the derivative flat and one calling it climbing, on one history read through two windows, and the dial separating them moves the slope by 0.370920348 across its range. The well row also carries 74.5 percent water and every string prints 75 percent.

## What a block reason refuses to carry

No slope, no fit quality, no confidence, no window start, no sample count. The diagnosis holds all five and none reaches the block reason. Nor is any of it checked: `screenTreatments` is asserted against nothing, and the published file carries no expected verdict and no expected block reason.

## The mistake

Pasting a block reason into a report as the evidence. It is the branch restating itself, identical on every well that reaches it. The defensible sentence names the mechanism, the derivative slope, the window it was taken over and the confidence, then quotes the block reason as the consequence.

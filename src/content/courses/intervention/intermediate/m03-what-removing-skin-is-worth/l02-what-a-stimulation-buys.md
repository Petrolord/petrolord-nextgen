# What a stimulation buys

The same unit of skin removed from a heavily damaged well and from a nearly clean one are not worth the same. On one published geometry the two buy 3.717372 percent and 12.657067 percent.

## One unit of skin, seven starting points

A derived sweep on the published geometry, a drainage radius of 2000.000000 ft and a wellbore radius of 0.350000 ft. Every row removes exactly one unit of skin, and only the starting point changes:

| Skin, from | Skin, to | Multiplier | Uplift, percent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20.0 | 19.0 | 1.037173720 | 3.717372 |
| 12.0 | 11.0 | 1.052908025 | 5.290802 |
| 8.0 | 7.0 | 1.067110830 | 6.711083 |
| 5.0 | 4.0 | 1.084028497 | 8.402850 |
| 3.0 | 2.0 | 1.101002709 | 10.100271 |
| 2.0 | 1.0 | 1.112350404 | 11.235040 |
| 1.0 | 0.0 | 1.126570669 | 12.657067 |

The denominator is a sum, so a unit of skin is a larger fraction of a small denominator than of a large one. A stimulation on a lightly damaged well buys the most per unit of skin, and one on a badly damaged well buys the most in total, because it removes so many units.

## What a whole job buys

Teaching well ELELENWO-4, designed for this course rather than published, has a drainage radius of 1180 ft, a wellbore radius of 0.354 ft and a skin of 7.5. Its denominator before treatment is 14.861728083308 and its flow efficiency against an undamaged well is 0.495348054, so it is making a little under half of what the same rock would give with no skin on it.

A designed acid job taking that skin from 7.5 down to -2.2 returns a multiplier of 2.879215612184. The denominator falls from 14.861728083 to 5.161728083 and the flow efficiency after the job is 1.426213850, above one because a negative skin is better than no skin at all.

## Reading the two together

Flow efficiency is measured against an undamaged well, so 0.495348054 says how much is being lost. The multiplier is measured against the well as it stands, so 2.879215612184 says what the job changes. Neither is a rate.

## The mistake

Carrying an uplift percentage from one well to another. A treatment that bought 12.657067 percent on a well at a skin of 1.0 and one that bought 3.717372 percent on a well at a skin of 20.0 did exactly the same thing to the rock. Quoting either figure as a treatment's performance describes the well it was done on, not the treatment.

## What the multiplier does not include

Drawdown, permeability, thickness, fluid properties, the lift the well has, and whether the treated skin holds. Everything else in the rate cancels between the two denominators, which is why the ratio is trustworthy and why it is not a forecast.

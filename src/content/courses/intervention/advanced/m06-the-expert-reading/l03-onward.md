# Onward

A Chan diagnosis is a short object with a long reach. Be precise about what it earns and where it hands over.

## What it earns

It turns a ratio history into a statement a production log can refute: two slopes, two fit qualities, a span in log cycles and the mechanism they point at. It does that arithmetic well. On the published power law the engine reproduces the independently computed slope of 1.350000000000 with a difference of 0.0000e+0, and on the published skin pairs it reproduces a radial Darcy calculation built in SI, the first pair matching its published multiplier of 2.012565355861 with a difference of 0.0000e+0. It also ranks what to do about the answer, in the order candidate, consider, marginal, blocked, unknown, no.

## Where it hands over

It sizes nothing. The only dimensional number it returns anywhere is the start of the late window in days; every verdict it returns is a string or a boolean. There is no rate in it, no volume, no time to payout and no cost, so a treatment it calls a candidate is a candidate on the evidence and not yet on the money.

It hands over sooner than most people expect on the physics too. Channelling and displacement are separated by steepness alone, because for any power-law history the ratio and its derivative have the same log-log slope, so a boundary at 1.3 with an ambiguous band of 0.25 is a convention about where one picture stops. Coning at a slope below -0.1 is the one qualitatively different reading, and its answer is not a treatment at all: less drawdown, a different completion, or living with it.

## The limit worth remembering

The two arithmetic functions are checked to machine precision and every function that returns a verdict is checked against nothing. A number in this module can be exactly right and the sentence beside it still wrong, because the sentence was never asserted by anyone.

## What you have finished

Which samples a fit declined to draw, what the window is worth against the threshold that decides the spend, which spellings of no value turn into a reassuring answer, where the guards sit and where the well needs them to sit, and what a screening cannot know about the fluid it was handed.

Read the window before the slope, count what survived the filter, and name the fluid beside every verdict.

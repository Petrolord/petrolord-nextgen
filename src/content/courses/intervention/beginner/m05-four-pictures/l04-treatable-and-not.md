# Treatable and not

One of the four labels carries treatable = true: channelling. Coning, displacement and not determined all carry treatable = false, and that flag is a field in a lookup table, not a calculation.

{{panel:pd-diagnostic-explorer}}

## What the flag stands for

Channelling is treatable: "Water is arriving through a path of its own, behind pipe, a thief zone, a fracture or a high-permeability streak. That is plumbing, and plumbing can be sealed."

Coning is not: "There is nothing to squeeze: shut off the bottom perforations and the cone re-forms above them." Displacement is not: the water arrives as it does in a swept reservoir. Not determined is not: the history has not settled the question.

## The same well row under four mechanisms

Nothing about the well changes down these rows, only the diagnosis handed to the screening. These are derived runs on the published histories.

| Mechanism read | Water shutoff | Reduce drawdown | Recompletion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| channelling, confidence high | candidate | no | candidate |
| coning, confidence high | blocked | candidate | consider |
| displacement, confidence high | blocked | no | consider |
| flat history, read as displacement | blocked | no | consider |

Coning is the row worth staring at. It carries treatable = false and is the only one that turns reducing drawdown into a candidate. Treatable false does not mean nothing to do, it means nothing to seal.

## What a block actually says

On a displacement reading the squeeze comes back blocked with one reason: "The diagnostic says ordinary displacement. The water is arriving because the reservoir is swept, which is not a well problem and no treatment on this well will change it."

On a channelling reading the drawdown cut comes back no: "Only worth it for coning, and the diagnostic does not say coning. Choking a channelling well back gives away rate without touching the water path."

Both are decisions, not failures.

## The verdict with no diagnosis at all

Hand the screening no diagnosis and the squeeze is still blocked: "The mechanism has not been established. A shutoff squeeze on a coning well is money down a hole, and this history does not say which it is. Get more production history, or a production log, before spending anything."

That refusal is the part of the module that gets this right.

## The mistake

Reading treatable = false as a verdict on the whole well. The mechanism gates the water treatments only. Matrix acid is gated on skin alone: candidate above a skin of 2, marginal above 0, no at or below 0, and unknown when no skin was entered. Recompletion is a candidate when the mechanism is channelling or the skin is above 8. A well can be untreatable for water and still worth a job.

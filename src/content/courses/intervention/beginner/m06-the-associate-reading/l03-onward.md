# Onward

A fitted line and a geometry group are small things with a long reach. Be precise about what each one earns and where it stops.

## What they earn

The fit turns two columns into five numbers that can be checked by somebody else: a slope, an intercept, a fit quality, a point count and a span in log cycles. On a clean power law it is exact, and the check is real, because the published slope comes from Theil-Sen, the median of every pairwise slope, which shares no mean, no square and no covariance with least squares. On the published eleven points the engine returns 1.350000000000 and 1.308332819650 against those same two values, a difference of 0.0000e+0.

The group prices removing skin as a pure ratio. A skin of 8.000 taken to 0.000 on the published geometry returns 2.012565355861, and a skin that does not change returns 1.000000000000000 exactly, to the last bit, because it is one number divided by itself.

## Where they hand over

`logLogSlope` knows nothing about wells. Nothing in that call knows the x axis was days or the y axis a ratio, and it will fit a straight line through anything positive.

The module sizes no treatment. The only number with a unit it produces anywhere is the start of the late window, in days. A multiplier is a fold increase and not a rate, so no barrel, no pressure and no time appears in it. The group is pseudo-steady state on a circular drainage area, and the 3/4 is that convention rather than a tuned constant.

The mechanism side hands over soonest of all. `chanDiagnosis`, `screenTreatments`, `rankTreatments` and `skinFromPiRatio` are asserted against nothing at all, so every mechanism, confidence and verdict this module prints is its own claim.

## The limit worth remembering

A fit quality is a statement about the line, not about the well. On the published coning history the engine returns an r-squared of 0.948751314445 while the two independent slope routes differ by 1.514312e-2, on data with no noise in it whatever.

## What you have finished

What a fitted line licenses you to say, what its point count hides, what a geometry group is worth and where its floor sits, and why a mechanism label is a slope compared against a constant somebody chose. Read the window before the slope, and name the threshold beside every label.

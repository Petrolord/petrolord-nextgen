# Where this course hands over

This course follows a gas from a flare at Egbema to a bus at Ibafo. At several points its engines call another engine or hand a figure on, and at those points this course stops. This lesson names each point and what the engine prints there.

## The functions lpgCng calls

The LPG & CNG Rollout Studio calls lpgCng, which calls the loading-rack queue in terminalDepot for its carousel and forecourt, the gas Z factor in production/gasProperties and the compressor train in facilities/compression. Three of this tier's results rest on another engine's work.

## The queue

The carousel and the forecourt are the loading-rack queue. This tier read what the queue printed on KANO's 16 positions and IBAFO's 2 or 3 dispensers. In this academy, the supply course (`supply`) is where the queue itself is taught, on a depot's loading rack.

## The compressor

cngCompression does not compute compression itself. Its basis sentence: "Staging, polytropic head and real-gas Z from the Facilities compression engine; this converts units and does not reimplement the thermodynamics." This course read the bridge: qMMscfd 0.4473, 72.5189 psia, 4 stages. Head, efficiency and power are the Facilities engine's, and the Facilities course teaches them.

## The discount rate

Two engines here hand a cash flow on and do not discount it. conversionEconomics says so on the bus's result: "Simple payback is undiscounted. Anything needing a discount rate belongs in the sanctioned economics engine." Its cash flow is year0 -1200000.0000 and recurring 3825710.1400 naira. The Professional tier met the same hand over in routeEconomics, whose note reads: "Capital, operating cost and revenue are assembled here and handed to the sanctioned economics engine. A second discounted cash flow in this module would be a second answer." This course grades a simple payback and a breakeven credit price. The Economics & Commercial module teaches the discounted figures.

## The inventory

This course computed a flare's CO2, methane and CO2e from the analysis under two efficiencies, and on the bus it printed kgCo2eAvoidedPerYear, 2365.8000, on illustrative emission factors. A methane GWP here is the case's stated input. A flare is also a line in an emissions inventory, and the carbon course (`carbon`), beside this one in Energy Transition, is where the inventory is taught.

## What stays here

What this course owns is the gas as a measured quantity and each vessel, queue, bank and cascade on the basis its engine states. The one sentence the course was built on reads: a flare is a measured gas before it is an emission or a product. Everything past the hand over points above is another course's.

In practice, a rollout study moves between these courses as its questions change.

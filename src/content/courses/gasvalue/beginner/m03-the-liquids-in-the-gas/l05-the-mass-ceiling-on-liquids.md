# The mass ceiling on liquids

The gallon figures say how much liquid the gas holds by volume. characteriseGas also reports it by mass, against the mass of the whole gas, and the engine names that pair as a ceiling.

{{panel:gasvalue-flare-explorer}}

## Two masses in one Mscf

kgPerMscf is the mass of one Mscf of the gas. Module two built it: the mass in one Mscf is the moles in a thousand standard cubic feet times the molar mass.

c3PlusKgPerMscf is the part of it that is propane and heavier. It is the same mass, counted over C3, IC4, NC4 and C5 alone.

The course states what the two figures are: they are the most any route can take out of a thousand standard cubic feet, by mass.

## The three gases

| gas | kgPerMscf | c3PlusKgPerMscf | c3PlusKgPerMscf over kgPerMscf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EGBEMA | 26.7066 | 6.6647 | 0.2496 |
| OGUTA | 21.0224 | 0.9798 | 0.0466 |
| studio opening gas | 25.4954 | 5.5819 | 0.2189 |

One Mscf of EGBEMA carries 26.7066 kg, and 6.6647 kg of it is propane and heavier. The last column prints the share: c3PlusKgPerMscf over kgPerMscf is 0.2496.

OGUTA carries 21.0224 kg in one Mscf, 0.9798 kg of it propane and heavier, a share of 0.0466. The studio's opening gas carries 25.4954 kg, 5.5819 kg of it propane and heavier, a share of 0.2189.

## A ceiling on mass

This course calls the pair the mass ceiling, because of what the two figures are: the most any route can take out of a thousand standard cubic feet, by mass. The pair is the whole mass of one Mscf and the propane and heavier part of it. No route can take more than 26.7066 kg out of one Mscf of EGBEMA, and no route can take more than 6.6647 kg of propane and heavier out of it.

This tier reads the ceilings. The Professional tier sets each route's yield against what the gas can give, and the course's one sentence states the rule it applies there: a route can yield no more than the gas holds.

## Gallons and kilograms side by side

The two ways of reading the liquids sit side by side:

| gas | gpmC3Plus (propane and heavier) | c3PlusKgPerMscf |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EGBEMA | 3.2205 | 6.6647 |
| OGUTA | 0.4890 | 0.9798 |
| studio opening gas | 2.6894 | 5.5819 |

gpmC3Plus is the propane and heavier as liquid gallons per Mscf, derived with the liquid densities. c3PlusKgPerMscf is the same cut as a mass per Mscf. The richness word is read off the gallons. The ceiling is stated on the mass.

The gallons carry the liquid densities in them, and a blank density leaves them null.

## Reading the ceiling in the explorer

The flare explorer shows the mass ceiling beside the liquids. Load EGBEMA and read kgPerMscf, c3PlusKgPerMscf and the share. Then load OGUTA and the studio's opening gas and read the same three figures against the table above.

# What the flare model leaves out

The flare model's limits are stated, and this lesson reads them as limits.

## Four stated limits

The course states four limits of the flare model, taught and never computed with:

- The efficiencies have no default.
- An unlit flare is not modelled.
- The methane GWP and any credit price are case inputs; the engine ships neither.
- The component heating values and liquid densities are typical figures.

## The efficiencies have no default

The rule the engine follows is 40 CFR 98.233(n). Its paragraph (n)(1) is a United States rule with tiered default pairs. EGBEMA is a flow station in Imo State. Whether a Nigerian flare study defaults to any of those tiers, or to the basis of the NUPRC flare regulations, is a regulation reading. Both efficiencies stay inputs.

abatement refuses a blank destruction efficiency, and its refusal gives its reason in its own words: "A flare destruction efficiency in (0, 1] is required. For a flare it is most of the answer and it is contested, so it is not assumed."

EGBEMA's 0.97 and 0.955 are the invented flare study's inputs, and this course does not say which tier a study should use.

## An unlit flare is not modelled

The engine's flare is lit. Gas sent to a flare that is not lit is vented, all of it methane. Every CO2, methane and CO2e figure you have read is a lit flare's, at the efficiencies typed.

## The GWP and credit prices are case inputs

The engine ships no methane GWP and no credit price. EGBEMA's GWP of 29.8 is the case's stated input, and the rows at 20 and 40 are for comparison only. Credit prices belong to the Professional tier, where they are also case inputs.

## The reference figures are typical

The component heating values and liquid densities in GAS_COMPONENT_REFERENCE are typical figures. The engine's note on the table says: "Molar masses and carbon numbers are definitional. Heating values and liquid densities are typical: the gas analysis and the certificate govern, and a measured value should replace these."

## The studio opens blank

The Flare Gas to Value Studio opens with both efficiencies and the GWP blank. On the studio's opening gas, at its opening parcel of 10 MMscfd and 350 days, with the efficiencies blank, abatement answers:

REFUSED: A flare destruction efficiency in (0, 1] is required. For a flare it is most of the answer and it is contested, so it is not assumed.

The studio does not open on a flare figure. It opens on the refusal, and the flare is computed once the efficiencies are typed.

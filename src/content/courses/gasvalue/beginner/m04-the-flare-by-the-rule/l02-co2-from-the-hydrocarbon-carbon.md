# CO2 from the hydrocarbon carbon

The flare's CO2 has two terms. This lesson reads the second of them: the combustion efficiency times the hydrocarbon carbon.

{{panel:gasvalue-flare-explorer}}

## The term in the rule

The engine's basis sentence gives the CO2 as: CO2 = the CO2 in the gas plus the combustion efficiency times the hydrocarbon carbon.

The second term has two factors.

The hydrocarbon carbon is the figure module two built. The hydrocarbon carbon per mole counts only the carbon that can burn. On EGBEMA it is 1.3320 per mole, against a carbon per mole of 1.3600 that counts every carbon atom, the CO2's included.

The combustion efficiency is the second factor. The rule separates two efficiencies, and the COMBUSTION efficiency is the share oxidised to CO2 and sets the CO2. EGBEMA's flare study gives 0.955.

So the carbon the combustion efficiency multiplies is the hydrocarbon carbon, 1.3320 per mole on EGBEMA. The CO2's own carbon, the gap of 0.0280 between the two counts, is outside this term.

## A gas that is only hydrocarbon

One of the engine's probes isolates this term. It flares a gas that is all methane, at EGBEMA's volume and days and at EGBEMA's efficiencies:

| probe | flareCo2Tonnes | flareCh4Tonnes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all methane, EGBEMA efficiencies | 133751.879 | 1531.658 |

Set the probe beside the rule. The rule reads the CO2 as the CO2 in the gas plus the combustion efficiency times the hydrocarbon carbon, and this probe's gas is methane and nothing else. The engine gives it 133751.879 tonnes of CO2 a year, at EGBEMA's combustion efficiency of 0.955. The CO2 is weighed at the molar mass the flare uses for it, 44.009 kg/kmol.

## Move the combustion efficiency, move the CO2

The engine was run on EGBEMA with both efficiencies given, and again with the combustion efficiency left out. Left out, the destruction efficiency stands in for it:

| EGBEMA | combustion efficiency used | flareCo2Tonnes | flareCh4Tonnes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| both efficiencies given | 0.955 | 182079.024 | 1136.490 |
| combustion efficiency left out | 0.97 | 184877.310 | 1136.490 |
| left out minus given | 0.0150 | 2798.286 | 0.000 |

The combustion efficiency used moves by 0.0150, from 0.955 to 0.97. The CO2 moves by 2798.286 tonnes a year, from 182079.024 to 184877.310. The methane stays at 1136.490 and its row in the difference reads 0.000.

The course reads the pair of rows in one sentence: the stand-in moves the CO2 and leaves the methane where it was, because the methane is set by the destruction efficiency alone. In the rule, the combustion efficiency is a factor of the CO2's second term and appears nowhere in the CH4 half.

## The whole CO2

On EGBEMA as given, the flare's CO2 is 182079.024 tonnes a year. It is the sum of the two terms. This lesson read the second. The next lesson reads the first, the CO2 already in the gas, through two probes on a gas that is all CO2.

## Reading it in the explorer

In the flare explorer, set up EGBEMA's flare with both efficiencies, then leave the combustion efficiency out and read the CO2, the methane and the note the engine attaches. Type 0.955 again and confirm the CO2 returns to 182079.024.

Say what the combustion efficiency multiplies in the rule, which figure moves when it moves, and which does not.

# The CO2 in the gas passes through

The first term of the flare's CO2 is the CO2 already in the gas. This lesson reads it through two probes on a gas that is all CO2.

{{panel:gasvalue-flare-explorer}}

## The term in the rule

The engine's basis sentence gives the CO2 as: CO2 = the CO2 in the gas plus the combustion efficiency times the hydrocarbon carbon.

The first term carries no efficiency. It is the CO2 in the gas, added as it stands.

Module one read the CO2 row: the CO2 row of the reference table carries one carbon per molecule and is marked inert: its carbon is counted when the carbon per mole is counted, and it is not a fuel. Module two read the gap it leaves between the two carbon counts: carbonPerMol minus hydrocarbonCarbonPerMol is the CO2 mole fraction, and the only carbon that cannot burn is the carbon already in CO2.

## Two probes on a gas that is all CO2

The engine flared a gas that is all CO2 at EGBEMA's volume and days, first at EGBEMA's efficiencies and then with both efficiencies set to 0.5:

| probe | flareCo2Tonnes | flareCh4Tonnes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all CO2, EGBEMA efficiencies | 140054.324 | 0.000 |
| all CO2, both efficiencies 0.5 | 140054.324 | 0.000 |

Both rows read 140054.324 tonnes of CO2 and 0.000 tonnes of methane a year.

The course states what the probes show: the CO2 already in the gas leaves the flare as CO2 at every efficiency, and none of it is methane.

## Against the rule

The efficiencies change between the rows, from 0.97 and 0.955 to 0.5 and 0.5, and the CO2 does not. In the rule, the efficiencies sit in the second term of the CO2 and in the methane, and a gas that is all CO2 has no hydrocarbon carbon and no methane for them to act on. What is left is the first term, the CO2 in the gas, and it passes through.

The methane reads 0.000 in both rows. The CH4 half of the rule is the methane in the gas times one less the destruction efficiency, and a gas that is all CO2 has no methane in it.

The flare weighs its CO2 at 44.009 kg/kmol. That figure was found the same way, by asking the engine about a gas that is all CO2 flared.

## On EGBEMA

EGBEMA's gas carries co2MoleFraction 0.0280. That CO2 is in EGBEMA's flare as the first term, at every efficiency, beside the combustion efficiency times the hydrocarbon carbon of 1.3320 per mole. Together the two terms give EGBEMA's flare 182079.024 tonnes of CO2 a year.

The engine counts the CO2 in the gas in the flare's CO2 with no efficiency on it, and none of it is methane.

## Reading it in the explorer

In the flare explorer, set the analysis to a gas that is all CO2 and run it at EGBEMA's volume and days. Read the CO2 and the methane at EGBEMA's efficiencies, then set both efficiencies to 0.5 and read them again.

# What the next tier changes

## Where this tier stops

The Associate tier called two functions of flareToValue. characteriseGas answered what is in this gas, and how much liquid could it give. abatement answered the first half of its question: what the flare emits.

Every figure in this tier came from an analysis and a flare study. The gas was read by the mole, the liquids were derived from the composition and the liquid densities, and the flare followed 40 CFR 98.233(n) with its two efficiencies. Nothing in this tier sold the gas.

## The Professional tier sells it

The course's cases name EGBEMA as a flow station in Imo State flaring associated gas, and the four ways to sell it. The Professional tier reads those four ways. It calls the rest of flareToValue, and each function answers one of the studio questions module one listed:

| question | function |
| --- | --- |
| which way of selling it does the gas allow | screenRoute against a route's requirement envelope |
| what does a route make, earn and cost in a year | routeEconomics |
| what does the flare emit, and what does recovering it abate | abatement |
| does the project need carbon credits to clear its hurdle | creditSensitivity |
| how do the routes compare in a bid | compareRoutes |

## What this tier hands on

Two things read here carry into the Professional tier, and one thing is new there.

The first is the mass ceiling. kgPerMscf and c3PlusKgPerMscf are the most any route can take out of a thousand standard cubic feet, by mass. On EGBEMA they are 26.7066 and 6.6647 kg. A route's yield is read against what the gas holds.

The second is the flare itself. abatement's question has a second half, what recovering the flare abates, and the Professional tier reads it beside the flare this tier computed. On EGBEMA that is 215946.438 tonnes of CO2e a year at the study's GWP of 29.8.

New in the Professional tier is capital. flareToValue scales capital with the power law in modularRefinery, and routeEconomics reports what a route makes, earns and costs in a year.

## What stays the same

The rules this tier read carry into the next.

A missing input is missing. A blank destruction efficiency is refused, a blank GWP leaves the CO2e null and names what blocked it, and a blank density leaves the liquids null.

Case inputs stay case inputs. The methane GWP and any credit price are case inputs, and the engine ships neither. The Professional tier adds credit prices to that list and treats them the same way.

Every figure stays invented and illustrative. Egbema is a real place, and the flow station and every figure attached to it are invented.

## Further on

The Expert tier leaves flareToValue for lpgCng: the LPG vessel and its reorder, the vaporizer, the carousel and the cylinders, the gas in a CNG bank and the cascade, the station compressor and forecourt, and whether a customer saves by switching fuel. Its cases are KANO, an LPG storage and bottling plant, and IBAFO, a CNG mother station on the Lagos-Ibadan expressway with a Lagos bus operator switching to CNG.

# What burning the product emits

The second term of the net abatement is taken away. Recover the gas and somebody burns it. The net abatement is the avoided flare, less what burning the product emits, plus what the product displaces.

## An input the counterfactual declares

What burning the product emits is an input. The study types it with the counterfactual, and it is invented and illustrative like every figure here.

On EGBEMA's CNG route, the three counterfactuals all carry the same product combustion:

| counterfactual | avoidedFlareCo2eTonnes | product combustion (input) | displaced fuel (input) | netAbatementTonnesCo2ePerYear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CNG displacing diesel in haulage trucks | 190032.865 | 128000 | 156000 | 218032.865 |
| CNG displacing pipeline gas already burned | 190032.865 | 128000 | 128000 | 190032.865 |
| CNG sold into a market that burned nothing | 190032.865 | 128000 | 0 | 62032.865 |

The avoided flare is 190032.865 and the product combustion is 128000 on every row. The three nets differ, and the only input that differs across the three rows is the displaced fuel. Lesson 4 reads that term.

## A route that adds emissions

Gas to power recovers 0.94. Its counterfactual, "Gas to power for a new load that burned nothing", declares a product combustion of 205000 and displaced fuel of 0.

| counterfactual | avoidedFlareCo2eTonnes | product combustion (input) | displaced fuel (input) | netAbatementTonnesCo2ePerYear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gas to power for a new load that burned nothing | 202989.652 | 205000 | 0 | -2010.348 |

The net is below zero: this route adds emissions. The avoided flare is 202989.652 t/yr. The product combustion typed is 205000. With nothing displaced, the net prints -2010.348 t/yr.

Read the sign. The engine prints the negative net as it stands, -2010.348. In module 5 the credit test reads this same net and refuses: "The net abatement is -2010.348 tCO2e a year: the project does not abate, so there are no credits to sell."

## The term that is taken away

On both routes, the product combustion is subtracted from the avoided flare. On the CNG row with nothing displaced, the avoided flare of 190032.865 less the product combustion of 128000 leaves a net of 62032.865. On the gas to power row with nothing displaced, 202989.652 less 205000 leaves -2010.348.

Those two rows share a counterfactual shape: the product goes to a market or a load that burned nothing. Each prints its own net from its own two figures.

The diesel row carries all three terms. The avoided flare of 190032.865, less the product combustion of 128000, plus the displaced fuel of 156000, gives the net of 218032.865. The product combustion is the same 128000 as on the row where nothing is displaced. The two nets differ, and the displaced fuel is the only input that differs between the two rows.

## While the counterfactual is undeclared

The product combustion belongs to the counterfactual, and abatement gives no net while the counterfactual is undeclared. With no counterfactual label, or with no displaced fuel figure, blockedBy reads "the counterfactual is not declared: what the product displaces, and what burning it emits". The warning printed while it is undeclared ends: "State what the product displaces and what burning it emits." Lesson 5 reads the blocked cases in full.

In the panel, pick the gas to power route with its counterfactual and change the product combustion. Read the net and its sign as the figure changes.

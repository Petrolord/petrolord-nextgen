# The ceiling on each route

A ceiling belongs to a gas and a route together. Change the gas and every ceiling moves with it. This lesson reads the four ceilings on three gases the Associate tier characterised: EGBEMA, the associated gas; OGUTA, a lean non-associated gas beside it; and the studio's opening gas.

## Four routes on three gases

| route | yield unit | ceiling basis | EGBEMA ceiling per Mscf | OGUTA ceiling per Mscf | studio opening gas ceiling per Mscf |

| Compressed natural gas | kg | gas mass | 26.7066 | 21.0224 | 25.4954 |
| Mini LNG | t | gas mass | 0.0267 | 0.0210 | 0.0255 |
| LPG and condensate extraction | t | propane and heavier | 0.0067 | 0.0010 | 0.0056 |
| Gas to power or gas to wire | MWh | heating value | 0.3659 | 0.3035 | 0.3548 |

Read the table one row at a time. Each row is one route, and its three figures are that route's ceiling on each gas, in the route's own unit and on the route's own basis.

## Each ceiling traced to the gas

The gas mass rows trace to kgPerMscf. The Associate tier printed kgPerMscf of 26.7066 for EGBEMA, 21.0224 for OGUTA and 25.4954 for the studio's opening gas. Those are the three CNG ceilings, figure for figure. The mini LNG row prints the same basis in tonnes: 0.0267, 0.0210 and 0.0255.

The propane and heavier row traces to c3PlusKgPerMscf, which the Associate tier printed as 6.6647 kg for EGBEMA, 0.9798 kg for OGUTA and 5.5819 kg for the studio's opening gas. The LPG ceilings print in tonnes: 0.0067, 0.0010 and 0.0056. The Associate tier also printed c3PlusKgPerMscf over kgPerMscf for each gas: 0.2496 for EGBEMA, 0.0466 for OGUTA and 0.2189 for the studio's opening gas.

The heating value row traces to ghvBtuScf: 1248.4110 for EGBEMA, 1035.6050 for OGUTA and 1210.7800 for the studio's opening gas. The three gas to power ceilings are 0.3659, 0.3035 and 0.3548 MWh per Mscf. Lesson 3 of this module shows how the heating value becomes megawatt hours.

## A typed yield read against its ceiling

The studio's LPG route on its opening gas is typed at 0.0045 t/Mscf. It is within its ceiling of 0.0056 t/Mscf, and the typed yield over the ceiling is 0.8062. It is the only typed yield over ceiling this tier prints.

EGBEMA's typed yields are 18.5 kg, 0.0175 t, 0.0052 t and 0.085 MWh, and every EGBEMA yield typed sits at or below its ceiling. No ratio is printed for them, so this lesson quotes each pair and stops: 18.5 against 26.7066 on CNG, 0.0175 against 0.0267 on mini LNG, 0.0052 against 0.0067 on LPG, 0.085 against 0.3659 on gas to power.

## Above the ceiling

The same studio gas that holds 0.0056 t of propane and heavier per Mscf refuses an LPG yield of 0.02 t/Mscf. The engine answers: "Route "LPG and condensate extraction" yields 0.02 t per Mscf, more than the 0.005582 t the gas holds (propane and heavier). A yield above what the gas contains is refused."

The refusal prints the ceiling as 0.005582 t. The table prints it as 0.0056 t. Both are printed for the LPG ceiling on the studio's opening gas: the refusal to six decimals, the table to four.

In the panel, switch the gas from EGBEMA to OGUTA and to the studio's opening gas, and read each route's ceiling change while its basis word stays.

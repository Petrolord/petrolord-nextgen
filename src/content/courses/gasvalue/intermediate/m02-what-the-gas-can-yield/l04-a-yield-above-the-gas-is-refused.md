# A yield above the gas is refused

A typed yield is the study's figure. The ceiling is the gas's. When the typed figure is above the ceiling, the engine gives no product, revenue or margin for it. It refuses it.

## Three refusals

| probe | engine |
| --- | --- |
| studio opening gas, LPG route at 0.02 t/Mscf | REFUSED: Route "LPG and condensate extraction" yields 0.02 t per Mscf, more than the 0.005582 t the gas holds (propane and heavier). A yield above what the gas contains is refused. |
| EGBEMA, CNG route at 30 kg/Mscf | REFUSED: Route "Compressed natural gas" yields 30 kg per Mscf, more than the 26.706618 kg the gas holds (gas mass). A yield above what the gas contains is refused. |
| EGBEMA, gas to power at 0 MWh/Mscf | REFUSED: Route "Gas to power or gas to wire" needs a positive product yield per Mscf. |

A yield above the ceiling is refused, and a yield of zero is refused.

## Reading a refusal

The first two refusals carry the same four parts in the same order:

- the route's label, in quotation marks;
- the typed yield, with the route's unit: 0.02 t, 30 kg;
- the ceiling the gas holds, to six decimals: 0.005582 t, 26.706618 kg;
- the basis in brackets: (propane and heavier), (gas mass).

Then each closes on the same sentence: "A yield above what the gas contains is refused."

The ceiling tables print the same two ceilings to four decimals: 0.0056 t for LPG on the studio's opening gas, and 26.7066 kg for CNG on EGBEMA.

## A figure that looks finished

This tier's trap is a figure that looks finished. For a yield above what the gas holds the engine prints no such figure: it answers with the refusal, and the refusal prints the ceiling beside the typed yield.

The CNG probe at 30 kg/Mscf is typed on EGBEMA, the same gas whose CNG yield of 18.5 kg the study typed and the engine carries into a year. The two differ in the yield alone. One is at or below the ceiling of 26.7066 and gives a year. The other is above it and gives a refusal.

## Zero is refused too

The third probe types a yield of 0 MWh per Mscf on the gas to power route. It is below the ceiling of 0.3659, and it is still refused: the route "needs a positive product yield per Mscf." This refusal carries no ceiling and no basis. It names the route and the one thing the yield lacks. The gas to power yield EGBEMA's study typed is 0.085 MWh per Mscf, and the engine carries that one into a year of 212733.7500 MWh.

The two kinds of refusal bound the yield from both sides. A yield must be more than zero, and it must be no more than what the gas holds on the route's basis.

In the panel, type the CNG yield on EGBEMA up past its ceiling, then down to zero, and read the refusal each time.

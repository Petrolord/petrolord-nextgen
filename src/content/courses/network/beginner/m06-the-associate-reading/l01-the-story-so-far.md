# The story so far

Every quantity a gathering system has exists before anything is solved together, and this tier is the work of naming them and saying which function owns each one.

## What a line is

A pipe row is three numbers where two would do, because the redundancy is the check: 12 rows carry an outside diameter, a wall and a published bore, and one of them already fails strict equality by -8.8818e-16 in while the shipped gate admits anything within 5.0000e-4 in.

A wall becomes a rating only when somebody supplies a design factor. The published case, 6.625 in outside diameter, 0.28 in wall and X52 at 52000 psi, rates at 3164.739623 psi at a design factor of 0.72 and returns a bare hoop stress of 4395.471698 psi with no factor at all, 1.388889 times the rated pressure. A sweep of that same pipe through every grade the module ships walks the rating from 2130.113208 psi to 3955.924528 psi.

## What a fitting is

A fitting is a resistance coefficient, and it becomes a length of pipe only once the pipe's friction factor is known. Four long radius elbows and two gate valves on a 6.065 in bore sum to K of 1.500000 and come to 42.118056 ft at a friction factor of 0.018 and 63.177083 ft at 0.012, 1.500000 times as long on the smoother line. The thirty-diameter rule of thumb answers 15.162500 ft to the same question.

One fitting can be the whole answer. On a list summing to K of 14.450000, a single globe valve at K of 10 is 69.204152 percent of it and 373.774510 ft of 540.104167 ft.

## What makes a drawing a network

Three node kinds, eleven refusals and one index. A well injects and its pressure is unknown, a junction balances, a sink holds a pressure. AGBADA WEST indexes as 8 nodes, 8 branches, 7 unknown pressures and 1 delivery point, and not one pressure is computed at that stage.

## Where one well lands

Alone on its flowline against 265 psia, AGBADA-2 lands at 892.889543025 psia making 6890.874160167 lb/d, and both numbers came out rather than in. The four wells solved that way add to 15683.052292561 lb/d, while the system they share produces 13300.677150912 lb/d, 15.190761959 percent less.

AGBADA-12 alone reports `converged: true` with a residual of 0.0000e+0 lb/d and a conservation gap of 3.450000e+2 lb/d, relative 3.502538e-1.

## The habit to carry out

Ask of every number which of the six functions produced it, what unit it is in, whether the function that produced it had a way to refuse, and what checked it that was not the same iteration that made it.

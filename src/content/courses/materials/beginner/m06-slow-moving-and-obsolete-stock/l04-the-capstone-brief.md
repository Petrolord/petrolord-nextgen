# The capstone brief

{{panel:materials-register-calculator}}

The Associate capstone asks you to apply this tier's question, criticality, classes and the order quantity, to a register you have not seen. You work it in the register calculator, the same panel every lesson of this tier has used. The register is synthetic and is its own: its items, scores, usages, costs, stock and policy appear in no lesson and in none of the Ekene files.

## What you are given

One case file comes with the capstone and is offered on the capstone card. It is written in the shape the register calculator reads, with one block for each of the four views: criticality, ABC by annual usage value, the economic order quantity, and slow-moving and obsolete stock. Paste the whole file into the box of any view and that view runs its own block.

## What the capstone states

Every input a figure depends on is stated in the file: each criterion, weight and score, the score scale, the class minimums and the override; each item's annual usage and unit cost, the cut-offs and the boundary rule; the demand, the cost of an order, the holding rate with the unit cost, and the rounding rule; each item's stock on hand, unit cost, months since the last issue and monthly usage, the bands with their write-downs and the cover limit. Nothing is left for you to assume.

## What is graded

Six quantities, each a value the engine returns on the case file:

| graded quantity | where you meet it | view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| one item's weighted score | module 2 | Criticality classes |
| one item's cumulative share | module 3 | ABC by annual usage value |
| the EOQ of the ordered item | module 4 | The economic order quantity |
| its relevant cost a year at the quantity ordered | modules 4 and 5 | The economic order quantity |
| its rounding penalty, in percent | module 5 | The economic order quantity |
| the register's total write-down | module 6 | Slow-moving and obsolete stock |

Enter each to six decimals, as the panel prints it. The grading tolerance is set by the course in one place, so you never need to guess it. No graded figure depends on a reading the engine states: each is the same number under at or above and strictly above, under halves upward and halves downward, and under every tie rule. This tier has no Monte Carlo, so nothing in it is sampled.

## How to work it

Run each view on its block and read the reasons before the figures. In criticality, check whether the override fires and whether any score sits on a class minimum. In ABC, find the item whose cumulative share crosses a cut-off and read which class the stated rule gives it. In the EOQ view, keep the EOQ and the quantity ordered apart, and read the relevant cost at the quantity ordered. In slow-moving, check every band and the cover limit.

## The traps this tier has shown you

A score on a class minimum is in the class. A maximum on an override criterion forces the top class; one below does nothing. The item crossing a cut-off moves with the boundary rule. The EOQ is unrounded; the relevant cost is at the rounded quantity. A band minimum is reached at or above it, and excess is strictly above the cover limit.

## Exercise

Rehearse on the Ekene register before you open the case file. In the register calculator, work through the views in the order of the table above and write down PSV-KIT's weighted score, CEM-G's cumulative share, baryte's EOQ, its relevant cost a year and its rounding penalty, and the total write-down. Check each against the lessons of modules 2 to 6.

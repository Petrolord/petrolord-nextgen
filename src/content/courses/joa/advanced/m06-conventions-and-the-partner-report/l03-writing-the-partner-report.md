# Writing the partner report

{{panel:joa-agreement-calculator}}

The engine's figures reach a partner through a report, and a partner who holds the terms beside each figure can recompute it. This lesson sets out what a partner report names and writes one section for the Ekene joint venture.

## What a partner report names

- the licence and its parties, with their participating interests (synthetic, in this course);
- every source applied, with its edition and the date it was read;
- each carry with its carriers and the paying interests it produces;
- the cash call terms (lag, negative call rule, threshold) and each month's calls with the engine's reasons;
- the budget and its tolerances; the overhead scale and its exclusions;
- any default with its dates, interest terms, cover and consequences;
- each carry recovery with its uplift, recovery share, cap and basis;
- any back-in with its target, refundable costs and refund form;
- any sole risk operation with its premium multiple and mode;
- the PSC terms, with the limit base stated;
- the discount rate and base year beside every NPV;
- each reading the figures rest on.

## A section, written

The Ekene joint venture below is synthetic, and every figure is the engine's.

> Ekene-4 sidetrack (synthetic). Cost 18000000.000000. PB (participating interest 15.000000 percent) declined; EKO, PA and NOC consented and carry the cost in their shares of the project, 47.058824, 29.411765 and 23.529412 percent. Terms: mode recover-from-production, premium multiple 400.000000 percent of PB's proportionate share of the cost, 2700000.000000. PB's premium is 10800000.000000, recovered from its share of net value (gross value less stated deductions) from 2031; its interest reverts inside 2035, when it receives 150000.000000 of that year's share of 1500000.000000 (engine convention: reversion inside the payout period). Source: Norway JOA Art. 18.6 (unofficial translation, 2007 text, read 2026-09-26); premium recovery from production is a contract term, stated.

Every figure carries its term, and the source its edition and date.

## Readings belong in the report

Where a figure rests on a reading, the report says so in a sentence. A default section would add, beside the cover of 1230769.230769 by EKO and 769230.769231 by PA: the cover is by paying interest among the non-defaulting parties, the engine's stated reading of Norway JOA Art. 9.1 while a carry is live. A PSC section would note that the tax is charged on the contractor's profit oil, the engine's stated reading, and give the limit and its base.

## What a report leaves out

It names as outside the figures whatever the engine does not compute: the compensation on an assignment, the expert determination of unrecovered costs, a haircut, a sliding scale worked elsewhere. It forecasts nothing a partner will pay.

# The capstone brief

{{panel:joa-agreement-calculator}}

The Expert capstone grades the question this tier asked: what an agreement does when the parties do not all agree, and how each figure rests on the terms stated. It is worked in the course's own agreement calculator, which calls the same vendored engine every lesson has quoted. There is no Suite app for this course: the calculator is the practical.

## What you receive

The capstone card carries one case file for a synthetic joint venture of its own. It holds five blocks, one for each view you will use: `soleRisk`, `buyIn`, `carry` (with its discount rate and base year), `psc` (with its limit and base) and `default` (with its dates, interest terms and consequences). Every term a graded figure depends on is stated in the file.

## What you report

Six quantities, each to six decimals, and the card names each exactly. They are the kinds of figure this tier has read:

- a non-consenting party's premium;
- what a non-consenting party receives in one year of its premium recovery ledger;
- the share of an entry payment that one consenting party receives on a buy-in;
- one party's NPV under a carry;
- the government profit oil of one PSC year;
- one party's participating interest after a forfeiture.

None depends on a reading the engine states: each was proved the same under every reading and under the alternative the engine names.

## How to work it

1. Paste the whole case file into "Sole risk: the premium recovered from production". The view reads the sole risk block. Read the premium and the ledger.
2. Paste the whole file into "Buy-in at a stated multiple" and read the payer to party table.
3. Paste it into "A carry, its recovery and the NPV" and read the NPV table.
4. Paste it into "PSC cost recovery" and read the year's row.
5. Paste it into "A default and forfeiture" and read the table of interests after the forfeiture.
6. Read the reasons and notes under the tables; the case as issued runs without a refusal.

Each view picks its own block out of the whole file.

## How it is graded

Each field is compared with the engine's own return on the issued case, within a tolerance set once for the whole course. Report the figure the calculator prints, at six decimals, with no rounding of your own. Reasons round money to the cent, so take each figure from its table.

## Exercise

Rehearse the method on the Ekene terms before opening the card. In "Sole risk: the premium recovered from production", read PB's premium and what PB receives in 2036. In "Buy-in at a stated multiple", read what EKO receives of PB's entry payment. In "A carry, its recovery and the NPV", read NOC's NPV and the discount rate and base year in the box. In "PSC cost recovery", start from "The Ekene PSC variant" and read the government profit oil of 2033. In "A default and forfeiture", start from "The same default left open" and read the interests after PB's forfeiture. For each figure, name the term that sets it and the lesson of this tier that taught it. The rehearsal joint venture is no capstone: its figures are printed in the lessons, and the card's are not.

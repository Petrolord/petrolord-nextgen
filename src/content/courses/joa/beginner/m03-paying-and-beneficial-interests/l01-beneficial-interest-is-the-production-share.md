# Beneficial interest is the production share

{{panel:joa-account-calculator}}

A carry lets one party keep its share of a venture while others pay its share of the cost. The question this lesson answers is what the carried party keeps. The answer, in this engine, is its whole share of production: its beneficial interest stays equal to its participating interest while it is carried.

## What a carry is

The IMF's working paper on cash flow analysis of fiscal regimes (WP/24/89, April 2024) describes the arrangement from the state's side:

> "The parties may establish a financing arrangement to cover (“carry”) the state’s proportional share of development spending." (IMF WP/24/89 (April 2024))

The words "financing arrangement" are the key. A carry finances the carried party's cost share. It does not buy any of the carried party's production. The Petroleum Industry Act 2021 (Act No. 6, Official Gazette No. 142, Vol. 108, 27 August 2021) requires one kind of concession agreement to carry such a term:

> "(4) A contract as provided for under section 85 (2) (d) of this Act shall include a carried interest provision, whereby" (PIA s.85(4))

The terms that follow that opening, and the carry recovery they lead to, are Professional tier material. At this tier you need only the effect of a carry on the interests.

## The Ekene carry

In the Ekene joint venture NOC is carried for 100 percent of its cost share, and the carriers are the three other parties pro rata. The engine explains the result in its own words:

> NOC: 100% of its 20% cost share is carried (20 points), paid by EKO 10, PA 6.25, PB 3.75 (pro rata to their participating interests); its share of production stays 20%

Read the last clause: "its share of production stays 20%". NOC's beneficial interest is 20.000000 and its paying interest 0.000000. The carry moved NOC's twenty points of cost to the carriers and left its production untouched.

## Why production does not move

A carry keeps ownership where it is: the carried party still owns its whole share of the venture. The carriers are paid back in a way the agreement states, and the IMF paper describes the usual one, a financing arrangement repaid from the state's share of production. That later repayment is carry recovery. It is taken from the carried party's share of production, which is why the beneficial interest must stay whole while the carry runs.

## The rule, once more

The engine states the rule in its basis:

> beneficial interest = participating interest (the share of production); paying interest = participating interest - the carried part of a carried party's share + the carriers' shares of every carry

The beneficial side of that rule has no term for a carry at all.

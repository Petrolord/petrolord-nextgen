# The capstone brief

{{panel:joa-recovery-calculator}}

The Professional capstone asks this tier's question: recovery, default and cost recovery. It gives you a synthetic joint venture of its own, with its own parties, months, costs and terms, and asks for six values the engine returns. This lesson says what the capstone asks, where each value sits in the recovery calculator, and how to rehearse on the Ekene joint venture first.

## What the capstone gives you

The capstone card carries one case file with a block for each call: `cashCalls`, the parties, the carry, the months and the reconciliation terms; `carry`, the carried party, the years with their costs and entitlements, the uplift, the recovery share, the basis and any cap; `backIn`, the back-in party, its target, the cost lines with their kinds, the basis, the refund form and the recovery share; `default`, the call, the due date, the defaulters, the default interest terms and the consequences; and `psc`, the years, the royalty, the cost oil limit and its base, the profit share, the tax and the opening cost pool.

## The six values

| value | module | where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a stated party's cash call in a stated month | the cash call ledger | the call column of "The cash call ledger" |
| the carry balance at the end of a stated year | carries with uplift and caps | the closing column of "A carry and its recovery" |
| the back-in refund a stated party receives | back-in under the Act | the refund received column of "A back-in under the Act" |
| the default interest | default cover and interest | the default interest column of "A default: cover, interest and consequences" |
| a non-defaulting party's cover | default cover and interest | the cover column of the same view |
| the cost recovered in a stated year | PSC cost recovery | the cost recovered column of "PSC cost recovery" |

All six are reported to six decimals, as the panel prints them. Each is a return value of the engine on the card's terms, so there is exactly one right answer, and each is the same number under every reading the engine states.

## How to load the case

Paste the whole case file into each of the five views. Each view reads the block it needs and leaves the rest of the file alone. Nothing needs to be copied between views: every value comes from one view on one block.

## Things to check before you copy a figure

Check the controls above each box against the card: the lag and the negative call rule of the ledger; the uplift, recovery share, basis and cap of the carry; the basis, target and refund form of the back-in; the rate, day basis, method and grace of the default; the limit, its base and the opening pool of the PSC. Read the reasons: a call names the month whose difference it adjusts, a carry names the year it is recovered or written off, and a default names its days. If a view refuses the case, a term has been changed or mistyped: read the field the refusal names and restore the card's term.

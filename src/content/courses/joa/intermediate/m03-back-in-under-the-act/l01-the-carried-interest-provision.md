# The carried interest provision of the Act

{{panel:joa-recovery-calculator}}

The Petroleum Industry Act 2021 (Act No. 6, Official Gazette No. 142, Vol. 108, 27 August 2021, read on 2026-09-26) writes a carried interest provision into one kind of contract, and fixes some of its terms in law. This module reads that provision as contract mechanics. The Nigerian fiscal system around it (royalty by terrain, hydrocarbon tax, companies income tax) is the subject of the Petroleum Industry Act course.

## Which contract

Section 85(2) lists the contract types. One of them is a concession agreement that may include a joint venture with NNPC Limited:

> "(d) a concession agreement for exploration, development and production of petroleum, which may include an incorporated or unincorporated joint venture with NNPC Limited ; and" (PIA s.85(2)(d))

Section 85(4) opens by naming that contract, and a production sharing contract is a different item, s.85(2)(a):

> "(4) A contract as provided for under section 85 (2) (d) of this Act shall include a carried interest provision, whereby" (PIA s.85(4))

## What the Act fixes

The Government may participate up to a stated ceiling:

> "(a) the Government through the NNPC Limited has the right to participate up to 60% in the contract or identified as a bid parameter ;" (PIA s.85(4)(a))

The engine carries two figures from the Act: a ceiling of 60 percent on the target interest (s.85(4)(a)), and development and production as the refundable cost kinds (s.85(4)(c)). It applies them only when a call states the basis "pia-s85-4". Under that basis a carry is recovered with no uplift, and the engine's basis says why:

> PIA s.85(4): the Government refunds its proportionate share of the unrecovered proven costs, with no bonuses, penalties, interest, premium or markups (s.85(4)(c)), in cash or in kind from future production (s.85(4)(f))

A call that states the Act's basis and an uplift is refused:

> uplift.type must be "none" under basis "pia-s85-4": the refund excludes interest, premium or markups on cost (PIA s.85(4)(c)); got "compound"

## What the Act leaves to a procedure

The Act does not state the unrecovered costs themselves. They are settled by an agreed procedure:

> "(e) the nature, the validity and quantum of the unrecovered costs to be refunded shall be determined or verified by an agreed expert determination procedure ;" (PIA s.85(4)(e))

The engine does not stand in for that procedure. Its basis on the back-in says what it leaves out:

> the expert determination of the unrecovered costs (s.85(4)(e)); the unrecovered cost figures are stated inputs

Every cost figure on the Ekene back-in is a synthetic stated input.

# The refund from future entitlement

{{panel:joa-recovery-calculator}}

Under the Act, the Government does not pay its refund on the day it backs in. It pays out of what the venture later produces. This lesson follows the Ekene refund to the year it is cleared.

## What the Act says

The Petroleum Industry Act 2021 (Act No. 6, Official Gazette No. 142, Vol. 108, 27 August 2021, read on 2026-09-26) rules out an upfront payment and names the form the refund may take:

> "(d) the contract shall not require any upfront payment by the Government ;" (PIA s.85(4)(d))

> "(f ) the refund obligation under this subsection may be in the form of cash or kind from future share of production or entitlements from the effective participation date ; and" (PIA s.85(4)(f))

The engine's basis on the form, verbatim:

> recovered with no uplift from recoverFromPct % of the back-in party's share of future entitlement, in cash or in kind (s.85(4)(f)), the whole refund owed from the first year

The share of entitlement the refund may take each year is a stated input; the Ekene back-in states 50.000000 percent of NOC's new share. A call under the Act's basis that asks for the refund at once is refused:

> refundForm must be "from-future-entitlement" under basis "pia-s85-4": no upfront payment by the Government (s.85(4)(d)); the refund is in cash or in kind from future production or entitlements (s.85(4)(f)); got "upfront"

## When the refund outlasts the years

> 2036: 4000000 of the refund is not recovered by the last year

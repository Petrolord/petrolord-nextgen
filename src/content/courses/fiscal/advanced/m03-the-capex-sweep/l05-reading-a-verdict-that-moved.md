# Reading a verdict that moved

A verdict can change for three quite different reasons, and only one of them means the fiscal answer changed.

{{panel:ec-comparison-explorer}}

## The field moved

The capex verdict on the default project names Angola - Deepwater PSC the most resilient, giving up 88.6123 million USD, and USA - Gulf of Mexico the most exposed at 267.7301. On the teaching field ODIDI the same six templates return a different verdict: Ghana - Deepwater gives up the least at 181.1922 million USD, Angola - Deepwater PSC the most at 252.6075. Angola crossed from best to worst with all four of its instruments unchanged. What changed was the field underneath, so a resilience verdict is a statement about a regime on a project.

## The range moved

The second reason is that the swept range itself can change. Until the 2026-09-15 repair the loop accumulated its multiplier in floating point, arrived at 1.5000000000000004, failed its own test and stopped at 1.4, so an axis labelled 0.8 to 1.5 delivered seven points and every loss under it was understated. EC2-3 replaced the accumulation with a step count, and USA - Gulf of Mexico now gives up 267.7301 million USD over a range that reaches 1.5. The names did not move on this project. The amounts did.

## Nothing moved at all

The third reason is that there is nothing to rank. On the published comparison built with capex of 20000, all six templates give up 12727.272727 million USD of contractor NPV across the sweep, identical to six decimals, and the verdict says so: no regime can be ranked on resilience to cost overrun, and all six are named as giving up 12,727.3 million USD each, within 0.1 million USD of each other. The retired reduce picked with a strict less-than, which keeps the first of two equal values, and it named USA - Gulf of Mexico on this case.

## The mistake

The mistake is to treat a change in a verdict as a finding. A verdict is only a verdict when the quantities it ranks are separated by more than the precision they are printed to, and these sentences print one decimal place. Two regimes whose losses both print as 12,727.3 million USD have not been ranked. The engine now enforces that on both sweeps, at 0.1 million USD for capex and one percentage point for price. Read the ranked quantities anyway, and check that they differ, that the range is the one you meant and that the project is.

## What the verdicts refuse

They report a name and an amount, never a margin. They will say that a pick was a tie, and they will not say that every regime on the project returns a negative NPV. On ODIDI they name Brazil - Concession best for the contractor at -5.9 million USD: the verdict is correct and the project is still a loss.

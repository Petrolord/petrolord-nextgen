# Reading a verdict that moved

A verdict can change for three quite different reasons, and only one of them means the fiscal answer changed.

{{panel:ec-comparison-explorer}}

## The field moved

The capex verdict on the default project names Angola - Deepwater PSC the most resilient, giving up 84.8591 million USD, and USA - Gulf of Mexico the most exposed at 228.7953. On the teaching field ODIDI the same six templates return a different verdict: Ghana - Deepwater gives up the least at 150.9021 million USD, Angola - Deepwater PSC the most at 215.1075. Angola crossed from best to worst with all four of its instruments unchanged. What changed was the field underneath, so a resilience verdict is a statement about a regime on a project.

## The range moved

The second reason is that the swept range is not what the axis says. Losses over the seven points the loop visits are smaller than losses over the eight points 0.8 to 1.5: 84.8591 against 107.3918 for Angola - Deepwater PSC, 228.7953 against 267.7301 for USA - Gulf of Mexico. On the default project the ordering holds, so the sentence names the same winner while the amount inside it moves.

## Nothing moved at all

The third reason is that there was nothing to rank. On the published comparison built with capex of 20000, all six templates give up 10909.090909 million USD of contractor NPV across the sweep, identical to six decimals, and the verdict still names USA - Gulf of Mexico the most resilient and Nigeria - PIA (2021) the most exposed, quoting both as 10909.1 million USD. The comparison behind the pick is a strict less-than, which keeps the first of two equal values and, when two differ only in their last bits, keeps whichever rounding error was smaller.

## The mistake

The mistake is to treat a change in a verdict as a finding. A verdict is only a verdict when the quantities it ranks are separated by more than the precision they are printed to, and these sentences print one decimal place. Two regimes whose losses both print as 10909.1 million USD have not been ranked. Read the ranked quantities themselves, and check that they differ, that the range is the one you meant and that the project is.

## What the verdicts refuse

They report a name and an amount, never a margin. They will not say that a pick was a tie, that the swept range stopped short, or that every regime on the project returns a negative NPV. On ODIDI they name Brazil - Concession best for the contractor at -5.9 million USD: the verdict is correct and the project is still a loss.

## Exercise

Name the three reasons a resilience verdict can differ between two runs. Then give the capex loss every template returns on the comparison built with capex of 20000, and say why the sentence ranking them is not a result.

# Onward

The Associate reading stops at one regime's ledger with no clock on it. Two numbers it already prints show why the other tiers exist.

## Two readings of one field

The teaching field ODIDI under "Brazil - Concession" returns the contractor 192.9896 million USD over 25 years and a net present value of -5.8662 at the project's 12 percent, and no single internal rate at all, its value being zero at both -18.8 and 11.4 percent. Same rows, two verdicts and a refusal. The Associate reading quotes each with its basis. Which one a decision rests on takes a discount convention, and the conventions are the Professional tier: what a flow in year 25 is worth in year 1, why a mid year convention differs by a square root, and why a solver returns null with a status rather than a rate it cannot justify.

## The instruments that move

Every regime here was read at one setting. Three of the six carry an instrument that moves on its own: a royalty keyed on price, so a deck stepping from 45 to 65 USD per bbl changes the rate inside a field's life, and a profit split keyed on the R factor, whose tiers step the contractor's share from 60 to 40 to 30 percent under PIA. The R factor is a ratio of cumulatives, so it can fall back below a threshold it passed and step the share up again. The tax stack has the same depth: resource rent tax of 40 percent under Brazil and 50 under Angola, with a capital uplift that sizes a one-time pool of relief and whose default moved a published life tax from 2207.5549 to 2127.5549.

## What to carry forward

Three habits survive into the other tiers. Build the first row by hand and prove it to the last decimal, because -310.0117 is either right or it is not. Name the regime beside every reading, because payback in year 6 and payback in year 8 are both true of ODIDI. And read what the engine refuses as carefully as what it returns: a payback of null and a closing pool of 2543.7575 are answers.

## What this tier cannot tell you

Whether a regime is well designed. It can say that ODIDI hands the state between 232.9950 and 316.7898 depending on which of six templates prices it, and that the contractor keeps between 109.1947 and 192.9896. It cannot say which of those splits is fair, which survives a price collapse, or which of the numbers on a comparison screen is a measurement at all. Those are the Expert tier.

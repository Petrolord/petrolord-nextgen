# The story so far

One field, six regimes, and every column this tier owns, read once from the volumes to the closing totals.

## The field before any regime

The teaching field ODIDI produces 8000 bbl/d of oil declining 14 percent a year, 40 Mscf/d of gas declining 9 percent and 900 bbl/d of NGL declining 15 percent. Its capex is 260 drilling, 120 facilities and 40 subsea, all charged in year 1 as 420.0000 million USD. Opex is 12 million USD a year fixed plus 4 USD per boe, which prints 25.0037 in year 1 and 12.3405 in year 25, and totals 390.2137. The discount rate is 12 percent.

The engine generates the profile rather than reading one: year 1 lifts 2.920000 million bbl of oil, 0.014600 million Mscf of gas and 0.328500 million bbl of NGL, and year 25 still lifts 0.078225, 0.001518 and 0.006646, because the horizon is fixed at 25 years and no economic limit closes it. The deck holds 45 USD per bbl through year 5, steps to 65 at year 6 and to 85 at year 12, and never interpolates between those points.

## One regime, read down

Under "USA - Gulf of Mexico", ODIDI sells 139.6563 in year 1 and pays royalty of 26.1856, leaving 113.4707 of revenue after royalty. Year 5 sells 76.1939 and year 6 sells 93.9859: revenue rose while every stream declined, because the deck stepped. Year 25 sells 6.9081, pays 1.2953 of royalty and leaves 5.6128 against opex of 12.3405, which is why this ledger closes with 24.2824 of cost still unrecovered at a limit of 100 percent.

The rate column is null down its whole length, because on a field whose late years turn negative no single rate in the band the engine searches brings a ledger to zero, and a null with a reason is the answer rather than a gap.

## The conventions, each with its number

A regime is four instruments and nothing else: a royalty, a cost recovery limit, a profit split and a tax stack. Royalty comes off gross, so ODIDI pays 26.1856 in a year it also spends 420.0000. Cost recovery is capped on revenue after royalty, and what it cannot take is carried forward and never lost. Profit oil is the residual, and the split acts on it alone. Tax acts on the contractor's share of that residual, which is why a year of 0.0000 profit oil pays 0.0000 of tax.

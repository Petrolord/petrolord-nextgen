# Onward

From a friction force to what it does to the steel.

## What the Expert tier adds

This tier stopped at the friction force. The Expert tier asks two questions about its consequences.

**What happens to the string when the force pushes back.** Compression, buckling, and the point where the model stops describing a drill string at all.

**What happens to the casing while the string rubs against it.** The same side force, spent over hours, taking metal off the wall.

## What you will find there

**Two buckling limits and a constant ratio.** The sinusoidal limit is where the pipe first lies in a sine wave against the low side; the helical limit is where it wraps. The helical limit is 2 root 2 less 1 times the sinusoidal one, to nine decimal places, whatever the pipe, the hole or the inclination, because both come from the same expression with a different constant in front.

**Which capacity actually binds.** On the horizontal well rotating on bottom the drill pipe is at roughly a quarter of its torsional capacity and well under a tenth of its tensile one. On the vertical well the two swap over: about a fifth tensile and a fortieth torsional. The ratings were written for the second case and extended reach wells live in the first.

**Casing wear.** Fifty hours at 120 rpm slides the tool joints most of two hundred kilometres against the casing. On the horizontal well's side forces that takes more than a quarter off the wall at the shoe joint, and the wear depth is not linear in the wear factor because the groove is a crescent rather than a rectangle.

**Where the model stops.** Lock-up, the string that will not go in, and the four things this course teaches and does not certify.

## Before you go

Two habits from this tier.

**Fit one observation, predict another.** A fitted factor with no cross-check is a tautology. The cheapest cross-check available is a torque prediction from a hookload fit, and it catches the errors people most often make in a string description.

**Refine the step once on anything you will rely on.** It is one run, and it separates a number you have checked from a number you have not.

## The one sentence

You can now compute a friction force and say what it did and did not come from. The next tier is what that force does to the pipe and to the casing.

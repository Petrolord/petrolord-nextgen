# Onward

Where the string stops and the rest of the module begins.

## What this course certified

The soft-string loads on a stated geometry with a stated friction factor: hookload for six operations, surface torque, tension and side force along the string, the two buckling limits, the two utilizations, and the casing wear a stated rotating schedule produces from those side forces.

That is the mechanical state of a drill string in a hole. It is one of the three things you need before a well can be drilled.

## The other two, and where they are

**The hydraulics.** Whether the mud can be circulated at a rate that cleans the hole, at pressures the formation will take, through a bit and a motor that consume some of it. Cuttings beds are the largest single thing the friction factor absorbs in this course, and they are a hydraulics problem.

That is the next course in this module.

**The rock.** Whether the hole will stand up, what mud weight it needs, and where the pressure it contains comes from. A hole that collapses generates cuttings that raise the friction, and a hole that fractures loses the mud that was carrying them.

That is the geomechanics course.

## What this course leaves for the module to close

**Stiff-string analysis**, which matters most for the casing wear this course computes, since wear is entirely a local contact question and a soft-string model smeared the contact.

**Dynamics**, which is what most of the friction factor's day-to-day variation actually is.

**Post-lock-up behaviour**, which sets the real reach limit.

All three are named in module 5 and graded nowhere, and the go-live migration for this course asserts that separation.

## Before you go

Three habits from this tier.

**Read a buckling flag as a statement about the answer, not about the well.** It says the friction below that depth is an underestimate, and how far past the limit tells you by how much.

**Report wall loss as a percentage.** A depth in millimetres is not comparable between casing strings and a percentage is.

**Quote both a relative and an absolute measure of any disagreement.** Either alone can be made to say anything, and the two together are honest.

## The one sentence

Torque and drag is one friction force resolved in two directions, computed from a geometry you can check and a constant you fitted, and everything this tier added is what that force does to the steel on both sides of the contact.

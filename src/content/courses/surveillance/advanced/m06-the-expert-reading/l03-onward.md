# Onward

The ninth course of this module ends where the first one started: at a rate, with a question about which rate it is.

## What this course certified

Turning a ledger row into rates and ratios, and saying where each one refuses. Rolling a field up into mean rates, a watercut, a gas-oil ratio and an uptime. Comparing a recent window against a baseline window on the same well and printing a type, a severity, a value and a baseline. Splitting one metered stream across wells on a test in force and an uptime, with diagnostics for what would not split. QCing a test against the ledger and against a well model. Reading an effective decline off a fit that the canonical Arps engine performs. Screening six lift methods and reconciling that matrix against the design pass, where the design wins.

## What it hands on

The lift half ends at a duty and a fluid, and four of the six methods have a real design chain elsewhere in this module: the gas lift, ESP and rod pump studios, and the gas well performance studio that owns the plunger. What those chains do begins with the rate this course produced, so its phase is the first thing to state and the easiest to lose.

## What is left open

Both readings of a period ratio are in the shipped studio, and the golden commits the size of their disagreement rather than resolving it, because resolving it would move numbers a running application prints. The lift handoff is documented one way and used another. Both are owner decisions, not defects a lesson can close.

The four oracles are genuinely independent routes and they gate what each function returns on published inputs. They do not gate which of two functions a caller should have used, what a missing column means, or what a refusal sentence says, and most of this course lives there.

## Three habits to carry

Name the function beside the number. Almost every finding here is a disagreement between two functions in one file.

Say which reading a ratio is. A mean of daily ratios and a ratio of sums are two quantities, and only one of them can be multiplied by days to book a volume.

Ask what a returned number would look like if the input were missing. In these four modules a silence scores 100, takes a full share, or turns a limit off.

## The one sentence

Surveillance never measures a well, it compares a well against a reading of itself, and a reading is a choice somebody made in a line of code that does not appear on the screen.

# The story so far

Five modules, and not one of them measured a well. Every number in this tier is the distance between two other numbers.

## The claim

A comparison needs a second quantity, and it is chosen by whoever wrote the call: a baseline window, a test rate, a facility meter. The row prints the distance and never the pair.

## What each module established

**Module one.** The two windows are half open, they do not overlap, and both are measured back from the field's latest ledger date rather than the clock. On the teaching field OGUTA, invented for this course and neither real nor published, asOf 2024-11-20 gives a recent window of 2024-11-14 to 2024-11-20, a baseline of 2024-10-15 to 2024-11-13, and 8 exceptions. Each well widens its own windows on its own cadence, and no row says which window produced it.

**Module two.** Seven types, two of which never compare two windows at all, and a severity that is the name of a second threshold crossing rather than a size. `rateDropPct` is 20 per cent with the doubling to high at 40. OGUTA-2 reads a recent oil mean of 618.142857142857 stb over 7 rows against a baseline of 1038.900000000000 stb over 30 rows, a change of -40.500254390 per cent, and those are calendar volumes over rows.

**Module three.** A single test rate carries a well until it ages out, and `maxTestAgeDays` is a limit only while it is a finite positive number. OGUTA-17 is carried on 2024-11-05 by a test 180 days old and has no test in force on 2024-11-06. `validateWellTests` and `groupTests` share a file and never consult each other.

**Module four.** An allocation is one metered stream divided by a sum of models. On the teaching field the run closes exactly, 58426.784897363 stb of measured oil against 58426.784897363 stb allocated on a theoretical of 54713.920833333 stb, and four wells of eight take any share, on 57 `no_test_in_force` diagnostics.

**Module five.** The meter against the wells' own books, the test against the well's model, and the loss book against nothing. On 2024-11-20 the OGUTA oil imbalance is 437.869938006 stb at 19.806426169154 per cent of what the wells booked.

## The one sentence

A surveillance number is a difference, an allocated number is a share, and neither is a measurement of the well whose name is printed beside it.

## What this tier did not settle

Which reading a ratio is. A period watercut and a period gas-oil ratio can each be formed two defensible ways from identical rows, and two functions in one file disagree. Nor did it settle which column an exception reads, or what happens to an allocation when the tests it refused are put back in.

## Exercise

Write the five claims in one sentence each, and beside each the two quantities that were compared.

Then say which of the five would change if the ledger were reread a month later with no new rows added.

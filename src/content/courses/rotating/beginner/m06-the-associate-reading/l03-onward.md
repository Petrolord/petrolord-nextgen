# Onward

This tier stopped at the discharge of one pump. Two tiers follow it, and it is worth knowing what they are for before deciding which to take.

## The Professional tier

The suction side, the changes, and the second machine. A duty point that works on paper fails in three ways and that tier is all three.

It fails on suction, where the available NPSH is built from the real suction survey rather than from a figure typed into a box, and where having a margin over the required NPSH is a different claim from being adequate.

It fails on change, where a speed change follows the affinity laws exactly and an impeller trim does not, and where applying either law to a duty point does not give a new duty point, because the system curve did not move.

And it fails on the fluid, because a catalogue curve is a water curve. That tier ends with two identical machines in parallel, where the combined curve is re-intersected with the same system curve and the duty is solved afresh.

## The Expert tier

The compressor, and where both engines stop. The fluid is no longer incompressible and the machine is no longer describable by a curve anybody can type in, so every answer comes out of a thermodynamic path instead of an intersection: the polytropic exponent, the stage count as the larger of two limits with the engine naming which governed, the train and its interstage cooling, the driver and its fuel. Then the audit of what a refusal is and where each guard turns over.

## What is held for literature

Eight things in this course are used, printed and never graded, because this repository holds no publication behind them. The viscosity correction. The impeller trim shortfall model. The operating-region bands at 50, 70, 120 and 140 percent, which this tier met. The NPSH margin rule, the larger of 3 ft and 35 percent of required. The machine-screening thresholds. The 300 degF default discharge limit. What the implied water density of this tier is away from real water. And every one of the published golden cases, each of which was written by an oracle rather than measured on a machine.

## What the course does answer

The duty a vendor should be quoting against. The power and the stage count to expect. The suction margin a selection has to survive. And the reasons behind all four, which is the part that transfers to the next machine you are handed.

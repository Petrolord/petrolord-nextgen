# The band the engine reports without comment

The affinity laws are exact and a real pump is not, so the engine carries a range of speed ratios it answers inside without comment. Both ends can be measured, and what kind of limit it is matters more than where it sits.

{{panel:fc-suction-explorer}}

## Both ends, walked

Walk the speed ratio up from 0.010000 until the warning changes state and the lower end comes out at 0.500000000000. Walk it down from 5.000000 and the upper end comes out at 1.500000000000.

Neither figure was read out of a constant. Each is the ratio at which the engine's own behaviour changes.

## What it does outside the band

It answers. At a speed ratio of 100.000000 the engine returns 123445.296915 gpm, 4178010.181407 ft and 183041883.582474 brake hp, and it attaches this:

"a speed ratio of 100 is far outside the range over which the affinity laws describe a real machine: the power leg goes as the cube, so this is an extrapolation on the number that sizes the driver"

The laws still apply at that ratio and the engine still applies them. The arithmetic is the arithmetic of every row inside the band. What changed is that the engine says out loud that no real machine should be expected to behave this way, and it names the leg that makes the extrapolation dangerous instead of leaving that to be inferred.

## What kind of limit this is

It is a sanity bound rather than a published correlation limit. No publication in this repository says where the affinity laws stop describing a real machine, so the two ends are a judgement about when a reader should be told to think, and no measurement of where the physics fails.

That is why the band is held for the literature and why the engine warns on it and never refuses over it. Nothing computed from the band decides a value anywhere in this package, and no graded answer in this course sits on it.

So the honest reading is modest. Inside the band the engine had nothing to say, and outside it the engine had something to say.

The warning singles out power for a reason a speed-change table already shows: flow follows the ratio, head its square and power its cube. The power figure is the one that sizes a driver and gets bought.

## The mistake

The mistake is treating the band as a validity range. A ratio inside it is not certified and a ratio outside it is not forbidden. The engine returns an answer either way and comments in one case.

The second mistake is suppressing the warning because the answer looked reasonable. At a ratio of 100.000000 the flow of 123445.296915 gpm is absurd on its face. Just outside the band the numbers look ordinary, and that is where the warning does its work.

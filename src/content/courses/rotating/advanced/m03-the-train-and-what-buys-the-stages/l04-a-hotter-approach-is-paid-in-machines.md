# A hotter approach is paid in machines

A warmer cooler does not show up as a hotter train. It shows up as another machine, and this is the lesson that reads the price list.

{{panel:fc-compressor-explorer}}

## The count that the approach buys

Walking the SOKU intercooler approach upward with everything else held, the stage count holds at 3 for approaches of 90.0000, 100.0000, 104.0000, 110.0000 and 130.0000 degF. It rises to 4 at 150.0000 degF and to 5 at 180.0000 degF.

Nothing about the gas, the rate or the two pressures changed across that walk. The duty is the same duty. What changed is the temperature every stage after the first starts from, and the temperature limit answered by demanding more machines.

## Why the count is where it is paid

A warmer approach makes every stage after the first start hotter, so at a fixed count the train finishes hotter. That is affordable while there is room under the limit, and the room shrinks as the approach rises: on the three-stage rows it goes from 48.7044 degF down to 41.1365 and then 15.9102 degF.

Once it is no longer affordable the count moves, and the count moving is what keeps the discharge inside the limit. At 150.0000 degF the fourth machine brings the hottest stage to 265.9556 degF with 34.0444 degF of room, and at 180.0000 degF the fifth brings it to 275.6056 degF with 24.3944 degF of room. Across the whole walk 0 stages sit over the limit and 0 stages carry a warning.

## A case cut to press on it

Take the same gas from 92.000000 psia to 420.000000 psia at 104.0000 degF, with a stated discharge limit of 230.0000 degF and an approach of 165.0000 degF, which is 61.0000 degF above the suction.

The engine returns 5 stages, governed by discharge temperature. The stage discharges are 156.5982, 223.2904, 223.2904, 223.2904 and 223.2904 degF. The hottest is 223.2904 degF against a limit of 230.0000 degF, which leaves 6.7096 degF of room, and 0 of the 5 stages sit over the limit or carry a warning.

The first stage runs from the suction and reaches 156.5982 degF. Every later stage runs from the 165.0000 degF approach and reaches 223.2904 degF, a difference of 66.6921 degF at the same ratio. That gap between stage one and the rest is the approach being paid for, and what it bought was the count.

## The studio opens on the warm side

The Compressor Station Designer ships with an approach of 110 degF against a suction of 100 degF, so a studio opened and not touched is already above its suction. What sits on that side of the comparison is a stage count, and a stage count is a cooler, a foundation and a machine.

## The mistake

The mistake is arguing about a cooler approach as though it were only a utilities question. It is a machine-count question first, and a warmer approach shows up here as a capital item.

# The story so far

Compression is the same discipline as pumping with the fluid no longer incompressible and the machine no longer described by a curve anybody typed in. Every answer comes out of a thermodynamic path instead of an intersection.

## One stage, end to end

The SOKU K-2101 stage takes 26.000000 MMscfd of a 0.648000 gravity gas at 92.000000 psia and 104.0000 degF through a ratio of 3.200000, with k = 1.285000 at a polytropic efficiency of 0.755000.

The exponent ratio is 0.293761435 and the same function at an efficiency of one gives 0.221789883, a quotient of 1.324503311. The discharge is 294.4000 psia and 333.5954 degF, which is 63.7036 degF above what the isentropic exponent would have predicted. Compressibility is 0.987732307 at suction and 0.988949105 at discharge, averaged to 0.988340706. The mass flow is 53577.2205 lb per hr, the polytropic head 63604.9582 and the isentropic head 60870.3418 ft lbf per lbm, and the gas horsepower is 2279.6019 by either route because the two routes are one expression.

## One duty, staged

SOKU from 92.000000 psia to 985.000000 psia with a ratio limit of 4.000000 and a discharge limit of 300.0000 degF has an overall ratio of 10.706521739. The ratio rule demands 2 stages, the temperature limit demands 3, and the answer is 3 governed by discharge temperature, at 2.204023061 per stage.

The count was tested at the inlet the stages really have, which on this duty is 110.0000 degF rather than the 104.0000 degF suction.

## One train, built

Three stages, equal ratios, cooled back to 110.0000 degF. Gas horsepower 4337.6686, brake horsepower 4481.0626, cooling 8.5503 MMBtu per hr, final discharge 258.8635 degF against a stated limit of 300.0000 degF with 41.1365 degF of room.

Walk the approach upward and the count holds at 3 through 130.0000 degF, rises to 4 at 150.0000 degF and to 5 at 180.0000 degF. A hotter approach is paid in machines.

## The habits this tier is for

Name the exponent and the efficiency that produced it. Say which limit governed the stage count. Attach the stage count to any statement about cooling. Read the reason line beside a machine recommendation. Check the returned property rather than catching an exception, and read what a refusal carries beside its sentence.

# What a screening rule is worth

The honest status of a two-number indication.

{{panel:ps-sand-explorer}}

## What it is

Four thresholds on two numbers, from the published selection literature. The thresholds are round: two, three, five, ten. They are round because they are judgement, written down by people who had seen a lot of completions.

The engine attaches a provenance string to every indication saying exactly that, and naming what governs instead.

## What governs instead

Laboratory sand retention testing: the actual formation sand, the actual candidate screen, the actual completion fluid, flowed under representative conditions, with the retained permeability measured before and after.

That test answers the question directly rather than by proxy. It costs weeks and real money, which is why a screening rule exists at all.

## What the screening rule is for

Narrowing five options to two. Sending a lab programme the right two candidates rather than all five is worth a lot, and getting the right two out of a sieve analysis you already have is close to free.

## What it is not for

Being the answer. An indication of "gravel pack" is a statement that gravel pack is where the evidence points on two summary numbers, not a design decision.

And it is emphatically not for arguing against a lab result. If retention testing says a premium screen holds and the ladder said gravel pack, the ladder is wrong for that sand.

## The failure modes

A bimodal distribution, which two summary numbers cannot see.

Clay mineralogy, which the ladder has no input for and which changes everything about how fines behave.

A sample that is not the formation, either because the fines washed out during coring or because it was caught at surface and is therefore what the well was willing to give up.

## Why the provenance string is in the return value

Because a number returned without its status gets copied into a table, and a table gets copied into a report, and by then nobody remembers it was a screening rule.

Attaching the provenance to the value rather than to the documentation is the only way it survives the copying.

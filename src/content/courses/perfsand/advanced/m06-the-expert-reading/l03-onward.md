# Onward

Where this leaves you, and what to read next.

## The three tiers together

The Associate tier read the two inputs: a charge data sheet measured in the wrong material, and a sieve curve on a reversed convention with log interpolation between its points.

The Professional tier turned the first into a skin in four components, ranked a gun catalog, converted skin into a productivity ratio, and found the one catalog row whose numbers are an extrapolation.

The Expert tier turned the second into hardware and then asked whether the rock will fail at all, and found a defect in the sweep that asks it.

## The one sentence

A perforation is both a flow path and a cavity, and this course computes what the flow path is worth and whether the cavity will hold.

## Where to go in the academy

Geomechanics, which supplies the stress and strength profile the sanding sweep consumes. If you have not taken it, take it: everything in the last two modules rests on a profile this course does not derive.

Completion Design, for the string above the packer, and Stimulation Design, which is the alternative way of improving the connection this course improves geometrically.

Well Integrity and P and A, for what a screen and a set of perforations look like after twenty years.

## Where to go on a real well

Get the vendor's charge data sheet for the actual gun, charge and casing, and rerun the skin. Every number moves and no conclusion does.

Get the sieve certificate for the actual gravel rather than the nominal cut, and recompute the gauge margin from the real smallest grain.

And get a thick-walled cylinder test before you rely on any boost factor, or compute the boost at which the margin reaches zero and see whether the answer is anywhere near a plausible one.

## The habit worth keeping

When a routine walks a range, check both ends of what it gave you.

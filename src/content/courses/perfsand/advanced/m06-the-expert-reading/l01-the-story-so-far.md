# The story so far

What the Expert tier established.

## Five modules

A completion type comes from an ordered ladder of four rules on two numbers, and the ladder is exhaustive by construction. In the middle of the range the fines decide and the uniformity only takes over at the extremes.

Gravel is sized at five to six times the formation median, a band a factor of one point two wide against commercial cuts nearly two to one wide, so three of seven sample sands have no match at all and the nearest row is reported instead.

A gravel-pack screen is sized against the SMALLEST gravel grain, on a discrete gauge series that saturates at both ends, so the margin between the bound and the chosen gauge is an artefact rather than a decision. A standalone screen is sized on the coarse decile instead, because there the formation itself does the bridging.

Sanding onset is a Kirsch hoop stress at the cavity wall against the unconfined strength, with a factor of two on the flowing pressure that makes drawdown the dominant variable. Two cavity geometries pick two different stress pairs, and the perf tunnel is the tighter of the two in a normally stressed basin.

And the sweep along an interval, which used to drop its last rows.

## The three results worth keeping

That two summary numbers decide a completion type, and which of the two is doing the work depends on where in the range the sand sits.

That a narrow sizing band against a coarse product catalog leaves real gaps, and that a tool should say so rather than silently picking the nearest.

And that a margin computed at the wrong end of an interval can have the wrong sign.

## What you can claim

That you can take a sieve analysis to a completion type, size a gravel and both kinds of screen from it, compute a critical drawdown along an interval, and say what each of those results is worth.

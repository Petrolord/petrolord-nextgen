# When nothing matches

Three sands in seven, and what a designer does about it.

{{panel:ps-sand-explorer}}

## The sweep

Seven representative formation medians, from sixty microns up to three hundred, each through the Saucier band and against the seven-row catalog.

Four have a match. Three do not: the ones at a hundred, two hundred and three hundred microns.

## Where the gaps are

Between adjacent catalog rows, wherever the ratio between two consecutive row medians exceeds the one point two width of the band.

The catalog medians step by roughly a factor of one point three to one point six between adjacent rows, which is wider than the band. So there is a gap between every adjacent pair, and whether a given formation lands in one is a matter of where its median falls.

That means roughly speaking a formation has less than an even chance of having a clean match, which is worth knowing before you build a workflow that assumes one.

## The three responses

Take the nearest row and accept that the ratio is outside five to six. That is what most jobs do, and the direction of the miss matters: going finer than five times risks impairing the pack, going coarser than six risks letting the formation through. The conservative miss is fine.

Specify a custom cut. Vendors will sieve to a narrower specification at a price and a lead time.

Or reconsider the completion type. A formation that cannot be gravel packed cleanly might be a better candidate for a frac pack, where the pack conductivity requirement is different.

## What the engine will not do

Choose. It returns the band, the matches, the flag and the nearest row, and stops.

That is the right place to stop, because the choice depends on price, lead time, what is already on the boat and what the rest of the field used, none of which is in a sieve analysis.

## Exercise

Explain why the direction of a Saucier miss matters and which direction is the conservative one.

Then give the three responses to a no-match result and say what information each one needs that the engine does not have.

# Two cavity geometries

Which two far-field stresses go into the criterion, and why the answer depends on the completion type.

{{panel:ps-sand-explorer}}

## The choice

The criterion needs the maximum and minimum far-field stresses in the plane NORMAL to the cavity axis. Which two those are depends on which way the cavity points.

## The perforation tunnel

A perforation in a cased vertical well points horizontally, outward from the wellbore, at whatever azimuth the phasing put it.

The plane normal to a horizontal tunnel contains the vertical stress and one horizontal stress. Since the phasing puts tunnels at several azimuths, the worst-case tunnel is the one whose normal plane contains the largest stresses available, which the engine takes as the vertical stress and the maximum horizontal stress.

So for a perf tunnel the pair is the maximum and minimum of the overburden and the maximum horizontal stress.

## The open hole

A vertical open hole or a standalone screen completion has a vertical cavity: the wellbore itself.

The plane normal to a vertical axis is horizontal, and it contains the two horizontal stresses. So the pair is the maximum and minimum horizontal stress, and the overburden does not appear at all.

## Why the two give different answers

Because the overburden is usually the largest of the three stresses in a normally stressed basin, and it enters the perf-tunnel case and not the open-hole one.

Three times the larger stress dominates the expression, so swapping the overburden in for a horizontal stress raises the critical flowing pressure substantially. On the published profile the perf-tunnel margin is about half the open-hole one.

## Which is right for a given well

Whichever cavity the sand is coming from. A cased and perforated well fails at the perforations; a standalone screen completion in open hole fails at the borehole wall.

A gravel-packed cased hole is the perf-tunnel case, because the tunnels are still the cavities even though gravel is packed around them.

## The worst-case assumption

The engine takes the worst azimuth rather than an average over the phasing. That is conservative and it is stated: a real gun puts tunnels at several azimuths and only some of them are the worst one.

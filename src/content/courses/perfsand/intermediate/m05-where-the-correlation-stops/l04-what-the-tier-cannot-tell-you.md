# What the tier cannot tell you

The honest boundary of the Professional tier.

## What is now covered

A perforation skin in four components, each with its own mechanism and its own sensitivity. A gun catalog ranked at one rock, with the crossing from damaging to stimulating located. A productivity ratio, its reference, its bound and its weak dependence on the drainage radius. And the range over which the correlation was fitted, with the one catalog row that leaves it.

## Not covered: the rest of the total skin

Drilling damage, partial penetration, deviation, turbulence, scale and time. A well test sees the sum; this course computes one term.

## Not covered: the transient

Nothing here has time in it. Cleanup, the surge during underbalanced perforating, and the decline of a tunnel over years are all transient processes and all absent.

## Not covered: the charge

Penetration and entrance hole are inputs from a catalog measured in concrete. This course does not predict them, does not correct them for downhole conditions, and does not model the jet.

## Not covered: whether the gun will fit or fire

Gun clearance, running, detonation, misfires, debris, and the effect of the shot on the casing. All real, all absent.

## Not covered: multiphase and non-Darcy

Single phase Darcy flow throughout. A high rate gas well is neither.

## What the tier does claim

That the perforation contribution to skin, for the geometry given, is what the published correlation says it is, computed to the correlation's own precision, with its development range checked and reported.

That is a narrow claim, and it is the claim the correlation itself supports. Anything wider would be borrowed authority.

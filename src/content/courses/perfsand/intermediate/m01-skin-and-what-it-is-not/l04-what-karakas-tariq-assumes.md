# What Karakas and Tariq assume

The conditions the correlation was derived under, and which of them your well will violate.

## The model

A finite element solution of steady, single-phase, incompressible flow into a periodic array of identical perforations around a cylindrical wellbore, in a homogeneous anisotropic medium, with the results fitted to closed-form expressions with tabulated constants.

Every word of that is an assumption.

## Steady and single phase

No transient, so nothing about cleanup, and no time. No gas coming out of solution near the wellbore, no water coning, no relative permeability.

A gas well perforated at high drawdown has multiphase flow in the tunnel, and the skin the well shows will not be this number.

## Identical and periodic

Every perforation the same length, the same radius, evenly spaced, at exactly the phasing angle. A real gun has scatter in penetration from charge to charge, misfires, and a phasing that is nominal.

The periodic assumption is what allows one unit cell to stand for the whole interval. It is a good approximation for a long uniform interval and a poor one for a short one.

## Homogeneous

One permeability everywhere except the crushed shell. No laminations, no shale streaks, no cemented bands.

Laminated reservoirs are where this matters. A perforation that lands in a shale streak contributes nothing, and the correlation has no way to represent one.

## No turbulence

The flow is Darcy everywhere. High rate gas wells develop non-Darcy pressure drop in the tunnels, which appears as a rate-dependent skin on top of everything here.

That term can be larger than the whole perforation skin at high rate, and it is not in this model.

## The one assumption everybody forgets

The tunnel is OPEN. The correlation describes a clean cylindrical hole with a damaged shell around it. A tunnel still full of charge debris, crushed rock and mud solids is not that geometry at all, and no permeability ratio describes it.

That is what underbalance is for, and it is why a perforation skin computed before the well has been cleaned up is a statement about the intended geometry rather than the actual one.

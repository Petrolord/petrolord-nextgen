# The story so far

What the Professional tier established.

## Five modules

Skin is an additive dimensionless correction to a radial logarithm, and this course computes ONE contribution to it from geometry alone. The absolute permeability cancels; only ratios survive.

Four components. Plane flow, through the effective wellbore radius, the only one that can be negative and by far the largest. Converging flow, from the vertical path between shots, attacked by shot density. Wellbore blockage, tiny when phased and not tiny when in line. And the crushed zone, the only one an operation can remove.

Nine guns at one rock rank monotonically by carrier size and cross from damaging to stimulating exactly where the catalog starts phasing its shots.

The productivity ratio converts a skin into a fraction of an ideal, needs no permeability because everything cancels, depends only weakly on the drainage radius, and has a bound that fractures reach and perforations do not.

The correlation has a stated development range, and one catalog row leaves it. That row is the best-looking row in the table.

## The idea that runs through it

Every result in this tier is a comparison of two geometries at the same everything else. That is what makes a dimensionless number worth having and it is what makes each result transferable.

It is also what makes each result narrow. The comparison is clean precisely because so much has been held constant, and the held-constant list is where the real well differs.

## What you can claim

That you can compute a perforation skin in four components, explain what moves each, rank a gun catalog, convert a skin to a productivity ratio and state what that ratio is a ratio of, and recognise when a result is an extrapolation of a fit.

## Exercise

Without looking, name the four skin components and say which is largest and which can be negative.

State where the catalog crosses zero and why it crosses there.

Then explain why the absolute permeability does not appear in a perforation skin.

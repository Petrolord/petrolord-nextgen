# Which one moves the answer

Ranking the four components by how much they actually vary, and what that says about design.

{{panel:ps-skin-explorer}}

## The experiment

Take the nine gun catalog, run every row at the same rock, the same wellbore and the same crushed zone, and look at how far each component swings from top to bottom of the table.

That isolates the effect of the GUN, which is the thing a designer chooses, from everything else.

## The ranking

The plane-flow term swings by nearly two and a half skin units, from slightly positive on the smallest gun to about minus two point one on the biggest.

The crushed-zone term swings by about one and four tenths, from nearly one and a half down to a few hundredths.

The converging-flow term swings by about half a unit.

The blockage term swings by about a third of a unit, almost all of it in the two in-line guns.

## What that says

The plane-flow term is the design, and phasing and penetration are its two inputs.

But the crushed-zone swing is the surprise. It is second largest, and it is second largest not because the damage varies, which it does not in this experiment, but because the spacing over tunnel length factor varies enormously across the catalog.

So a gun choice changes the crushed-zone skin without changing the crushed zone.

## The one-at-a-time view

Holding the gun and moving one input at a time gives a different ranking, because the catalog moves several inputs together.

Doubling the tunnel length on the published high-shot-density gun helps, and halving it hurts more than the doubling helped, which is the logarithm at work. Doubling the shot density lowers the converging term. Raising the anisotropy to ten to one raises the converging term substantially. Raising the crushed contrast to twenty costs more than any of them.

## What a designer should take from it

Choose the phasing first, because zero phasing is a formula change and nothing else on the sheet competes with it.

Then the penetration, because it enters both the plane-flow term and the crushed-zone factor.

Then the shot density, because it attacks the converging term and the crushed factor together.

And then spend the effort on cleaning the tunnel, because a severe crushed zone can undo a third of what the first three bought.

## Exercise

Rank the four components by how much they swing across the gun catalog.

Explain why the crushed-zone term swings so much when the crushed zone itself is held constant.

Then write down the design order this lesson recommends and justify each step.

# Skin into a flow number

The one-line conversion from a dimensionless skin to something a client can read.

{{panel:ps-skin-explorer}}

## The expression

The productivity ratio is the logarithm of the drainage radius over the wellbore radius, divided by the same logarithm plus the total skin.

That is the whole calculation. It follows directly from the steady radial flow equation: rate is proportional to the pressure drop divided by the logarithm plus the skin, and the ratio compares that against the same well with no skin.

## What it means

The flow rate of the perforated completion, as a fraction of the flow rate the same well would have with no skin at all, at the same drawdown.

A ratio of zero point eight means eighty percent of the ideal. A ratio of one point three means thirty percent BETTER than the ideal, which is what a negative skin buys.

## Why a ratio rather than a rate

Because a rate would need a permeability, a thickness, a viscosity, a formation volume factor and a drawdown, and none of those is in this model.

The ratio needs none of them, because they all cancel. Both the actual and the ideal well have the same permeability, thickness, viscosity and drawdown, and the only thing that differs is the skin.

That cancellation is what makes a dimensionless number useful: it isolates the one thing being compared.

## What it is comparing against

An open hole of the same radius, with no skin. Not another gun, not the well before perforating, and not the well as drilled.

So a ratio of one point three does not mean thirty percent more than the next gun in the catalog. Comparing two guns means computing both ratios and comparing those.

## The published pair

The through-tubing gun gives about zero point eight and the high-shot-density gun about one point two seven. So the second gun flows about fifty nine percent more than the first at the same drawdown, which is a ratio of ratios rather than either number on its own.

# Phasing is the biggest lever

The sweep that proves it, and the reason the effect is a discontinuity rather than a trend.

{{panel:ps-skin-explorer}}

## The sweep

Hold one gun completely fixed and change only the phasing angle through the six tabulated values. Nothing else moves: same tunnel, same radius, same density, same rock, same crushed zone.

## The result

Forty five degrees is the best of the six. Zero is by far the worst. The step between them is worth over one and four tenths of a skin unit on the published high-shot-density gun, and more than ninety percent of that is the plane-flow term alone.

Between the five non-zero angles the variation is much smaller: the constant falls monotonically from forty five degrees to a hundred and eighty, so more spread is worse rather than better, and the whole range from forty five to a hundred and eighty is a fraction of the step from zero.

## Why forty five and not ninety

Because the fitted constant is largest at forty five, which makes the effective wellbore radius largest, which makes the plane-flow skin most negative.

That is empirical rather than obvious. A naive argument says the more directions you shoot, the more symmetric the drainage, so a hundred and eighty degrees alternating should be worst and sixty or ninety best. The fitted constants say otherwise, and they come from a finite element solution rather than an argument.

## Why zero is a cliff and not a step

Because it is a different expression. Every phased angle uses a constant times the sum of the wellbore radius and the tunnel length. Zero uses a quarter of the tunnel length, with the wellbore radius absent.

On a well with a four and a quarter inch wellbore radius and a foot-long tunnel, dropping the wellbore radius from the sum and replacing a constant near nine tenths with a quarter is a large change to the effective radius, and it goes through a logarithm.

## What a designer does with this

Never accept zero phasing unless the gun size forces it. And when it is forced, know what it costs, which is around a skin unit and a half of pure geometry that no amount of penetration will buy back inside a logarithm.

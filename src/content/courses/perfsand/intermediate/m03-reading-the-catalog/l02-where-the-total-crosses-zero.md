# Where the total crosses zero

The most useful single fact in the catalog, and what it means for a well.

{{panel:ps-skin-explorer}}

## The crossing

Between the two and an eighth inch through-tubing gun, whose total is positive, and the two and seven eighths inch gun, whose total is negative.

Above the line: two guns, both through-tubing, both at zero phasing, both at four shots per foot.

Below the line: seven guns, one through-tubing and six casing, all at real phasing angles and at six or twelve shots per foot.

## What positive means here

That perforating the well with that gun leaves it flowing WORSE than an open hole of the same radius with no damage. The tunnels are too short and too far apart to make up for the fact that the casing has replaced a continuous cylinder with a few small holes.

## Why the line falls where it does

Not because of size directly. The two guns above the line share three features: in-line phasing, four shots per foot, and short tunnels.

Phasing is the strongest of the three, and the crossing coincides exactly with the phasing change from zero to sixty degrees in the catalog. The two and seven eighths gun is only three quarters of an inch bigger than the one above it and it is a different animal, because it is the smallest carrier that can phase.

## What that means operationally

Through-tubing perforating is a compromise you accept to avoid pulling the completion. It buys you a live well and a short job, and it costs you the difference between a positive skin and a negative one.

Whether that trade is worth it depends on the value of the deferred workover, not on anything in this calculation. The calculation's job is to price the trade.

## What it does not mean

That a through-tubing gun ruins a well. A positive perforation skin of around two, on a well whose reservoir logarithm is around eight, costs about twenty percent of the ideal rate. That is a real cost and it is not a catastrophe.

## Exercise

Say where the catalog crosses zero and which two guns bracket the crossing.

Give the three features the guns above the line share and say which is the strongest.

Then price the trade: for a well with a reservoir logarithm of about eight, what fraction of the ideal rate does a skin of about two cost?

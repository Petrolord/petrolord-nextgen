# Nine guns, one rock

The experiment that makes the catalog readable, and the first thing it shows.

{{panel:ps-skin-explorer}}

## The setup

Every one of the nine catalog guns, run at the same wellbore radius, the same permeability anisotropy and the same crushed zone. Each gun brings only its own penetration, entrance hole, shot density and phasing.

Everything else is held. So every difference down the table is the gun.

## The first result

The table is monotone in gun size. The smallest gun has the highest total skin and the largest gun has the lowest, and every step in between goes the same way without exception.

That is a stronger result than it sounds. Nine guns vary in four ways at once, and four-way variation does not usually line up into a single ranking. Here it does, because all four inputs improve together with carrier size: bigger guns carry longer charges, wider charges, more of them, and can afford to phase them.

## The productivity ratios

From about zero point seven five on the smallest gun to about one point three five on the largest.

So the choice of gun, at one rock, spans a factor of about one point eight in productivity against the ideal. That is a large number for a decision made in an afternoon from a catalog.

## What the monotonicity hides

It is a property of THIS catalog, in which nobody has offered a small gun with a long charge or a big gun with a low shot density. Real vendors do offer combinations that break the ordering, particularly slimline deep-penetrating charges.

So the lesson is not that bigger is better. It is that in this catalog four things improve together, and the ranking is what happens when they do.

## The caution the table carries

It is one rock. Change the anisotropy and the converging term moves for every row, but not by the same amount, because the rows have different shot densities. The ordering happens to survive that here; it is not guaranteed to.

## Exercise

Describe the experiment and say what is held constant.

State the ranking result and explain why four-way variation lining up into one ranking is not automatic.

Then say what would have to be true of a vendor catalog for the ranking to break, and whether such guns exist.

# Two numbers that decide everything

The uniformity and the fines, together, and what a two-number rule can and cannot do.

{{panel:ps-shot-explorer}}

## The pair

Every completion type indication in this course comes from exactly two numbers: the uniformity coefficient and the fines percentage.

Not the median. Not the sorting. Not the absolute grain size. Two numbers, on an ordered ladder of four rungs.

## Why those two

Because they describe the two ways a screen fails.

A sand that is not uniform defeats the bridging mechanism, because there is a wide range of sizes and the bridge is leaky. That is the uniformity.

A sand with a lot of fine material plugs whatever bridges, because the fines travel through and lodge. That is the fines.

A sand can fail either way independently, which is why the rule needs both and why neither alone is enough.

## What the median does not decide

Nothing, on the ladder. A coarse uniform clean sand and a fine uniform clean sand get the same indication.

That is correct and it surprises people. Grain size sets what SIZE of screen or gravel you need. It does not set what TYPE of completion you need, because a screen can be manufactured to almost any slot width and a gravel can be bought in almost any mesh.

The type is decided by shape of the distribution, and the size is decided afterwards.

## The limitation

Two numbers cannot describe a bimodal distribution. A sand that is half coarse and half fine, with nothing in between, can have a perfectly ordinary uniformity and a perfectly ordinary fines percentage, and behave like neither.

Nor can two numbers see mineralogy, angularity, cementation or the fluid that will flow through.

## What the rule is actually for

Narrowing the options before the expensive tests. A screening rule that puts a well on the right rung tells the completion engineer which two candidate designs to send for laboratory sand retention testing, out of five.

It is not a substitute for that testing, and the engine says so in the provenance string attached to every indication it returns.

# The story so far

What the Associate tier established, in the order it established it.

## Five modules

Two questions share one app, and they meet at the perforation: what the hole does to the flow, and what the sand does to the completion.

A charge is sold on an entrance hole and a penetration, both measured in an API concrete target, both flagged approximate. Shot density is quoted per foot and used as a spacing, which is its reciprocal. Phasing is one of six tabulated angles, and zero is a different formula rather than a smaller angle.

Four lengths and nothing else describe the geometry: tunnel length, tunnel radius, spacing and wellbore radius. The effective wellbore radius turns the whole set of tunnels into one equivalent well. The crushed zone is the only skin term an operation can remove.

A sieve analysis is cumulative RETAINED, so D10 is the coarse decile, and every value between sieves is interpolated on a log scale.

Two numbers, the uniformity and the fines, decide the completion type. The median decides the size and not the type.

## What holds it together

Both halves of the tier are about reading an input honestly.

A charge data sheet is a measurement in the wrong material, and a sieve curve is a weight distribution read on a reversed convention with log interpolation between its points. Get either of those wrong at the input and everything downstream is confidently wrong.

## What you can claim

That you can read a gun catalog and a sieve table, convert a shot density into the spacing the calculation uses, name the four geometric inputs to a perforation skin, and compute the D-values, the uniformity and the fines from a sieve curve.

Not yet what any of it does to the flow, and not yet which gravel or screen follows.

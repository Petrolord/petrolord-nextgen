# Mobilisation and first deliveries

Mobilisation is the stretch between signing and steady running: people, equipment and paperwork move into place, and the first work or goods arrive.

## Why the phase matters

The UK Sourcing Playbook (June 2023), Chapter 12, says:

> "Immediately following contract award and prior to the contract start date, mobilisation is a key phase in setting up an outsourcing project for success."

The UK Government Functional Standard GovS 008: Commercial (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026) treats mobilisation as the start of delivery (5.4.1). Both are UK practice.

## Securities and insurances before money moves

The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024) sets out start-up checks (pp.13 to 14), taught here by concept. The employer checks that any performance security matches the contract in amount, validity and wording; verifies a doubtful security with the institution that issued it before paying an advance; pays the advance on time once an acceptable advance payment guarantee is in; and checks that the required insurances are in place.

## A mobilisation fee in Nigerian public contracts

Nigeria's Public Procurement Act 2007 (Act No. 14, Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007) binds federal procuring entities. For an operator such as Ekene it is a published statement of public practice. Section 35(1) provides that "a mobilization fee of not more than 15% may be paid to a supplier or contractor supported by the following", and s.35(2) adds:

> "Once a mobilization fee has been paid to any supplier or contractor, no further payment shall be made to the supplier or contractor without an interim performance certificate issued in accordance with the contract agreement."

Section 36 makes a Performance Guarantee a precondition for any contract on which a mobilisation fee is paid, of not less than 10% of the contract value or an amount equal to the fee requested, whichever is higher. The Ekene contracts follow their own terms on advances. The public rule is a useful model of the discipline: security first, money second, and a certificate before the next payment.

## First deliveries

The first call-off, the first delivery or the first milestone tests every arrangement at once. The contract manager checks what arrived against the specification, records it, and confirms the invoice route works before volume builds up.

EKC-05 is a synthetic flowline replacement at the Ekene manifold. Supplier FW-E (synthetic) works for a lump sum paid against milestones, with a retention held from each payment until completion, a performance security and a defects period. In this scenario, FW-E asks Ekene to release a first payment next week, before any milestone.

Write a checklist of four items you would clear before any money goes to FW-E, with the source each rests on.

### A reading

First, confirm the contract allows a payment before a milestone at all; the contract governs. Second, check the performance security against the contract in amount, validity and wording, and verify it with the issuing bank if in doubt, as the World Bank guidance describes. Third, if the contract provides an advance, hold it until an acceptable advance payment guarantee is in. Fourth, confirm the required insurances. The Public Procurement Act 2007, ss.35 and 36, shows the same order in Nigerian public practice.

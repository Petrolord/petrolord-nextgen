# From tender team to contract team

At award, the people who wrote and evaluated the tender step back and the people who will run the contract step forward. What the first group knew has to reach the second, or the contract starts with gaps nobody can see.

## Where this course starts

Tender evaluation, the award decision and Nigerian content at the tender belong to the procurement course, Procurement, Tendering & Contracting. This course picks the contract up at award.

## Handover is a principle

The UK Contract Management Principles (Crown Commercial Service, undated, page updated 12 December 2025) state principle 1:

> "Make sure that adequate resource is identified and appointed well before award of contracts and that there is an effective handover or transition from sourcing to contract management."

The UK Sourcing Playbook (June 2023), Chapter 12, adds that how a contract will be managed is a key strategic decision to be settled early and reflected in the contract.

## What passes across

A handover file usually carries the signed contract and every schedule, the clarifications agreed during tender, the supplier's key people, the reasons behind unusual clauses, and the risks the tender team saw. On an Ekene contract it also carries the Nigerian content numbers given at award. Under the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (the content Act; Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010), s.22, the operator gives the Board, before award, the selected contractor, designated sub-contractors, start and completion dates and, for construction or service contracts, the estimated Nigerian employment in person-hours. Those award-stage numbers become the baseline the contract manager later reports against.

## Authority and conflicts

Two checks belong to handover. The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024) asks the contract manager to confirm at the start that each party has its authorisations and delegations in place, since a decision taken without authority may not be valid. The UK guidance on conflicts of interest under the Procurement Act 2023 (Define phase guidance, Crown copyright 2024) says the conflicts assessment should be refreshed when responsibility passes from the procurement team to a contract management or operations team (para 24). That is UK practice, and a useful habit.

## Exercise

EKC-01 is a synthetic well services call-off framework. Supplier WS-A (synthetic) provides coiled tubing, nitrogen and pumping services ordered by call-off, priced from a schedule of rates. The framework states KPIs for mobilisation on time, non-productive time caused by the supplier and safety reporting, a quarterly performance review, and a Nigerian content plan with quarterly reporting.

You are the new contract manager. Write a handover checklist of five lines you want from the tender team before the first call-off.

### A reading

A sound list asks for the signed framework with its schedule of rates and every schedule; the stated KPIs and how each is measured; the Nigerian content plan with the award-stage figures given to the Board under the content Act, s.22, as the reporting baseline; the named people and delegations on both sides, confirmed in writing; and a note of the tender team's known risks and any clarifications agreed during tender. A line on refreshing conflicts of interest for the new team is a good addition drawn from UK practice.

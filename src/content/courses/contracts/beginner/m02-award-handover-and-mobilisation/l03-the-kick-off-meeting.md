# The kick-off meeting

The kick-off meeting is the first time both contract teams sit together with the signed contract in front of them. Done well, it settles the questions that would otherwise surface as arguments three months later.

## What the meeting is for

The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024) describes a kick-off meeting for consulting services (p.82), and its list serves any contract. In this course's words, the meeting should at least:

- introduce the parties and their roles;
- set how the parties will communicate and report;
- walk through the contract documents and the payment schedule;
- agree the escalation route for a problem that stalls;
- confirm that everyone shares one understanding of the obligations, the timeline and the risks.

The World Bank texts are taught here by concept, as published practice.

## Walking through the documents

The guidance adds detail to the walk-through (item 3): everyone should come away knowing the key provisions, which document takes priority over which, the conditions, the terms of reference, the payment schedule and the milestones. Module three of this tier teaches the order of precedence. At the kick-off, the point is to say it out loud with the supplier in the room, so that both sides read the contract the same way from the first day.

## Roles and authority

The UK Government Functional Standard GovS 008: Commercial (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026) treats mobilisation as the start of delivery (5.4.1). A simple, low cost contract may need no commercial specialist support at all; for a more complex contract the standard expects the roles for managing it to be confirmed during mobilisation. The kick-off is where that confirmation happens in front of both parties. The World Bank guidance also asks the contract manager to confirm at the start that each party has its authorisations and delegations in place. A kick-off that lists names and leaves out what each person may decide has done half the job.

## The record of the meeting

The minutes are the first entry in the contract's record. They list who attended, what was agreed, the actions with owners and dates, and any question left open. Both sides should see and accept them.

## Exercise

EKC-07 is a synthetic environmental monitoring consultancy. Supplier EM-G (synthetic) carries out quarterly environmental sampling and reporting, is paid a time-based fee against approved timesheets, and has a named key person and a report acceptance step.

Write a kick-off agenda of six items for EKC-07, with one line under each saying what you want settled by the end of the meeting.

### A reading

A sound agenda follows the World Bank list and fills it for this contract. Parties and roles: EM-G's named key person, the Ekene contract manager and whoever accepts the report. Authority: who may approve timesheets and who may accept or return a report, confirmed in writing. Communication and reporting: the quarterly report's format and delivery route. Documents: which document prevails, the terms of reference and the fee basis. Payment: timesheets first approved, then invoiced. Escalation: the route if a report is returned twice. The meeting closes with actions and owners minuted and agreed by both sides.

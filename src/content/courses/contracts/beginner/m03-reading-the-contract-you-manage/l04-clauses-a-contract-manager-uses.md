# Clauses a contract manager uses every week

A long contract holds dozens of clauses, but a manager returns to a handful again and again.

## The working clauses

The UK Contract Management Framework Summary (Crown Commercial Service, undated, based on the National Audit Office framework of December 2008) sets the expectation in Area 8, item 10:

> "Contract management staff understand the contractual terms including contract extension (services and duration), termination warranties, indemnities, insurance, security and confidentiality and dispute resolution."

The UK Sourcing Playbook (June 2023), Chapter 7, adds the clauses users of its model services contract should tailor, among them performance indicators and service credits, insurance, invoicing and indexation, and financial distress. Both are UK practice.

## Change control

The change clause is the one a manager touches most. The UK Government Functional Standard GovS 008: Commercial (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026) says at 5.4.5:

> "Contract changes shall be justified and controlled to ensure the requirements continue to meet the organisation’s business needs and align with the organisation’s financial processes"

The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), taught by concept, asks for clear roles in change management, with named people holding delegated authority to act on a change request or escalate it. At Associate level, know where the change clause is and who may act under it. The Professional tier teaches how a change request is assessed.

## Reporting duties the law puts in the contract

Two clauses in Ekene contracts exist, where they apply, because the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (the content Act; Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010) requires them. Section 65:

> "The operator shall ensure that its partners, contractors and subcontractors are contractually bound to report Nigerian content information to the operator and, if so requested by the Board, directly to the Board"

And s.34 requires that "all projects or contracts whose total budget exceeds $100 million (USD) shall contain a "Labour Clause" mandating the use of a minimum percentage of Nigerian labour in specific cadres as may be stipulated by the Board." Whether a contract crosses that budget is a fact to check in its file.

## Ending the contract

The UK Cabinet Office guidance on contract terminations under the Procurement Act 2023 (April 2026 PDF) says contracting authorities should always set out in the contract what happens when it is terminated (para 18). A manager knows where that clause is; using it belongs to the Expert tier.

## Exercise

EKC-01 is a synthetic well services call-off framework. Supplier WS-A (synthetic) provides coiled tubing, nitrogen and pumping services by call-off, priced from a schedule of rates, with stated KPIs, a quarterly performance review and a Nigerian content plan with quarterly reporting.

Write a clause map of six lines for WS-A: the clause, and when you would expect to open it.

### A reading

A sound map names: the call-off procedure, opened with every order; the schedule of rates, with every invoice; the KPI schedule, monthly and at each quarterly review; the Nigerian content reporting clause required by the content Act, s.65, each quarter; the change clause and its named authorities, whenever work outside a call-off is asked for; and insurance and indemnities, at mobilisation and when an incident occurs. Termination and dispute resolution are located and noted for later.

# Records that survive an audit

Sooner or later someone who was not there will read the contract file: an internal auditor, the Board, a successor, or a tribunal. A record survives an audit when a stranger can follow what happened, and why, from the file alone. Audit as a discipline belongs to the compliance course.

## What to keep

The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), taught here by concept, lists what to record: how the supplier performed and delivered, every communication and notice, the dates, and who was involved. A manager adds the documents that move money and scope: orders, inspection records, certificates, checked invoices and approved changes. The UK Government Functional Standard GovS 008: Commercial (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026) expects change control to leave an audit trail in a change register, with evidence kept for later procurement or disputes (5.4.5).

## Who may inspect

On an Ekene contract the Nigerian content record is open to the regulator. The Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (the content Act; Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010), s.64, provides that "all operators and contractors shall provide the Board or its designated agent with access to their facilities and all documentation and information required for substantiating the Nigerian content reported." Among the Board's functions, s.70(k) lists to "make auditing procedures and conduct regular audits for the purposes of monitoring and implementing compliances with the provisions of this Act;".

Nigeria's Public Procurement Act 2007 (Act No. 14, Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007) binds federal procuring entities, and for an operator it is published public practice. Its s.38(5) makes their procurement records available for inspection by the Bureau, an investigator it appoints and the Auditor-General on request.

## How long to keep it

The Public Procurement Act 2007 requires procuring entities to keep file and electronic records of procurement proceedings (s.16(12)), but the printed Act gives the retention period in garbled words, so this course teaches no period from it. An Ekene manager follows the retention rule in the contract and in the company's own policy.

## A record that works

A good record is made at the time, dated, named, filed where the next reader will look, and linked to its clause.

## Exercise

EKC-04 is a synthetic casing and tubulars frame agreement. Supplier TB-D (synthetic) supplies casing and tubing at stated unit prices per item, with delivery lead times stated per order, and is the only supplier qualified for one premium connection. In this scenario, a delivery of premium connection casing arrives two weeks late, and three joints fail inspection.

List the records that should be in the file for this delivery, so that an auditor could follow it a year from now.

### A reading

The file holds the order with its stated lead time and unit prices; the delivery note with the date received; the inspection report naming the three failed joints and who inspected them; the notice to TB-D about the late delivery and the rejected joints, with its date and any reply; the invoice with the check showing the rejected joints were not paid; and the replacement delivery and its inspection. Each is dated and named, as the World Bank guidance describes.

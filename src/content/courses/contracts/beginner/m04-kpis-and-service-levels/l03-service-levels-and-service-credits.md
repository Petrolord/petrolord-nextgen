# Service levels and service credits

Some contracts go a step beyond measuring performance: they attach money to it. A service level states the standard of service. A service credit is the stated deduction the contract applies when a level is missed. Both come from the contract's own words.

## Where they appear

Two of the synthetic Ekene contracts carry them. EKC-03, camp catering and facility services with CF-C (synthetic), has service levels for meal service, cleanliness audits and response to maintenance requests, with service credits deducted from the fixed monthly fee when a level is missed. EKC-02, the platform supply vessel charter with MV-B (synthetic), states the vessel's availability as its service level, and its off-hire provision reduces what is paid when the vessel breaks down.

The UK Sourcing Playbook (June 2023), Chapter 7, lists performance indicators and service credits among the parts of its model services contract that users should tailor to the service. The World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers (Seventh Edition, September 2025), taught by concept, describe a performance-based contract as one that ties payment to measured outputs meeting needs of quality, quantity and reliability (Annex VIII, para 3.4). Service credits are one way of building that link into a fixed fee.

## Applying a credit

A credit is applied the way the contract says: the measurement period, the evidence, the amount or formula, any cap, and when the deduction is made. The manager does not negotiate a credit away informally, and does not add one the contract does not state. Each application is recorded with its evidence.

## Checking that payment reflects service

The UK Government Functional Standard GovS 008: Commercial (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026) says at 5.4.4:

> "Payments should be audited periodically to ensure they reflect the contract terms and service levels received from suppliers."

That is UK practice, and a useful check on any contract with service levels.

## A credit is a signal

A credit adjusts the price. It does not fix the service. The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024) expects the underlying cause of a missed KPI to be found and dealt with through an action plan. The same habit fits a missed service level: apply the credit, then ask why.

## Exercise

EKC-03 is a synthetic camp catering and facility services contract. Supplier CF-C (synthetic) is paid a fixed monthly fee, with service levels for meal service, cleanliness audits and response to maintenance requests, and service credits deducted from the monthly fee when a level is missed. In this scenario, two cleanliness audits this month scored below the stated level, and CF-C's invoice shows the full monthly fee.

Write a short note to CF-C and a one-line entry for the contract file.

### A reading

The note cites the cleanliness service level and the credit the contract states, attaches the two audit results, and says the credit will be deducted from this month's fee as the contract provides, asking CF-C to reissue the invoice or accept the deduction. It asks CF-C for the cause and a plan to correct it. The file entry records the month, the level missed, the evidence, the credit applied and the date the note was sent.

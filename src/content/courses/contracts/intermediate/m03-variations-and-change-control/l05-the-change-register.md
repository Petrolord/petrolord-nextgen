# The change register

Every change this module has taught ends as a line in one place. The change register is the contract's memory of how it moved away from what was signed. It lets anyone, years later, see what changed, why, who approved it, and what it cost.

Every text in this lesson was read on 27 September 2026.

## Why a register

GovS 008 (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026), at 5.4.5, a UK text read here as practice, expects change control to leave an audit trail in a change register, with approvals obtained before implementation and evidence retained for later procurement or disputes.

The World Bank Procurement Regulations (Seventh Edition, September 2025), taught by concept, list among the usual contents of a contract management plan both variation and change control and record keeping, and they expect the key deliverables to be kept up to date for change orders. The register is where those two meet.

## What a line carries

| field | what it records |
| --- | --- |
| number | a unique change number that is not reused |
| date raised and by whom | the request and its source |
| description | what changes, in the contract's own words |
| scope check | why it is outside the existing scope |
| justification | the operational need |
| price basis | contract rate, historical cost or market rate |
| time effect | any change to dates or duration |
| cumulative total | this change added to all earlier ones |
| approval | who approved, under which delegation, on what date |
| instruction | date of the written instruction to the supplier |
| status | raised, assessed, approved, instructed, closed or rejected |

The cumulative total is the column that shows a pattern. Four small changes look harmless one by one, and their total tells the contract owner something the single lines cannot.

## Rejected changes stay

A rejected request stays in the register with its reason. If the supplier raises the same matter later, the record shows it was considered and why it was refused.

## Linked to the rest of the record

Each line points to its evidence: the request, the assessment, the approval and the instruction. The World Bank's Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), by concept, lists what a contract manager records: performance and delivery, every communication and notice, the dates, and who was involved. The register is the index to that part of the record. The Expert tier returns to the register at close-out, when the changes are evaluated for what they taught.

## Exercise

EKC-02 is the synthetic Ekene platform supply vessel time charter with MV-B, paid a day rate with off-hire for breakdown. The scenario adds its own facts: the Ekene logistics lead asks for MV-B's vessel to carry out an extra standby duty at a second installation each week, and the contract owner approves the change under her delegation after the contract manager assesses it at the charter's day rate.

Write the register line for this change, one entry per field.

### A reading

A good line gives the next free number; the date and the logistics lead as source; the standby duty described in the charter's own terms; the scope check (the charter covers one installation); the justification (the second installation's need); price basis at the charter's day rate; the time effect; the running total with earlier changes; the contract owner's approval, her delegation and the date; the date of the written instruction to MV-B; and status "instructed". Vessel planning itself belongs to the marine course.

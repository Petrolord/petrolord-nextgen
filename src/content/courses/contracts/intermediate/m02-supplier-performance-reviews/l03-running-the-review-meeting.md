# Running the review meeting

A review meeting has one job: to leave both parties agreeing what happened, why, and what each will do next. This lesson sets out an agenda that does that job and the record it produces.

Every text in this lesson was read on 27 September 2026.

## Before the meeting

The scorecard goes to the supplier ahead of the meeting, with its evidence, so the supplier comes with its own records and the time goes on causes.

## An agenda that works

1. **Actions from the last review.** Each one is closed with evidence, or carried with a new date.
2. **The scorecard.** KPI lines first, then management measures, each with its rating.
3. **Causes.** For every KPI missed, why it was missed.
4. **Actions.** What each party will do, who owns it, and by when.
5. **Nigerian content.** The period's report against the contract's plan.
6. **Look ahead.** Planned work, known risks, and anything either party needs from the other.

## Causes before actions

The World Bank's Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), by concept, expects that when a KPI is missed the underlying cause is found and the problem is dealt with through an action plan.

GovS 008 (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026), at 5.4.3, sets the bar for what follows:

> Where performance is unlikely to, or does not meet the requirements of the contract, preventative and/or corrective action should be taken, within the terms of the contract, to remediate the situation, such as through formal notice or a performance improvement plan.

The words "unlikely to" matter. A review can act on a trend before a KPI is missed.

## Nigerian content on the agenda

The content Act (Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010) applies to every Ekene contract. Its s.66 reads: "The operators shall effectively communicate its Nigerian content policies and procedures to its contractors and subcontractors and to monitor and enforce their compliance." The review is where that monitoring happens.

## The record

The minutes carry the ratings agreed, the causes found, each action with owner and date, and any point still disputed, with both views. They join the record the World Bank guidance asks a contract manager to keep: performance, communications and notices, dates, and the people involved.

## Exercise

At the second quarterly review of EKC-01 (synthetic), WS-A missed its KPI on non-productive time caused by the supplier. The scenario adds its own facts: two coiled tubing jobs were delayed while a replacement injector head was brought from WS-A's base, and WS-A's quarterly Nigerian content report arrived on the day of the meeting.

Write the minute for item 3 and item 4 of the agenda.

### A reading

Item 3 records the cause as spares held at the base and away from the well site, agreed by both parties or recorded with both views. Item 4 records an action for WS-A to hold a spare injector head at the Ekene shore base, with an owner and a date, and an action to send the content report ahead of the next review so it can be read. Following GovS 008 at 5.4.3, a repeat at the next review would lead to a performance improvement plan or formal notice.

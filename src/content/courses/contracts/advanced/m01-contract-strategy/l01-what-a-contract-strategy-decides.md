# What a contract strategy decides

The Associate and Professional tiers took a contract as given and managed it. The Expert tier starts one step earlier, at the contract strategy: the decisions that fix what kind of contract a contract manager will later run. A strategy that ignores how the contract will be managed hands the contract manager problems the contract cannot solve.

## Management is part of the strategy

The Sourcing Playbook (June 2023), a UK Cabinet Office text taught here as published good practice, puts it plainly:

> "How a contract will be managed is a key strategic decision that needs adequate consideration early in the procurement process and shall be reflected in the contractual agreement."

The UK Contract Management Principles add how much resource to give: "Adopt a differentiated approach based on risk; distinguish between tactical and strategic contracts, direct the most/strongest resource to contracts where the risks and rewards are highest." (principle 4).

## The decisions a strategy records

The World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers (Seventh Edition, September 2025), taught by concept as another body of practice, tie the choice of contract type to what is bought, how risky and complex it is, and value for money (Section V, para 5.27). They list the contracting strategy as a part of the procurement strategy: how the work is packaged and which contract types are used (Annex V, para 3.19(e)).

Read together, a strategy for an Ekene contract records at least:

- what is bought, and why a contract is the way to get it;
- how the work is packaged;
- the contract type, the price basis and who carries which risk;
- how the contract will be managed: by whom, how often, against which measures;
- the duties of the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (the content Act, Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010), which binds every Ekene contract.

Tender evaluation, the award decision and the cost of each contract type under uncertainty belong to the procurement course, Procurement, Tendering & Contracting.

## Exercise

EKC-06 is the synthetic instrument maintenance service with supplier IM-F (synthetic), reimbursed at hourly rates plus materials at cost, with KPIs for planned work completed and repeat failures. The scenario adds its own facts: the contract ends next year, and the contract owner asks for a one-page note listing the decisions the renewal strategy must record. Write the headings, with one line under each saying what the contract manager needs settled.

### A reading

A sound note names the need (instrument reliability); the packaging (instrument work alone or joined to other maintenance); the price basis, since reimbursable hours leave the cost risk with Ekene; the management design, with resource set by risk as principle 4 advises and the KPIs kept or changed; and the content Act duties the contract must carry. The World Bank Regulations support tying each choice to what is bought, its risk and value for money.

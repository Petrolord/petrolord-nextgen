# Exit planning and handover to a successor

Every contract ends, by expiry or by termination, and on most Ekene contracts the work goes on with a new supplier or in-house. Exit planning makes that handover safe.

## Plan the exit in time

The UK Contract Management Framework Summary, published by the Crown Commercial Service as good practice, sets the standard: "Exit planning and a strategy for when the contract ends is put in place in a timely manner and includes input from the contract manager." (Area 11, item 15).

The Sourcing Playbook (June 2023), a UK text, adds a point about the last months: "There should be sufficient means within the contract to incentivise the incumbent supplier to maintain both resources and performance up to the end of the contract." (Chapter 13, key point 2, p.73).

## Write the ending into the contract

The UK Contract Terminations guidance under the Procurement Act 2023 (April 2026 edition), used as published good practice, says: "Contracting authorities should always set out in the contract what happens when the contract is terminated, including under the implied term in section 78." (para 18). It names practical terms to include: the notice period, the transfer of assets and data, help with re-procurement, payment of sums owed, and any breakage costs for early termination (para 18).

## Close the old contract properly

GovS 008 (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026) says at 5.4.7: "On contract closure, information systems should be updated, staff and facilities (if any) reassigned and the contract documentation archived in accordance with the organisation’s information retention policy and procedures."

## Nigerian content through the handover

The Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (the content Act, Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010) applies across a handover: s.28(1) provides that "Nigerians shall be given the first consideration for employment and training in any project executed by any operator or project promoter in the Nigerian oil and gas industry." A successor contract carries that duty from its first day.

## Exercise

EKC-03 is the synthetic camp catering and facility services contract with supplier CF-C (synthetic), for a fixed monthly fee with service levels and service credits. The scenario adds its own facts: the contract expires in six months and will not be renewed; a new caterer will start the day after. CF-C holds the kitchen equipment inventory, the maintenance request log and the staff rota. The contract says nothing about exit. Write the headings of the exit plan and one clause you will ask for in the new contract.

### A reading

Headings: the timetable to expiry; data to transfer (maintenance log, open requests, audit results, equipment inventory); assets and who owns them; performance to the last day, kept under the existing service levels and service credits; payment of sums owed and final service credits; staff arrangements under the content Act s.28(1); and closure under GovS 008 5.4.7. The clause for the new contract: an exit schedule covering notice, asset and data transfer and help to a successor, as the UK Contract Terminations guidance recommends.

# Grounds for termination

Termination ends a contract before its natural end. It is the most serious remedy a contract manager can recommend, and every source in this course treats it as the last one.

## Last resort

GovS 008 (version 2.2, issued 1 April 2026), the UK commercial functional standard used as published good practice, says at 5.4.7:

> "Early termination of a contract should be a last resort only enacted after other provisions for the delivery of the contract, including contractual remedies for improving performance, have been exhausted."

The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), taught by concept as published practice, calls termination the ultimate remedy for a default (p.38).

## Where the grounds come from

The grounds are written in the contract: the events on which a party may terminate, the notice to give and what follows. For UK public contracts the law adds one: the Procurement Act 2023 (UK, as enacted), s.78(1), provides: "It is an implied term of every public contract that the contract can, if a termination ground applies, be terminated by the contracting authority in accordance with this section." Before relying on the implied term, the authority tells the supplier it intends to terminate, which ground applies and why, and gives it a reasonable opportunity to respond (UK Contract Terminations guidance, para 15). That UK rule binds no Ekene contract; it is a published model of fair process.

For Ekene, Nigerian law sits behind the contract. The Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 (the content Act, Act No. 2 of 2010, commenced 22 April 2010), s.68, makes carrying out a project contrary to the content Act an offence, with "a fine of five per cent of the project sum for each project in which the offence is committed or cancellation of the project."

## Exercise

EKC-07 is the synthetic environmental monitoring consultancy with supplier EM-G (synthetic), paid a time-based fee against approved timesheets, with a named key person and a report acceptance step. The scenario adds its own facts: the named key person left two months ago, the last two reports were late, and the asset manager wants the contract terminated this week. Write the checklist you will complete before recommending termination.

### A reading

The checklist: find the termination clause and the ground it gives; check that the key person's departure and late reports fall within it; confirm that earlier concerns were raised in writing and that contractual remedies such as a request to replace the key person were used, since GovS 008 treats termination as a last resort; confirm that Ekene met its own obligations, including approving timesheets and paying on time; and check the notice the contract requires. The two World Bank case studies show why the written record and Ekene's own performance come first.

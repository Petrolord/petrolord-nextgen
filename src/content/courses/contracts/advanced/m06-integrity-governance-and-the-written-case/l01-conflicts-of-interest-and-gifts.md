# Conflicts of interest and gifts

A contract manager makes decisions worth money to the supplier every week: certifying work, assessing claims, rating performance. Integrity means declaring any interest that could sway them and refusing anything offered to influence them.

## What a conflict is

The UK Conflicts of Interest guidance under the Procurement Act 2023 (2024), used as published good practice, defines it: "A conflict of interest arises in a procurement context where there is a conflict between the interests of a person acting in relation to a procurement and those of the procurement itself." (para 2).

The Public Procurement Act 2007 (Act No. 14, Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007) binds federal procuring entities; for an operator it is a published statement of public procurement practice. Its s.57(12) lists what counts as a conflict, including a direct or indirect interest in or relationship with a supplier or contractor that could bring personal gain, taking personal advantage of an opportunity that belongs to the entity, and passing confidential information to a supplier or contractor.

## Declare at once

Section 57(10) is direct: "Any person engaged in the public procurement and disposal of assets who has assumed or is about to assume, a financial or other business outside business relationship that might involve a conflict of interest, must immediately declare to the authorities any actual or potential interest."

## Conflicts do not end at award

The UK guidance says the conflicts assessment should be refreshed when people or the contract change, for example when responsibility passes from the procurement team to a contract management team (para 24), and that the duty to keep it under review runs until the contract termination notice is published (para 22). On an Ekene contract, a new contract manager declares on arrival.

## Gifts and hospitality

The World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers (Seventh Edition, September 2025), taught by concept, define a corrupt practice as offering, giving, receiving or soliciting anything of value, directly or indirectly, to improperly influence another party's actions (Annex IV, para 2.2 a.i). Ekene's own policy sets any limits; this course states none.

## Exercise

EKC-02 is the synthetic platform supply vessel time charter with supplier MV-B (synthetic), paid a day rate with off-hire for breakdown. The scenario adds its own facts: MV-B's commercial manager invites the Ekene contract manager on an all-expenses trip to the vessel's owners' shipyard abroad, in the same month the contract manager must decide a disputed off-hire period. The contract manager's cousin also works in MV-B's crewing office. Write the contract manager's response and declaration.

### A reading

Decline the trip: it offers something of value from a supplier while an off-hire decision is pending, which falls within the World Bank definition of a corrupt practice if meant to influence it. Declare the cousin's role in writing at once, as the Public Procurement Act 2007 s.57(10) models; it is a relationship with a supplier under the kinds listed in s.57(12). Record both, ask the contract owner whether someone else should decide the off-hire period, and refresh the conflicts record.

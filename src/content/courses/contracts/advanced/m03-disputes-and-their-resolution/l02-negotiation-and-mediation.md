# Negotiation and mediation

Negotiation is the parties settling a matter between themselves. Mediation brings in a neutral mediator who helps them reach their own settlement without deciding it for them. For mediation seated in Nigeria, the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 (Nigeria), which repeals the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, sets the rules this lesson reads by section.

## Negotiating from the record

Negotiation on an Ekene contract starts from the contract terms, the record and a written assessment of the claim, and from knowing who holds authority to settle. The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), taught by concept, notes that a contracting decision is valid only when the person taking it holds the authority to take it (p.11).

## Starting a mediation

Where a contract requires mediation before court or other proceedings, the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023, s.70(1) and (2), has the party concerned propose a mediation agreement in writing. If no acceptance arrives within 30 days of sending the invitation, or any other time stated in the invitation, the inviting party may treat it as rejected.

## Time stops running

Section 71(1) protects the time a party has to bring its claim:

> "When the mediation proceedings commence, the running of the limitation period regarding the claim that is the subject matter of the mediation is suspended."

## What is said stays confidential

Under s.76, unless the parties agree otherwise, everything about the mediation stays confidential. The exceptions are disclosure required by law, disclosure needed to implement or enforce the settlement, and disclosure needed to prevent or reveal a crime or a threat to a party. The contract manager keeps mediation papers apart from the ordinary contract file.

## The settlement binds

Section 82(2) states that "the settlement agreement resulting from the mediation is binding on the parties and enforceable in Court as a contract, consent judgment or consent award." It is written with the care of a contract change.

## Exercise

EKC-07 is the synthetic environmental monitoring consultancy with supplier EM-G (synthetic), paid a time-based fee against approved timesheets, with a named key person and a report acceptance step. The scenario adds its own facts: Ekene has rejected two quarterly reports as incomplete and withheld the related fees; EM-G says the reports meet the terms of reference. Senior meetings have failed, and the contract requires mediation before arbitration. Write the headings of the mediation invitation and a note of what you will do with the outcome.

### A reading

The invitation, in writing as s.70 of the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 requires, names the matter (the two rejected reports and withheld fees), proposes a mediator or a way of choosing one, and states a time for acceptance; without a stated time the 30-day rule applies. The note records that the limitation period is suspended once mediation starts (s.71(1)), that the papers stay confidential (s.76), and that any settlement binds and is enforceable (s.82(2)), so it goes into the contract record and the change register if it changes fees or scope.

# Arbitration and the courts

Arbitration is the top rung on most Ekene contracts: a tribunal chosen under the contract hears the dispute and makes an award. For arbitration seated in Nigeria, the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 (Nigeria), which repeals the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, sets the rules this lesson reads by section. Lawyers run an arbitration; the contract manager knows the rules, keeps the record and watches the time limits.

## What arbitration is for

Section 1(1) states the objective of the arbitration Part:

> "The objective of this Part is to promote fair resolution of disputes by an impartial tribunal without unnecessary delay or expense."

The World Bank Contract Management Practice guidance (Second Edition, 2024), taught by concept as published practice, treats arbitration and litigation as the last resort (p.35). In Nigerian public contracts, the Public Procurement Act 2007 (Act No. 14, Official Gazette No. 65, Vol. 94, 19 June 2007), which binds federal procuring entities and serves an operator as published practice, requires at s.16(26) that procurement contracts contain provisions for arbitral proceedings as the primary forms of dispute resolution.

## When a party goes to court

If a party sues on a matter covered by an arbitration agreement, s.5(1) has the court refer the parties to arbitration when a party asks no later than its first statement on the substance, unless the court finds the agreement void, inoperative or incapable of being performed.

## The tribunal

Where the contract is silent on the number of arbitrators, s.6(2) settles it: "Where there is no agreement as to the number of arbitrators, the arbitral tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator."

## After the award

Section 55(4) provides: "An application for setting aside shall not be made after three months have elapsed from the date on which the party making that application had received the award". Under s.57(1) and (3), an award is recognised as binding whatever country it was made in, and on written application the Court enforces it; with the Court's leave it may be enforced like a judgment or order.

## What the contract manager supplies

A tribunal decides on the record: the notice register, claim assessments, change register, meeting notes and correspondence built in earlier modules.

## Exercise

EKC-04 is the synthetic casing and tubulars frame agreement with supplier TB-D (synthetic), with unit prices per item and delivery lead times per order. The scenario adds its own facts: the agreement contains an arbitration clause seated in Nigeria and says nothing on the number of arbitrators. A dispute over late deliveries has reached the top of the ladder, and TB-D has filed a suit in court. Ekene's lawyers ask the contract manager for a short note of the points that bear on the next steps.

### A reading

The frame agreement's arbitration clause covers the matter, so Ekene can ask the court to refer the parties to arbitration under s.5(1) of the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023, and the request must come no later than Ekene's first statement on the substance. With no number agreed, s.6(2) gives a sole arbitrator. Once an award is received, any application to set it aside must be made within three months (s.55(4)). The contract manager also lists the records available: delivery notes against order lead times, correspondence and meeting notes.

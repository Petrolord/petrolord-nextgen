# The capstone brief

{{panel:pia-hct-calculator}}

The Professional capstone grades six figures, and every one answers this tier's question: how does the hydrocarbon tax work as a system? Each is a return value of the engine on fixed terms and rows, so each has exactly one right answer.

| graded quantity | the module it rests on |
| --- | --- |
| the production royalty on crude oil and condensate in one stated year, at your share | the small-field tranches |
| the production allowance in one stated year, at your share | allowances |
| the hydrocarbon tax chargeable profit in one stated year, at your share | deductions and the cost price ratio, and allowances |
| the cost the cost price ratio carries out of one stated year, at your share | deductions and the cost price ratio |
| the cost forfeited when the ledger ends, at your share | deductions and the cost price ratio |
| companies income tax over the life of the ledger, at your share | companies income tax alongside |

## What it grades and why

It grades the base of the hydrocarbon tax and the tax beside it. It grades no hydrocarbon tax line itself. Where the capstone states a reading on an open question, it says so on the brief, and none of the six values depends on it. It grades nothing the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 moved at the edges and no decomposition of an outcome by provision: those are the Expert question.

## The data are new

The capstone runs its own synthetic lease, with its own name, terms and rows. None of its values appears in any lesson, and none of the figures in this tier's lessons is a capstone answer. The Ekene cases are worked examples: use them to check that you drive the calculator correctly. The capstone card offers the case as a file in the shape the ledger views accept (cfg, prodRows, capexRows, opexRows). Paste it into the hydrocarbon tax calculator.

## How to work it

Read the stated terms before any figure: terrain, licence, lease status, the working interest, the prices, the preceding year's opex for the first year's HCDT, and the barrels produced before the ledger, which place the field against its allowance cap. Every money value is at the stated share; the tranches, the cap and the allowances are read at field level first. Scaling to the share is the cash flow course's arithmetic and the engine does it for you.

Name the part before you read the royalty: the graded royalty is the production royalty on crude oil and condensate alone, the engine's `liquids_production_royalty`, with the gas royalty and the royalty by price left out. For the carry, read the "CPR carried out" column in the stated year, and for the forfeiture the "Forfeited at cessation" tile. For companies income tax over the life, take every year of the ledger.

Keep every figure at full precision until the end and report six decimals. A note in `kpis.pia_notes` is part of a result. A refusal names the input it could not use, and the fix is in the terms.

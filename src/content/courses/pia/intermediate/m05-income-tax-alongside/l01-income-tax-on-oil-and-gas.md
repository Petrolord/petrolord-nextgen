# Companies income tax on oil and gas

{{panel:pia-hct-calculator}}

A company producing crude oil under the Act pays two profit taxes on the same field. The hydrocarbon tax is one. Companies income tax is the other, and it is built on a different base, charged on a wider scope and blind to the first. This lesson sets the two side by side.

## Two taxes, one field

The Act says in s.302(5): "(5) In determining the companies income tax, the hydrocarbon tax under this Act shall not be deductible." The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 says the same at s.78(3)(a): "(3) In determining the income tax under Chapter Two of this Act: (a) hydrocarbon tax is not deductible; and".

So the two taxes run in parallel. Neither reads the other's liability. Companies income tax is charged on oil and gas together, where the hydrocarbon tax charges crude oil and condensate only. The rate is 30 percent unless stated; the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 s.56(b) charges "any other company, at the rate of 30 per cent from the commence- ment of this Act."

## A gas field pays only one of them

The Ekene gas field (synthetic, shallow water, no crude oil) pays no hydrocarbon tax and a full companies income tax:

## The hydrocarbon tax rate does not reach it

On the Ekene new onshore lease (synthetic), the hydrocarbon tax rate is a stated reading, 15 or 30. Changing it doubles the hydrocarbon tax and leaves companies income tax exactly where it was:

| year | HCT (stated 15) | HCT (stated 30) | CIT, both readings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | 22711732.336957 | 45423464.673913 | 50223464.673913 |
| 2027 | 18591904.385322 | 37183808.770645 | 40183808.770645 |

That is s.302(5) on numbers. If companies income tax deducted the hydrocarbon tax, a higher stated rate would have lowered it.

## Why the parallel structure matters

Because neither tax deducts the other, the two burdens add. A field's combined profit tax is the hydrocarbon tax plus companies income tax, each computed on its own base. The parallel structure also makes companies income tax a figure the open reading on a new lease never reaches, which is why the course can grade it on such a lease. The next lesson reads what the companies income tax base deducts; the lesson after reads a limit on its capital allowance in years under the Act alone.

How the two combine with royalty into a share of the field's value for government is the Associate tier's stack, and reading that total by provision is the Expert tier's question.

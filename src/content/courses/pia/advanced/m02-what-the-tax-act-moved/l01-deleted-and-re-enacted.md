# Deleted and re-enacted

{{panel:pia-ledger-calculator}}

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (Act No. 7; Official Gazette No. 117, Vol. 112, 26 June 2025; effective 1 January 2026 by the State House statement; re-gazetting ordered in December 2025 and no Certified True Copy read; read on 2026-09-26) reaches into the Petroleum Industry Act 2021 and removes large parts of it. It then writes most of what it removed back into its own sections.

## What s.197 deletes

NTA s.197(1) lists the deletions from the Petroleum Industry Act: "(a) Part I - X of Chapter Four; (b) the Fifth and Sixth Schedules; (c) paragraphs 6, 9, 10, 11 and 12, of the Seventh Schedule; and (d) paragraph 14 (6) of Part IV of the Seventh Schedule."

Beside the deletions sits a list of repeals. S.196 opens: "196. From the commencement of this Act, the following enactments are repealed (repealed enactments): " and its list includes "(c) Companies Income Tax Act, Cap. C21, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria, 2004; (d) Deep offshore and Inland Basin Act, Cap. D3, Laws of the Federation".

## Where each piece reappears

Most of what was deleted comes back with a new address. The provisions this course cites pair up like this:

| provision | Petroleum Industry Act 2021 | Nigeria Tax Act 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| what the hydrocarbon tax charges | s.260 | s.65 |
| deductions in the hydrocarbon tax base | s.263(1) | s.68(1) |
| loss relief by class | s.265 | s.70 |
| the 30 and 15 percent rates | s.267 | s.72 |
| additional tax at the fiscal price | s.268 | s.73 |
| consolidation across terrains | s.272 | s.76 |
| hydrocarbon tax not deductible for income tax | s.302(5) | s.78(3)(a) |
| stabilisation | s.305 | s.88 |
| leases that do not convert | s.303(1) | s.87(1) |
| capital allowance | Fifth Schedule para 17(1) | First Schedule Part II para 14(1) |
| production allowance and cost price ratio | Sixth Schedule paras 1 and 2 | Sixth Schedule paras 1 and 2 |
| royalty by terrain and by price | Seventh Schedule paras 10 and 11 | Seventh Schedule para 6(2) and (3) |

The royalty move is the clearest. NTA Seventh Schedule para 6(2)(b) restates the terrain rates: "(i) onshore areas - 15%, (ii) shallow water (up to 200m water depth): 12.5%, (iii) deep offshore (beyond 200m water depth): 7.5%, and (iv) frontier basins: 7.5%;". The administration moved as well: NTA Seventh Schedule para 1(1) reads "1. (1) The Service shall administer royalties payable in accordance with the provision of the Nigeria Tax Administration Act, 2025."

## Re-enacted is not always unchanged

Most of the pairs carry the same words. A few carry different ones, and those differences are what the rest of this module and the next one read: the education tax gives way to a development levy, the fifth year of the capital allowance changes, the two thirds restriction goes, the deep offshore wording changes, the deep offshore new-lease allowance is left out and a decommissioning fund meets a new condition.

## How the engine carries the move

The engine reads each year of a ledger under the law of that year. A provision re-enacted word for word gives the same figure under both frameworks, and the engine's royalty and royalty by price functions take no framework input at all. A provision that changed gives a different figure, and it shows up only in the lines it touches.

So run one ledger twice, as it stands and with every year forced to the Act alone, and see which provision totals move.

# The capstone brief

{{panel:pia-ledger-calculator}}

The Expert capstone grades the question this tier asked: reading a ledger across the move of 2026, provision by provision. It is worked in the course's own ledger calculator, which calls the same engine every lesson has quoted. There is no Suite app for this course: the calculator is the practical.

## What you receive

The capstone card carries one case file with the terms and the production, capex and opex rows of a synthetic Ekene lease of its own. The card states every setting the engine needs, including the framework override, the working interest, the prices, any open reading the terrain calls for and whether the escrow condition of NTA s.86 is met. Every money value you report is at the stated working interest share.

## What you report

Six quantities from the ledger, each to six decimals. They are the kinds of line this tier has read by provision: a royalty total in a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, a hydrocarbon tax base line, the education tax in a year under the Act alone, the development levy in a year under the new Act, and companies income tax in one year and over the life of the ledger. The card names each one exactly. None of the six depends on an open reading: each was proved identical under every reading of all three open questions.

## How to work it

1. Paste the case file into the ledger calculator's view "The whole ledger, year by year" and read any refusal first; the case as issued runs.
2. Read the framework tiles and mark which years are under each Act.
3. Read the totals by year from the ledger table. For the hydrocarbon tax base, use the hydrocarbon tax calculator in the lesson on the capital allowance and the restriction by year: its view "The tax base and the cost price ratio on a ledger" takes the same case file and prints the chargeable profit.
4. Read every note under the ledger and say which provision each belongs to.
5. Check the readings. Open "One case under every stated reading" with the case pasted in and read total companies income tax on every row that runs; on the issued case it stays put.

## How it is graded

Each of the six fields is compared with the engine's own return on the issued case, within a tolerance set once for the whole course. Report the figure the calculator prints, at six decimals, with no rounding of your own.

Before you start, list for each of the six quantities the provision that puts it on the ledger.

## Exercise

Rehearse the method on ekene_onshore_across_2026 before opening the card. In "The whole ledger, year by year", read the tertiary education tax in 2025, the development levy in 2027 and companies income tax in 2028, and name the provision behind each. Then open "One case under every stated reading" with the same case and read total companies income tax on each row. Explain which row moves it, and why this rehearsal case, unlike the capstone, is not proof against every reading.

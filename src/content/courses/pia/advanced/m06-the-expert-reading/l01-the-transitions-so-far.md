# The transitions so far

{{panel:pia-ledger-calculator}}

The Expert tier asked one question: how does a ledger change when the texts under it change, and how is a fiscal outcome read by provision? Five modules answered it in parts.

## The door into the Act

A lease enters the Petroleum Industry Act 2021 by conversion, which is voluntary and has a deadline under s.92(4). Conversion sorts an oil mining lease into mining leases on the 30 percent class, prospecting licences on the 15 percent class and relinquished acreage (s.93). A producing marginal field converts under s.94(1) into the 15 percent class, and no new marginal field can be declared. A lease that never converts keeps its old terms and stays outside the engine. Production sharing contracts under the Act are concept-only.

## The move of 2026

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (Official Gazette No. 117, Vol. 112, 26 June 2025; re-gazetting ordered in December 2025 and no Certified True Copy read) deletes large parts of the Act and re-enacts most of them. The engine reads each year of assessment under its own law: before 2026 a year under the Act alone, from 2026 a year under the new Act. At the switch the education tax gives way to the development levy, the fifth-year capital allowance becomes 20 percent, and the two thirds restriction stops.

## The edges

Four edges changed. Deep offshore is brought inside the hydrocarbon tax with no rate printed for it, which leaves an open question the course shows under three readings. The new-lease allowance for deep offshore and frontier is left out of the re-enacted schedule. A decommissioning fund is deductible only if the escrow condition is met. A minimum effective tax rate tests companies, and the engine offers only a labelled approximation of it. Royalty, the royalty by price, the rates onshore and in shallow water and the cost price ratio carry across unchanged.

## Gas and incentives

Gas pays royalty at 5 percent, or 2.5 percent in-country, and sits outside the hydrocarbon tax. The associated gas line, the greenfield credit and the pipeline reliefs are concept-only. The one gas relief the engine carries is the exemption from the restriction.

## Reading an outcome

Government cash flow is the sum of royalty, the taxes, the levy, HCDT and NDDC, and the take divides it by the pre-take value. One change at a time shows which provision moved.

## What the capstone asks of this

The Expert capstone runs one ledger that crosses the switch and asks for six quantities, each read by provision. None of them depends on an open reading, and each rests on the parts above.

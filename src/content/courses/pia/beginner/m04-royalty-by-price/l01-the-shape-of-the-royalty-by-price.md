# The shape of the royalty by price

{{panel:pia-royalty-calculator}}

The production royalty reads volume. The Act adds a second royalty that reads price. It charges nothing at a low price, rises as the price climbs and stops rising at a high price, so the state's royalty share grows in the years when a barrel is worth most. This course calls it the royalty by price and always states its base year beside a figure.

## The rule in the text

The Seventh Schedule para 11(1) charges it on top of the production royalty:

> "a royalty by price with respect to crude oil and condensates at the rates set out below: (a) below US $50 per barrel: 0%, (b) at US $100 per barrel: 5%, (c) above US $150 per barrel: 10%,"

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 restates the rule in its own Seventh Schedule, para 6(3)(a), and names the method between the points:

> "on linear interpolation, as an example, if in 2020 the price is US $75 per barrel, the royalty by price shall be 2.5%,"

## Three points and two straight lines

The engine reads the rule as three benchmarks. At or below the low benchmark the rate is 0. At the middle benchmark it is 5 percent. At or above the high benchmark it is 10 percent. Between the points the rate moves in a straight line. The benchmarks start at 50, 100 and 150 USD/bbl and rise every year, which the next lesson reads.

The engine's rates in 2025, on the Regulations base, which is the engine default:

| price USD/bbl (stated) | royalty by price rate, Regulations base, 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| 50.000000 | 0.000000 |
| 54.120000 | 0.000000 |
| 75.000000 | 0.019290 |
| 95.000000 | 0.037768 |
| 108.240000 | 0.050000 |
| 120.000000 | 0.060865 |
| 162.360000 | 0.100000 |
| 170.000000 | 0.100000 |

In 2025 the Regulations' benchmarks are 54.120000, 108.240000 and 162.360000, so the rate is exactly 0 at 54.120000, exactly 5 percent at 108.240000 and exactly 10 percent from 162.360000 up.

## Each stream at its own price

The rate applies to crude oil and to condensate, each valued and rated at its own price. On the case with crude at 95 and condensate at 88 USD/bbl in 2025, on the Regulations base, crude pays 0.037768 and condensate 0.031301, and the royalty by price comes to 20694235.033259 USD. Gas pays no royalty by price.

## Where it sits on a ledger

The royalty by price comes off revenue with the production royalty, and together they make the total royalty. It never replaces the production royalty; a field pays both. On Ekene Alpha in 2026, on the Regulations base, the royalty by price rate on oil is 0.017935 and the royalty by price is 4037323.188406 USD.

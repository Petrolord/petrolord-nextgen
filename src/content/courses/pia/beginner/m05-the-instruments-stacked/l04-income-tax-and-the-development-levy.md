# Income tax and the development levy

{{panel:pia-royalty-calculator}}

Two taxes on profit sit beside the hydrocarbon tax. Companies income tax reads the whole oil and gas profit, and an education charge reads the same base. Which education charge applies depends on the year: the tertiary education tax in a year under the Act alone, the development levy in a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. This lesson reads both from the texts.

## Companies income tax

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 sets the rate, NTA s.56(a),(b):

> "(a) a small company, at 0%; and (b) any other company, at the rate of 30 per cent from the commence- ment of this Act."

The engine charges 30 percent unless a rate is stated. The base is oil and gas together: gross revenue less every royalty, opex in full, HCDT, the NDDC levy and any deductible decommissioning contribution, less its own capital allowance. The hydrocarbon tax is never deducted, PIA s.302(5), restated in NTA s.78(3)(a):

> "(3) In determining the income tax under Chapter Two of this Act: (a) hydrocarbon tax is not deductible; and"

On the worked example in 2025 companies income tax is 299472494.456763 USD. That figure, like every line after the royalty, reads the royalty by price on the Regulations base, the engine default.

## The tertiary education tax

In a year under the Act alone the education charge is the tertiary education tax, assessed and collected by the Service (PIA s.259(a)(ii)). The Finance Act 2023 set it at 3 percent with effect from 1 May 2023 (s.26 and s.30); the engine applies 3 percent to the whole of 2023, and 2.5 percent before it, a rate that rests on a secondary source. On the worked example, a year under the Act alone, the rate is 3.000000 percent and the tax is 31747249.445676 USD.

## The development levy

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 removed the tertiary education tax: NTA s.197(5) deletes the charging sections of the Tertiary Education Trust Fund Act, so a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 carries no tertiary education tax at all.

In its place it charges a development levy, NTA s.59(1):

> "59. (1) A development levy of 4% is imposed on the assessable profits of"

The engine charges 4 percent of the companies income tax assessable profit in a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Small companies and non-resident companies are outside it, and it is never charged on hydrocarbon tax profits (NTA s.59(4)).

| year type | education charge | rate | citation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a year under the Act alone, from 2023 | tertiary education tax | 3 percent | Finance Act 2023 s.26 |
| a year under the Act alone, before 2023 | tertiary education tax | 2.5 percent | Finance Act 2021 (secondary) |
| a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 | development levy | 4 percent | NTA s.59(1) |

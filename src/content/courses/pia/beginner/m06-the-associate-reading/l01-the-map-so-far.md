# The map so far

{{panel:pia-royalty-calculator}}

This tier set out to draw a map of the Act: who decides, which licence and terrain a lease sits in, what royalty it pays by volume and by price, and how the instruments stack on one year. This lesson lays the map out on one page, so that the capstone can be read against it.

## The map in one table

| question | the provision that answers it | what the engine returns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| who assesses the taxes | PIA s.259(a) | nothing; concept-only |
| who collects royalty | PIA s.259(b)(i); NTA s.89(2) | nothing; concept-only |
| where the money lands | PIA s.258(2); Seventh Schedule para 11(3); NTA s.59(3) | nothing; concept-only |
| which licence | PIA s.70(1) | PML or PPL, or a refusal |
| which terrain | Seventh Schedule para 10(2) | one of four strings, or a refusal |
| royalty on liquids | Seventh Schedule para 10(3), (4); Regulations r.13 | a weighted rate, 0.059976 on Alpha in 2026 |
| royalty on gas | Seventh Schedule para 10(6) | 0.050000, or 0.037500 with half used in-country |
| royalty by price | Seventh Schedule para 11(1), (2) | a rate between the benchmarks, with its base year |
| the stack | PIA ss.240, 263, 267, 302; NTA s.59 | one row a year and the totals |
| the take | the shared fiscal conventions | 66.564877 percent on Alpha |

The take and every royalty figure above that depends on the royalty by price read the Regulations base, the engine default.

## The one open reading at this tier

The royalty by price base year is open: the Act starts the benchmarks in 2020 and the Regulations in 2021. This tier taught both, showed where they agree and where they differ, and grades neither against the other. Any graded figure that depends on the royalty by price names the Regulations base. The other two open readings, the tax rate of a new lease onshore or in shallow water and the deep offshore tax under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, belong to later tiers.

## What stayed concept-only

Several provisions on the map are never computed and never graded on a number. The fiscal oil price the Commission sets stands behind every royalty, and the engine uses the realised price in its place and says so in a note. A field lying in two terrains, conversion itself and the terms of a lease that does not convert are taught from their text. The institutions and the destinations of the money are taught the same way. Each of these can appear in a question as a text to read, never as a number to compute.

## What the next tier takes up

The hydrocarbon tax appeared at this tier as one line on the stack, with its citation and its base named. The Professional tier opens it up: what the tax charges, the two classes and their rates, the deductions and the cost price ratio, the allowances, and companies income tax beside it. The Expert tier then takes up the transitions and the reading of a whole outcome by provision.

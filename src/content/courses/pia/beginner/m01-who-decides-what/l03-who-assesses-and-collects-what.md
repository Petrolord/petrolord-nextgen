# Who assesses and collects what

{{panel:pia-royalty-calculator}}

The previous lesson named three bodies. This one gives each line of a ledger its owner, with the words of the text behind the assignment. The aim is a table a learner can carry into every later module: for every instrument, who assesses it, who collects it, and which provision says so.

## The Service: the taxes

Section 259 of the Act gives the Service the hydrocarbon tax in s.259(a)(i), and then adds, in s.259(a)(ii):

> "(ii) companies income tax and tertiary education tax in accordance with this Act as it relates to taxable petroleum operations"

So all three taxes on the profit of a lease sit with the Service under the Act. The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 adds a fourth line with the same collector. Its development levy is collected by the Service into a special account, in NTA s.59(2):

> "other than small companies and non-resident companies. (2) The Service shall collect the levy and pay it into a special account created for that purpose."

## The Commission: royalties and the rest

The Commission determines and collects royalties, signature bonus and rents under PIA s.259(b)(i). Royalty in this course means the production royalty on liquids and gas plus the royalty by price; when only one part is meant, the course names it. Under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 the Service administers royalty (NTA s.89(2) and its Seventh Schedule para 1(1)), which the course reads as a change of administration with the rates left as they were.

## The contributions

Two lines on the ledger are contributions to a fund. The host communities development trust contribution is paid by each settlor into the trust fund set up for its host communities (PIA s.240(2)). The NDDC levy is charged under the Niger-Delta Development Commission Act 2000, which this course has read only through secondary commentary, and it says so wherever the levy appears. Neither is a tax assessed by the Service.

| hydrocarbon tax | the Service | PIA s.259(a)(i) |

| tertiary education tax | the Service, in years under the Act alone | PIA s.259(a)(ii) |

## Two years on one ledger

The tertiary education tax and the development levy never sit on the same year. A year under the Act alone carries the tertiary education tax; a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 carries the development levy. The same collector takes both, and the Expert tier takes up how the engine reads the year.

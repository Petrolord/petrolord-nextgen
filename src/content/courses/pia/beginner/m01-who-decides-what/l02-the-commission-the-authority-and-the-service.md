# The Commission, the Authority and the Service

{{panel:pia-royalty-calculator}}

Before any rate is read, a learner needs to know who holds the pen. The Act names three bodies, and reading the fiscal terms well starts with knowing which of them decides, assesses or collects a given line. None of this is computed by the engine. It is taught from the text and never graded on a number, but every figure later in the course lands on a desk belonging to one of these bodies.

## The three bodies the Act names

| short name | full name | what it does in this course |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the Commission | Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission | determines and collects royalties, rents and bonuses under the Act |
| the Authority | Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority | regulates midstream and downstream operations |
| the Service | Federal Inland Revenue Service | assesses and collects the hydrocarbon tax and companies income tax |

The Commission is the upstream regulator. The Act has it determine the fiscal oil price of each field, which this course treats as a concept, and determine and collect royalties. The Authority is the regulator for the midstream and downstream, and it appears in this course mainly where a fund may be approved by "the Commission or Authority". The Service is the tax authority.

## The words the Act uses

The Act puts the collection of government revenue in two pairs of hands. Section 259 opens:

> "collection of Government revenue in the petroleum industry shall be the function of the Federal Inland Revenue Service (the Service) and the Commission, and: (a) the Service shall be responsible for the assessment and collection of: (i) hydrocarbon tax"

The same section gives the Commission its side, in PIA s.259(b)(i):

> "(b) the Commission shall be responsible for the determination and collection of: (i) royalties, signature bonus, rents, and related payments and its enforcement under this Act, and"

## What the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 moved

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 moved the administration of royalty. Under it the Service is the relevant tax authority for royalty (NTA s.89(2)), and the Seventh Schedule of that Act says:

> "1. (1) The Service shall administer royalties payable in accordance with the provision of the Nigeria Tax Administration Act, 2025."

So a royalty figure in a year under the Act alone and the same figure in a year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 can be computed by the same rule and still be administered by a different body. The rate does not change; the administration does. The Expert tier takes up the rest of what the 2025 Act moved.

## Why the institutions matter to a figure

A learner who can name the body behind a line can find the text behind it. A royalty question leads to the Commission, the Seventh Schedule and the Royalty Regulations. A tax question leads to the Service and the fiscal provisions. The engine keeps the lines apart for the same reason: every line it returns carries its own citation, and the citation names the body that owns it.

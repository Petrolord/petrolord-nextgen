# The story so far

Five modules, and one idea underneath all of them.

## The idea

A fracture is a pressure problem with a fixed height, and every closed form in this tier was bought by fixing something, at a price that was stated at the time.

That is the whole tier in one sentence. Read the modules again against it and they stop being five topics and become one argument.

## The argument, module by module

Module one fixed the reference. Net pressure is the excess over closure, so closure has to come from a stress profile before any of this means anything. Then it fixed the stiffness, because confinement makes the rock respond through the plane strain modulus rather than through Young's modulus. On the published rock, 25000000000 Pa and a Poisson ratio of 0.28 give a plane strain modulus of 27126736111.11111 Pa. The price was that the rock is now one elastic number.

Module two fixed the geometry. PKN takes the height as its compliance length, KGD takes the half-length, and that single choice is worth a factor of 2.6055319672793997 in average width at the published conditions. It also reverses the trend. Across half-lengths from 40 m to 300 m, PKN net pressure rises from 2076588.0505800513 Pa to 3436494.6050675157 Pa while KGD net pressure falls from 1166434.0645127255 Pa to 425921.49931737053 Pa. The price was that the model is now a declaration about shape.

Module three fixed the shape of the opening. Pi over five and pi over four are averages of profiles nobody computed, and the quarter power makes width almost indifferent to rate and viscosity. Height came out as an input rather than a result. The price was that you can no longer buy width by pumping harder.

Module four fixed nothing and gained nothing in return, which is why it needed an iteration. Leakoff on the reciprocal square root of time makes the pumped volume depend on the pump time, and the pump time depends on the efficiency, so the balance is a fixed point. Nine iterations on the published case give an efficiency of 0.1728566723633056, with 209.09714590747427 m3 injected to store 36.143836842230584 m3.

Module five read the bill. No third dimension, no height growth, no tip physics, no transport.

## What to carry

Not the numbers. The habit of naming the fixed thing, then naming what it cost.

# The skin a wormhole gives

Negative skin here is not a correction. It is a bigger wellbore, written as a logarithm.

{{panel:st-acid-explorer}}

## The expression

The engine sets the skin to minus the natural logarithm of the wormhole radius divided by the wellbore radius. Nothing else is in it.

That is the definition of an effective wellbore radius, run in reverse. A well with negative skin behaves as though its radius were the true radius multiplied by the exponential of the skin magnitude. Here the wormholed cylinder IS that effective radius, so the skin is simply the logarithm of the enlargement.

For the published case, an effective radius of 0.39 m against a drilled 0.108 m gives a skin of -1.29.

## The diminishing return

| Volume, m3 | Wormhole radius, m | Skin |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.22 | -0.70 |
| 4 | 0.29 | -0.98 |
| 8 | 0.39 | -1.29 |
| 16 | 0.54 | -1.61 |
| 32 | 0.76 | -1.95 |

Each row doubles the acid. Read down the skin column and the step is very nearly the same size every time, settling towards a constant, which is half the natural logarithm of two. A fixed decrement of skin per doubling is a brutal bargain: the cost of the next unit of skin doubles each time you claim one.

Read the radius column the same way. Doubling the acid does not double the radius. The ratio between successive rows creeps up towards the square root of two, because the radius follows the square root of the volume.

Sixteen times the acid, from 2 m3 to 32 m3, moves the skin from -0.70 to -1.95. It does not even triple it.

## What that means for a design

There is no optimum inside this model. The skin improves for ever, slowly, so the model alone will always say pump more. The stopping point comes from outside it, where the flattening skin curve meets a cost curve that has not flattened at all.

That is why carbonate jobs cluster at modest volumes. The first few cubic metres do most of the work.

## The honesty note

This skin is pure geometry. It assumes the wormholed cylinder is perfectly conductive and that the channels connect to rock worth producing. If the wormholes run into a tight or non productive interval, the geometry is unchanged and the well is not improved.

# Why the two routes differ

Two jobs, two skins, and two different baselines they are measured against.

{{panel:st-acid-explorer}}

## The published pair, side by side

| | Sandstone | Carbonate |
|---|---|---|
| Acid pumped | 29.55 m3 | 8 m3 |
| Skin before | 8.48 | not modelled |
| Skin after | 1.62 | -1.29 |
| Best the model allows | 0 | no limit |

The sandstone job pumps nearly four times the acid and ends with a positive skin. The carbonate job pumps 8 m3 and ends below zero. Taken at face value that reads as a rout, and it is not.

## Why one has a floor and the other does not

The sandstone model RESTORES permeability inside the acid front. Restoration cannot make rock better than it originally was, so the best achievable skin is zero, reached at a front radius of 0.9 m for 67.72 m3.

The carbonate model REMOVES rock. That makes the flow area larger than the well was drilled with, so the skin goes negative, and nothing in the equation stops it going further. The two answers have different ceilings because they describe different physical acts.

## Reading the gain honestly

The right comparison is what each job recovers, not where each lands.

The sandstone job starts from a damage skin of 8.48, a very large productivity loss, and takes it to 1.62. That is most of a badly damaged well returned to service. The carbonate case reaches -1.29 from an undamaged baseline, a real gain but a smaller one than the sandstone job just delivered.

Whichever rock you are in, the useful question is the same: how much skin did this remove, and what did it cost.

## What the model will not do for you

A damaged carbonate ought to get both effects at once. The wormholes bypass the damaged shell entirely and then keep going, so the well should end up better than undamaged.

The engine does not combine them. It returns the Hawkins damage skin and the wormhole skin as separate results and leaves them separate. It will not hand you a single treated skin for a damaged carbonate, and you should not manufacture one by adding the two together.

## The choice you do not get to make

You do not select a route. The rock does. Quartz framework means damage removal and the arithmetic of module three. Carbonate framework means wormholes and the arithmetic of this one.

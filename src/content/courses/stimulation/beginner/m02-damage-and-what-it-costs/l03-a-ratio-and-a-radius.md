# A ratio and a radius

Damage takes two numbers to describe, and neither of them on its own tells you anything.

{{panel:st-acid-explorer}}

## Hold the radius, move the ratio

Keep the damaged radius at the published 0.9 m and the wellbore at 0.108 m, and vary only the severity.

| Contrast k/ks | Skin |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 2.120263536200091 |
| 3 | 4.240527072400182 |
| 5 | 8.481054144800364 |
| 8 | 14.841844753400636 |
| 12 | 23.322898898201 |
| 20 | 40.28500718780173 |

Severe damage in a thin ring is expensive. A contrast of 20 inside 0.9 m gives a skin of 40.28500718780173.

## Hold the ratio, move the radius

Now keep the contrast at the published 5 and vary how deep the damage goes.

| Damaged radius rs, m | Skin |
| --- | --- |
| 0.2 | 2.4647445576952682 |
| 0.4 | 5.23733327993505 |
| 0.6 | 6.8591937123677065 |
| 0.9 | 8.481054144800364 |
| 1.5 | 10.524356639864328 |
| 3.0 | 13.296945362104108 |

Mild damage reaching a long way is also expensive. A contrast of only 5 reaching 3.0 m gives 13.296945362104108.

## Why one number is never enough

Look at the two tables together. A skin of about 5 appears in both: contrast 3 at 0.9 m gives 4.240527072400182, and contrast 5 at 0.4 m gives 5.23733327993505. Those are two completely different wells. One has moderate damage reaching deep, one has worse damage in a shallow ring.

A well test hands you the skin. It does not hand you the pair. So when someone says the well has a skin of 8, they have told you the cost of the damage and nothing about its shape.

That matters because the treatment depends on the shape. An acid job is designed by choosing how far out to push the front, and how far out you need to push depends entirely on the radius, which is the number the test did not give you.

## Exercise

Find two more pairs from the tables above that give a similar skin by different routes, and describe each well in words.

Say which of the two numbers you would want measured first, and why.

Then state what a well test alone can and cannot tell you about the damage.

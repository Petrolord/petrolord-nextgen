# The gate that could not fail

Everything in this lesson is history. It describes the state of this engine and its published cases before the repair, and each item is a general lesson with an example here. Nothing here describes the engine or the suite you have been using.

## Item three, a constant hidden inside its own input

The general lesson: a number applied twice is invisible on the answer and visible only in a ratio. The way to find one is to rebuild the quantity from first principles and divide.

Before the repair the blowdown mass flow multiplied the caller's discharge coefficient by a second, hard-coded coefficient of its own. A typed value therefore ran about two and a half percent low, the time came out that much long, and the fifteen-minute verdict was read off it.

Notice what that does to a reader. The answer stays plausible, it moves the right way when you turn the coefficient, and every case is wrong by the same factor, which is the one pattern a sanity check cannot see.

Today the closed-form comparison in the first module of this tier is what would catch it: the marched time against the exact integral of the same balance, with the ratio column reading one.

## Item four, a gate that could not fail

The general lesson: a validation case is only worth what it can tell apart, and there are two separate ways it can be worth nothing. It can be MISSING, so a route has no case at all. Or it can be PRESENT AND UNABLE TO DISCRIMINATE, sitting where the constant it is supposed to check is worth less than the tolerance it is checked at.

Before the repair, twenty defects planted one at a time in the engine left the published suite entirely green in seven cases, including an inverted division inside the drum's own length equation. Three whole routes had no published case and no oracle route at all. Five oracle routes were transcriptions of the engine rather than independent derivations, and that was proved by planting the same defect in both files and regenerating the published set.

Read the method, because it is the reusable part. To find out whether a suite can discriminate, break the engine on purpose and watch. A suite that stays green has told you something about itself.

Today the published set carries 11 blocks and the suite is materially larger, the five transcriptions are independent derivations, and the two routes nothing can discriminate are named rather than left to be discovered. The audit module of this tier is the standing form of that lesson, about the engine as it is now.

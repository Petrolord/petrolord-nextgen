# Where the setback is taught

You now have a relation that gives an intensity at a distance and a route that gives the distance an allowable intensity demands. The obvious next step is a flare setback. This course does not take it, and the reason is worth more to you than the calculation would be.

## The handback, by name

A flare setback computed from a heat release, the pool fire behind it, and the customary allowable intensities with their held-for-literature caveat are all owned by the Separation and Slug Catching course. That course is already live, it computes a setback, it does the pool fire and the flame height beside it, it teaches the limitations of the point source, and its Professional capstone grades a setback of its own.

So no setback is computed anywhere in this course. What this module teaches instead is that the relief engine carries a second copy of the same point-source model, and that the copy and the original are asserted to stay equal.

## Why a duplicate model is the more interesting subject

Two engines in one package holding the same screening relation is a fact about the package rather than about flare radiation, and it raises a question a single copy never would: what keeps them the same.

The answer for the customary allowable intensities is a test that asserts the two tables stay equal, table row by table row, wording included. That is a tripwire. Change one copy and the suite goes red, which makes the change a reviewed act rather than a quiet one.

Read what a tripwire is and is not. It is not a validation. It does not say either table is right. It says the two copies have not drifted apart, which is a different and smaller claim, and the next lesson is about how to hold a claim that small without overselling it.

## The allowable is yours to state

Because the setback lives elsewhere, the discipline this course asks for is simple. When you use the inverse route, state the allowable intensity you are solving against and say where it came from, in the same place you write the distance down.

Do not reach for a label. The four customary intensities in this engine are held for literature, their wording was written by this package, and nothing here checks either the values or the words. A distance quoted against a named exposure category inherits the authority of the name, and the name has none to lend.

A stated project allowable, sourced and written down, is the honest input. It also makes the answer auditable, because a reader can disagree with the allowable without having to reverse-engineer which row of a table you used.

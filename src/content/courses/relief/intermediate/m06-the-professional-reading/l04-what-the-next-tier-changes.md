# What the next tier changes

This tier read a relief load into existence from geometry, and sized the drum that catches what the valve passes. The Expert tier goes to the one thing in this module that moves in time, and then turns round and audits the whole thing.

## A vessel emptying itself

Everything in this tier and the last is a steady state. A load, a pressure, an area, a length: none of them changes while you read it. Depressuring is different. A vessel blowing down to a flare is emptying, so its pressure falls, its contents cool, and the mass leaving through the orifice falls with them.

That means a march rather than a formula. The Expert tier follows mass out through a choked orifice and an isentropic expansion inside the vessel, step by step, and reads the answer off the trajectory. It also asks the question a plant actually asks, which is what orifice buys a given depressuring time, and answers it by searching rather than by asserting.

A march brings its own class of question with it. The step size becomes part of the answer, and a whole module is given to what a step size owes the reader.

## The radiation field, and where the setback lives

The point-source radiation model is asked both ways in the Expert tier: the intensity at a distance, and the distance an allowable intensity demands. The two directions are one model asked twice, and a round trip through a function and its own inverse proves the two implementations agree and nothing about the model.

One thing that tier deliberately does not do is compute a flare setback. The setback and the four customary allowable intensities belong to the Separation and Slug Catching course, which is merged and grades one. The Expert tier names that course and hands the subject back rather than teaching it twice.

## The audit

Then the module is read as an object rather than as a set of answers. What was computed, what was typed, what no published case can discriminate, and what nothing checks at all. On this engine that reading is unusually rich, because the number of held items is large and each one is named.

The final module of that tier is the one place whose subject is the engine's own history, and its title says so. Everything you have read in this tier describes the engine as it ships today.

## What carries forward

Three habits go with you. Read the boundary before the answer. Keep the independent readings of one object apart. And ask whether a quantity is entitled to be compared before comparing it.

The arithmetic ahead is not harder than the arithmetic here. There are simply more places where a number can be the right answer to a question nobody asked.

# Onward

The electrical half of an ESP design is a short calculation with a long reach. Be precise about what it earns and where it hands over.

## What it earns

It turns a head requirement into equipment somebody can buy. A stage count and a duty give a shaft power, a motor plate turns that into amps, a conductor and a well temperature turn the amps into a drop, and the drop decides the cable and the switchboard. Golden electrical case 1 ends at 2481.51704573 V and 143.98680570 kVA, which is an order, not an opinion.

It also gives surveillance something to compare against. The same curve read backwards puts a running pump at a head ratio and a region, and those two numbers outlive any verdict attached to them.

## Where it hands over

Ampacity is the first handover. The shipped table carries conductor resistance and no ampacity column, because ampacity belongs to the insulation system and the well temperature, so 192.000000 A can be taken down 6 AWG at a drop of 3.733233 percent with nothing objecting. That number comes from a manufacturer, and until it does the pick has run on half the method.

Motor efficiency is the second. Nothing in the surface call knows it, so consumption and heat rejection are calculations held elsewhere.

Viscosity is the third. This engine will not guess at a correction, and a curve read on a fluid it was not published for is a curve read wrongly.

## The limit worth remembering

None of it improves the decision. A perfectly sized motor on a well that should not have been pumped is an accurate account of horsepower nobody wanted.

## What you have finished

Which shaft power the chain is built on, which load fraction a field name means, what the cable was actually picked on, what the surface numbers can and cannot be balanced into, and how far a diagnosis can be trusted. Quote the ratio beside every flag, and name the module beside every field.

Write the three places where this module hands the problem to somebody else, and the number or the data that would have to arrive for each.

Then say why a design that is correct in every field can still be an unsafe installation.

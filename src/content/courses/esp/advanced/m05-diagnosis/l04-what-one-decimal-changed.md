# What one decimal changed

It changed nothing about the well. It changed everything about whether a user believes the warning, and that is the only reason it was allowed.

{{panel:pd-power-explorer}}

## The audit of the change

Thresholds changed: 0. Returned fields changed: 0. Message templates changed: 3.

Every flag that fired before the change fires after it, at the same ratio. Every number the function returns is bit for bit what it was. A head ratio of 0.8461 raised `underCurve` before and raises `underCurve` now; the only difference is that the sentence beside the flag reads 84.6 percent rather than 85 percent.

No arithmetic moved. That sentence is the whole justification for the change, and it is also the whole test of whether any other change in this wave could be made.

## Why this one and not the other four

Five findings were examined in this wave and one was changed. Each of the others moves a number that a live application is showing somebody today.

Two powers for one pump: the electrical chain is built on brake power at the head required, 125.69771587 hp on golden design gassyOffshore, while the published sizing method takes brake power at the head the stack makes, 125.74467535 hp, an understatement of 0.037345 percent there and 2.503380 percent on teaching well IBENO-2. Switching would move the amps, the drop, the cable pick and every surface number. Recorded, not fixed.

Two fields named `loadFraction`: the selection fraction against the derated rating and the electrical fraction against the plate, 12.2337 points apart at a load fraction of 0.89714 and a 12 percent derate. Both are right for what they mean. Adjudicated correct.

Two conversions for one gradient: the exact 0.433333333333 against the rounded 0.433, 0.076982 percent apart, worth 3.832442 ft of total dynamic head on golden design gassyOffshore. Adjudicated a convention.

An ampacity check that is true by construction, so 192.000000 A goes down 6 AWG. Confirmed as failing open, and the repair there is a data column, not arithmetic.

## The criterion

A change is safe when nothing downstream consumes what it touched. A returned field is consumed by a design, a spreadsheet and a report. A message is read once by a person and consumed by nobody, so the format of a message is the one surface in a shipped engine that can be repaired without asking anyone's permission.

## What it did not buy

It did not make the diagnosis better. A stack making 4760.000000 ft against an expected 5600.000000 ft still raises nothing, because that is a ratio of 0.8500 and the band still opens below it. The reader is better informed inside the band and no better served at its edge.

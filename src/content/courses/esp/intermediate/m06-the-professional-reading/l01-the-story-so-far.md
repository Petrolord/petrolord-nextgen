# The story so far

Five modules, and one fluid underneath all of them.

## The claim

A well becomes a head requirement and a stage count, and every step of that is a property of the fluid the pump actually swallows rather than of the fluid the reservoir produces.

## What each module established

**Module 1.** The pump sees the flowing bottomhole pressure less the annulus column above it. On the published gassyOffshore design, 1500.0 psia less 160.0000 psi over 500 ft at 0.3200 psi/ft gives an intake of 1340.0000 psia. At those conditions 240000.00 scf/d of gas is free, 288.000000 bbl/d of it at depth, and the stream is 0.0975609756 gas by volume. A separator at 0.7000 vents 201.600000 bbl/d and leaves 86.400000 bbl/d through the pump, so the gas volume fraction through the pump is 0.0314136126 and the verdict is standard. The published limits are 0.10 and 0.25.

**Module 2.** Taking gas out leaves the pump swallowing something heavier: 50.53658537 lbm/ft3 for the whole stream against 53.80104712 lbm/ft3 through the pump, a gradient heavier by 0.0226698733 psi/ft. The package carries the conversion twice, 62.4 divided by 144 against the rounded 0.433000, 0.076982 percent apart, worth 3.832442 ft of head on that design. Derive the specific gravity from the design gradient and the two routes disagree by 0.000000000000 ft.

**Module 3.** A barrel at the tank is not a barrel at depth. 1200.0 stb/d of oil and 1200.000000 stb/d of water become 1440.000000 and 1224.000000 bbl/d, 2664.000000 bbl/d of liquid, 2952.000000 bbl/d of stream and 2750.400000 bbl/d through the pump. The rate at depth is 1.11000000 times the tank liquid rate there, and 1.02100000 times on the published highWaterCut design.

**Module 4.** 1860.0000 psi over 0.3736183828 psi/ft is 4978.341767 ft, splitting into a net lift of 3413.452705 ft, a friction of 494.277929 ft and a wellhead term of 1070.611133 ft, which sum back to within 0.000000000000 ft. The net lift is 68.5661 percent of it.

**Module 5.** 4978.341767 ft over 25.93855015 ft is 191.92829740 stages, so 192, making 4980.20162863 ft. The two powers, 125.69771587 hp at the head required and 125.74467535 hp at the head made, stand in the ratio 1.000373590543, which is also the ratio of the two heads.

## The one sentence

The pump sees a fluid nobody measured directly, and every number after the intake is that fluid carried forward, so a head, a gradient and a rate mean nothing until you say which fluid they belong to.

This tier does not size a motor. A stage count with no shaft power behind it is a shopping list, not a design.

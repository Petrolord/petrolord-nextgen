# What an ESP is

An electric submersible pump makes head by repetition. One stage is small, and a few hundred on one shaft is a pump.

## One stage, then hundreds of it

A stage is an impeller and a diffuser. At a given rate it adds a fixed amount of head and nothing more, so head is bought by stacking stages while rate is not. Four reference stage models ship in the catalogue, and each makes the same trade.

| Stage | Housing OD, in | Rate at BEP, bbl/d | Head at BEP, ft | Peak efficiency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ref-400-1000 | 4.00 | 1000 | 33 | 0.63 |
| ref-540-2500 | 5.13 | 2500 | 28 | 0.70 |
| ref-562-4000 | 5.62 | 4000 | 23 | 0.72 |
| ref-675-7000 | 6.75 | 7000 | 18 | 0.74 |

A wider housing passes more rate and makes less head for it, while peak efficiency runs the other way, 0.63 fraction on the smallest and 0.74 fraction on the largest.

Each stage carries the rate range its vendor is prepared to publish: 500 to 1450 bbl/d on ref-400-1000, 1250 to 3500 on ref-540-2500, 2200 to 5600 on ref-562-4000, 4000 to 9800 on ref-675-7000.

## The motor on the end and the cable to it

The stack turns at one speed because one motor turns it. Five nameplates ship in the catalogue, from 60 hp at 1000 V and 38 A on a 4.56 in series to 400 hp at 3300 V and 78 A on a 5.62 in series. Power reaches that motor down five copper sizes, 6 AWG at 0.4028 ohms per 1000 ft through to 1/0 AWG at 0.1002, quoted at 77 degF.

## The mistake

Quoting a stage head as the pump head. One ref-540-2500 stage makes 28 ft at 2500 bbl/d. A well wanting thousands of feet of lift needs more of that same stage, not a different one, and the rate it passes is set by the stage no matter how many are stacked.

## What it refuses

The catalogue holds no manufacturer part numbers with invented curves behind them. Its four stages are shapes with four named parameters each, a rate and a head at best efficiency, a shutoff to BEP head ratio of 1.40, 1.35, 1.32 or 1.30, and a peak efficiency. None is a real pump.

The cable table carries resistance and nothing else. There is no ampacity column, because ampacity belongs to the insulation system and the well temperature, and is an input rather than a property of copper.

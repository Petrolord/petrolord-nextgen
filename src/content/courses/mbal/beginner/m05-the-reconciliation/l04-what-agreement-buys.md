# What agreement buys

A number becomes a booking when somebody signs it. The signature is the point of this whole course, and it is worth being precise about what the reconciliation actually contributes to that signature, because it is easy to overclaim and easy to undersell.

## The argument in one sentence

An error that survives two independent methods has to be an error both methods make. That sentence is the entire value of the reconciliation, and everything below is unpacking it.

Volumetrics can be wrong. Its map, its contact and its cutoffs can all be wrong, and none of those failures announces itself. Material balance can be wrong too. Its pressures can be unrepresentative, its depletion can be too small to constrain the answer, its tank can be two tanks. Also silent failures.

But the two lists have almost nothing in common. A mispicked fluid contact does not touch a pressure survey. A gauge reading a flowing pressure instead of a static one does not touch a seismic surface. So when the geometry route says 12139208.107496763 stb and the thermodynamics route says 12139208.1074968 stb, the space of errors that could produce that coincidence has collapsed to almost nothing. You are not twice as confident. You are confident in a different way, which is worth more.

## What you can say out loud

With a reconciliation in hand, these statements are defensible:

**The oil in place is supported by two methods with disjoint inputs.** Say which two, name the data behind each, and give the gap in barrels with its denominator. That single line does more for a reserves submission than three extra decimal places on either number.

**The tank model is behaving.** The straight line of $F$ against $E_t$ had an R-squared of 1.00000000000000 and an intercept of -6.11180439591408e-10 rb, and the drive indices closed to 1.00000000000000 with a depletion drive index of 0.607003891050583. A model that reproduces its own inputs and apportions all of the drive is a model that has not quietly absorbed something you failed to tell it about.

**The mechanism is identified.** The engine returns a drive mechanism of depletion_drive and an aquifer strength of none, and those readings are consistent with the straight line and with a water drive index of exactly 0.00000000000000. Knowing what is pushing the oil is as much a part of the booking as knowing how much oil there is, because it determines what happens next.

**The remaining volume is quantified.** Cumulative production at the last survey is 261475.039999678 stb against an oil in place of 12139208.1074968 stb, a recovery of 2.15397114609312 percent, leaving 11877733.0674972 stb still in the ground. That framing is what turns a reconciliation into a development conversation.

## What agreement does not buy

Four honest limits, and an engineer who states them unprompted is the engineer people believe.

**It does not turn oil in place into reserves.** Ekene has recovered 2.15397114609312 percent of its oil in place so far, and depletion drive alone will not take that number very high. Oil in place is the size of the prize. Reserves are what you can produce, at a profit, with a stated development. The reconciliation says nothing about the second thing.

**It does not validate a shared assumption.** Both routes consume the initial oil formation volume factor and the initial water saturation, as lesson 1 flagged. It is tempting to conclude that a shared error would therefore pass unnoticed. Test it rather than assume it. Suppose the laboratory oil formation volume factor is biased high by 0.05 rb/stb along the whole pressure path, so the initial value is 1.25 rather than 1.20. The volumetric booking, which divides by that factor, falls to 11653639.7831969 stb, down 4 percent. The material balance slope, which consumes the same factor through the expansion terms, rises to 12433800.5908325 stb, up 2.42678501535640 percent. The two routes move in opposite directions and a gap of 780160.807635600 stb opens up, 6.69456772433017 percent of the volumetric figure. So the check is not blind to that error, but it does not name it either. All you learn is that something is wrong, which is exactly why the reconciliation is a screen and not a diagnosis.

**It does not certify the data.** On the Ekene fixture the pressure history was constructed from the volumetric booking, so the agreement is a closed loop, as lesson 2 said plainly. On a real field, two routes can agree because both were tuned by the same person towards the number the partner expected. Independence is a property of how the numbers were produced, not of the numbers themselves, and you can only claim it if you know the provenance of both.

**It does not remove the need to say what would change the answer.** A booking without sensitivities is an assertion. The reconciliation gives you a very good starting point for those sensitivities, because you already know which inputs each route is most exposed to.

## Worked example: the reconciliation note

Here is the Ekene reconciliation written the way it should appear in a report. Every number in it has come from this tier.

"Ekene, oil in place. Volumetric route, from the mapped closure above the 1560 m contact with a net to gross of 0.8, a porosity of 0.2 and an initial water saturation of 0.35: 12139208.107496763 stb. Material balance route, Havlena-Odeh on six static pressure surveys from 2020-01-01 to 2023-01-01, over a depletion of 1103.99173733300 psi from an initial 3200 psia, with 261475.039999678 stb produced: 12139208.1074968 stb. Absolute gap 7.45058059692383e-8 stb, relative gap 6.13761666407432e-15 of the volumetric figure. The straight line returned R-squared 1.00000000000000 with an intercept of -6.11180439591408e-10 rb. Drive indices at the last survey: depletion 0.607003891050583, segregation 0.392996108949419, water 0.00000000000000, sum 1.00000000000000. Mechanism read as depletion drive with no aquifer support. Recovery to date 2.15397114609312 percent. Note that this dataset is engine-consistent by construction, so the agreement verifies the method rather than the field. The material balance answer is most exposed to the representativeness of the static pressures; the volumetric answer is most exposed to the contact pick and the net to gross cutoff."

Read the last two sentences again. They cost nothing and they are the difference between a number and a defensible number. The disclosure of the closed loop, in particular, is the sort of thing an engineer is tempted to leave out because it weakens the headline. Leave it out and the first reviewer who notices it will discount everything else you wrote.

## Exercise

Write the same note for the disagreeing field from lesson 3: volumetric 14000000 stb, material balance 12139208.1074968 stb, six good surveys, a straight line with no curvature. Keep the structure of the worked example, name the gap in barrels and as a percentage with its denominator, state the direction, offer one mechanism, and finish with the two sensitivity sentences.

Then do the harder half. List the three inputs most likely to move that answer, rank them by how much, and for each name the measurement or reinterpretation that would tighten it. If you cannot rank them, that is useful information: it means you do not yet know which route your booking is resting on.

# Onward

Where the ratings go next.

## What this tier gave you

Four numbers for any joint in the catalog, at any grade, under any axial load. That is a capacity.

## What it did not give you

A load. Nothing in this tier said how much pressure is actually inside the pipe, or outside it, or how much weight is hanging below any particular depth.

A rating with no load is half of a design.

## The Professional tier

Load cases. Seven of them, each a pair of pressure columns down the hole plus an axial profile, standing for a thing that happens to a real string: a gas kick, a pressure test, an evacuation, a cement job, running the string.

Then the comparison. Every rating from this tier against every load from that one, at every depth, with a design factor on each of the four checks, producing a verdict per section.

Two things in that tier will surprise you. The first is that the worst point on a string is often not the shoe, and on one of the seven cases it is the wellhead. The second is that the check that actually produces the one non-passing verdict in the whole suite is not any of the three named ones.

## The Expert tier

The tubing, which is a different problem, because it is not cemented. A free string inside a hole, sealed to a packer, that gets longer when it warms up and shorter when it cools, and pulls or pushes on the packer accordingly.

Three forces, three length changes, two ways to fail, and a temperature band inside which it does neither.

## Where else this goes

**Torque, Drag and Casing Wear** computes how much wall the drillstring has removed from this casing, and every rating in this tier should really be recomputed on the worn wall.

**Cementing** decides what is outside the pipe when it sets, which is the external column in two of the seven load cases.

**Geomechanics** and **Well Control** between them set the pressures that most of the load cases are built out of.

## One thing to keep

The regime. Whatever else you forget, keep the habit of asking which of the four collapse formulas a joint is using before you say anything about how to make it stronger.

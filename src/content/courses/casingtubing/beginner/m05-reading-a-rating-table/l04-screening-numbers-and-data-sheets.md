# Screening numbers and data sheets

Where this catalog stops, and what has to come from elsewhere.

## The claim

Everything in this tier is a SCREENING calculation. It narrows a choice from a hundred rows to two or three. It does not sign off a string.

## What the catalog is good for

Comparing rows. The formulas are consistent, published and identical for every row, so a ranking produced from them is trustworthy even where the individual numbers are approximate.

## Where the connection efficiencies stop

The efficiency table in this catalog is customary planning values. The catalog's own comment says they are not connection-specific ratings.

A real connection data sheet gives, at minimum: a tension rating, a compression rating, an internal pressure sealing rating, an external pressure sealing rating, a bending capacity, and a combined-load envelope. None of those six is a single efficiency and none of them is derivable from one.

## Where the collapse formulas stop

Three of the four regimes are empirical fits to a test population. The published values are intended to be lower bounds on that population, but they are still statistics about pipe made decades ago.

They also assume the pipe is round and uniform. Ovality and residual stress from manufacture both reduce collapse resistance, and neither appears anywhere in the calculation.

## Where the whole model stops

**Wear.** Casing that has had a drillstring rotating inside it for weeks has lost wall. That is the subject of the Torque, Drag and Casing Wear course, and the worn wall is what a real check should be run on.

**Corrosion.** Same effect, slower, and it does not stop when drilling does.

**Temperature.** Yield strength falls with temperature, and none of the ratings here are temperature-derated.

**Sour service.** The single most important thing about a grade name is whether it is qualified for hydrogen sulphide, and no formula in this course reads it.

## What to do with that

Use these numbers to choose the candidates. Then verify the candidates against the manufacturer's data, the wear prediction and the service environment, and expect the verification to eliminate some of them.

A screening tool that eliminates the right ninety percent has done its job. It has not made the decision.

# What a rating is, and is not

A published number with a definition attached, and the definition is the important half.

## The idea

Every rating in this course is the answer to a precisely stated question. Change the question and the number changes.

## Burst

The internal pressure that yields the pipe wall, computed on the MINIMUM permitted wall rather than the nominal one, with zero external pressure.

It is not a rupture pressure. Steel yields long before it tears. Passing the burst rating means the pipe has permanently deformed, not that it has opened up.

## Collapse

The external pressure at which the pipe becomes unstable and buckles inward, with zero internal pressure.

Three of the four collapse formulas are empirical fits to test data rather than derivations, which is a different kind of statement from the burst formula. They carry the scatter of the test programme with them.

## Pipe body yield

The axial force that takes the whole steel section to its yield stress. It is an exact statement about a defined section, which makes it the most trustworthy of the four.

## Joint strength

The pipe body yield times a connection efficiency. Screening-level, and the efficiencies in this catalog are customary values rather than manufacturer ratings.

## What none of them are

None of the four accounts for wear, corrosion, an earlier overload, a manufacturing defect, or the presence of any of the other three loads at the same time.

That last one matters most, and it is why there is a fourth check. Burst, collapse and tension are each computed as though the other two were absent, and a real string never has that luxury.

## Why they are still worth having

Because they are cheap, they are conservative in a stated way, and they are comparable. Two engineers computing the burst rating of the same joint get the same number, which is not true of anything that requires judgement.

A rating is a common language, not a prediction.

## Exercise

Write down, in one sentence each, the loading condition assumed by the burst rating and by the collapse rating.

Then say what is wrong with the sentence "this joint is rated to 65 MPa".

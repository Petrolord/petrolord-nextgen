# What it refuses to be

A screening engine is defined as much by what it leaves out as by what it computes. Each refusal is a place where a normal looking number means something a reader did not expect.

## It has no economic limit

Every year of the life is produced and charged, including years whose net cash flow is negative. The edge field OKPOMA shows it: 6800 bopd declining 18 percent a year, oil at 78 USD per bbl, capex of 260 million USD, fixed opex of 4 million USD a year, variable opex of 10 USD per bbl, royalty 10 percent, tax 40 percent, discounted at 10 percent from 2027.

| field | year | grossRevenue | opex | ncf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OKPOMA | 2046 | 4.4603 | 4.5718 | -0.5576 |

In 2046 opex alone exceeds gross revenue. A real operator would have shut the field in. The engine charges the year, keeps the -0.5576 in the ledger and carries it into an NPV of 167.4389 million USD. After the two capex years it is the only losing year, so the tail is small here, and the engine would have kept a large one just as readily.

## Its quick life is fixed at twenty years

The quick form always builds a 20 year case. ISIALA runs from 2027 to 2046 and still produces 141552.0984 bbl in its last year, for a net cash flow of 2.6534. No quick input lengthens or shortens the life, and the life is not derived from reserves.

## It discounts mid-year, always

Year index i is discounted by (1 + rate/100)^(i + 0.5). On ISIALA that gives 81.0464 where the same rows discounted at year end give 76.5817, a ratio of 1.058301 at 12 percent. EC1's Petroleum Economics Studio discounts at year end, so its numbers and these are different quantities and are not converted into each other.

## It is one fiscal form, and a simple one

The quick form builds TaxRoyalty only. There are no PIA terms, no cost oil in the quick form, no working interest and no inflation basis. Those live in Petroleum Economics Studio. Royalty is one flat rate off gross revenue and tax is one flat rate on a positive base, for every year of the life.

## The mistake

The careful mistake is to assume the engine would have stopped a losing tail, and so to quote OKPOMA's 167.4389 as the value of the years worth producing. It is the value of all twenty, including 2046. A screening NPV says whether a case deserves a proper model, and the proper model is where an economic limit belongs.

The second mistake is to put this NPV beside an NPV from Petroleum Economics Studio for the same field and read the gap as a disagreement about the field. Mid-year and year end discounting alone move ISIALA from 76.5817 to 81.0464, before either ledger differs in any line.

## Exercise

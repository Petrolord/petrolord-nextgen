# Onward

A screening Monte Carlo models how ranges on a few inputs spread one simple case, and a breakeven distribution models how three stated beliefs spread one price. Be clear about what that earns and where it hands over.

## What it earns

It turns one number into a stated range that anyone can reproduce. ISIALA's deterministic NPV of 81.0464 million USD becomes a Low case P90 of 48.7439, a Best case P50 of 81.1835 and a High case P10 of 109.8980, and its breakeven becomes 62.1713, 73.3297 and 85.5912 USD per bbl at the 10th percentile, the median and the 90th percentile. Anyone running seed 20260829 gets the same numbers to the fourth decimal.

It also honours a belief. Capex stated as 150 / 180 / 220 is fitted to a triangle of 127.2260, 168.6738 and 252.3607 that passes back through all three, where the old endpoints reading gave 164.4914, 182.5834 and 203.2668 and deleted the tails.

## Where it hands over

It is a screening tier. It has no PIA terms, no cost oil in the quick form, no working interest and no inflation basis, and those live in Petroleum Economics Studio, the EC1 course, which discounts at year end on its own ledger. It has no economic limit, so a late year such as OKPOMA's 2046 at -0.5576 is produced and charged in every iteration. It never samples opex, royalty or tax in the Scenario Builder, never correlates one uncertainty with another, and never says how far a percentile would move under another seed. On ISIALA the median breakeven prices of ten seeds span a range of 0.4949 USD per bbl, and only running the seeds shows it.

## The limit worth remembering

A probabilistic number without its label, its rule, its settings and its seed is not a number. ISIALA's Low case P90 is 48.7439 under the screening rule and 48.8335 under the breakeven rule on the same sample, and its NPV is 81.0464 mid-year and 76.5817 at year end on the same rows. Each is right for its question, and none is right without it.

## What you have finished

One meaning of a P-label; the Scenario Builder's sampler and what its seed guarantees; two percentile rules and how much a percentile wobbles; the edges that used to break and the one that still does; and the numbers to read only with their method.

## Exercise

Write the sentence that reports ISIALA's Low case P90, with its engine key, its rule, its iterations, its ranges and its seed. Then list three questions this tier's engines answer and three they hand to someone else.

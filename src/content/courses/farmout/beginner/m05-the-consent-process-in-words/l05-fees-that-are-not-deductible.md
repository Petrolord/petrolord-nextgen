# Fees that are not deductible

{{panel:farmout-earning-calculator}}

Consent comes with a fee. The Act says the fee is a percentage of the value of the transaction, and in three places it says that fees for assigning rights are not deductible for tax. The Professional tier computes the fee and its day rules.

## The Act sets a fee on the value of the transaction

> "(12) The Commission shall make regulation to prescribe for payment of fees as a condition for any transaction under subsection (1), which fee shall be based on a percentage of the value of the transaction and shall not be tax deductible." (PIA s.95(12))

The 2024 Regulations do the prescribing in reg. 19. At this tier, note two things. The fee is charged on the value of the transaction, a figure a deal states with its source; it is no figure the earning calculator returns. And consent waits on payment:

> "(6) The consent of the Minister and the Commission shall not be granted until the appropriate application and processing fees have been fully paid." (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 19(6))

## The same rule, repeated

The Regulations repeat the Act's rule for every part of the fee:

> "(5) Any payment made as processing fee, consent fee, or premium under these Regulations shall not be tax deductible." (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 19(5))

The Act's tax sections say it again for hydrocarbon tax and for companies income tax. For hydrocarbon tax the items not deductible include:

> "bonuses or fees paid for renewing petroleum mining lease or petroleum prospecting licence or marginal field or fees paid for assigning rights to another party ;" (PIA s.264(f))

And for companies income tax:

> "signature bonuses or fees paid for renewing petroleum mining lease or petroleum prospecting licence or fees paid for assigning rights to another party including for marginal fields ;" (PIA s.302(12)(c))

The Nigerian fiscal system as a whole is the Petroleum Industry Act course's subject; this course quotes these two lines only.

## A plan for decommissioning

One more provision of the Act reaches every farm-out agreement:

> "(10) Where the licensee or lessee is a party to a farm out agreement with one or more third parties, a decommissioning and abandonment plan funded in whole or in part by the applicable third parties shall be provided for in the applicable farm out agreement." (PIA s.233(10))

The engine computes no decommissioning share.

## Where the fee sits beside the deal

The earning calculator's consideration is carry, bonus and reimbursement, and its farminee outlay is the well payment, bonus and reimbursement. Neither contains the fee. The fee is a payment by the assignor to the Commission's account, and the Professional tier's deal calculator adds it as a stated input when it values the deal for each side.

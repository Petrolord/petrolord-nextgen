# An assignment needs consent

{{panel:farmout-earning-calculator}}

A farm-out moves a participating interest in a licence from one company to another. In Nigeria that move is an assignment, and an assignment needs the government's consent before it takes effect. This module reads the consent process in words, from the Act and the 2024 Regulations. The engine computes no consent; the Professional tier computes the fee.

## A farm-out is an assignment

The Petroleum Industry Act 2021 (Official Gazette No. 142, Vol. 108, 27 August 2021) puts every assignment of a licence or lease under s.95, and makes it subject to consent. The Nigerian Upstream Petroleum (Assignment of Interests) Regulations, 2024 (S.I. No. 67 of 2024, Official Gazette No. 61, Vol. 111, 9 April 2024) name a farm-in agreement among the petroleum agreements they cover:

> "“Petroleum Agreement” includes any or all of the following documents and or arrangements including, production sharing contracts, joint operating agreements, joint venture agreements, farm-in-agreements or any other document" (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 24, "Petroleum Agreement")

And their definition of an assignment reaches a joint venture partner under any upstream arrangement:

> "(d) assignment by a contractor or joint venture partner under an upstream petroleum arrangement, including joint venture arrangement, production sharing contract, production sharing agreement, service contract, under a sole risk award or a marginal field." (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 24, "Assignment" (d))

## Consent before the work, or after it

HMRC's manual, describing the United Kingdom trade, notes the same condition in passing:

> "The assignment is normally made, subject to government consent, before the work is undertaken and is called a Farm in." (HMRC Oil Taxation Manual OT30021)

So whichever order a deal chooses, assigned before the work or after it, the assignment waits on consent. The engine's vesting rule reports when a participating interest vests under the deal's terms, and says nothing about consent: its participating interests after the deal assume consent is in place.

## What the engine reports about consent

The engine carries the consent rule in the basis of its fee call, in its own words:

> a PPL or PML assignment needs the prior written consent of the Minister on the Commission's recommendation (PIA s.95(1) and (2)); a change of control above 50% is an assignment (s.95(3) and (14)); a PEL assignment needs the consent of the Commission (s.95(15); reg. 16)

That one line is the map for this module. The next lesson takes the Minister and the Commission, the one after it a change of control, then the notification and the application, and the last the fees and their tax treatment.

## Why an Associate reads this

A farm-out that is signed but not consented has moved no participating interest. The consideration, the carry and the participating interests after the deal all assume the assignment goes through.

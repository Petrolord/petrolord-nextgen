# The notification and the application

{{panel:farmout-earning-calculator}}

Consent is asked for in steps. The holder tells the Commission what it intends, the Commission answers, and the application for consent follows. This lesson reads the steps in order, with the time limits the texts set, and shows how the figures in the earning calculator feed the paperwork.

## The Act: apply to the Commission

A holder that wants to transfer its participating interest applies to the Commission, in the Commission's format:

> "shall make an application for approval of the transfer to the Commission in the format prescribed by the Commission," (PIA s.95(4))

## The Regulations: first a notification of intention

The 2024 Regulations put a notification of intention before the application. It says why, how and to what end:

> "stating the reason for the intended assignment, the method intended to be used for the conduct of the transaction, and possible technical and economic benefits derivable from the transaction" (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 4(4)(b))

The Commission then has 15 working days to answer, and silence is a deemed approval:

> "(7) The Commission shall within 15 working days from date of receipt of the notification of intention under sub-regulation (4)(a) of this regulation, approve or disapprove the transaction, failing which the application is deemed approved" (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 4(7))

The application for the Minister's consent is then made to the Commission (AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 4(2)), and the Minister's silence for 60 working days after the Commission's recommendation is a deemed consent (PIA s.95(7)(b)).

## The steps in order

| step | text | time limit the text sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| notification of intention: reason, method, benefits | AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 4(4)(b) | the Commission answers within 15 working days, or it is deemed approved (reg. 4(7)) |
| application for consent, to the Commission | PIA s.95(4); AOI Regulations 2024 reg. 4(2) | none read here |
| the Commission's recommendation, the Minister's decision | PIA s.95(2) | deemed granted after 60 working days of silence (s.95(7)(b)) |

## A marginal field adds a plan

For a marginal field, the Act adds a condition on the incoming party:

> "(5) The consent of the Commission to the farm-out of a marginal field under subsection (4) (b) shall, amongst others, be subject to the farmee presenting a field development plan" (PIA s.94(5))

## What the calculator gives the notification

The notification asks for the method of the transaction and its economic benefits. The earning calculator states both for a farm-out: the method is the share paid for the participating interest earned, the cap, the bonus and the reimbursement; the benefits to the farmor include the carry and the consideration.

# The consideration to the farmor

{{panel:farmout-earning-calculator}}

The farmor gives up a participating interest. What it receives for it, in this course's words, is the consideration: the carry, the cash bonus and the reimbursement, added together. This lesson builds the sum, reads it for the worked cases, and separates it from a figure with a similar name that the consent fee uses.

## Three parts

HMRC's manual describes a farm out as a disposal "in return for consideration given by the Farmer In" (HMRC Oil Taxation Manual OT30020). The engine counts three parts of that consideration, each from an earlier lesson:

* the **carry**, the part of the farmor's cost share of the well the farminee pays;
* the **cash bonus**, a stated sum;
* the **past-cost reimbursement**, a stated share of stated past costs.

On the Ekene Deep deal the engine writes the sum as a reason line:

> consideration to EKO: carry 4400000 + cash bonus 2000000 + past-cost reimbursement 3600000 (30% of 12000000) = 10000000

| worked case | carry | cash bonus | reimbursement | consideration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| earn-ekene-single | 4400000.000000 | 2000000.000000 | 3600000.000000 | 10000000.000000 |
| earn-bonus-and-reimbursement | 4000000.000000 | 1500000.000000 | 2700000.000000 | 8200000.000000 |
| earn-cap-gross-below | 4000000.000000 | 0.000000 | 0.000000 | 4000000.000000 |
| earn-heads-up | 0.000000 | 0.000000 | 0.000000 | 0.000000 |

## Reading the rows

On the Ekene Deep deal EKO receives 10000000.000000. Less than half of it is the carry: the rest is cash. On `earn-cap-gross-below` there is no cash at all, so the consideration is the carry alone, 4000000.000000. On the heads-up deal there is no carry and no cash, and the consideration is 0.000000: EKO gives up a participating interest and is paid only in the sense that FIN now pays its own share of the well.

## The consideration counts completed events

The totals count completed events only, and the consideration is a total. On the worked case `earn-none-completed` the event is not completed, and the engine's line reads:

> consideration to EKO: carry 0 + cash bonus 0 + past-cost reimbursement 0 (0% of 0) = 0

The event row still shows the carry the obligation would bring, 4000000.000000, and the total waits until the event is done.

## The value of the transaction is a different figure

Two words sound alike and are kept apart. The consideration is what the engine adds up from the deal's terms. The "value of the transaction" is the amount the 2024 Regulations charge the consent fee on, which reg. 19(3) takes from the application or contract, or from the Commission. A deal states that amount as its own input, and the Professional tier computes the fee on it. Neither figure is read off the other in this course.

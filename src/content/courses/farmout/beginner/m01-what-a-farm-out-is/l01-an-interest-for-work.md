# An interest for work

{{panel:farmout-earning-calculator}}

A farm-out is a trade. A party that holds a participating interest in a licence gives up part of it, and the party that takes it pays for the right, usually by paying for work on the licence. This course calls the party giving up the participating interest the farmor and the party earning it the farminee. The trade is a set of stated deal terms, and every term can be written down and computed.

## What the texts say a farm-out is

The HM Revenue and Customs Oil Taxation Manual (Open Government Licence v3.0, page OT30020 updated 8 August 2019) describes the trade in one sentence:

> "A Farm Out involves the disposal of a licence interest by the owner - the Farmer Out in return for consideration given by the Farmer In." (HMRC Oil Taxation Manual OT30020)

On acreage that has seen little drilling, the price is mostly work. The next page of the manual says so:

> "For relatively unexplored acreage consideration will generally consist wholly, or mainly, of the Farmer in undertaking to bear future costs (e.g. drilling)." (HMRC Oil Taxation Manual OT30021)

The Petroleum Industry Act 2021 (Official Gazette No. 142, Vol. 108, 27 August 2021) defines a farm-out in its marginal field section:

> "(b) “farm-out” means an agreement between the holder of a petroleum mining lease or petroleum prospecting licence and a third party, which permits the third party to explore, prospect, win, work and carry away any petroleum encountered in a licence or lease area" (PIA s.94(8)(b))

The engine's arithmetic applies to any farm-out a deal states.

## The price is a share of a well

The simplest farm-out asks the farminee to pay a stated share of the gross cost of one well, and in return it earns a stated participating interest. On the course's Ekene Deep prospect, which is synthetic, FIN pays 40.000000 percent of the exploration well to earn 30.000000 percent of the licence from EKO, which holds 70.000000 percent before the deal.

| term of the Ekene Deep deal | stated figure |
| --- | --- |
| share of the gross cost FIN pays | 40.000000 |
| participating interest FIN earns | 30.000000 |
| EKO's participating interest before the deal | 70.000000 |

FIN pays more of the well than the participating interest it earns. That gap is the promote, and a later module of this tier measures it.

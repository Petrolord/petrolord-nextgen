# Refundable costs and the refund

{{panel:farmout-valuation-calculator}}

A party backing in pays for the interest it takes by refunding part of the costs the others have already borne. Which costs count, how much is refunded and when it is paid are stated terms. On the Ekene back-in two bases give very different answers.

## The refund rule

The joint venture engine's rule, as the farm-out engine prints it:

> new interest of another party = old x (100 - target) / (100 - current); refund = (target - current) / 100 x refundable costs, received in proportion to the interest given up

The refund is the percentage points taken times the refundable costs. The parties who give up an interest receive it in proportion to what each gives up.

## Two bases on the same licence

| golden case | basis (stated) | refundable | excluded | refund | refund form | recovered in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| backin-ekene | contract | 480000000.000000 | 46000000.000000 | 24000000.000000 | upfront | upfront |
| backin-ekene-pia | pia-s85-4 | 480000000.000000 | 46000000.000000 | 144000000.000000 | from-future-entitlement | 2034 |

On backin-ekene EKO takes the points between 40.000000 and 45.000000, so the refund is that share of 480000000.000000: 24000000.000000, paid upfront. PA and FIN gave up equal shares, and each receives 12000000.000000 (engine). The exploration well is excluded because the contract's stated refundable kinds do not include it:

> Ekene Deep-1 exploration well: 46000000 (exploration) is not refundable (not a stated refundable kind)

## A back-in under the Act

The golden case backin-ekene-pia uses a different party and a different basis: PA backs in from 30.000000 to 60.000000 percent under PIA s.85(4), and the refund is recovered from PA's future entitlement (engine):

| golden case | party | before | after the back-in | ceded | refund received | refund paid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| backin-ekene-pia | EKO | 40.000000 | 22.857143 | 17.142857 | 82285714.285714 | 0.000000 |
| backin-ekene-pia | PA | 30.000000 | 60.000000 | 0.000000 | 0.000000 | 144000000.000000 |
| backin-ekene-pia | FIN | 30.000000 | 17.142857 | 12.857143 | 61714285.714286 | 0.000000 |

The engine's reasons, verbatim:

> PA backs in from 30% to 60%: the others keep 40 / 70 of their interests; refund 30% x refundable costs 480000000 = 144000000 (46000000 excluded)
> Ekene Deep-1 exploration well: 46000000 (exploration) is not refundable (PIA s.85(4)(c): development and production costs only, no bonuses, penalties, interest, premium or markups)
> 2034: the balance 24000000 is recovered with 24000000 of the 120000000 available; the back-in party receives 216000000 of its share 240000000

EKO gave up more than FIN because it held more, so it receives more of the refund. Under the Act the exploration well is excluded by the text of s.85(4)(c); under the contract basis it is excluded because the call's stated kinds leave it out. The Act's back-in and its place in the Nigerian system belong to the Petroleum Industry Act course and the joint ventures course.

## Upfront or from future entitlement

An upfront refund is paid at once. A refund from future entitlement is recovered out of the back-in party's new share of production until it is paid, here in 2034. The two forms move cash at different times. A farminee whose interest may be backed into reads both the refund amount and when it arrives.

# The capstone brief

{{panel:farmout-valuation-calculator}}

The Expert capstone grades the question this tier asked: what information is worth to one side, what a working interest is priced at, and what happens after the farm-in. It is worked in the course's own valuation calculator, which calls the same vendored engine every lesson has quoted. There is no Suite app for this course: the calculator is the practical.

## What you receive

The capstone card carries one case file for a synthetic deal of its own, with its own parties, prospect and terms. It holds four blocks, one for each view you will use: `information`, `price`, `devCarry` and `backIn`. Every term a graded figure depends on is stated in the file.

## What you report

Six quantities, each to six decimals. The card names each exactly. They are the kinds of figure this tier has read:

- the EVII of the side valued;
- the chance of success after one signal;
- the risked value per percent of working interest;
- the stated price over the value per percent;
- the balance of the development carry at the close of one year;
- the refund one party receives on the back-in.

None depends on a reading the engine states: each is the same number under every reading and under the alternative named beside it.

## How to work it

1. Paste the whole case file into "The value of information to one side". The view reads the information block. Read the EVII tile and the per-signal table.
2. Paste the whole file into "A price for a working interest" and read the per-percent tiles and the price tiles.
3. Paste it into "A development carry after the farm-in" and read the ledger row for the year the card names.
4. Paste it into "A back-in after the farm-in" and read the refund received column.
5. Read the reasons and notes under each table. The case as issued runs without a refusal; if you see one, you have changed the box.

Each view picks its own block out of the whole file.

## How it is graded

Each field is compared with the engine's own return on the issued case, within a tolerance set once for the whole course. Report the figure the calculator prints, at six decimals, with no rounding of your own. Reasons round money to the cent, so take each figure from its tile or table.

## Exercise

Rehearse the method on the Ekene terms before opening the card. In "The value of information to one side", start from "The Ekene survey to the farminee" and read FIN's EVII and the chance of success after the bright signal. In "A price for a working interest", start from "The Ekene Deep price, risked" and read the risked value per percent and the price over value per percent. In "A development carry after the farm-in", start from "The Ekene carry, compound uplift" and read the closing balance of 2034. In "A back-in after the farm-in", start from "The Ekene back-in, contract, upfront" and read what PA receives. For each figure, name the term that sets it and the lesson of this tier that taught it. The rehearsal deal is no capstone: its figures are printed in the lessons, and the card's are not.

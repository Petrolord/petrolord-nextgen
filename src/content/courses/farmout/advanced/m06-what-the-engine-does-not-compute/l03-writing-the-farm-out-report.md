# Writing the farm-out report

{{panel:farmout-valuation-calculator}}

Every figure in this course depends on terms, and a report that quotes a figure without them cannot be checked. This lesson assembles the farm-out report for a deal team.

## A figure travels with its terms

The course's rule for quoting binds a report: an EMV with its chance, payoffs and value basis; a break-even with its position; a value per percent with its basis; a carry recovery with its uplift and recovery share; an NPV with its rate and base year.

## What the report names

1. The licence and its parties, synthetic in this course, with their participating interests before and after the deal; the farmor and the farminee.
2. Every source applied, with its edition and the date read.
3. Each event with its gross cost, share paid, interest earned, cap and overrun rule; the vesting rule and the events completed.
4. The cash bonus, the reimbursement, the consideration and the equivalent working interest.
5. The chance of success, the well costs and the success-case value with its rate and base year.
6. Each side's EMV and best action, the break-even promote and the break-even chances.
7. The value of information to each side, with its likelihoods and cost.
8. The price per percent and any transaction ratio, with its basis.
9. The development carry or back-in, with its terms.
10. The consent needed, the value of the transaction with its source, the fee and the dates.
11. Each reading the figures rest on.

## The Expert sections, on Ekene Deep

For the Ekene Deep prospect (synthetic), the Expert sections read like this. The seismic survey, with likelihoods of 75.000000 and 25.000000 percent and a cost of 1500000.000000, is worth 6745331.458602 to FIN and 6892331.458602 to EKO before its cost; a bright signal turns FIN from decline to farm in and EKO from farm out to drill alone. The 30.000000 percent interest is worth 263125.842605 a percent on the risked basis at a chance of success of 25.000000 percent; the stated price is 2.026914 times that, a ratio reported only. The development carry, 50.000000 percent of EKO's cost share with a compound uplift of 8.000000 percent a year, recovered from 50.000000 percent of EKO's share, is recovered in 2036.

## What the report says it leaves out

The report names what the engine does not compute and what was done in its place: tax on the deal, a decommissioning share, the application fee, any market value.

## The words the report uses

The course's vocabulary binds a report too: "interest" is always qualified, an EMV is always of a named position, a break-even always of a named term, and each text's own word for a party is quoted as it prints it.

## Exercise

Open the valuation calculator and draft the Expert sections of a report for your own deal. Start from the Ekene cases on the views "The value of information to one side", "A price for a working interest" and "A development carry after the farm-in", then change at least two terms in each to describe a prospect of your own. For each figure you quote, write the terms it depends on beside it, and name the reading, if any, that it rests on.

# Vesting event by event

{{panel:farmout-deal-calculator}}

A drill-to-earn deal must say when the farminee's earned interest becomes its own. The first vesting rule the engine accepts, "per-event", vests each event's stated interest as soon as that event is completed. It is the order HMRC's manual calls a farm in: the assignment made with the work. This lesson runs the Ekene Deep drill-to-earn under that rule with one of its two events done.

## The rule in the engine's words

The engine states the rule in its basis:

> each completed event vests its stated interest (a farm in: assignment with the work, HMRC OT30021)

The vesting rule is a stated deal term with no default. The panel's "Vesting (stated)" control offers the two rules the engine accepts, and "not stated" removes the term, which the engine refuses by name:

> vesting must be one of "per-event", "all-events"; got nothing

## The Ekene Deep drill-to-earn, one event completed

The two events are the exploration well (40.000000 percent paid to earn 20.000000 percent) and the appraisal well (45.000000 percent paid to earn 15.000000 percent more). With "per-event" vesting and the exploration well completed:

| figure | engine |
| --- | --- |
| events completed | 1 |
| vested | 20.000000 |
| gross cost of completed events | 40000000.000000 |
| farminee paid | 16000000.000000 |
| farmor paid | 12000000.000000 |
| carry | 8000000.000000 |
| consideration | 8000000.000000 |
| equivalent working interest | 40.000000 |

The engine's reason for the vesting:

> vesting "per-event": 1 of 2 events completed; 20% vests

## The interests after one event

Under this rule the licence already changes hands in part. The participating interests after the exploration well are EKO 50.000000, PA 30.000000 and FIN 20.000000. FIN's 20.000000 percent comes out of EKO's interest alone; PA keeps its 30.000000.

The consideration line counts the carry of the completed event only. No cash bonus and no reimbursement are stated in the drill-to-earn, and the engine says so:

> consideration to EKO: carry 8000000 + cash bonus 0 + past-cost reimbursement 0 (0% of 0) = 8000000

## What the rule means for each side

For the farminee, "per-event" vesting means it holds something after each well it pays for. If it stops after the exploration well, it keeps the 20.000000 percent it has earned. For the farmor, the rule means its interest falls step by step, and a farminee that walks away after the first event leaves the farmor with a new partner and an appraisal well still to fund. The engine prefers neither: the rule is stated, and the engine applies whichever the deal names.

With both events completed, "per-event" and "all-events" arrive at the same place, 35.000000 percent vested. They differ only before the last event, which is where the next lesson begins.

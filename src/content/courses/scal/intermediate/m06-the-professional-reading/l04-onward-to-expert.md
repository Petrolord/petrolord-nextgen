# Onward to Expert

You arrived at this tier able to say how the Ekene sand displaces oil. You leave it able to say where the oil sits before the flood starts, on the strength of a lab measurement a few centimetres long, carried to the field through one dimensionless curve, and cashed in as a free water level and a crest saturation you can defend line by line. That is the working core of special core analysis.

The Expert tier turns from reading to judging, and four things wait there.

**Fitting the lab.** This tier fitted a power law to J data. Expert fits the Corey exponents themselves to a measured relative permeability table, in log space, with confidence intervals, and asks the questions a fit always raises: which points were allowed to speak, what the intervals mean when the data are clean, and what happens when the model family is simply wrong for the rock.

**The average that drifts.** Module 4 taught you to average several samples into one reservoir candidate and told you the refit should be checked against the raw fits. Expert quantifies that instinct. The averaging machinery resamples each curve before it refits, and resampling is not free. Knowing when the averaged answer and the direct answer differ, and which to carry forward, is an Expert judgment, and the Expert capstone grades it.

**Gravity and the dip term.** Every fractional flow curve at the Associate tier was horizontal. Real sands dip, and the engine's fw carries a gravity term that rewards injecting downdip and pushing oil up. Expert works the designed Ekene dip cases, both directions, and learns the term's most useful property: it scales with rate, so patience buys stability.

**Polymer as a screening lever.** Thickened water changes the mobility ratio, and the mobility ratio changes everything downstream of it. Expert runs the screening case through the engine's own honest path, warning string and all, and weighs what the front gains against what the label screening-only refuses to promise.

None of it needs mathematics you do not already have. It needs the same discipline this tier has been building: name the chain, name the units, name what the tool refuses to model, and let the engine's own numbers carry the argument. The Expert certificate also opens the Suite bridge, where the discount on the Reservoir module makes the professional tools this course has been teaching against available for real work.

Bring your capstone sketch of the Ekene column with you. Expert will ask harder questions about that column, and the answers start from exactly where you drew them.

# The story so far

A flowline loses heat through a stack of resistances in series, and the whole of that stack is geometry, conductivity and two films. No fluid, no rate and no clock appears anywhere in it.

## One pipe, three builds

The published 6.065 in bore with steel to 6.625 in at k 26.0000, then 2.0 in of syntactic polypropylene foam to 8.625 in at k 0.09, then a 4.0 ft trench in wet soil at k 1.2.

| Build | Overall U, Btu/(hr ft2 degF) |
| --- | --- |
| Bare, steel wall alone | 105.9799311355 |
| Insulated | 1.3348791131 |
| Buried 4.0 ft | 0.7132000377 |

Bare to insulated is 79.39290539. Insulated to buried is 1.87167561. Bare to buried is 148.59776436. The foam did nearly all the work, and the trench, added to an already dominant term, had little left to do.

## A share belongs to a build

The foam layer is 0.4665266247 hr ft degF/Btu per foot in every one of those builds. Its share is 98.88212788 percent insulated and 52.83080440 percent buried, and across four builds the share of that unchanged layer spreads over 46.05132348 percentage points.

The steel wall carries 9.09721378 percent of even the bare stack, because a layer resistance is ln(Do/Di) over k and steel has the thinnest log and the largest k in the drawing. A resistance is a property of a layer. A share is a property of a stack.

## A U is referred to a diameter

The published buried build is 0.713200037662 Btu/(hr ft2 degF) at the 6.065 in bore and 0.501513997498 at the 8.625 in coated outside diameter. The same layers, the same films, the same trench, and a total resistance of 0.883057962117 identical to the last figure in both. The ratio of the two coefficients is 1.4220939819, which is the ratio of the two diameters and nothing else.

What does not move is the product: U times its own reference diameter is 0.360463185702 Btu/(hr ft degF) per foot on either reading, and pi times that is one over the total resistance. A U without its reference is half a number.

## The line has a mass

The published wall is 18.9921056882 lbm/ft and the published contents 11.0344753586 lbm/ft, from two helpers that are geometry and a density. The cooldown that consumes them offers two slots, and the foam carrying 98.882128 percent of the insulated resistance fits into neither.

## What none of this decides

Where the hydrate boundary is. Neither engine computes it, both headers say so, and every verdict built on any of these numbers is conditional on a laboratory temperature somebody else supplied.

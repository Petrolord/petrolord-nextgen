# The layers that carry none

`overallU` takes an unbounded list of layers. The cooldown takes exactly two masses, contents and shell. There is no third slot, no helper that lumps one, and no warning when the list is longer than two.

{{panel:pd-thermal-explorer}}

## The count

On the published insulated build `overallU` accepted 2 layers. The cooldown offers 2 mass slots. The number of those slots the foam fits into is 0.

The steel wall takes the shell slot because that is what a shell is. The foam has nowhere to go, and the API neither says so nor asks.

## The layer with the resistance is the layer with no slot

The foam carries 98.882128 percent of the resistance of the published insulated build, and 52.830804 percent of it once the trench is added. It is the term the whole stack is built around and the term the mass side does not have a place for.

## What that foam weighs

Nothing in the engine knows. There is no density catalog for insulation, and no golden publishes one, so the figures here are TEACHING numbers this course chose: syntactic polypropylene foam at 44.0 lbm/ft3 with Cp 0.28 Btu/(lb degF). On the published pipe, from 6.625 in to 8.625 in, that is 7.3194745506 lbm/ft and a heat capacity of 2.0494528742 Btu/(ft degF).

Set against the published cooldown case, that is 26.943904 percent of its M Cp, and none of it is in that case at all.

## A coated line makes it larger

Teaching line AKASO SPUR carries polyurethane foam and a concrete weight coat outside its steel. The two mass slots hold 68.7787096055 lbm/ft of it and leave out 112.4591995215 lbm/ft. The two layers left out carry 67.414840 percent of its resistance. Most of the mass and most of the resistance sit outside the API. AKASO SPUR is a TEACHING LINE, not a published case and not a real line.

## The mistake

Reading the two slots as a description of the line. They are a description of the API. A careful reader fills contents and shell correctly, checks both masses, gets a clean return, and has silently assumed that everything outside the steel weighs nothing and stores no heat.

Lumping the coatings into the shell slot by hand is the only route the module allows. It is a legitimate route, and it stops being legitimate the moment it is not written down, because two people can lump differently and both returns look identical.

## What to record

Which layers went into which slot, at what density and at what specific heat, and which layers went nowhere. A stack of five terms and a mass of two is a fact about the answer, and it belongs in the report beside it.

# A trench is a resistance too

Soil is not a boundary condition. It is one more term in the same series stack, sitting outside the last coating, and the engine adds it the way it adds a layer.

## The published buried build, term by term

A 6.065 in bore, steel to 6.625 in at k 26, 2.0 in of syntactic polypropylene foam to 8.625 in at k 0.09, in a 4.0 ft trench to centreline in wet soil at k 1.2.


| Term | Resistance, hr ft degF/Btu per foot | Share, percent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| insideFilm | 0.0025191879 | 0.28528002 |
| layer0, carbon steel wall | 0.0005406116 | 0.06122039 |
| layer1, syntactic PP foam | 0.4665266247 | 52.83080440 |
| burial | 0.4112572083 | 46.57193819 |
| outsideFilm | 0.0022143296 | 0.25075700 |

Five terms, a total of 0.8830579621 hr ft degF/Btu per foot, and an overall U of 0.7132000377 Btu/(hr ft2 degF) referred to the 6.065 in bore. The shares sum to 100.00000000 percent, which is the arithmetic of a series stack and nothing cleverer.

## What the trench bought

Without the trench the same pipe returns 1.3348791131 Btu/(hr ft2 degF), a U ratio of 1.87167561. With neither trench nor foam it returns 105.9799311355, a bare to insulated ratio of 79.39290539 and a bare to buried ratio of 148.59776436.

Foam bought a factor of seventy nine here, and four feet of wet soil bought less than a factor of two on top of it, because once one term dominates a stack the next one added has little left to do.

## A share belongs to a build, not to the soil

TEACHING LINE AKASO SPUR is buried 3.00 ft to centreline in wet soil at k 1.20 under a 16.750 in coated outside diameter, and its trench resistance is 0.2835082263 hr ft degF/Btu per foot. With its polyurethane foam in place that trench is 32.148093 percent of the stack. Take the foam out and carry the weight coat straight from the steel to the same outside diameter, and the same 0.2835082263 becomes 77.505874 percent.

Nothing about the soil changed. The denominator did.

## The mistake

Quoting a burial share as though it described the seabed. A reader sees 46.57193819 percent, decides a trench is worth about half of any stack, and carries that to a line with different insulation, where the same soil at the same depth returns the same resistance and a different share entirely.

## What burial refuses to know

A pipe lying on the bottom adds nothing. At 2H/D = 1.00000000 the shape factor is 0.0000000000 and the U of the full buried build is 1.3348791131, the exposed answer to the last figure.

It also knows nothing about what is inside the pipe. It would return 0.4112572083 for a line carrying nothing at all.

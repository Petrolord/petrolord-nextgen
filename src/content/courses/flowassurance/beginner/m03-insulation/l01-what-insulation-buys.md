# What insulation buys

Two inches of foam on the published pipe takes U from 105.9799311355 to 1.3348791131 Btu/(hr ft2 degF), a factor of 79.39290539, and buys it with one added term.

## One term, added to a small total

The bare stack totals 0.0059426060 hr ft degF/Btu per foot. The foam layer, 6.625 in to 8.625 in of syntactic polypropylene at k 0.0900, is worth 0.4665266247 on its own, and the insulated total comes to 0.4718007538. The coating is not competing with the rest of the stack. It is many times the whole of it.

That is also why the foam ends up carrying 98.88212788 percent of the insulated build. A share that large is not a claim about the material being remarkable. It is a claim about everything else being small.

## The other terms did not improve

The inside film is 0.0025191879 on both builds and the steel wall is 0.000540611570 on both. What changes is their share: the inside film goes from 42.39197245 percent to 0.53395165 percent and the steel from 9.09721378 percent to 0.11458472 percent, resistances untouched. The outside film moves for a different reason, from 0.0028828065 to 0.0022143296, because the outside face is now the 8.625 in coated diameter and a film resistance carries an area.

## The mistake

Expecting U to follow k. The layer resistance is exactly inverse in conductivity, so the aerogel layer is 7.50000000 times the syntactic foam layer. The two U values differ by 7.42733831, because the films and the steel wall are still there and did not move. Quote the layer ratio as though it were the coefficient ratio and you have promised a performance the pipe cannot deliver.

The second half of the same mistake is the concrete row. A concrete weight coat is a coating, it is 2.0 in thick, and at k 0.9000 it leaves U at 12.1285554677. Thickness is not insulation.

## What it does not complicate

Nothing here hides anything. Insulation is a resistance in series with the others, the stack adds, the largest term dominates, and the engine returns the share each term carries so a reader can see which one that is. Not every module in this programme is like that, and this one is worth taking at face value.

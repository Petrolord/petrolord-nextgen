# Onward

A flow assurance verdict is a temperature set against a line somebody else drew. Be precise about what the two engines earn and where they stop.

## What they earn

A resistance stack that adds in one direction, with every share readable: on the published buried build the foam carries 52.83080440 percent and the trench 46.57193819 percent, and that same foam layer carries 98.88212788 percent once the trench is taken away, on an unchanged resistance of 0.4665266247 hr ft degF/Btu per foot. From that stack a relaxation length, an arrival at any station, an inverse returning the U a target needs, a lumped cooldown, and a dose in weight percent with an injection rate under it.

They also refuse well in places. An unknown insulation id returns NaN rather than carbon steel, worth the 2166.666667 between steel and aerogel on one layer. A target at or below ambient is refused, "A line cannot arrive above ambient (40 F) no matter how well it is insulated."

## Where they hand over

The hydrate boundary. Neither engine computes it, both headers say so, and every margin and subcooling in this course rests on a laboratory number somebody supplied. A verdict is conditional on it and should be reported that way.

Then the pressure profile, an input assumed linear. Then salinity, which inhibits, is left out, and is said to be. Then the ground model: a shape factor for an isothermal cylinder in a semi-infinite medium, one uniform soil conductivity, no groundwater movement, and a burial measured to the centreline, which read to the top of pipe instead moves the trench term by 2.784925 percent with nothing to catch it.

## The limit worth remembering

`ok: true` is not a verdict on the answer. A trench entered at 0.3 ft instead of 3.0 ft on the published pipe drops the ground term and returns U 79.035960 percent high, with `ok: true` and no note. A cooldown asked to fall to a target above its start returns hours = -4.6959175559 with `ok: true` and a station table that warms by 24.2422513458 degF after a shutdown.

## What you have finished

Where the Joule-Thomson term belongs and what applying it undamped is worth, which failures this module refuses and which it swallows, and how a dose is sized and by what it is then checked.

# The story so far

Five modules, four volumes, and one number nobody measured.

## The claim

A cement volume sheet is cylinder arithmetic on lengths and diameters, and every input to it is a measurement except one.

## What each module established

**Module 1.** Cement gives zonal isolation, support, corrosion protection and a foundation for the next section, and its failures show up decades later rather than during the job. The job has four requirements and they conflict: raising the top of cement fights the fracture gradient, pumping faster helps mud removal and fights the fracture gradient too. This engine computes volumes, placement and centralization, and no chemistry at all.

**Module 2.** Three diameters at every depth, of which two belong to the casing and one to the bore around it. The annulus is a list of capacity rows cut at the section boundaries and the shoe and nowhere else, and the volume between two depths is the overlap sum over those rows. The inside is one capacity, 0.01937743444976952 square metres, because the string is one size. Six inputs are refused by name rather than guessed at.

**Module 3.** Open hole is bigger than the bit and nobody measured how much bigger. The excess multiplies the open hole capacity only, the effective bore is back-solved from the inflated capacity rather than assumed, and the slurry volume is exactly linear in it. At the published 15 percent the washed-out open hole is WIDER than the cased annulus it hangs from, and at zero excess it is narrower. Getting the excess wrong low by 15 points costs 212 m of uncemented annulus.

**Module 4.** Four volumes. The annular slurry between the top of cement and the shoe, spanning two capacities. The shoe track, which is pumped and deliberately never placed, so that the contaminated tail stays inside the casing rather than at the shoe. The lead and tail split, which moves no total and changes the placement completely. And the displacement, measured to the FLOAT COLLAR rather than the shoe, which is more than twice the slurry on both wells.

**Module 5.** Sacks are the slurry over a yield, and the engine uses one yield for a job that may have two slurries. Job time is the total over the rate and is a lower bound on the real one. And the two wells have IDENTICAL slurry volumes and different displacements, because the volume sheet is a function of lengths and diameters and cannot tell a horizontal well from a slant one.

## The numbers to carry

- The inside capacity is 0.01937743444976952 square metres on this casing.
- The cased annulus is 0.013356688045922537 and the bit-size open hole 0.011780948889016823.
- Displacement is measured to the float collar, and the shoe track is the rest.
- Lead plus tail is the slurry volume, whatever the split depth.
- The excess multiplies open hole capacity only, and the slurry is linear in it.

## The one sentence

Every number on the sheet is a measurement except the excess, and the answer is linear in the excess.

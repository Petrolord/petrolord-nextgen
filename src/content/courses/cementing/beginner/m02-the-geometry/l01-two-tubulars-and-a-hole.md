# Two tubulars and a hole

Three diameters at every depth, and only two of them belong to the casing.

{{panel:cm-volume-explorer}}

## The three

**The casing outside diameter.** Fixed for the whole string in this engine. On both of this course's wells it is 0.1778 m, which is 7 inch casing.

**The casing inside diameter.** Also fixed, at 0.1570736 m, which is the 7 inch 29 lb/ft bore.

**The bore around it.** This is the one that changes with depth: previous casing near surface and open hole below the previous shoe.

## The wellbore

Two sections on each well.

On the SLANT well, cased from surface to 1400 m with a bore of 0.2204974 m, which is 9-5/8 inch 47 lb/ft casing, and open hole from 1400 to 3000 m at 0.2159 m, which is an 8-1/2 inch bit.

On the HORIZONTAL well the same two sizes, with the shoe at 1200 m and the open hole running to 2800.

So this course cements the 7 inch production string inside the 9-5/8 inch casing the previous course sized.

## Two annuli, two capacities

    capacity = pi / 4 x (bore squared - casing OD squared)

Cased section: pi/4 times (0.2204974 squared less 0.1778 squared), which is 0.013356688045922537 square metres per metre.

Open hole at the bit size: pi/4 times (0.2159 squared less 0.1778 squared), which is 0.011780948889016823 square metres per metre.

The open hole annulus is NARROWER than the cased one, because an 8-1/2 inch bit drills a smaller hole than the 9-5/8 inch casing it is drilled through.

Module 3 is about what happens to that comparison once the washout is accounted for.

## Inside the casing

One capacity for the whole string, because the casing is one size.

    pi / 4 x 0.1570736 squared = 0.01937743444976952 square metres per metre

Every inside volume in the course is that number times a length.

## Why the outside diameter is a single number

Because this engine cements a full string of one weight and grade. A tapered casing, which the Casing and Tubing course spends a tier on, would need a capacity row per section on the INSIDE as well as the outside, and version one does not do that.

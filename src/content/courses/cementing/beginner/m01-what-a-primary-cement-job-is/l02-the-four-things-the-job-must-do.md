# The four things the job must do

Get the volumes right, get the cement to the top, keep the hole intact, and remove the mud.

## One

**Order the right volumes.** Too little slurry and the top of cement is short of the target, which means an uncemented interval nobody planned. Too much and cement goes somewhere it was not wanted, most often up into a section it is expensive to drill out of.

The volume calculation is arithmetic on cylinder capacities, and the Associate tier is that arithmetic. It has one soft number in it, and the soft number is worth a whole module.

## Two

**Get the cement where it was meant to go.** That is a placement question rather than a volume one: the fluids have to arrive in the right order, at the right depths, and stay there while the plug lands and the floats close.

## Three

**Do not break the hole while doing it.** A column of cement is much heavier than the mud it replaces, and pumping it up an annulus adds friction on top. The equivalent circulating density at the weakest exposed formation rises through the job and peaks near the end of it. If it goes past the fracture gradient, the cement leaves the annulus into the formation and the top of cement never arrives.

## Four

**Remove the mud.** Cement does not scour a channel clean. If the casing is lying against one side of the hole, the cement takes the wide side, where it flows easily, and leaves the narrow side full of mud. That channel is the failure mode that shows up decades later.

## The order they bind in

They are not independent, and the interesting thing about the subject is where they conflict.

Raising the top of cement satisfies the first and makes the third worse, because a taller cement column is a heavier one. Pumping faster helps the fourth and makes the third worse. Pumping slower helps the third and can make the second worse, because a slow job can free fall.

The Professional tier is that conflict quantified.

## What a good job looks like on paper

The volumes reconcile. The simulation puts the cement top where the programme says. The peak circulating density stays under the leak-off value at the shoe above. The standoff stays above the API target. And the checklist that reports all four says so item by item rather than with a single score.

# What a volume sheet hides

Six things that are not on it, and every one of them can lose the job.

## One, whether the cement will be pumpable

The thickening time comes from a laboratory test on the actual blend at the actual bottomhole circulating temperature. A volume sheet has neither, so it cannot tell you whether the slurry it is ordering will still be liquid when it reaches the annulus.

## Two, whether the hole will take it

The volume sheet computes what the annulus holds. It says nothing about whether the formation will hold the pressure of putting it there, which is a placement question and a fracture gradient question.

## Three, whether the mud will get out of the way

The single biggest determinant of whether a cement job isolates anything, and there is no term for it on a volume sheet. A perfectly ordered volume placed around a casing lying on the low side of the hole leaves a mud channel down one side and isolates nothing.

## Four, whether the excess is right

Already a whole module. The slurry volume is linear in a number nobody measured, and the sheet prints it to fifteen significant figures.

## Five, whether the top of cement is where the sheet says

The sheet computes the volume for a TARGET top. Whether the cement arrives there depends on the excess, on losses to the formation, on the plug bumping at the calculated displacement, and on the fluids not intermixing more than the plug-flow model assumes.

The Professional tier's placement simulation reports an ACHIEVED top and warns when the two differ by more than 30 m, which is the check the volume sheet cannot make on itself.

## Six, contamination

The plug-flow model has sharp interfaces. Real interfaces are tens of metres of mixture, and a slurry contaminated with mud can fail to set at all. The volume sheet counts the spacer's volume and knows nothing about what it is for.

## What the sheet IS good for

Ordering. It converts a well geometry into a purchase order and a pumping schedule, reproducibly, and two engineers working from the same geometry get the same sheet.

That is genuinely valuable and it is all it is.

## The framing to carry forward

A volume sheet is a necessary condition and not close to a sufficient one. The next two tiers are the two biggest things it hides: whether the job can be pumped, and whether the mud gets out of the way.

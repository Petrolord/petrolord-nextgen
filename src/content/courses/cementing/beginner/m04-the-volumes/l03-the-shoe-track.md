# The shoe track

Cement that is pumped, paid for, and deliberately never placed.

{{panel:cm-volume-explorer}}

## The volume

    shoe track = inside capacity x (shoe depth - float collar depth)

On both of this course's wells the float collar is 40 m above the shoe, so

    0.01937743444976952 x 40 = 0.7750973779907808 cubic metres

## What it is for

The last cement out of the casing is the most contaminated. It has been in contact with the top plug, with the casing wall the plug wiped, and with whatever mud film was left there.

The shoe track keeps that contaminated tail INSIDE the casing, where it does no harm, instead of putting it in the annulus at the shoe, which is the single most important place for the cement to be good.

## Why the shoe is where it matters most

Because the shoe is where the next hole section starts. The leak-off test that sets the mud weight window for the whole next section is performed against the formation just below the shoe, and it is only as good as the cement seal there.

A poor seal at the shoe gives a low leak-off, which narrows the drilling window for kilometres of hole below.

## And it is drilled out

The shoe track cement sets inside the casing and is drilled out at the start of the next section, along with the float collar, the shoe and the plugs. That drill-out is a real rig operation with its own time and its own way of going wrong.

Which is why 40 m and not 200: every metre of shoe track is a metre of hard cement somebody has to drill.

## The volume it is confused with

    displacement = inside capacity x FLOAT COLLAR depth

Not the shoe depth. The displacement stops at the float collar because the top plug lands there, and the shoe track is exactly the volume between the two.

    inside capacity x shoe depth = displacement + shoe track

On the slant well: 0.01937743444976952 times 3000 is 58.13230334930856, which is 57.357205971317775 plus 0.7750973779907808.

## The job with no shoe track

The engine accepts a float collar exactly at the shoe, which gives a shoe track of zero. That is a real configuration on some small strings, and it means the whole slurry goes to the annulus and the contaminated tail with it.

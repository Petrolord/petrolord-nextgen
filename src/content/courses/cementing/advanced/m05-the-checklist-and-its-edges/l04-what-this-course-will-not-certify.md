# What this course will not certify

The line between what is taught and what is graded.

## The claim

This course teaches several things that no capstone field asks for, deliberately, and the go-live migration asserts it.

## Taught and not graded

**Casing rotation and reciprocation.** Named in two modules as the most effective mud removal technique there is, computed nowhere.

**Mixing and contamination.** The whole reason a spacer exists. Plug flow says it never happens.

**Thickening time and compressive strength.** No chemistry anywhere in the engine.

**Temperature.** No thermal model, so no bottomhole circulating temperature and no comparison against a laboratory schedule.

**Losses to the formation.** No loss model, so the volume balance is always exact.

**Gas migration after placement.** The mechanism behind most sustained casing pressure, and it happens hours after the last number.

**The transient free-fall rate.** Detected, reported, and deliberately not computed.

**Tension times dogleg in the standoff.** The engine's own header says it is out of version one.

**Cement bond evaluation.** How you find out afterwards whether any of this worked.

## Why the distinction

A course that TEACHES a topic says it is worth knowing about.

A course that GRADES it says two further things: that the learner can produce the answer, and that the answer is worth producing.

For every item on that list, at least one of those two is false. Either the engine cannot produce the answer at all, or it can produce a screening version that should not carry a certificate.

## What IS graded, and why each earns it

**Six volumes**, which are exact cylinder arithmetic on stated lengths and diameters, reproduced by an independent oracle.

**Three placement results and the two rate-window edges**, which come from a deterministic plug-flow calculation and two bisections, both reproducible to the last bit.

**Six centralization numbers**, which are closed-form API 10D algebra plus one bisection.

Eighteen quantities, all exact given their inputs, none of them a correlation and none of them a score.

## The assertion

The go-live migration for this course refuses to run if any capstone field name matches a pattern from the not-graded list: rotation, reciprocation, contamination, mixing, thickening, compressive strength, temperature, losses, gas migration, bond, or a free-fall rate.

A scope decision written as a check, so that a later edit which quietly added a thickening-time field is stopped rather than reviewed.

## The honest summary of the whole course

It computes what to order, where it goes, what pressure that takes, and whether the pipe is centred enough for the mud to get out of the way.

It does not compute whether the cement sets, whether it bonds, whether the mud actually left, or whether the well is isolated. Those are the four things that decide whether a cement job worked.

## Exercise

Take the nine items on the not-graded list.

For each, say which of the two claims fails: that the learner could produce the answer, or that the answer is worth producing.

# The story so far

Five modules, one line of arithmetic, and a knob with two constraints on it.

## The claim

Placement is volume bookkeeping on a single coordinate, and the pressure it produces is three terms and a floor at zero.

## What each module established

**Module 1.** The flow path is parametrised by PUMPED VOLUME, not by depth, because depth is not single valued along a U. Every front advances by exactly the volume pumped, wherever it is. The fronts partition the path into intervals whose lengths always sum to the path length, and two different maps convert volume back into measured depth, the annulus one running backwards from the shoe. Plug flow buys exactness and reproducibility and costs the entire subject of intermixing.

**Module 2.** Pump pressure is the maximum of zero and the annulus head plus friction less the inside head. The heads use the exact minimum-curvature true vertical depths and the friction imports the same loss kernel the hydraulics course uses, once per segment, at a velocity that changes with the annular capacity. A fluid with no rheology contributes no friction, which is a feature the vertical fixture uses and a trap on a real run. The free-fall test carries a one pascal deadband, which is a hundred thousand times smaller than any real deficit and a billion times larger than the rounding.

**Module 3.** The pressure falls by a factor of nine and then rises by a factor of twenty three, and it MUST, because cement going down the inside raises the inside head and cement coming up the annulus raises the annular one. The ECD at the previous shoe is flat for two thirds of the job and peaks at the last step, because nothing above that shoe changes until the end. The ECD at the CASING shoe on the horizontal well is 1837.9218199066345 kg/m3 before anything has been pumped, because it divides the friction of a 1600 m lateral by 1214.859173174059 m of true vertical depth.

**Module 4.** Two programmes place the same slurry to the same top and differ on every pressure in the job, and the neat one is worse on all of them. On the horizontal well the lead slurry is the difference between free falling and not. The rate is a straight trade: friction cures free fall and raises the circulating density, so one knob is pulled two ways. Both edges are bisected rather than swept.

**Module 5.** The achieved top of cement is the shallowest LEAD OR TAIL segment, and the spacer is deliberately not counted. Six warnings, each naming its own numbers. Seven things the simulation cannot see, of which mixing and casing movement are the two biggest levers a cementer has. And 61 steps, which found the horizontal well's free-fall dip at exactly one sample and understated its depth by six percent.

## The numbers to carry

- Pump pressure is the maximum of zero and annulus head plus friction less inside head.
- The inside head HELPS. It is a U-tube.
- The pressure falls first and rises later, always, and a job that does not is a job to stop.
- The ECD at the previous shoe peaks at the plug bump.
- The free-fall deadband is one pascal.

## The one sentence

One knob, two constraints, and whether a window exists between them is a property of the programme rather than of the driller.

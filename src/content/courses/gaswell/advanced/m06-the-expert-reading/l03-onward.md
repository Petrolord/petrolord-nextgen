# Onward

A loading verdict is a small object with a long reach. Be precise about what it earns and where it hands over.

## What it earns

It turns a gas rate and a station into a statement a gauge can refute: a critical velocity, a critical rate and one ratio that decides. It prices a remedy in the same currency, because tubing enters the calculation only through the flow area, which is why a velocity string works at all and why the pick moves by 0.264 in when the station moves on the teaching well EBOCHA-5. And it will say no in a form you can act on, returning `largestUnloaded` as null rather than the least bad candidate when nothing on the list can carry the rate.

## Where it hands over

There is no inflow performance anywhere in these modules. The gas rate is an input, so every verdict is a verdict at a rate somebody supplied and not a prediction of what the well does next. The traverse is passed in, station by station, with its own pressure, temperature and compressibility, and nothing here solves multiphase flow or invents a gradient.

The droplet model hands over sooner than most people expect. It carries one droplet at its terminal velocity, with a drag coefficient of 0.44 for a rigid sphere in the Newton regime, and it does not model a droplet population, coalescence, break-up in transit, or the film flowing on the tubing wall, which is the other way a gas well carries liquid. Interfacial tension and liquid density are inputs, not functions of anything the module knows.

The plunger balance is static: no velocity, no slippage past the plunger, no fallback of the slug, and no friction unless it is handed in.

## The limit worth remembering

A remedy that clears every check can still be the wrong remedy. On the teaching well OGUTA-2 a cycle carries 14.83375148 bbl/d against a well that makes 194.91525424 bbl/d, and no flag in the screen is built from that comparison.

## What you have finished

Which station controls, what the correlation choice is worth, what a sizing discarded, which constants are conventions, and where a returned number stops meaning what its name says. Read the station before the number, and report the margin beside every verdict.

# The story so far

Every number in a deliquification study is a measurement, a convention or an artefact of where somebody stood. This tier is the work of sorting them.

## Where somebody stood

`recommendCorrelation` takes one pressure and returns guidance. Ask it at the wellhead of the teaching well EBOCHA-5, 880.0 psia, and it returns coleman. Ask it at the 7500.0 ft shoe, 1500.0 psia, and it returns turner. The answer from the gauge is used at every station, including the one that controls.

The two correlations share a droplet balance and differ by an adjustment of 1.200000 against 1.000000, so the terminal velocity is identical and the choice is worth 1.200000000000 on every critical rate after it. On that well it moves the sizing pick from 3.476 in to 3.068 in. Move the station instead and the Coleman pick moves from 3.476 in to 3.740 in, worth 0.264 in of tubing, while the returned object records the correlation on every row and the depth nowhere.

## The conventions

`PSI_PER_FT_SG` is 0.4330000000000 psi/ft per unit specific gravity where the exact water gradient is 0.4335275040010, a fixed 0.1216771707 percent of every slug it touches. The gate pins the constant and loosens one assertion to a relative tolerance of 5e-3, which is 4.109234 times the disagreement it is covering. In the same domain, `gasWellLoading.AIR_MW` is 28.9647 and `gasProperties.AIR_MW` is 28.9625, 75.9603 parts per million apart, and the two modules take temperature in different units at the door.

Neither changes a decision. Both change a number, and a number two modules disagree about is a convention, not a measurement.

## The artefacts

On the teaching well OGUTA-2 the required gas-liquid ratio falls from 11338.72941173 scf/bbl at 900.0 psia of casing to 3339.72840586 at 90.0 psia, 70.545832 percent, and `glrOk` turns from false to true as the well dies. Part of that fall is real expansion and part is an average taken across a crossing that should have stopped it, and nothing in the return marks the boundary. On the same well `maxSlugLengthFt` returns 0.00000000 ft at 130.0 psia while the balance with no slug at all is short by 45.3101604102 psi.

## What nobody checks

The cycle on that well carries 14.83375148 bbl/d against a well liquid make of 194.91525424 bbl/d, 13.13998380 times, and `feasible` is built from a pressure flag and a gas flag and nothing else.

## The habit to carry out

Ask of every number where it was read, which constant it rests on, and whether the function that produced it had a way to refuse. Most of the findings in this tier are visible from the returned object alone, once a returned value and a computed answer stop being the same thing to you.

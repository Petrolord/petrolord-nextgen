## More gas

The same well and the same string, at rates it might reach.

At 3000.0 Mscf/d the margin is -6.907853 percent and the shallowest loading station is 6000.0 ft. At 3100.0 Mscf/d the margin is -3.804781 percent and the shallowest loading station is still 6000.0 ft. At 3200.0 Mscf/d it is -0.701710 percent and only the 7500.0 ft shoe is loading. At 3450.0 Mscf/d the margin is 7.055969 percent and no station loads at all. At 3700.0 Mscf/d it is 14.813648 percent and at 4000.0 Mscf/d, 24.122863 percent.

Rate buys the whole hole at once, because it lifts every ratio in the column.

## Lower surface pressure

The critical rate a station demands falls with its pressure. Through 2.441 in at 540.0 degR the published water rows give 2496.154595078 Mscf/d at 2500.0 psia, 1614.343188395 Mscf/d at 1000.0 psia and 892.895047041 Mscf/d at 300.0 psia. The bar comes down as the pressure does.

## A plunger

It buys a different mechanism rather than a better ratio. On OGUTA-2 the cycle carries 0.9261160790 bbl a trip at 16.01716223 trips a day, so 14.83375148 bbl/d comes up the hole whatever the gas is doing, and the casing has 471.8102677127 psi more than the 248.1897322873 psia the lift needs. That is liquid delivered on a timer rather than by a velocity, so it does not care what any ratio in the column reads.

## The mistake

Reading a ratio of 1.0022156322 as a comfortable result. It clears one in the third decimal, at one station, at one rate, under one correlation. The 3.068 in candidate at 1.2865006128 is the one that survives a bad assumption, and the sizing will not recommend it because it is not the largest that clears.

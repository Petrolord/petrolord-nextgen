# Where the crossing sits

The profile brackets the crossing. It never locates it, and the difference matters when somebody asks how much of the string is drowning.

{{panel:pd-profile-explorer}}

## The bracket on EBOCHA-5

At 3100.0 Mscf/d under Coleman the deepest healthy station is 4500.0 ft at a ratio of 1.0340528848, and the shallowest loading station is 6000.0 ft at 0.9979085215. The crossing sits between those two. That places it inside the deepest 40.0000 percent of the 7500.0 ft string and outside the deepest 20.0000 percent.

Those two percentages are the answer. Not a depth, a bracket, and the bracket is 1500.0 ft wide because the survey was cut at 1500.0 ft spacing.

## Why a fraction of the string is the useful form

The ratio falls steadily per interval: 0.0420944780, then 0.0423035811, then 0.0421094894, then 0.0361443633, then 0.0359563360 across the five intervals from surface to shoe. The decrement steps down once, between 3000.0 ft and 4500.0 ft, and that step is above the crossing rather than at it. Nothing about the crossing is special. It is where a steady decline passes one, so what a report can honestly say is which fraction of the 3.548 in string sits below it. On EBOCHA-5 that fraction is between a fifth and two fifths.

Between 2700.0 and 3000.0 Mscf/d the reported crossing jumps from 1500.0 ft to 6000.0 ft. It skipped 3000.0 ft and 4500.0 ft because those stations went from loading to healthy inside one rate step, and the profile has nothing to report between the rows it was given.

Between 3000.0 and 3100.0 Mscf/d it does not move at all while the margin walks from -6.907853 to -3.804781 percent. The crossing is a coarse instrument and the margin is a fine one.

## The mistake

Quoting the shallowest loading station as the depth liquid starts falling back. It is the shallowest surveyed station that read below one. The crossing itself is somewhere in 1500.0 ft of hole nobody measured, and 6000.0 ft is the label the survey allows.

## What it refuses

The profile refuses to interpolate. It will not fit those five decrements and solve for the depth where the fit crosses one, and it should not, because the decrements come from stations that were supplied rather than solved.

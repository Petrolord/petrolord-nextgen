# The capstone brief

{{panel:gsa-contract-calculator}}

The Expert capstone grades the question this tier asked: a whole gas sales agreement read from its price formula to its present value. It is worked in the course's own contract calculator, which calls the same vendored engine every lesson has quoted. There is no Suite app for this course: the calculator is the practical.

## What you receive

The capstone card carries one case file for a synthetic agreement of its own, with its own buyer, quantities, index series and terms. The file is a priced case: a price block (the monthly index, the oil-indexed formula with its S-curve, the averaging, lag, reset, rounding and any reopeners), a pricing block saying which annual price the ledger uses and the make-up price, a contract block (the years, the take-or-pay percentage, the make-up terms and the carry-forward terms), and the royalty terrain and share, the discount rate and the base year. Every term a graded figure depends on is stated in the file.

## What you report

Six quantities, each to six decimals, and the card names each exactly. They are the kinds of figure this tier has read: a monthly contract price, an annual average price, a carry-forward credit in one year, the refund at the end of the term, the NPV of the seller revenue, and the gas royalty of one year. None depends on a reading the engine states: each was proved the same under every reading and under the alternative the engine names. None uses the domestic base price.

## How to work it

1. Copy the case's price block into "Prices on an S-curve". That view reads priceSeries inputs as they stand, so paste the price block alone; the whole file is refused there by its first unknown key. Read the monthly prices, their segments and the annual rows.
2. Paste the whole case file into "The whole contract in money". The view prices the ledger from the price block by the stated basis and runs the contract.
3. Read the ledger table for the carry-forward credit and the refund, the money table for the royalty, and the NPV tile for the present value.
4. Read every reason under the tables, and every refusal first if one appears; the case as issued runs.

## How it is graded

Each field is compared with the engine's own return on the issued case, within a tolerance set once for the whole course. Report the figure the calculator prints, at six decimals, with no rounding of your own.

## Exercise

Rehearse the method before opening the card. In "Prices on an S-curve", which starts on Figure 51, read the price of 2026-03, its segment and the 2026 annual average. Then open "The whole contract in money", which starts on the Ekene export feed (synthetic), and read the 2032 carry-forward credit, the 2036 refund, the 2029 royalty and the NPV of the seller revenue. For each figure, name the term in the contract that sets it and the lesson of this tier that taught it. The rehearsal agreement is no capstone: its figures are printed in the lessons, and the card's are not.

# The export feed ledger

{{panel:gsa-ledger-calculator}}

The export feed agreement puts every term of this tier into one ledger: make-up in a stated order, carry-forward with a cap, buyer force majeure, expiry of credits and a refund at the end of the term. This lesson reads it year by year.

## The terms

The Ekene export feed agreement is synthetic: 2027 to 2036, DCQ 63000.000000 MMBtu per day, take-or-pay 90 percent, make-up for 5 contract years taken only after the year's take-or-pay quantity, refunded at the end of the term; carry-forward of takes above the take-or-pay quantity for 3 contract years, at most 50 percent of a year's deficiency. Each year's contract and take-or-pay price is the annual average of the monthly prices of its oil-indexed formula (the next module), and make-up gas is invoiced at 10 percent of that price.

## The years that matter

2027 and 2028 build surplus above the take-or-pay quantity. In 2029 a train outage leaves a deficiency of 6438500.000000; the cap lets the credit cover half, 3219250.000000, drawn first in first out as 2299500 from 2027 and 919750 from 2028, and the other half is paid at the 2029 take-or-pay price of 9.808450. The paid half opens a make-up entry of 3219250.000000. The engine's reason carries the whole year:

> 2029: 14257000 counted against the take-or-pay quantity 20695500 leaves a deficiency of 6438500; a carry-forward credit of 3219250 (at most 50% of the deficiency, first in first out: 2299500 from 2027, 919750 from 2028) leaves 3219250; the deficiency payment is 3219250 x 9.80845 = 31575852.6625; the buyer may make up 3219250 in the 5 contract years after 2029, to the end of 2034

In 2030 the buyer declares force majeure of 1260000.000000, which lowers the Adjusted ACQ and so the take-or-pay quantity to 19561500.000000. The buyer takes 1823500.000000 of make-up above it, and in 2031 the last 1395750.000000 of the 2029 entry. 2032 and 2035 repeat the pattern at a smaller scale, each deficiency half covered by credit. In 2036, the last year, 691800.000000 of the 2035 entry is taken and the rest is refunded at the 2036 take-or-pay price.

Work in the course's own ledger calculator, on the view "The take-or-pay ledger". It starts with the power plant, so build the export ledger yourself.

1. Write ten entries of `years` from the two tables above: `year`, `acq`, `taken`, `forceMajeure` in 2030, and `contractPrice`, `topPrice` and `makeUpPrice`. Add `topPct` 90, `makeUp` with `periodYears` 5, `order` "after-top-quantity" and `endOfTerm` "refund", and `carryForward` with `periodYears` 3, `base` "top-quantity" and `capPct` 50.
2. Check the 2029 row, the four total tiles and the make-up account.
3. Set `capPct` to 100 and write the 2029 deficiency payment and the make-up entry it opens.
4. Restore `capPct` and set every `makeUpPrice` to 0. Write which totals move and which do not, and say why the deficiency payments stay put.

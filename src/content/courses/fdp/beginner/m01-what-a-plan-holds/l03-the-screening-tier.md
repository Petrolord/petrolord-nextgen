# The screening tier

Screening economics run through the sanctioned engine on one price deck and one production shape, post royalty and tax, discounted mid year. The terms are stated defaults, and the answer is a screening answer.

{{panel:ec-plan-explorer}}

## The four default terms

| term | default |
| --- | --- |
| royalty | 12.5000 percent |
| tax | 30.0000 percent |
| discount | 10.0000 percent |
| variable operating cost | 5.0000 USD a barrel |

Those four sit behind every scenario the studio prices unless the scenario overrides them, and the discounting is mid year. Mid year treats a year of production as arriving in the middle of the year rather than at its end, which is closer to how a producing field actually earns, and it gives a slightly higher present value than year end discounting on the same cash flow. The variable operating cost of 5.0000 USD a barrel rides with the barrels, so it falls as production declines. A reader who quotes an NPV from this tier without quoting the terms has quoted half a number.

## Where the money goes

Take the EGINA Base case at 70.0000 USD a barrel on the FPSO concept. Gross revenue over the life is 16095.0492 million USD. Royalty takes 2011.8812 and tax takes 3310.0565, so government take is 5321.9377 million USD, which is 0.330657 of gross revenue. Capex is 2250.0000 and operating cost over the life is 3049.6464. What survives all of that, discounted at 10.0000 percent, is an NPV of 2047.5653 million USD with an IRR of 29.5998 percent and payback in 3.8273 years.

The deepest the cash position ever gets is -2250.0000 million USD, which is year 0, the year that carries the capex and no production at all.

## A price of zero is still a price somebody typed

The tier prices what it is given. A scenario priced at zero is accepted, because zero is a number somebody entered, and the result is an NPV of -3517.4133 million USD. The engine refuses a missing oil price and prices a zero one, and the difference between those two cases is the difference between a gap and a decision.

## What the tier is not

This is not a sanction case. There is one price deck, one shape and one set of fiscal terms, so the tier can tell you whether a concept is worth carrying further and cannot tell you what to sign. Full fiscal detail under the PIA and the Nigeria Tax Act belongs to Petroleum Economics Studio. Nothing here models ring fencing, uplift, cost recovery pools or a tax holiday, and nothing here says how likely 70.0000 USD a barrel is.

## The mistake

The mistake is to carry a screening NPV into a room where a sanction number is wanted. The second mistake is quieter: reading 2047.5653 million USD as though it were robust because it has four decimals. It is one deck at one price on one shape, and at 18.0000 USD a barrel the same concept is worth -1797.2732 million USD.

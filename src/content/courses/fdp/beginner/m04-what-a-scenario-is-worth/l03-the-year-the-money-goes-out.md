# The year the money goes out

A screening case is a list of years, and the first of them carries the whole capex of 2250.0000 million USD and not one barrel of production.

The profile has 21 rows in all. Year 0 is the build: the money goes out, the field produces nothing, and the cumulative position opens at -2250.0000, which is also the deepest cash position the case ever reaches. From year 1 the capex column is 0.0000 for the rest of the life, and every later row is revenue against royalty, operating cost and tax.

## Plateau, then decline

Years 1, 2 and 3 all earn 1533.0000 of gross revenue, because the concept's screening shape holds the peak of 60.0000 kbpd for three years. Year 4 is year 3 multiplied by 0.900000, which takes the rate to 54.0000 kbpd and the revenue to 1379.7000. Every year after it declines by the same factor, down to 10.0063 kbpd at the end of the 20 year life. Operating cost falls with the barrels, from 204.5000 in year 1 to 193.5500 in year 4, because part of it is charged on each barrel produced.

## Why the order matters

Discounting punishes late money, so a plan that spends first and earns later is worth less than the same totals in the other order. That ordering is also what makes the other two headline figures mean anything. A rate of return exists only where the cash flow changes sign, and payback can only be measured because there is a hole to climb out of. At 18.0000 USD a barrel the same concept never climbs out: the NPV is -1797.2732, the payback is never, and the rate of return has status no-root because no rate makes that flow worth zero. Timing is the whole reason those two figures are reportable at all, and a case whose years never change sign has neither of them.

## The mistake

Reading the 0.0000 revenue in year 0 as missing data and typing something into it. It is the answer: sanction to first oil takes 36 months on this concept, and the studio puts the whole capex in the year before production starts. The related error is to read the deepest cash position of -2250.0000 as a loss. It is the maximum the plan is out of pocket at any moment, and on this case it turns into a gain of 137.4375 by the fourth row.

## What it refuses

The shape is a screening curve, a plateau and a decline drawn from a peak rate and a life. It is not a reservoir forecast and it knows nothing about drawdown, water or pressure. Nothing in these rows was read from a clock: every year is dated from the concept's own start.

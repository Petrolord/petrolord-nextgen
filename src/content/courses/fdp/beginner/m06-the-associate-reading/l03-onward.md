# Onward

The Associate tier is the plan and its money. The Professional tier is the same plan in time and in space, where the money turns into days, wells, equipment and things that can go wrong.

## The schedule stops being a list

Eight activities with dependencies typed between them make a network, and the method walks it forward and backward to find out how long the work must take. On the teaching field that is 870 days, along a critical path of a1, a3, a4, a7 and a8. The activities off that path carry float: 180 days on detailed engineering, 180 on topsides fabrication and 480 on subsea installation. Before the repair that shipped with this course, every one of them read critical.

## Dates answer a different question

The dates typed on those same activities span 933 days against a network of 870, and the 63 days between them is float already spent in the calendar. Days are counted whole, from the dates in the plan.

## Wells and facilities have prices

A rig day is the unit of a drilling campaign. At 310000 USD a day with services at 1.5 times the rig cost, four wells take 182 rig days and cost 141050000 USD, and the same four wells at 250000 USD a day cost 113750000. Facilities price by size, and not in proportion: 60000 to 150000 bopd is a factor of 2.500000 in nameplate, 1.899144 in capex and 1.732862 in operating cost.

## Risk gets one scale

One scale, four bands, and a register that reads Critical 1, High 1, Medium 1, Low 1 and Unscored 1. Health is 58 with the unscored risk and 58 without it, because a risk nobody has scored neither helps nor hurts. Exposure is 269.0000 million USD, of which the largest single contribution is 108.0000 from the schedule risk.

## What stays true

Every habit from this tier carries forward. Numbers come from the field they were typed in, units travel with their figures, and an engine that cannot answer says so instead of producing something that looks like an answer. The Expert tier then turns the same discipline on the value itself, and on measuring a project against its own plan at a stated date.

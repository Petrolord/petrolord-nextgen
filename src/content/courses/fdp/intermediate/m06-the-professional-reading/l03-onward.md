# Onward

A plan that holds together in time and space still has to survive a reader hunting for the number that does not fit. That is the work the Expert tier takes up, and it begins with the measure a plan is judged by most often and trusted with least care.

## The rate that may not exist

A rate of return exists only when a cash flow changes sign, and it is not unique when the flow changes sign more than once. The engine searches between -99 and 1000 percent and reports a rate only when it verifies a root inside that band. Otherwise it returns no rate and a status: no-sign-change, above-clamp, multiple-roots, no-root or ok. On the teaching field 18.0000 USD a barrel gives a net present value of -1797.2732 and a status of no-root, and 30.0000 USD a barrel gives -898.3507 and multiple-roots. A clamped search once reported 1000 percent here, in green.

## What a sensitivity is worth

Moving one driver at a time by 30 percent gives a swing of 3092.0051 on oil price, 2839.5965 on production, 1287.1745 on capex and 323.8828 on operating cost. The ranking and the widths are real. What is absent is any statement of how likely a 30 percent move is.

## Progress measured to a date

Earned value only means something against a stated as-of date. At 2028-12-31 a teaching project carries a planned value of 10178668, an earned value of 8360000, a schedule index of 0.821326 and a cost index of 1.027027 against a budget at completion of 32000000. The completion ratio at that same date is 0.261250, and it is progress against the whole budget rather than a schedule index. A real schedule index can read above one, 1.795082 in one published case, which the ratio never could.

## Three estimates of one cost

The last discipline is reading a plan against itself. A concept's capex of 2250.0000 million USD, a cost ledger totalling 2250.0000 million USD and a facility screening estimate of 1363.3524 million USD are three different estimates of overlapping things. On the schedule the same test compares 870 days of work against a 933 day window, a difference of 63 days. A plan where these agree has been reconciled, and one where they differ has a question in it that somebody has to answer before sanction.

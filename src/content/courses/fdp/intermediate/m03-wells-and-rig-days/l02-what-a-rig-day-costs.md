# What a rig day costs

EGINA's plan carries a rig rate of 310000 USD a day, and the well cost the engine returns is more than the days multiplied by that rate, because services are charged at 1.5 times the rig.

{{panel:ec-schedule-explorer}}

## Two numbers price a well

The rate is the rig alone. Services around it are billed at 1.5 times the rig cost, so every day of drilling bills the rig once and its services at one and a half times over. Both numbers come from the plan, and the engine reads the rate the plan carries rather than one of its own.

## The campaign, well by well

| well | days | cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EG-01 | 61 | 47275000 USD |
| EG-02 | 45 | 34875000 USD |
| EG-03 | 42 | 32550000 USD |
| EG-04 | 34 | 26350000 USD |

61 plus 45 plus 42 plus 34 is 182 rig days. 47275000 plus 34875000 plus 32550000 plus 26350000 is 141050000 USD, which is 141.0500 million USD for the four well campaign.

## Cost is days, priced

Nothing in the cost column responds to anything except the day count and the two pricing numbers. EG-03 at 10800 ft costs 32550000 and EG-02 at 11600 ft costs 34875000, and the ordering of the costs is the ordering of the days, because every day bills identically.

The same holds across trajectories. 12000 ft vertical is 40 days and 31000000 USD, deviated is 46 days and 35650000 USD, horizontal is 53 days and 41075000 USD. Depth, trajectory and complexity settle the days. The rate and the service multiple settle what a day is worth. The cost is the product of the two halves and holds no other information.

## What this figure is and is not

It is a drilling estimate from depth, trajectory and complexity at a stated day rate. It is not an authorisation for expenditure built line by line, and it carries no completion detail, no contingency and no standby.

The plan's own cost item for development drilling reads 520.0000 million USD, and the four well campaign comes to 141.0500 million USD. Those are two estimates of overlapping things arrived at two different ways, and a difference between them is a question to ask rather than an error to correct.

## The mistake

Multiplying days by the rig rate and stopping there. 182 rig days at 310000 USD a day is not the campaign cost, because the services are 1.5 times the rig on top of it, and a campaign priced without them understates every well by the same wide proportion.

The second mistake is reading a well cost as precision. 47275000 USD is printed to the unit and it is a day count multiplied by a rate. Every digit after the first few is the arithmetic being tidy, and the uncertainty in the answer lives in whether EG-01 really takes 61 days.

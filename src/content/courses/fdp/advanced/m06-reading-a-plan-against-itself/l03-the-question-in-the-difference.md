# The question in the difference

A plan is a set of numbers that must agree with each other, and the work of planning is knowing which number each answer came from, what it was measured against, and when the honest answer is that there is none.

The economics difference is 0.0000, and it is zero only because the plan was costed against the concept it is running. A difference of zero earned by reconciliation is a finding. A difference of zero assumed in advance is nothing at all.

## Differences that are not errors

The facility screening estimate of 1363.3524 million USD sits beside the concept's facilities field of 1350.0000 million USD because one is a class 5 figure from a type and a nameplate. Oil and gas never reconcile at all: EGINA's oil is 130.0000 MMbbl at P50 and its gas is 70.0000 Bcf, and adding them gives 200.0000 of nothing. A schedule index of 0.821326 and a completion ratio of 0.261250 on the same date are both correct, because one divides by a planned value that moved with the date and the other by a budget at completion that never moves.

## When the answer is that there is none

The strongest reading a plan gives is sometimes a refusal. At 18.0000 USD a barrel the FPSO scenario returns an NPV of -1834.1220 million USD, no rate of return at all with a status of no-root, and a payback of never. A search that clamped at its own boundary used to print 1000 percent here, in green.

The same discipline runs through the risk register, where an unscored risk carrying a cost impact of 400.0000 million USD contributes 0.0000 to an exposure of 269.0000 million USD. It runs through earned value, where a project with no costed task reports no index rather than an invented ratio of one labelled as under budget.

## The habit

Three questions turn a set of outputs into a plan that has been read. Which number is this. What was it measured against. Does another number in the same plan answer the same question, and if it does, do they agree.

A screening NPV is not a sanction case. The tier here is screening, mid-year discounted, on default terms of royalty 12.5000 percent, tax 30.0000 percent, discount 10.0000 percent and variable operating cost 5.0000 USD a barrel. Knowing that is itself an answer to the second question.

## The mistake

The mistake is reconciling by deletion: two numbers disagree, so one of them is dropped and the plan reads clean. The disagreement was the information. The 63 days between 870 and 933 is the most useful number in EGINA's schedule precisely because nobody planned it.

# Three estimates of one cost

EGINA carries three estimates of what it costs to build: the concept's capex of 2250.0000 million USD, the cost items' CAPEX total of 2250.0000 million USD, and a facility screening estimate of 1363.3524 million USD for the FPSO alone.

{{panel:ec-value-explorer}}

## Three numbers, three sources

| estimate | figure | where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| concept capex | 2250.0000 | drilling 520.0000, facilities 1350.0000, subsea 380.0000 |
| cost items CAPEX | 2250.0000 | the plan's own budget lines |
| facility screening | 1363.3524 | the FPSO's type and nameplate of 60000 bopd |

The concept's figure is what the development is estimated to cost, held in three fields the engine reads together. The cost items' total is what the plan has budgeted line by line. The facility screening estimate is a class 5 figure the studio computes from type and size, and it covers one facility.

## Reconciling the first two

The cost items are Development drilling 520.0000, FPSO hull and topsides 1180.0000, Mooring and installation 170.0000 and Subsea system 380.0000. Development drilling matches the concept's drilling field of 520.0000 and Subsea system matches its subsea field of 380.0000. The hull and the mooring add to 1350.0000, which matches the facilities field. By phase the same money reads Execution 520.0000, Construction 1180.0000 and Installation 550.0000.

The two totals agree at 2250.0000 because this plan was costed against the concept it is running. Agreement is a result, not a guarantee. They remain two different numbers gathered by two different people for two different purposes, and the point of showing both is that a plan where they differ has a question in it.

## The third is not a budget

The screening estimate of 1363.3524 is the one that gets misread. It belongs beside the concept's facilities field of 1350.0000 and beside the hull and mooring lines that make 1350.0000. It does not belong beside 2250.0000, which also carries drilling and subsea. A reader who sets 1363.3524 against 2250.0000 concludes the plan is heavily over-estimated, and has simply compared a facility with a field development.

It also carries something the CAPEX total does not. The same screening gives the FPSO a decommissioning figure of 204.5029, which is 0.150000 of the capex it carries.

## What sits outside every total

The plan's cost items include a Decommissioning provision of 260.0000 typed as ABEX. The engine's CAPEX total is 2250.0000 and its OPEX total is 95.0000 a year, and the ABEX line is in neither, because it is neither development capex nor an annual operating cost. It is the plan's end-of-life cost, and the case charges it in the final production year, so a line can sit outside both totals and still reach the value the plan reports.

## The mistake

The mistake is quoting whichever of the three is nearest to hand. Each is defensible and they answer different questions, so a capex figure in a document is incomplete until it says which of the three it is. The second mistake is taking a screening estimate for a costed number: 1363.3524 came from a type and a nameplate, and no one has priced a hull at that figure.

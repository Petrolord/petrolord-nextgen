# The gravity limb

The falling half of the J. It is where the pressure looks best and the well is least safe, and those are the same fact.

{{panel:pd-vlp-explorer}}

## The term

The weight of the fluid standing in the tubing. At zero rate it is the whole charge above the wellhead pressure, the dead column.

| Well | Wellhead pressure, psia | Gravity constant, psi | Dead column, psia | Reservoir pressure, psia |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BONNY-7 | 420 | 2150 | 2570 | 2740 |

The form is the gravity constant over one plus the rate over a lightening constant: bounded, and halved at a rate equal to that constant. BONNY-7's is 375 stb/d and FORCADOS-3's 820 stb/d, which is most of the difference between the two left limbs.

## Why this half is dangerous

Adding rate REDUCES the pressure the tubing needs: positive feedback with no restoring force. A small fall in rate raises the demand, which suppresses the rate further, until the column loads up and the well dies. It looks attractive, BONNY-7 asking 1508.355629 psia at 484.34 stb/d against 2545.501142 at its loaded end.

At the far left the demand is the dead column, and against reservoir pressure that settles whether the well starts itself. BONNY-7 stands 170 psi below, so the digest's verdict is that the column does not outweigh the reservoir at low rate. FORCADOS-3 stands 590 psi above: yes.

## The dry gas exception

Nothing in Cullender and Smith lightens with rate, so a dry gas well has no gravity limb. The published golden curve rises from 952.986300 psia at 13.2893 Mscf/d to 1842.190804 psia at 13289.2963 Mscf/d, and its reported minimum is the first row. Any bend in a real gas well's outflow is liquid coming out of the pipe.

## The mistake, and one refusal

The lightening is a shape, not a mechanism, and it decays towards zero where physics does not: BONNY-7's 171.562832 psi at 4324.44 stb/d, off 2150 psi of dead weight, is lighter than any real string.

The mistake is reading a low demand as good news without asking which limb made it. BONNY-7 asks 2545.501142 psia at 4.32 stb/d and 2198.309991 at 1444.36 stb/d, similar heights on a chart. The first is 2125.489174 psi of dead weight, share 0.99999437. The second is 443.149256 psi against 1335.160735 of friction, share 0.24919685. One is an hour from dead; the other pushes back.

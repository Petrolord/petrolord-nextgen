# The stable window

Between the two crossings the residual is negative and the well is pushed upwards. That interval has a width worth reporting.

{{panel:pd-node-explorer}}

## The definition

FORCADOS-3's window runs from its unstable heading crossing at 234.488087 stb/d to its operating point at 2125.009203 stb/d: a stable window width of 1890.521117 stb/d, a pressure span of 1203.919667 psi, a fraction 0.45709481 of its absolute open flow of 4135.949669 stb/d.

## Three readings

The bottom is a threshold the well must be brought above. The interval is a basin: displace the well anywhere inside it and it returns to 2125.009203 stb/d. The width is a margin.

Depth is a fourth number, not the same as width. FORCADOS-3's minimum residual is -509.628610 psi at 985.078572 stb/d. Width says how far the well can be displaced; depth says how hard it is pushed back.

BONNY-7 has no window. Its residual is -192.336636 psi at 4.324444 stb/d, reaches -989.578610 psi at 477.119848 stb/d, and never turns positive below its crossing.

## Watching it close

| Wellhead pressure, psia | Dead column, psia | Operating rate, stb/d | Window, stb/d |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 860 | 4210 | 2246.821833 | 2064.445505 |
| 960 | 4310 | 2125.009203 | 1890.521117 |
| 1060 | 4410 | 1990.931611 | 1695.613297 |
| 1160 | 4510 | 1842.012114 | 1473.513228 |

The window degrades faster than the rate. A well that has lost a modest fraction of its rate may have lost a far larger fraction of its resilience.

## The confusion worth clearing

A wide window is not a comfortable margin at the operating point.

FORCADOS-3's window is 1890.521117 stb/d wide, and its operating point sits only 18.717814 psi above its tubing minimum pressure of 2348.191408 psia and 281.389786 stb/d right of its minimum rate of 1843.619418 stb/d.

BONNY-7 is the reverse: no window, and an operating point 585.899719 psi above a tubing minimum of 1476.243252 psia and 728.644315 stb/d right of 627.069742 stb/d.

One measures displacement along the rate axis, the other position on the tubing curve. Reporting either as the other is a real error, and it goes both ways.

## What sets the width

The reservoir side narrows it. FORCADOS-3's inflow slope is -0.63615646 psi per stb/d up to 1861 stb/d, then -0.90914074 at 3102 stb/d and -2.41200162 at 4012 stb/d. The faster the inflow falls, the sooner the residual returns. A well already producing near its open flow has a steep inflow and little window.

## What it does not promise

Not that the well is inside it now, not that it can reach it, and not that it survives a shut-in, which takes the rate outside every window there is.

Nor that it sits where the model says. FORCADOS-3's inflow was fitted from a test at 2180 psia taken below its bubble point of 2450 psia, the corrupted comparison: a straight line on that same test reads an open flow of 5797.402597 stb/d against the composite's 4135.949669 stb/d.

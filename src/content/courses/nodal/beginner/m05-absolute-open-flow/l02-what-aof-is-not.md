# What AOF is not

It is the rate at zero flowing pressure. That is a boundary condition, not an operating point.

{{panel:pd-ipr-explorer}}

## Nobody has produced at 0 psia

Zero flowing bottomhole pressure means an absolute vacuum at the sandface with a column of fluid standing above it. A boundary condition need not be attainable to be useful; it anchors the far end of the curve. The mistake is reading it as a rate a facility could be sized around, and it is made constantly because the number has the units of a rate and is the biggest one on the page.

## The approach to it is the expensive part

The published Vogel case, open flow 1500.000000 stb/d at 2400 psia, needs 936.931688 psia at 1200.0000 stb/d and 370.820393 psia at 1425.0000 stb/d. Its slope runs -0.91168461 psi per stb/d at 75.0000 stb/d and -3.57770876 at 1425.0000. The published Fetkovich case runs -10.47290881 psi per stb/d at 6.2383 stb/d and -66.86393931 at 118.5280, against an open flow of 124.766308 stb/d.

## It moves with the reservoir pressure

Taking the published Vogel case from 2400 psia to 1800 psia takes its open flow from 1500.000000 to 632.812500 stb/d. Taking the Fetkovich case from 3500 psia to 2500 psia takes 124.766308 to 49.625684 stb/d. The straight line goes 5760.000000 to 3600.000000 between 3200 and 2000 psia, and the composite 2533.333333 to 1813.333333 between 3000 and 2400 psia. An open flow without its reservoir pressure names an undefined quantity.

## It is a property of the family, not of the well

From one test of 720 stb/d at 2380 psia, BONNY-7 gives 5480.000000, 3233.247201 and 4324.444444 stb/d. From one test of 2400 stb/d at 2180 psia, FORCADOS-3 gives 5797.402597, 3946.987127 and 4135.949669 stb/d. Same well, same test, same day.

## It does not say the well flows

A published node case with an open flow of 600.000000 stb/d is recorded dead, with zero crossings, because the outflow requirement stands above the inflow at every rate. That relation would still report 600.000000 stb/d if asked, because the question was about the reservoir.

## What to say instead

For the completion, quote the index in stb/d/psi with a note of where the test sat: BONNY-7's was not below its bubble point, FORCADOS-3's was. For a plan, quote a rate with its required pressure as a pair, such as BONNY-7 at 2595 stb/d needing 1442.500000 psia. For how hard a well is worked, quote the fraction of open flow, which is the one job the number does well.

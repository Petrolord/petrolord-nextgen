# The numbers that travel

Six things leave an inflow analysis. Everything else on the list can be rebuilt from the first of them, and nothing on the list can rebuild it.

{{panel:pd-ipr-explorer}}

## The relation

A family, a reservoir pressure, a bubble point and the calibrating test. BONNY-7: composite, 2740 psia, 1300 psia, 720 stb/d at 2380 psia. FORCADOS-3: composite, 3720 psia, 2450 psia, 2400 stb/d at 2180 psia. From those four, every other number follows.

## The productivity index, stb/d/psi

2.00000000 on BONNY-7, 1.57194033 on FORCADOS-3, 1.8 and 1.2 on the published straight-line and composite cases, and 0.98684211 on the composite calibrated from a test below its bubble point.

Two things travel with it. The units, since the reciprocal in psi per stb/d also gets used. And where the test sat, since a below-bubble test read as a straight line gives 1.55844156 stb/d/psi on FORCADOS-3 against 1.57194033, an error of -0.01349877, while BONNY-7 gives 2.00000000 either way with an error of 0.00000000.

## The reservoir pressure, psia

2740 and 3720 on the two wells; 3200, 2400, 3000, 3500, 2800 and 4000 on the published cases. In psia, never psig. It carries a date whether or not anybody writes one: the published straight line reads an open flow of 5760.000000 stb/d at 3200 psia and 3600.000000 at 2000 psia.

## The bubble point, psia

1300 on BONNY-7 with a saturated share of 0.33401850, 2450 on FORCADOS-3 with 0.51731419, 2000 on the published composite. A bubble point of 0 psia, which the published straight-line, Vogel, Fetkovich and Jones cases all carry, is a modelling statement and should travel labelled as one.

## The open flow

4324.444444 and 4135.949669 stb/d on the two wells; 5760.000000, 1500.000000, 2533.333333, 124.766308 and 1098.809017 stb/d and 13289.296319, 5596.679697 and 88.806747 Mscf/d on the published cases. Its job downstream is normalising: 0.04994861, 0.29992292, 0.45000000, 0.60007708, 0.74992292, 0.90000000 and 0.97006680 on BONNY-7 against 0.05004896, 0.30005201, 0.44995712, 0.60010401, 0.75000913, 0.89991424 and 0.97003115 on FORCADOS-3.

## The inverse reading and the slope

The inverse travels as pairs, because a pair is checkable: BONNY-7 needs 1767.000000 psia at 1946 stb/d, 1442.500000 at 2595 and 1105.576792 at 3243. FORCADOS-3 needs 2536.112823 psia at 1861 stb/d, 2121.481541 at 2482 and 1622.391151 at 3102.

The slope is the price of one more barrel: -0.50000000 psi per stb/d on BONNY-7 above the bubble point, then -0.57666066 at 3243 stb/d and -1.57442483 at 4195; -0.63615646 on FORCADOS-3, then -0.72224059, -0.90914074, -1.41044926 and -2.41200162 at 2482, 3102, 3722 and 4012 stb/d.

## What must not travel alone

An index without where the test sat. An open flow without its reservoir pressure and family. Any reading without its model: three families reproduce BONNY-7's test at 720.000000 stb/d and then disagree by 1155.555556 and 1091.197244 stb/d at 0 psia.

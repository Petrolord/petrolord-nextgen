# What a reading cannot tell you

A reading is a property of the relation you calibrated, not of the well.

## It cannot tell you the family is right

BONNY-7, calibrated three ways from one test of 720 stb/d at 2380 psia:

| 2380 | 720.000000 | 720.000000 | 720.000000 |
| 1957 | 1566.000000 | 1451.888372 | 1566.000000 |
| 1300 | 2880.000000 | 2344.186047 | 2880.000000 |
| 1174 | 3132.000000 | 2481.320930 | 3121.144615 |

| 391 | 4698.000000 | 3088.297674 | 4133.021538 |
| 0 | 5480.000000 | 3233.247201 | 4324.444444 |

They agree by construction at the test, which is the one pressure you can check against a measurement. Vogel is 535.813953 stb/d under the composite at 1300 psia and 1091.197244 under at 0 psia; the straight line is 564.978462 over at 391 psia and 1155.555556 over at 0 psia. The evidence about which family fits lives where no measurement exists.

## It cannot tell you the test was clean

FORCADOS-3's test of 2400 stb/d at 2180 psia sits below its bubble point of 2450 psia. Its straight line reproduces that test exactly and still backs out 1.55844156 stb/d/psi against the composite's 1.57194033, an error of -0.01349877 stb/d/psi. It then reads -14.349195 stb/d against the composite at 2657 psia, -17.143440 at 2450 psia, then 180.248054 at 1594 psia, 512.714276 at 1063 psia and 1661.452928 at 0 psia. It crosses over, so agreeing at one pressure proves nothing about another.

BONNY-7's test sits above its bubble point, and both families back out 2.00000000 stb/d/psi with an error of 0.00000000.

Both readings print with the same number of decimals.

## It cannot tell you what precision it deserves

BONNY-7's 3121.144615 stb/d rests on four inputs: a test rate of 720 stb/d, a test pressure of 2380 psia, a reservoir pressure of 2740 psia and a bubble point of 1300 psia. Keep full precision inside the work and choose the reported precision from those.

## It cannot tell you the well will flow

There is no tubing, no wellhead and no lift in the relation, so it cannot know whether anything can hold the sandface where it is describing. There is also no clock: water breakthrough, packed perforations and growing skin move the real curve and appear nowhere until somebody takes a new test.

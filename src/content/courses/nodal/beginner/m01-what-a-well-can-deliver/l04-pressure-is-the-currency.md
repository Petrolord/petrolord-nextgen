# Pressure is the currency

Rate is bought with drawdown, and the price rises as you spend.

{{panel:pd-ipr-explorer}}

## The ladder

BONNY-7 has a reservoir pressure of 2740 psia and a bubble point of 1300 psia, and was tested at 720 stb/d and 2380 psia.

| Flowing pressure, psia | Drawdown, psi | Rate, stb/d |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2740 | 0.0000 | 0.000000 |
| 2380 | 360.0000 | 720.000000 |
| 1566 | 1174.0000 | 2348.000000 |
| 1300 | 1440.0000 | 2880.000000 |
| 1174 | 1566.0000 | 3121.144615 |
| 783 | 1957.0000 | 3731.238291 |
| 391 | 2349.0000 | 4133.021538 |
| 0 | 2740.0000 | 4324.444444 |

The first 360 psi buys 720.000000 stb/d. The last slice, from 391 psia to 0 psia, lifts the well only from 4133.021538 to 4324.444444 stb/d.

## The price, printed

The inflow slope is the psi given up for one more barrel a day. On BONNY-7 it is minus 0.50000000 psi per stb/d at 216, 649, 1297, 1946 and 2595 stb/d, then minus 0.57666066 at 3243 stb/d, minus 0.90088746 at 3892 stb/d and minus 1.57442483 at 4195 stb/d.

The flat stretch is exact rather than approximate. Minus one over the productivity index of 2.00000000 stb/d/psi is minus 0.50000000 psi per stb/d, and the curve is that line while the flowing pressure stays above 1300 psia.

FORCADOS-3 says the same at its own pressures: minus 0.63615646 psi per stb/d at 207, 620, 1241 and 1861 stb/d, then minus 0.72224059 at 2482, minus 0.90914074 at 3102, minus 1.41044926 at 3722 and minus 2.41200162 at 4012 stb/d.

## Where the last barrels are

On BONNY-7, 0.60007708 of the open flow costs a fall to 1442.500000 psia, and reaching 0.97006680 of it costs a further fall to 301.955326 psia: a comparable slice of pressure for a much smaller slice of rate. FORCADOS-3 reads 0.75000913 of its open flow at 1622.391151 psia and 0.97003115 at 420.700695 psia.

## The mistake

Reasoning linearly from one test. BONNY-7 gave 720 stb/d on 360 psi of drawdown; it reached 4133.021538 stb/d only at 2349 psi, and those two proportions are not the same. The flat price ends at the bubble point, reached at 1440 psi of drawdown and 2880.000000 stb/d.

## What drawdown will not say

Whether the completion tolerates it: there is no sand criterion, no coning limit and no damage model in an inflow curve. Whether the two pressures share a datum. And how long, since the reservoir pressure is a snapshot, and the published straight line case shifted to 2000 psia holds its index for an open flow of 3600.000000 stb/d.

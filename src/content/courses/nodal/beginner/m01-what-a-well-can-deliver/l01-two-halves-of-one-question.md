# Two halves of one question

Ask what a well will produce and you have asked two questions, because the reservoir and the tubing answer separately and only one rate satisfies both.

## The two statements

The reservoir delivers a rate at a flowing bottomhole pressure, and delivers more as that pressure falls. The tubing requires a bottomhole pressure to lift a rate, and that requirement is not monotonic. The well produces where the two name the same pressure, and nowhere else.

This tier owns the first statement. Rate runs horizontally in stb/d, bottomhole pressure vertically in psia, absolute and never gauge, alongside ft, degF, in and stb/d/psi. The engine converts nothing and does not warn you.

## BONNY-7

Reservoir pressure 2740 psia, bubble point 1300 psia, tested at 720 stb/d with a flowing pressure of 2380 psia, a drawdown of 360 psi. From those four numbers the inflow curve reaches an absolute open flow of 4324.444444 stb/d at a flowing pressure of zero.

Against a wellhead pressure of 420 psia the well runs at 1355.714057 stb/d and 2062.142971 psia, a drawdown of 677.857029 psi, which is 0.31350017 of the open flow. Its dead column is 2570 psia, standing 170 psi below the reservoir pressure, so the reservoir wins at zero rate and the well starts on its own.

The gap between 1355.714057 and 4324.444444 stb/d is not waste. It is the tubing. Drop the wellhead to 280 psia and the same inflow curve gives 1424.991968 stb/d at 2027.504016 psia; raise it to 490 psia and it gives 1319.581001 stb/d at 2080.209500 psia.

## The residual

Outflow requirement minus inflow supply at a rate. On BONNY-7 it is minus 192.336636 psi at 4.324444 stb/d, reaches minus 989.578610 psi at 477.119848 stb/d, and is 12517.903995 psi at the highest sampled rate: one sign change, one crossing.

The published deadWell case never turns. At 300 stb/d its inflow is 600.000000 psia against an outflow of 1890.000000 psia. Status dead, zero crossings, on a curve whose absolute open flow is 600.000000 stb/d. Dead describes the pair, never the reservoir.

## What this half refuses

It knows no time: one reservoir pressure, and no decline. It knows no tubing: 4324.444444 stb/d assumes a sandface at zero psia, which no completion produces. And it reproduces its own test exactly whatever family built it, so that agreement is never evidence.

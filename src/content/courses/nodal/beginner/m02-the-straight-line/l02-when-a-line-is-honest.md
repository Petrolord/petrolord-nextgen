# When a line is honest

Above the bubble point the straight line is not an approximation to the composite. It is the composite, exactly, and the evidence is a column of zeros.

{{panel:pd-ipr-explorer}}

## The claim

The composite is a straight line above the bubble point and a Vogel curve below, joined where they meet, so above the join there is nothing in it but the line. If the flowing pressure will never fall below the bubble point, choosing between the two is a choice of name.

## The evidence

BONNY-7 has a reservoir pressure of 2740 psia, a bubble point of 1300 psia and an index of 2.00000000 stb/d/psi, and was tested at 720 stb/d and 2380 psia, above the bubble point. Both families calibrated from that one test:

| 1566 | 2348.000000 | 2348.000000 | 0.000000 |

The last zero is at 1300 psia, the bubble point. The first departure is 10.855385 stb/d, which no chart would separate, and it grows without limit.

## The slope says it without a second model

BONNY-7's slope is minus 0.50000000 psi per stb/d at 216, 649, 1297, 1946 and 2595 stb/d, whose flowing pressures are 2632.000000, 2415.500000, 2091.500000, 1767.000000 and 1442.500000 psia, all above 1300 psia. Then minus 0.57666066 at 3243 stb/d, at 1105.576792 psia.

FORCADOS-3, with an index nobody would have chosen, does the same. Minus one over its 1.57194033 stb/d/psi is minus 0.63615646 psi per stb/d, and that is its slope at 207, 620, 1241 and 1861 stb/d, before minus 0.72224059 at 2482 stb/d.

## The two questions

Is the reservoir pressure above the bubble point. If not, there is no linear block anywhere.

Will the flowing pressure stay above it at the rates you intend. BONNY-7's rate at 1300 psia is 2880.000000 stb/d, so anything below that is exact. FORCADOS-3's rate at 2450 psia is only 1996.364220 stb/d, and it was tested at 2400 stb/d, already past the end.

## What it refuses

The straight line is defined down to zero psia and takes no bubble point, so it reads 4698.000000 stb/d at 391 psia against a true 4133.021538 stb/d with nothing announcing the extrapolation.

And where it is exact it is exact about the model. Both families share one index, so a wrong index makes them wrong together, in the same direction, and their agreement is a fact about two formulas rather than evidence about a reservoir.

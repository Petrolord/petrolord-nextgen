# Where the line breaks

Three failures, each with its own signature in the slope column and its own direction of error.

{{panel:pd-ipr-explorer}}

## Phase change

BONNY-7's straight line and composite agree to 0.000000 stb/d at 2740, 2380, 1957, 1566 and 1300 psia, then part: 3132.000000 against 3121.144615 stb/d at 1174 psia, 3914.000000 against 3731.238291 at 783 psia, 4698.000000 against 4133.021538 at 391 psia, 5480.000000 against 4324.444444 at 0 psia.

The signature is a slope that holds and then moves: minus 0.50000000 psi per stb/d at 216, 1297, 1946 and 2595 stb/d, then minus 0.57666066 at 3243 and minus 1.57442483 at 4195 stb/d. The break is at a pressure, and it is the bubble point.

## Turbulence

The Jones case takes a of 0.9 and b of 0.0015 against 2800 psia for an open flow of 1098.809017 stb/d.

| Flowing pressure, psia | Rate, stb/d |
| --- | --- |
| 2520 | 225.991128 |
| 2100 | 446.100976 |
| 1400 | 711.599394 |
| 700 | 920.655562 |
| 280 | 1030.413470 |

Its bubble point is 0 psia, so there is no phase change anywhere, and it still bends: minus 1.06482135 psi per stb/d at 54.9405 stb/d, minus 1.55928541 at 219.7618, minus 2.54821353 at 549.4045, minus 3.53714164 at 879.0472, minus 4.03160570 at 1043.8686. Fetkovich, also at a bubble point of 0 psia, runs from minus 10.47290881 at 6.2383 stb/d to minus 66.86393931 at 118.5280 stb/d.

A bending curve is not proof of a bubble point. The signature is a slope already moving at the first sampled rate, with no flat stretch at all.

## Extrapolation

FORCADOS-3's only measurement is 2400 stb/d at 2180 psia, and its straight line claims 5797.402597 stb/d at 0 psia against a composite 4135.949669 stb/d, a difference of 1661.452928 stb/d.

Every family extrapolates confidently, and this failure has no signature at all, because extrapolation looks exactly like interpolation from the inside. The only defence is knowing where the measurement was.

## Gas, where the line never existed

Gas deliverability is not linear in drawdown. The Rawlins and Schellhardt case at 4000 psia has an open flow of 13289.296319 Mscf/d; a strongly turbulent case of the same family, C of 0.004 and n of 0.62 against 3200 psia, has 88.806747 Mscf/d and reads 47.136086 Mscf/d at 2560 psia, 74.299168 at 1600 psia and 86.587286 at 640 psia. Everything below 640 psia adds almost nothing.

## What the engine will not catch

It warns about a Fetkovich n outside 0.5 to 1.0, and about Jones coefficients missing their own test point by more than 2 percent of the reservoir pressure. It does not warn about a wrong family, and one test has no curvature to diagnose one from.

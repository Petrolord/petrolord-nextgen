# The Ekene field and its projects

{{panel:prms-classification-calculator}}

Every worked example in this course comes from one field, Ekene. It is invented for teaching: a stated script writes it, and the file carries its own warning. Keeping to one field means the same projects meet you in every module, from their class in this tier to their totals in the Expert tier. The file opens with this statement:

> SYNTHETIC teaching data for the Ekene field (ours). No real company, field, licence, price, cost, reserves figure or regulator decision.

## The field and its operator

The Ekene field sits on the Ekene petroleum mining lease, both synthetic. The operator is EKO, Ekene Operator (synthetic), with a working interest of 70.000000 percent, and money is in US dollars. At this tier only the facts that set a class matter; the working interest comes back in the Professional tier.

## What the file states, and what it leaves to other courses

The file states the facts of each project: its discovery status, its recovery project, its sub-class, each commerciality criterion, its economic status and its chances. It also states estimates, forecasts, prices and costs for the later tiers. It does not work any of them out. The yearly forecasts come ready made; building them from well rates belongs to the decline curve analysis course. The estimates come ready made too; working them out from maps and rock properties belongs to the reservoir volumetrics course, and from pressure history to the material balance course. This course takes each as a stated input and classifies it.

## One prospect you may have met

Ekene Deep, EKN-6, is the same prospect the farm-out course prices, with the same chance of geologic discovery. Here it is classified; there it was traded.

## Reading a project in the calculator

The start selector of the classification calculator loads each Ekene project into the box as JSON. Above the box, a visible control shows each stated fact, or "not stated". The result shows the class and sub-class as tiles, a table of decisions with the section each applies, and the engine's reasons in order.

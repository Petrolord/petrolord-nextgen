# A significant discovery and its retention

{{panel:prms-classification-calculator}}

Some large discoveries cannot be developed yet, such as a gas find with no market. Nigerian law calls such a find a significant discovery and lets the licensee hold the area for a time. This lesson reads the Act and the Significant Crude Oil and Gas Discovery Regulations, 2023 (S.I. No. 37 of 2023, Official Gazette No. 111, Vol. 110, 20 June 2023, read on 2026-09-27).

## The figures the texts set

| figure | what it sets | where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 days | the notice of a significant discovery after the appraisal is complete | S.I. No. 37 of 2023 reg. 5(1) |
| 90 days | the Commission's approval of the notice | S.I. No. 37 of 2023 reg. 6(2) |
| 5 years | the shortest approval onshore and in shallow water | S.I. No. 37 of 2023 reg. 6(3) |
| 8 years | the shortest approval in deep water | S.I. No. 37 of 2023 reg. 6(3) |
| 10 years | the longest retention from the declaration | PIA 2021 s.78(9) |

## What makes a discovery significant

The Act describes a significant gas discovery as substantial and potentially commercial, but not yet declarable as commercial:

> "“significant gas discovery” means a discovery of natural gas that is substantial in terms of reserves and is potentially commercial, but cannot be declared commercial for one or more of the following reasons" (PIA s.318, "significant gas discovery")

Two reasons concern markets:

> "(a) no markets for natural gas within Nigeria ; (b) export markets need to be identified and developed ;" (PIA s.318, "significant gas discovery", (a) and (b))

A significant crude oil discovery is defined alike, one reason being that the oil would pay only if developed with other discoveries (s.318). The Act uses "reserves" in its everyday sense here; in this course the quantities are not Reserves, since they are not yet commercial.

## Retention, and its end

The licensee may keep the area for a period the Commission sets:

> "which shall not be more than 10 years from the day the declaration was made" (PIA s.78(9))

The Regulations set the minimums of reg. 6(3) in the table. If no commercial discovery is declared by the end, the area goes:

> "(13) Where, upon the expiry of the retention period determined under subsection (9), the licensee has not declared a commercial discovery, the area declared under subsection (9) shall be immediately relinquished by the licensee." (PIA s.78(13))

## Ekene East gas

EKN-3, a Contingent Resources project held back among other things by its market, is stated as a significant gas discovery declared 3 years ago. The engine's note ends "3 years since the declaration". The course's case "A significant discovery eleven years on" states a significant crude oil discovery 11 years on, and the note adds, in the engine's words, "11 years since the declaration: the retention period has ended, so the area is relinquished unless a commercial discovery was declared (s.78(13))". At 10 years the note carries no such warning: the tenth year is inside the period.

## Significant is never commercial

A project that meets every commerciality criterion cannot carry a significant declaration, and the engine refuses it:

> nigeria.declaration must be "commercial-discovery" for a project that meets every commerciality criterion: a significant discovery cannot be declared commercial (PIA 2021 s.318) and a discovery of no interest is not being developed (s.78(8)(c)); got "significant-gas-discovery"

# Writing the reserves report

{{panel:prms-aggregation-calculator}}

The engine writes no reserves report. It returns figures with their reasons and its basis, and the person who signs the report decides what the figures mean. This lesson lists what a report built on this engine names, and the conventions it must declare because a different choice would move a figure.

## What the report names

- The field and each project, with its discovery status, recovery project, class and sub-class, and the blockers of every Contingent project.
- Every source applied, with its edition, its licence and the date it was read.
- The effective date.
- The three technical forecasts and where they came from.
- The prices, costs, royalty and its form, tax, working interest, licence expiry and renewal expectation, reporting basis, discount rate and BOE factor.
- Each case's economic limit, the rule it rests on and the years cut.
- The categories in both forms on the stated basis.
- Each aggregation with its level, distributions, correlation, seed and draws, the arithmetic sums and what may be reported.
- Each risked figure with its chances.
- The reconciliation with its headings and whether it closes.
- Each reading the figures rest on.

## Conventions that are choices

| convention | the engine's choice |
| --- | --- |
| the class | Reserves only when every commerciality criterion and the firm intention are met with established technology |
| the chance of commerciality | Pg x Pd for Prospective, Pd for Contingent, none for Reserves |
| percentile labels | P90 the low case, P10 the high case |
| the economic limit | the canonical trailing trim, checked against the PRMS peak, refused where they disagree |
| the economic test | undiscounted net cash flow after tax and abandonment above 0 |
| 1P when the low case fails | 0, with 2P and 3P kept |
| entitlement | a royalty interest deducted from volumes; a production tax deducts no volume |
| the Monte Carlo low | the 0.1 quantile of the sampled totals |
| a risked mean | the sum of chance of commerciality times mean |
| reconciliation headings and signs | production out of every category; divestments subtracted; revisions and transfers signed |

Each is cited in the engine's basis or stated as a reading. A report that quotes an arithmetic 1P above the field level, a risked mean or a replacement ratio says which of these it rests on.

## Words the report uses

The vocabulary of this course holds in a report too. "Reserves" is the PRMS class; a national or company figure is "reported reserves", with its date and who reported it. "P90" is always the low estimate. "Proved" is the cumulative 1P, and the increment is Proved (P1). An economic limit names its rule and its case. "Risked" names its chance. Entitlement names its basis.

## Nigeria

The Nigerian Upstream Petroleum (Commercial) Regulations, 2025 (S.I. No. 7 of 2025, Official Gazette No. 84, Vol. 112, 5 May 2025) ask for a status report that includes a statement of the reserves situation; the course takes that by concept. No gazetted NUPRC reserves reporting regulation or booking guideline was found on the Commission's list read on 2026-09-27, so this course states no Nigerian booking rule. A report for a Nigerian licence names the PRMS classes it used and says the same.

## What a report does not claim

A class is what the stated facts give. A category is a stated estimate put in its place. A total is a sum or a sampled distribution of stated distributions. A reconciliation is stated movements added up. None is an audit of the facts, a forecast of what a field will produce, or a regulator's decision.

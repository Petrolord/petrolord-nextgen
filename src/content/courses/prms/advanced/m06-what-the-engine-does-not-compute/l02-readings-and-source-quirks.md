# Readings and source quirks

{{panel:prms-aggregation-calculator}}

A reading is a choice the engine makes where the texts leave room, stated in its own reasons or basis with the alternative it names. A quirk is something odd in a source text itself. This course teaches both openly, because a figure that rests on either is quoted with it.

## The readings

Each reading below is the engine's stated choice. None is keyed as the law, and no graded figure in this course moves under any alternative:

| reading | where it acts | the alternative the engine names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the five-year benchmark is met at five years | classify | five years exactly not met |
| an undiscounted net cash flow of exactly 0 is not economic | economicLimit | exactly 0 economic |
| the economic test includes abandonment | economicLimit | the test before the abandonment cost |
| the canonical trailing trim is the economic limit, checked against the PRMS peak | economicLimit | the limit placed at the PRMS cumulative peak |
| production comes out of every Reserves category alike | reconcile | none offered: production by category is refused |
| the replacement ratio counts every movement other than production | reconcile | the additions alone |
| the life index is read at the best estimate | reconcile | the life index at the low estimate |
| a difference equal to the tolerance closes | reconcile | a difference equal to the tolerance does not close |
| the Monte Carlo low is the 0.1 quantile of the totals | aggregate | the low read at the 0.9 quantile |

One more reading belongs to a golden case: the 2011 Guidelines' Table 6.2 read with normal marginals, the Guidelines' own symmetric reading. The lognormal reading beside it gives a different sampled total, and the course names both.

## The quirks

A table and a figure with two numbers. The Guidelines for Application of the PRMS (November 2011) print the arithmetic Proved of blocks A and B as 71.8 in Table 6.2 and as 72 on Fig. 6.5: the figure rounds the table. The engine returns 71.800000.

A superseded edition. The 2011 Guidelines were revised in 2022; the 2022 edition is sold and was not read. Lessons name the 2011 edition by its year.

A standard read from a bilingual edition. The English text of SPE-PRMS 2018 (June 2018; CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) was read from the SPE-hosted English-Chinese edition, whose English is the 2018 text; section numbers are the 2018 numbers.

An erratum on the word economic. The consolidated PRMS errata (May 2022) revise the glossary entry for economic to a zero percent discount rate, matching PRMS 3.1.2.1: the economic test is undiscounted, which is the test the engine applies.

A release that prints one figure short. As its page renders, the NUPRC release of 1 April 2026 prints the 2P crude oil figure truncated, beside a condensate figure of 5.92 and a total of 37.01 billion barrels. The course uses only the gas figures and the total, and computes nothing from the truncated one.

A file name that differs from the title. The Commission hosts S.I. No. 37 of 2023 under a file name that reads "Significant Crude Oil and Gas Recovery Regulations"; the gazette title is the Significant Crude Oil and Gas Discovery Regulations, 2023, and the course cites the gazette title.

Two capitals for one term. The Act's interpretation section prints "Significant crude oil discovery" with a capital S and "significant gas discovery" in lower case, and the course quotes each as printed.

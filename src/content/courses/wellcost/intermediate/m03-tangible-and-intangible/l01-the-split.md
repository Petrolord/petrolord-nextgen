# The split

One base cost, cut a second time along a seam that has nothing to do with how the lines bill.

{{panel:wc-afe-explorer}}

## Two subtotals from one list

The engine walks the item list once, prices every line, and then sums the amounts twice. Lines marked `tangible` go into one subtotal and lines marked `intangible` into the other. Adding the two gives the base.

On the golden the tangible subtotal is 1,050,000 USD and the intangible subtotal is 4,330,000 USD. Those add to the 5,380,000 USD base exactly, because every line has a category and no line has two.

As fractions of the base, tangible is 19.52 percent and intangible is 80.48 percent.

## Which lines are which

Two of the eight lines are tangible: casing and accessories at 800,000 USD, and the wellhead at 250,000 USD. Both are lump lines.

The other six are intangible: the rig dayrate, the integrated services spread, mud and consumables, cementing services, wireline logging and completion services. Between them they span all three bases.

## Where contingency sits

The contingency is not in either subtotal. The engine computes it as a fraction of the base after both sums are complete, and returns it as its own figure.

On the golden that is 538,000 USD at a fraction of 0.1, which sits outside the 1,050,000 and outside the 4,330,000 and is added to the base to reach the 5,918,000 USD total.

That is the standard convention and it has a reason. Contingency is a provision against work not yet identified, and until it is spent nobody knows whether it will buy steel or buy days. Assigning it to a category in advance would be a guess dressed as a classification.

## What the split does not do

It does not change any amount. Recategorise the wellhead from tangible to intangible and the base stays at 5,380,000 USD, the contingency stays at 538,000 USD and the total stays at 5,918,000 USD. Only the two subtotals move, by 250,000 USD each, in opposite directions.

So the split is invisible to the authority you are asking for. It matters entirely downstream of the signature, which is the subject of the third lesson in this module.

## Exercise

Read the tangible and intangible subtotals off the panel and confirm they add to the base.

Then move one line from one category to the other and record which two figures change and which three do not.

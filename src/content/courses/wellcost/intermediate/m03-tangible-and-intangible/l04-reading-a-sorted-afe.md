# Reading a sorted AFE

Sort the lines by amount and the sheet tells you in about fifteen seconds where the well's money and its risk actually are.

## The golden, sorted

| Line | Amount USD |
| --- | --- |
| Rig dayrate | 1,800,000 |
| Integrated services spread | 1,080,000 |
| Casing and accessories | 800,000 |
| Contingency | 538,000 |
| Completion services | 500,000 |
| Mud and consumables | 450,000 |
| Cementing services | 300,000 |
| Wellhead | 250,000 |
| Wireline logging | 200,000 |

Nine rows, of which eight are real lines and one is a provision.

## The three things to read first

**Where the mass is.** The top line alone is 33.46 percent of the base. The top three come to 3,680,000 USD of a 5,380,000 USD base. Whatever you do to the bottom four will not change this AFE much.

**What basis the top lines are on.** Here the top two are both per-day, which is 2,880,000 USD and 53.53 percent of the base. That single observation tells you this well's cost is mostly a schedule question before you look at anything else.

**Where contingency ranks.** On the golden it comes fourth, above five of the eight real lines. A provision sitting that high in the ordering is a statement about how much of the well is not yet defined, and it deserves the same scrutiny as the contracted lines above it.

## What the ordering will not tell you

It will not tell you the categories. Tangible and intangible are interleaved through the list, with the third and eighth rows tangible and everything else intangible. Sorting by amount deliberately destroys that grouping.

It will not tell you the timing either. The wellhead lands early on the cost curve and the completion lands last, and the sorted list has them four rows apart with no hint of it.

## The fast reading

Take the top lines until you have covered about two thirds of the base, note their bases, note where contingency fell, and stop. On the golden that is three lines, two of them per-day, and a provision at rank four.

Everything else is detail you can come back to once the two structural questions have been answered.

## Exercise

Sort the golden lines in the panel and mark the point where the cumulative total passes two thirds of the base.

Then note the basis of every line above that mark and say what the well is most exposed to.

# What decides which

One question, asked of the thing rather than of the invoice: at the end of the well, is it still down there?

{{panel:wc-afe-explorer}}

## The test

A tangible cost buys something that remains in or on the well and retains salvage value. An intangible cost buys something that is consumed in the act of making the well and cannot be recovered.

Ask it of the object, not of the contract. The question is not how you paid, or whether the price was fixed, or who supplied it. The question is whether anything durable survives the job.

## Applied to the golden

Casing and accessories at 800,000 USD is steel cemented into the hole. It stays. It is tangible.

The wellhead at 250,000 USD sits on top of the well for its whole producing life and can be pulled and reused. It stays. It is tangible.

Rig time is consumed the instant it passes. Mud is pumped, circulated, degraded and disposed of. A cementing job is a service performed once. A wireline run gathers data and leaves. Completion services are the labour of installing, not the hardware installed. None of that survives as a recoverable asset, so all of it is intangible, and it comes to 4,330,000 USD.

## The line that catches people

Completion services is intangible while the completion hardware would be tangible. Same well, same phase, same vendor possibly, and opposite categories.

That is not an inconsistency. The packers, tubing and screens stay in the hole and can be recovered. The engineering, rig time and pumping that put them there cannot. A real AFE splits the vendor into two lines for exactly this reason, and the golden carries only the services half.

## Why basis cannot answer it for you

On the golden both tangible lines happen to be lump, which tempts you to shortcut the test. Resist it. Three of the five lump lines are intangible, so lump tells you nothing about category.

The reverse shortcut is just as wrong. A drill pipe purchase on a footage contract would be per-metre and tangible, because the pipe survives.

## What about things that are damaged

Steel that is set and later milled out was still tangible when it was bought. The category records what the spend acquired, not what later happened to it.

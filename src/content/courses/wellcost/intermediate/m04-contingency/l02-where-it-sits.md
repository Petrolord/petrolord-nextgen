# Where it sits

Contingency is applied to the base, it is applied last, and both of those facts have consequences.

{{panel:wc-afe-explorer}}

## The order of operations

The engine works in one direction and never doubles back.

1. The programme gives a schedule, and the schedule gives elapsed days and metres drilled.
2. Each item is priced on its own basis against those two numbers.
3. The items are summed by category into tangible and intangible.
4. Tangible plus intangible is the base.
5. The contingency fraction multiplies the base.
6. Base plus contingency is the total.

Contingency enters at step five, after everything else is known. Nothing downstream of it exists except the total.

## On the base, which means on everything

Applying the fraction to the base means applying it to every line without discrimination. On the golden estimate the base is 1,050,000 USD tangible plus 4,330,000 USD intangible, or 5,380,000 USD, and the 0.1 lands on all of it.

That includes the 800,000 USD of casing and accessories and the 250,000 USD wellhead, which are tangible lump items. Those are steel with a quoted price, ordered against a purchase order. A ten percent provision on a firm quote is a strange thing to defend, and the model makes no attempt to defend it. It offers one fraction and applies it flat.

## What the split does not include

The category shares are shares of the base, not of the total. On the golden they are 0.19516728624535315 tangible and 0.8048327137546468 intangible, and those two add to one. The contingency is outside them.

So an AFE has two different denominators on the same page. Read a percentage and ask which one it used.

## The provision inflates with the thing it covers

Because the fraction sits on the base and the base moves with the schedule, a slip enlarges the provision that was meant to absorb the slip.

| non-productive allowance | elapsed days | base USD | contingency USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.125 | 18 | 5,380,000 | 538,000 |
| 0.25 | 20 | 5,700,000 | 570,000 |
| 0.50 | 24 | 6,340,000 | 634,000 |

Read that carefully. The base at a 0.5 allowance is 6,340,000 USD, which is 960,000 USD above the golden base, and the provision has grown by 96,000 USD at the same time. The provision did not cover the slip. It rode on it.

That is a property of a percentage, not a property of the well, and it is the first honest argument against sizing a provision that way.

## Exercise

In the panel, raise the non-productive allowance and watch the base and the contingency move together. Confirm the contingency stays at one tenth of whatever the base has become.

Then take the tangible fraction from the panel and say, in words, what would have to change for a percentage contingency on a firm steel quote to be defensible.

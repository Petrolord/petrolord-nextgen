# The curve against the plan

Being above the line at a given hour is a fact about money and time together, and on its own it does not tell you whether the well is in trouble.

{{panel:wc-risk-explorer}}

## What above the line means

Above the line means more has been committed by this hour than the programme said would be. There are two quite different ways to get there.

You are late. The activities are taking longer than costed, the per day lines keep billing, and the money climbs while the depth does not. On the golden estimate the per day exposure is 2,880,000 USD of the 5,380,000 USD base, a fraction of 0.5353159851301115, so time slip converts into money at 160,000 USD per extra day.

Or you are on depth and simply spending faster, because a lump landed early or a rate was understated. That is an estimating difference, not an operational one, and it does not compound.

The curve cannot separate these two on its own. You need the depth beside it.

## What below the line means

Below the line is not a saving until you know the depth. A well that is under the cost line because it has not drilled the section yet is behind, not ahead, and the money it has not spent is still owed.

The honest reading pairs the cost curve with the time depth curve. Under on cost and on depth is a genuine gain. Under on cost and under on depth is a deferral wearing the wrong clothes.

## What it does not mean

It does not tell you whether you will land inside the authority. The curve ends at 5,380,000 USD, the base. The approved figure is 5,918,000 USD, because 538,000 USD of contingency sits outside the curve entirely. A well finishing a little above the line can still be inside its AFE.

It also does not mean the plan was wrong. The base curve already carries a non-productive allowance, a fraction of 0.125 stretched onto productive time, and that allowance is spread evenly through the schedule. Real trouble is lumpy. A well can sit above the line for two days after a stuck pipe event and come back to it, having used the allowance the plan gave it.

## Exercise

Take the checkpoint at 211.5 hours and construct two ways a well could sit above it by the same amount, one operational and one estimating.

Then state which of the three cost bases in the golden AFE would move under each of your two stories, and which would not move at all.

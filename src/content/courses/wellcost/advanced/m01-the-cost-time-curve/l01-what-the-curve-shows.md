# What the curve shows

The cost time curve plots money committed against hours elapsed, which makes it the chart the rig floor reads rather than the one the office signs.

{{panel:wc-risk-explorer}}

## Both axes are elapsed

The horizontal axis is elapsed hours from spud. The vertical axis is cumulative base cost in USD. On the golden programme the curve starts at 0 hours and 0 USD and ends at 432 hours and 5,380,000 USD.

Those 432 hours are 18 days. Only 384 of them are productive. The other 48 are the non-productive allowance, and the curve makes no distinction between the two.

That is deliberate. Money leaves the account during non-productive hours exactly as it does during rotating hours. An axis that counted only productive time would flatter every well drawn on it, and would flatter the troubled ones most.

## Why it is an operations chart

The AFE is the finance document. It lists what each line bills, sorts the spend into tangible and intangible, adds a provision and carries a total forward for approval. It answers the question of who may spend how much.

The curve answers a different question, and it answers it at every hour of the well. At elapsed hour 211.5 the plan says 2,260,000 USD should have been committed. The rig either has or has not. That comparison is available to a night supervisor with a watch on the wall, and it needs no accounting close to produce.

This is why the curve belongs in the operations room. It turns a static authority into something you can check against the clock.

## The shape is the programme

The curve is not smooth. It is a run of straight segments, one per activity, with vertical steps wherever a lump line lands.

| Activity | Ends at hr | Cumulative USD | Lump step USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| a2, drill 26in surface hole | 49.5 | 405,000 | 0 |
| a3, run and cement 20in casing | 63 | 745,000 | 250,000 |
| a4, drill 17.5in intermediate hole | 175.5 | 1,720,000 | 0 |

Segment a2 climbs 225,000 USD over 22.5 hours, because two rates are running at once: the per day lines, and the mud line that bills with metres drilled. Segment a3 drills nothing, so only the per day lines accrue, and the 250,000 USD wellhead arrives as a step at its end.

Read the shape and you can name the activity without being told which one it is.

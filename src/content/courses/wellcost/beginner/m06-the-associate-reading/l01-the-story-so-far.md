# The story so far

Five modules, and not one currency symbol among them.

## The one idea

Every number in this tier is an hour. Every hour is either bought with rate and planning, or lost to trouble. That is the whole of it.

What you have built is a ledger of where a well's days go, assembled before anybody has priced a single one of them. The pricing is a later and shorter argument, because it has only two quantities to work with.

## What each module established

**Module 1.** Time is the cost. The engine builds a deterministic schedule from activities and closed forms, and refuses what it cannot represent rather than guessing. Two currencies come out, hours and metres.

**Module 2.** A programme is an ordered list of activities of four kinds: drill, trip, casing and flat. An activity carries a duration and a depth, and no cost at all. Keeping money out of the activity is what lets one schedule be priced three ways later.

**Module 3.** Three closed forms and one stated duration. Drilling is interval over rate, a hyperbola, so a third of the rate triples the hours: 1,500 m at 5 metres per hour is 300 hours against 100 at 15. A round trip is exactly twice the one way. A casing run is one leg plus a flat term no running speed can touch: at 2,000 m with a 19 hour flat term, going from 100 to 5,000 metres per hour moves the activity from 39 hours only to 19.4.

**Module 4.** The allowance is a stretch on productive time, not a share of elapsed. A fraction f gives a non-productive share of elapsed of f over one plus f, so the golden's 12.5 percent is 11.11 percent of elapsed. Productive hours never move when the allowance does, and every activity keeps its share of the total exactly. Only 57.29 percent of elapsed time is drilling.

**Module 5.** Twenty-four hours to the day, once, with no rounding. Drilled metres counts new hole only, and equals total depth here because the engine refuses to represent a redrill. Six totals come out, and two of them travel: 18 days and 3,000 metres.

## The numbers to carry

- The golden programme: 384 productive hours, 48 non-productive, 432 elapsed, 18 days, 3,000 m.
- The stretch identity: share of elapsed is f over one plus f, and 0.125 gives exactly one ninth.
- The round trip factor: exactly 2.
- Drilling share of elapsed time on the golden case: 57.29 percent.

## What this tier does not cover

Money. Nothing here has priced anything. The two numbers that leave, days and metres, are quantities waiting for rates.
